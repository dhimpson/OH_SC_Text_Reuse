SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Area of Wellall of that area in the Township of Beech Springs, in the County of Spartanburg, embraced in the following circle, to wit: Having the Richmond and Danville Railroad depot of the Town of Wellford as a centre, and embracing an area described by the radius of two and one - half miles from such centre, be, and the Creation and same is hereby, created a special School District in the County of Spartanburg, to be known as the Wellford School District.
