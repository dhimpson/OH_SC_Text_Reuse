SEC. 7. That said Board may assemble at any time and elect Boarda clerk and superintendent of city schools, whose duties and certain officompensation shall be prescribed by said Board: Provided, That no members of the Board of School Commissioners shall be eligible to any office within the provisions of this Act. The Powers and said Board shall have the following powers and duties: (1) To duties of discharge the same duties in the said new School District which are required of School Trustees in the Counties. (2) To determine the studies and class - books to be used in the school. (3) To cause examinations of teachers for the city schools. (4) To elect and dismiss superintendents and teachers, prescribe their duties and terms of office, and make rules for the government of schools. (5) To fill vacancies occurring in the Board by death, resignation, departure from the State, or refusal to qualify. (6) To establish, when expedient, a normal school department, and to grant diplomas. (7) To make, if required, an annual report to the Superintendent of Education. (8) To determine the manner in which the tax hereinbefore provided for shall be expended in maintaining the city schools, A. D. 1908. and to sell or exchange any school property, real or personal, for which there is no further use as school property.
