SECTION 11. That the moneys herein before set apart to be used as contingent funds and for other purposes by the various officers of the State Government shall be duly accounted for; and such officers shall make a detailed statement of the disposition made thereof to the General Assembly at the next regular session on or before the first day of December, A. D. 1888: Provided, That no officer authorized to make contracts or draw funds from said appropriations shall expend or make contracts for expending more than has been specified for any purpose by this act.
