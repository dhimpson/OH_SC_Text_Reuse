Sec. 10. It shall be the duty of the Adjutant and Inspector General 1. To keep and preserve the arms, accoutrements, ammunition, and other military property of the State 2. To keep on file in his office all rolls, returns, and reports made to him 3. To authenticate with his official seal all papers issued from his office which by law may require authentication under such seal 4. To keep an account of all moneys received or disbursed by him. 5. To issue all orders of the Commander-in-Chief relating to the militia, and to keep a record thereof.
