SEC. 41. The military units of the National Guard shall be composed and organized as follows: Infantry - A company shall consist of: one Captain, one First Lieutenant, one Second Lieutenant, one First Sergeant, one Quartermaster Sergeant, four Sergeants, six Corporals, two cooks, two musicians, one artificer, and not to exceed forty - three privates. Total maximum enlisted, sixty. The minimum strength of a company shall be forty enlisted men. A battalion shall consist of: one Major, one Battalion Adjutant (First Lieutenant), one Battalion Quartermaster and Commissary (Second Lieutenant), one Battalion Sergeant Major, and from two to four companies. A regiment shall consist of: one Colonel, one Lieutenant Colonel, one Adjutant (Captain), one Quartermaster (Captain), one Commissary (Captain), one Surgeon (Major), one Assistant Surgeon (Captain), one Assistant Surgeon (First Lieutenant), one Chaplain, one Sergeant Major, one Quartermaster Sergeant, one Commissary Sergeant, two Color Sergeants, a band consisting of one chief musician, one principal musician, one Drum Major, four Sergeants, eight Corporals, one cook, and not to exceed twelve privates, and a hospital corps detachment consisting of one Sergeant, first class, of the hospital corps; six privates, first class, of the hospital corps; three privates of the hospital corps, and three battalions. When it is impracticable to assign three battalions to a regiment, it may be composed of a smaller number, not less than two, each of which shall consist of four companies; but where four companies cannot be assigned to a battalion, it may be composed of three companies, with the fourth company skeletonized. Cavalry, a Cavalry - A troop shall consist of: one Captain, one First Lieutenant, one Second Lieutenant, one First Sergeant, one Quartermaster Sergeant, four Sergeants, six Corporals, two cooks, two farriers and blacksmiths, one saddler, one wagoner, two trumpeters, and not to exceed forty privates. Total enlisted maximum, sixty. The minimum strength of a troop shall be forty enlisted men. if of the grade of First Lieutenant. A Veterinary Surgeon must be a graduate of an incorporated school of veterinary science. A Chaplain must be a regularly ordained minister of some religious denomination. A squadron shall consist of: one Major, one Squadron Adjutant (First Lieutenant), one Squadron Quartermaster and A squadron. Commissary (Second Lieutenant), one Assistant Surgeon (Captain), one Veterinary Surgeon (First Lieutenant), one Squadron Sergeant Major, one Sergeant of the hospital corps, two privates, first class, of the hospital corps; one private of the hospital corps, and four troops. When it shall be impracticable to assign four troops to a squadron, it may be composed of a smaller number, not less than two. Troops which cannot be combined into squadrons shall exist as separate troops. Field Artillery - A battery shall consist of: one Captain, two Field artillery. First Lieutenants, one Second Lieutenant, one First Sergeant, one Quartermaster Sergeant, one Stable Sergeant, four Sergeants, six Corporals, two cooks, two artificers, two musicians, and not to exceed forty - one privates. Total maximum enlisted, sixty. The minimum strength of a battery shall be forty enlisted men. Coast Artillery - A company shall consist of: one Captain, one Coast artillery.
