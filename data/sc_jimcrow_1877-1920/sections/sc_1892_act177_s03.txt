SEC. 3. Long Ferry: -For every four - horse carriage or vehicle, Long ferry two dollars; for every two - horse carriage or vehicle, one dollar and twenty - five cents for every foot passenger, twelve and onehalf cents; for every horse and rider, twenty - five cents; for every head of cattle, goats and hogs, twelve and one - half cents: Provided, That children going to and coming from school, and voters going to and coming from the polls, militia going to and returning from muster, and clergymen, be passed free of toll.
