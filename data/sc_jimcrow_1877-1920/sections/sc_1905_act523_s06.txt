SEC. 6. That any balance or surplus arising from the tax Surplus. collected from property in said School District, remaining of the funds arising from the said annual tax levied, be set aside as a sinking fund for the liquidation of the said bonds at maturity, or for the payment of any part of said bonds that owners thereof may be willing to surrender; and the said Trustees may safely invest the sinking fund in good interest - bearing securities from time to time.
