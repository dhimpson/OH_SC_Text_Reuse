SEC. 2. The mode of his appointment shall be as follows: AppointThe Audubon Society of South Carolina shall send in the ment; bond; name of suitable person to the Governor. On approving this oath of office. nomination, the Governor shall transmit the name, with his recommendation, to the State Senate. Should the Senate approve, the Chief Game Warden shall assume the duties of his office by taking oath to adminster the laws before any Notary, Magistrate or Judge or Clerk of Court, and shall furnish bond in the sum of $ 2,000.00, to be approved by the Secretary of State. Should the Governor or the Senate not approve any nomination sent them, the name shall be returned to the said Audubon Society of South Carolina until a person suitable to both the Governor and the Senate shall have been named.
