§ 9. Plans and Specifications. - That the said Supervisor and County Board of Commissioners shall employ an architect to draw plans and specifications for said jail and shall advertise for bids for the erection of said jail, in accordance with said plans and specifications. Said bids shall be filed with the Clerk of said Supervisor and Board of Commissioners and shall be opened publicly on the day specified in said advertisement. The said Supervisor and Board of Commissioners shall accept the lowest responsible bid for said work: Provided, That any and all bids may be rejected by the said Supervisor and Board of Commissioners.
