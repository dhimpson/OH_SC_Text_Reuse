Section 1. Section 2937, Civil Code, 1912, Volume I, as Amended by Act (1918, XXX Stats., 851) Amended— Municipal Tax Levies. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That Section 2937 of Volume I, Code of Laws, as amended by Act No. 499 of the Acts of 1918, entitled " An Act to amend Section 2937, Volume I, Code of Laws, as amended by an Act entitled ' An Act to amend an Act entitled " An Act to amend Section 2937 of Volume I, Code of Laws, 1912, relating to the right of municipalities to impose a tax for municipal purposes, " so as to include the city of Greenville, ' approved the 11th day of February, 1915, so as to exclude the city of Greenville from the operation of said Act, approved February 11, 1916, with reference to municipal tax levy of the city of Spartanburg, approved the 9th day of March, 1918, " be amended by striking out the words beginning on line 9 of said section as amended, to wit: " Provided, That the provisions of this section shall apply only to the cities of Anderson and Spartanburg, " and also by striking out the words beginning on line 16 of said section as amended by said Act, to wit: " and nothing herein contained shall be construed as amending or changing the law now in force in regard to other municipalities, " and by changing the semicolon on line 16 after the word " purposes " to a period, so that said section, when so amended, shall read as follows: " Section 2937. All municipal corporations containing more than five thousand inhabitants shall have the power to impose by ordinance an annual tax sufficient to meet all expenses of the municipality, including current expenses, any school tax provided by law, fixed charges in the way of interest and sinking fund on bond, and any other matter for which such municipality may be now or hereafter liable: Provided, That ten days ' public notice thereof shall be given, setting forth the amount levied for the several purposes for which levy may be made: Provided, The levy in the city of Spartanburg shall not exceed fifteen (15) mills, and out of the taxes so levied the City Council of Spartanburg is authorized to appropriate such amount as may be necessary to pay the rents due by the said city under its contracts for rental of certain lands used by United States Government for camp purposes. "
