SEC. 18. That the presiding Judge of the said County Court Bailiffs; how may appoint a sufficient number of bailiffs, not exceeding three, pay of. to attend upon the said Court, and be subject to the orders thereof. That such bailiffs shall receive as a compensation for their service one dollar per day for the time actually engaged, and shall not be retained in attendance upon the Court longer than the exigencies of the business of the Court shall require.
