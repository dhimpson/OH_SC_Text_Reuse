Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the Secretary of State be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to confer with the South Carolina Monumental Association, or the representative thereof, and to select a suitable spot upon the State House grounds upon which to erect a monument to the Confederate dead. Sec. 2. And the said Secretary of State is further authorized and empowered in behalf of the State to grant to the said South Carolina Monumental Association all the rights and privileges necessary for the erection of said monument, upon such terms and such conditions as to him may seem fit.
