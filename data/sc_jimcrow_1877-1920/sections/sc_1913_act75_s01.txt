Section 1. Subsupervisors for Abbeville County. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That at the regular election, in 1916, there shall be elected at the general election in Abbeville county, by the qualified electors of said county, two men, who shall be known as Subsupervisors of Abbeville county, who shall hold their office for the term of four years, and until their successors are elected and qualified.
