SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Act creating State of South Carolina, That Section 2.be stricken out and lice Commisinsert in lieu thereof the following: " At the next regular election for the purpose of elecitng a Mayor and Aldermen for the City of Greenville there shall be elected by the qualified electors thereof five members of the Board of Police Commissioners, two of whom shall be elected for two years and three of whom shall be elected for four years, or until their successors shall be elected and qualify; at each subsequent regular city election successors to the members of said Board of Police Commissioners whose terms have expired shall be elected for a term of four years.
