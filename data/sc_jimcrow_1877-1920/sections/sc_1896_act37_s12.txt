Section 12. Said Town Council shall have power to armit on convicrest, and upon conviction to commit to the town guard house for a space of time not exceeding five days, and to fine not exceeding twenty dollars, any person or persons who may be guilty of disorderly conduct in said town, to the annoyance of the citizens thereof, and it shall be the duty of the Marshal of the town to make such arrest, and to call to his assistance the posse comitatus, if necessary, and upon failure to perform the said duty he shall be fined in a sum not exceeding twenty dollars for each and every offense.
