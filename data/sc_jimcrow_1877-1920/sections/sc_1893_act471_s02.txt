SEC. 2. The limits of said school district shall be as follows: A line drawn from the centre of the crossing of Cleveland and Main streets, in the town of Elloree, due North two and one - half miles long; also a line due South two and one half miles long, another East two and one - half miles long, and another due West two and one - half miles long. With these limes as diameters construct a square whose sides shall each be five miles long.
