SEC. 2. That every man who while he was a bona fide soldier limbs to be or sailor in the service in this State, or in the Confederate States, in the war between the States, lost a limb, shall, upon application to the State Pension Board, be entitled to be furnished with a first - class artificial limb to supply such loss: Provided. Such application must be approved by the Pension Commissioners of the County in which he resides.
