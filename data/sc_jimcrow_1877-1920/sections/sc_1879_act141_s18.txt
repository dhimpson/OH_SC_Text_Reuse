SEC. 18. That the sum of four thousand five hundred dollars be, A. D. 1879. and the same is hereby, appropriated, to wit: 1. For the salary of Special Commissioner to settle the consolidated debt of the State Court of Claims in accordance with the Supreme Court decision, two thousand five hundred dollars. 2. For clerk hire, eighteen hundred dollars. 3. For contingent expenses, two hundred dollars. To be paid on the application of said Commissioner on the warrants of the Comptroller General. SFC. 19. On all consols, without exception, now outstanding, which the Commissioner appointed or to be appointed for this purpose at the present session of the General Assembly shall ascertain to be valid in whole in accordance with the decision of the Supreme Court of in whole. this State rendered in September, 1879, in the bond cases, and which the said Commissioner shall ascertain have not been issued, in whole or in part, in exchange for either of the classes of bonds which the Supreme Court, in the said decision, has declared are not valid obligations of the State, or for the coupons of said bonds: Provided, That the certificates of the said Commissioner, setting forth that the said consols, the interest on which is herein provided for, are free from any percentage of disability, shall be filed in the office of the State Treasurer. 2. On the consols which shall be issued at the Treasury in lieu of and in substitution for all such consols, without exception, now outstanding, as the Commissioner hereinbefore mentioned may ascertain to be valid in part under the decision of the Supreme Consols valid Court aforesaid, the said consols (with the proper coupons attached to bonds) to be so issued, in lieu of consols now outstanding, upon the surrender of the latter at the Treasury for the amount of the valid portion of the same, and in accordance with the certificates of the said Commissioner only, which certificates shall be filed at the Treasury, and shall set forth the amount of the valid portion of the principal and of the interest due thereon. 3. On all consols which shall hereafter be issued under the Act " to provide for the funding of the interest past due on the valid consolidated debt of the State, " approved 24th December, 1878, or ed interest. which may be issued by virtue of such Act or Joint Resolution as may be passed at the present session of the General Assembly for the purpose of authorizing the funding of past due interest as aforesaid: Provided, That the said consols shall be issued in accordance with the certificates of the Commissioner aforesaid, filed at the Treasury, and setting forth that the said past due interest has accrued on valid consols, or otherwise specifying what portion of same is valid, principal and interest. 4. On the consols which have been issued at the Treasury since exchanged and the 31st October, 1879, or which may hereafter be issued, in exchange for such fundable bonds and stock certificates issued under Acts passed prior and subsequent to the 1st January, 1866, now outstanding and not yet consolidated, (or for the coupons and interest thereof,) as are valid obligations of the State, and which are so recognized as valid by the decision of the Supreme Court aforesaid filed in September, 1879: Provided, That such exchange shall be made in accordance with the certificates of the Commissioner aforesaid filed at the Treasury, and which shall specify the valid amount of the principal and interest to be consolidated in each case. 5. On the consols which the said Commissioner may ascertain to be valid in part according to the decision of the Supreme Court, viz., the detached coupons and outstanding interest orders thereof which, having passed out of the hands of the holders of the bonds and stock certificates to which they relate or for other cause may not be surrendered by the holders of the said bonds and certificates, the said designated detached coupons and outstanding interest orders to be payable on their surrender and cancellation at the Treasury for such portion only of the same as is valid and justly due by the State, as shall appear from the certificate of the Commissioner to be filed at the Treasury. 6. On all other consols or valid portion thereof which may not be included in the foregoing specification which the aforesaid Comsioner may ascertain to be so valid in whole or in part under the decision of the Supreme Court referred to, the interest on which shall be paid in accordance with the certificates of the Commissioner, filed at the Treasury, and setting forth the interest due 1st January and 1st July, 1879 and 1880, on the said consols.
