SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Lexington State of South Carolina, That the Board of Trustees of the County, to call meeting of taxSchool District of the town of Swansea, in the County of Lexington, shall call a meeting of the taxpayers of the district, to be held not later than July 1st of each year: Provided, That no person shall be allowed to participate or vote at such meeting who is not a bona fide taxpayer and a qualified elector within such district.
