SEC. 15. That in anticipation of the collection of the taxes for certain hereinbefore levied, the Governor and State Treasurer be, and purposes. they are hereby, empowered to borrow on the credit of the State, so much money as may be needed to meet promptly at maturity the interest which shall mature on the first day of July, 1899, and on the first day of January, 1900, on the valid debt of the State, and to pay the current expenses of the State government for the present fiscal year: Provided, That the sum so borrowed shall in no event exceed the amount of three hundred thousand dollars.
