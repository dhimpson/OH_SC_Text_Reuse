Sec. 5. That the Clerk of Council shall annually, on the second Tuesday in March and the Wednesday and Thursday succeeding, between the hours of nine o'clock A. M. and three o'clock P. M., at the office of said Clerk, open a list for Registration. the registration of qualified voters of said city, on which list shall be shown in writing the name, age, occupation and ward of residence of the voter, and any person who shall attain the age of twenty - one years after the said time of registration and before the next succeeding day of election, and is not otherwise disqualified, shall be allowed to register at such registration. Notice of said registration shall be given for two weeks previous thereto by publication in one or more newspapers published in said city of Abbeville and by posting on the court house door. The certificate of registration shall be signed by said Clerk and issued to each person registering in the following form: This is to certify that... of Ward...... of the city of Abbeville... years of age, and entitled to vote in the municipal election of said city. Clerk of Council. After each municipal election only such persons shall be required to register as shall become entitled to vote at each Subsequent approaching election. At each subsequent registration the said Clerk shall revise the registration lists and erase therefrom the names of all registered voters who have died or become disqualified to vote since the previous election. The said registration lists shall at all times be open to the inspection of the qualified voters of the city, shall be carefully preserved by the Clerk of Council, and on annual or any special elections shall be placed in the possession of the Managers appointed to conduct the election, and at the close of such election shall be returned by said Managers to said Clerk.
