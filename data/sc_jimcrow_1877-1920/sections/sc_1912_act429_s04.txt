Sec. 4. If after due hearing the Court shall assume the supervision, care or custody of the child, it shall require the parent or parents or persons with whom it resides, and in. the discretion of the Court, in addition to these or in lieu thereof, some suitable and proper person or persons to serve without compensation, and to be known as probation officer or officers, to look after said child and to report to the Court for as long a period and. as often as the Court shall require, as to the treatment and conduct of the said child; and the child shall report to said probation officer or officers from time to time, as the Court may prescribe; and said probation officer or officers shall at all times have the right and power to investigate the surroundings, conditions, treatment and conduct of the child, and report same to the Court.
