Sec. 6. That the County Auditor of Lancaster County shall receive the sum of twenty-five ($25) dollars and the County Auditor of Kershaw County the sum of fifteen ($15) dollars for his services in assessing the tax that may be levied under the provisions hereof, the same to be paid out of the funds so levied and collected. Sec. 7. That all Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
