SEC. 5. 1. That the following sums, if so much be necessary, be, and the same are hereby, appropriated for miscellaneous expenses, namely: 2. For the pay of extra clerical services in the Engrossing Department, one hundred dollars; to be paid on the joint certificates of the presiding officers of the two Houses, signed by the presiding officers thereof and attested by the Clerks, upon the application of the Attorney General. 3. For preparing for the printer, indexing and making marginal indexing Acts. notes of the Acts and Joint Resolutions of the Regular Session of 1886, to be done under the supervision of the State Reporter, two hundred and fifty dollars. To pay the deficiency in the amount appropriated by a Resolution of the House of Representatives passed December, 1885, to compensate John D. Brown, Sergeant at Arms, for services in removing furniture and fixtures from the Hall of the House of Representatives, in consequence of work to be done on State House, thirty dollars, to be paid out of the contingent fund of the House of Representatives for this session.
