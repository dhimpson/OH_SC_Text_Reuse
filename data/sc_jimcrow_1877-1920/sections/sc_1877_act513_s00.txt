AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED " AN ACT TO ESTABLISH BY LAW THE VOTING PRECINCTS IN THE VARIOUS COUNTIES IN THIS STATE. Secrion 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of thesame, That hereafter at all general elections held in this State the same shall be conducted at the voting precincts which are hereby fixed by law in the various Counties, cities and ‘towns of this State, and the number, locations and names of which said precincts for said Counties, cities and towns respectively are and shall be as hereinafter designated, to wit: In the County of Abbeville there shall be sixteen voting precincts, as follows Abbeville Court House, Calhoun’s Mills, Willington, Cokesbury, Greenwood, Ninety-Six, Donaldsville, Due West, Centreville, Childs’ Cross Roads, Lowndesville, Magnolia, Long Cane, Cedar Springs, McKetrick’s Mills and Bradley’s Mill In the County of Aiken there shall be eighteen voting precincts, as follows: Aiken Court House, Graniteville, Langley, Summerhill] Page & Hankerson’s Store, Silverton Academy, Lawtown Wells, Windsor, Montmorenci, Jordan’s Mill, John Hutto’s, H. D. Ott’s, Sally’s Store, Jones’ Cross Roads, Kneece’s Mills, Creed’s Store, Miles’ Mill and Fountain Academy. In the County of Anderson there shall be twenty voting precincta, as follows: Anderson Court House, Pendleton, Sandy Springs, Centreville, Bethany, Ball’s Mills, Greenwood, Brushy Creek, Williamston, Belton, Honea Path, Craytonville, Milford’s, Long’s Shop, Dark Corner, Williford’s Store, Brown & Farmer's Store, G. W. Maret’s, George Stevenson’s and Holland’s Store. In the County of Barnwell there shall be nineteen voting precincts, as follows: Williston, Blackviile, Graham’s, Bamberg, Allendale, Baldoc, Millett, Robbins, Mixson’s Mill, Red Oak, Barnwell, Barker’s Mill, Buford’s Bridge, George’s Creek, Ehrhardt’s Mill, Three Mile Sycamore, Elk and Farrell’s Store. In the County of Beaufort there shall be nine voting precincts, as follows: Beaufort, Port Royal, Grahamville, Bellinger’s Hill, Mitchellville, Brick Church, Garden’s Corner, Hardeeville and Bluffton. In the County of Charleston, exclusive of the city limits, there shall be twenty-one voting precincts, as follows: Christ Church Parish, Moultrieville, Mount Pleasant, St. Thomas and St. Dennis, Muster House, Ben. Potter’s, (at Half-Way Creek,) St. James’ Santee, Thirty-two Mile House, Henderson’s Store, St. Stephen’s Parish, St. Stephen’s Depot, Blackville, St. John’s Berkeley, Calamus Pond, Black Oak, Strawberry Ferry, Pinopolis, Biggin Church, St. Andrew’s Parish, Dill’s Bluff, John’s Island, Andell’s Store, Wadmalaw Island, Enterprise Landing, St. John’s Colleton Parish, Berlen’s Store, (at steamboat landing,) St. James’ Goose Creek Parish, Hickory Bend, Cooper’s Store, Twenty-two Mile House, on State road, and at Cross Roads. In the city of Charleston there shall be eleven voting precincts: City—Ward 1, City Hall. Ward 2, Court House. Ward 3, first precinct, Market Hall; second, Palmetto engine house. Ward 4, first, Hope engine house, Archdale street; second, Stonewall engine house, corner George and College streets. Ward 5, Eagle engine house, Meeting street. Ward 6, Washington engine house, Vanderhorst street; second, Marion engine house, Cannon street. Ward 7, Ashley engine house, Columbus street. Ward 8, Niagara engine house, Spring street.
