Sec. 6. That the Intendant and two Wardens, or, in the absence of the Intendant, three Wardens, shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business; and in case a vacancy shall occur in the office of Intendant or Warden by death, resignation, removal, or for any other cause, an election to fill such vacancy shall be held by order of the Intendant, or Intendant pro tem., ten days’ public notice being previously given, which election shall be conducted in all respects and be subject to all the regulations hereinafter provided for the regular elections; and in case of sickness, temporary absence or disability of the Intendant, the Wardens shall be empowered to elect one of their number to act as Intendant pro tem.
