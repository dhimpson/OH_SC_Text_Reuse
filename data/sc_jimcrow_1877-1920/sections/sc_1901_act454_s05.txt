SEC. 5. If a majority of the ballots cast at said election be Tax levy. for the exemption, then a tax of five mills shall be levied on all the property situate within said exempted territory, and all live stock kept therein. If the majority of the ballots cast be against the exemption, the said exemption shall be repealed, to take effect the 31st December, 1901, and no tax for the maintenance of the fence around said territory shall be levied.
