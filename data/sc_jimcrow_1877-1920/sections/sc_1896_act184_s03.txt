Section 9. That the Trustees herein provided for shall have exclusive control of the free school in said school district, and shall be invested with all the powers of School Trustees under the general school law. And may also fix the terms on which new residents of said school district shall be admitted to the schools thereof. The said Board of Trustees shall make a full report of receipts and disbursements and of all their transactions pertaining to the schools of said school district each year.
