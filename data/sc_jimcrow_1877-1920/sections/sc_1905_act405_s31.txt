SEC. 32. The Adjutant and Inspector General, with the approval of the Commander - in - Chief, is hereby authorized to make such rules and regulations from time to time as he may deem expedient, and when promulgated shall have full force and effect as the militia laws of the State. But the rules and regulations in force at the time of the passage of this Act shall remain in force until new rules and regulations are approved and promulgated. ARTICLE III. THE QUARTERMASTER GENERAL.
