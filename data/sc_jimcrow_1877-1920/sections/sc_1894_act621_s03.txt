Sec. 3. It shall be the duty of the Managers of said election to declare and return the result of said election to the said City Council, with all convenient speed; and if a majority of the votes cast in said election is for said tax, then it shall be the duty of said City Council, by ordinance, to immedi ately levy a tax of two mills for the year 1895 upon all taxable property in said city of Anderson, which tax shall be collected by the Treasurer of the said city at the same time that he collects other city taxes; and ever thereafter it shall be the duty of the said City Council, at the same time that other city taxes are levied, to levy annually a school tax of not less than two nor more than three mills upon all the taxable property in said city.
