SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the for women in State of South Carolina, That from and after the passage of establishments this Act the hours of labor of women employed in mercantile establishments in this State shall be limited to sixty hours per week, not to exceed twelve hours in any one day, and that such female employees shall not be required to work later than the hour of ten o'clock p. m. The enforcement of this law is placed in the hands of the Commissioner and Inspectors.
