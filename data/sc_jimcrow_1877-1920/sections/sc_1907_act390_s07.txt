SEC. 7. That Section 9 be amended to read as follows: " Section 9. That on or before the first Tuesday in October, Election 1907, and of each and every October thereafter, the City Counvates. cial of Greenville shall, by resolution or ordinance, fix the compensation and the number of officers and men of the police force of the city of Greenville for the ensuing year, the year to be reckoned from the first Tuesday in October until the first Tuesday of the following October of each and every year, and shall immediately transmit a certified account of their action to the Board of Police Commissioners. The Board of Police Commissioners shall, on the first Tuesday in October, 1907, and upon the first Tuesday in October in each and every year thereafter, proceed to elect by a majority viva voce vote the officers and privates provided for by the City Council, said officers and privates to be citizens of Greenville, to take the same oath now taken by the police of said city, and to hold their offices from that date, unless sooner removed by said Board. Said officers and privates shall be paid monthly by check, order or certificate upon the Mayor of said city (said check, order or certificate to be signed by the President and Secretary of the said Board), in accordance with the rate of compensation fixed by the City Council, and subject to such deductions for fines or lost time as may have been ordered by said Board. ”
