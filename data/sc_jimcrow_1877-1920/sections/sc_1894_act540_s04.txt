Sec. 3. That the proviso of Section 1263 of the General Statutes of South Carolina of 1882, being Section 1361 of the Revised Statutes of 1893, shall not apply to the ports of Georgetown and Beaufort; and that so much of the oath required by Section 1264 of the General Statutes of 1882, being Section 1362 of the Revised Statutes of 1893, as provides that applicants for a license or branch shall swear that they will not “at the same time be owner, part owner, nor be interested, directly or indirectly, in more than two boats employed in the business of the bar and harbor of Charleston (Beaufort or Georgetown as the case may be)” shall not hereafter be administered to applicants for license or branch at the ports of Georgetown and Beaufort.
