SEC. 3. That the said corporation shall have all the rights, powers and privileges set forth and granted by an Act entitled " An Act to provide for and regulate the incorporation of building and loan associations in this State, " approved December 22d, 1885, and shall be subject to all the provisions and limitations therein contained, except that the corporation shall have Loans to others the power to loan whatever amount may, at any time, be on hand to others than stockholders whenever the same shall remain unproductive and uncalled for the space of one week.
