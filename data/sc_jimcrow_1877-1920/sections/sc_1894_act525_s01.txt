Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That from and after the approval of this Act the names and locations of the voting precincts of this State shall be and remain the same as provided by law before the passage of this Act, except in the Counties hereinafter mentioned, in which names and location of voting precincts shall be as herein established. Aiken—In the County of Aiken there shall be voting precincts as follows: Aiken Court House, Vaucluse, Graniteville, Langley, Hamburg, Page and Hankinson’s Store, Silverton Academy, Love Townwell, Windsor, Montmorenci, Sally, Oak Grove School House, Sallv’s Store, Jones’ Cross Roads, Kneece’s Mill, Creed’s Store, Sunnyside, Fountain Academy, Central School House, and Eureka. Abbeville—In the County of Abbeville there shall be voting precincts as follows: Abbeville Court House, Mount Carmel, Willington, Cokesbury, Greenwood, Ninety-Six, Donaldsville, Due West, Antreville, Lowndesville, Magnolia, Long Cane Clatworthy’s Cross Roads, McCormick, Hodges, Troy, and Bradley’s. Barnwell—In the County of Barnwell there shall be voting precincts as follows: Williston, Blackville, Denmark, Bamberg, Allendale, Beldoc, Millet, Robbins, Mixon’s Mill, Barnwell, Deer’s Mill, Olar, Ulmer, George’s Creek, Ehrhardt’s Mill, Sycamore, Elko, Ferrell’s Store, and Midway. Beaufort—In the County of Beaufort there shall be voting precincts as follows: Beaufort, Port Royal, Grahamville. Genesis Cross Roads, Bellinger’s Hill, Brick Church, Gardner’s Corner, Hardeeville, Bluffton, Gray’s Hill, Lady Island, Chisolm’s Landing, Old Pocataligo, Levy’s Cross Roads, Paris Island, Clem’s Store on Hilton Liead. Wylie’s Store, Landsford, Cornwall, Richbourg, Wylie’s Mill, Academy, Baton Rouge, and Edgemoor.
