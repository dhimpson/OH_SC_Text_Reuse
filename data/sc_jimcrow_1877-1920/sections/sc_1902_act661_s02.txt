Sec. 2. That the Trustees of said School District are hereby Notice and required to order such election, and to give ten days previous notice, such notice to be posted in three public places in the said School District, and also to be published in one or more of the newspapers published in the said County of Orangeburg, of the time and place of such election, appoint the managers, prescribe the form of ballot, receive the returns and declare the result.
