SEC. 48. The commanding officer of the brigade shall issue the orders for the election of Colonels and Lieutenant Colonels of regiments, for the Majors of separate battalions and squadrons, and also for the election of the Captains and Lieutenants of separate troops, batteries or companies. Colonels of regiments shall issue the orders for the election of all officers of their respective regiments. The Majors of separate battalions or squadrons shall issue the orders for the election of the company officers of their respective organizations.
