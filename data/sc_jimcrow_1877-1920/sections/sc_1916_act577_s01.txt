Section 1. Previous Registrations Canceled.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That from and after the passage of this Act, all previous registration of electors of Beaufort County be and the same are hereby cancelled and made and void. § 2. Provisions for New Registration.—That immediately after the passage of this Act it shall be the duty of the Board of Registration of Beaufort County to open the Books of registration and register the qualified electors of Beaufort County commencing a new series of numbers of the certificates of registration and for the purpose of enabling all the electors of Beaufort County to qualify and register and obtain registration certificates they are hereby required to open such books of registration at each polling place from the hours of Nine A. M. to Two P. M. and Four P. M. to Six P. M. on each day for the number of days hereinafter specified or required. § 3. Notice of Opening of Books to Be Given.—The said Board of Registration immediately upon the passage of this Act shall advertise in the Beaufort Gazette the time of opening the books of registration at each polling place in said county for not less than two weeks before opening said books at each polling place and shall keep the same open during the hours above mentioned and specified in Section 2 of this Act for a period of Ten days at Beaufort, Six days at Bluffton and Four days at Hardeeville and Two days at each of the other polling precincts in said county. § 4. Entries to Be Made.—It shall be the duty of the said Board of Registration to enter in the Books of Registration the serial number and date of each certificate issued and of each reissue thereafter and also the age, color and occupation of each elector at the time of such registration and of any subsequent reissue of a registration certificate. § 5. Issue of Duplicate Certificates.—Whenever a renewal of certificate is issued by reason of loss or destruction of the original, such renewal shall be a duplicate and bear the serial number of the original and have printed across its face the word “Duplicate.” § 6. Immediately Effective—This Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
