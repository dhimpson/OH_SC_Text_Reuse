SEC. 3. If either of the Supervisors shall fail or refuse to recommend a surveyor, as provided in Section 1, then the Governor shall appoint such surveyor; and the survey and location of the boundary line, as herein provided, shall be made by or before the first of October, A. D. 1905, and the report shall be made to the Governor not later than December 1st, A. D. 1905.
