SEC. 2. That when the persons answering the above description shall have assembled in public meeting they shall have power: First, to select a Chairman and Secretary, adjourn from time to time, decide what special tax, if any, shall be levied, and appropriate the same in such manner as they may think best for the educational interests of said School District: Provided, That no tax thus levied shall be repealed at any subsequent meeting within the same year; second, they shall also have power to elect a Board of three Trustees, the term of office of one of whom shall be for three years, dating from the election, A. D. 1904, of one of whom for two years, and of the remaining one of whom for one year from the same date; subsequent elections to be every year thereafter for the term of three years, to fill the vacancies to be caused by expiration of the terms of one of the Board, so that two of each Board may continue in office after each election.
