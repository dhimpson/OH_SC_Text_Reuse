Sec. 6. That the Mayor and Aldermen collectively and severally are hereby vested with the jurisdiction and powers within the city limits which Trial Justices now have or may hereafter have, except in civil actions; that they shall be designated as the City Council of Abbeville; may have a common seal; may sue and be sued; may purchase or receive by gift or devise, and hold, personal and real property, not to exceed in value in the aggregate at any one time $ 100,000.00, and may sell, alien or lease the same; may borrow money and execute evidences of debt, but such debts shall not exceed $ 10,000 at any one time; may make such ordinances respecting sidewalks, streets, drainage, natural water courses, powder house or houses for storing explosives, the interment of human bodies, the running of railroad cars or engines, butchers, livery stables, public drays or vehicles, live stock, places of amusement, public houses, saloons, auctioneers, itinerant tradesmen or peddlers, shows, circuses or exhibitions, and such other matters pertaining to the concerns of said corporation as shall be proper, necessary or advisable for the preservation or safety of the peace, order, comfort, health, convenience and welfare of the citiFines and zens of said city; may fix and impose fines and penalties for the violation of law or its ordinances, but no fine or penalty shall exceed one hundred dollars or imprisonment in the guard house for a longer period than thirty days, or by sentence to work on the streets for thirty days, and the judgment may be in the alternative; and may abate and remove all nuisances in the said city..
