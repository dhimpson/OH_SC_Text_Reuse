SEC. 3. That the following sums, if so much be necessary, be, and the same are hereby, appropriated to pay the expenses of the House of Representatives from the twenty seventh day of November, 1888, inclusive, to the close of the session, as follows: For the per diem, mileage, and stationery certificates of the members of the House of Representatives, twenty one thousand five hundred dollars; for the pay of John T. Sloan, Sr., Clerk of the House of Representatives, one thousand dollars; for the pay of W. McB. Sloan, Assistant Clerk of the House of Representatives, two hundred and fifty dollars; for the pay of C. B. Northrop, Journal Clerk of the House of Representatives, one hundred and forty dollars, and four dollars per day for the number of days he may be actually engaged in the work of the House of Representatives after the adjournment thereof, not to exceed twenty days, said number of days of such engagement to be certified by said Journal Clerk; for the pay of R. M. Anderson, Reading Clerk of the House of Representatives, two hundred and fifty dollars; for the pay of John D. Brown, Sergeant at Arms of the House of Representatives, two hundred and fifty dollars; for the pay of F. B. Gary, Bill Clerk of the House of Representatives, one hundred and forty dollars; for the pay of H. M. Davidson, Clerk of the Committee of Ways and Means of the House of Representatives, one hundred and forty dollars; for the extra pay of H. M. Davidson, Clerk of the Committee of Ways and Means of the House of Representatives, forty dollars; for the pay of W. H. Parker, Jr., Clerk of the Judiciary Committee of the House of Representatives, one hundred and forty dollars; for the pay of W. B. R. Gasque, Doorkeepers. Charles Frankes, and Christopher Haynesworth, Doorkeepers of the House of Representatives, each, eighty four dollars; for the pay of Wardlaw Moorman, James Furse, Francis Cantwell, Pages and Julian B. Weston, Pages of the House of Representatives, each, fifty six dollars; for the pay of Counts Cross, James Adamson, H. Thielman, and G. H. Fowler, Laborers of the House of Representatives, each, fifty six dollars; for the pay of Boston Parker and Albert Nance, Porters of the Committee Rooms of the House of Representatives, each, fifty six dollars; for the pay of R. W. Butler, Keeper of the Speaker's Room and Porter to the Clerk of the House of Representatives, one hundred and twelve dollars; for the pay of N. O. Pyles, Mail Carrier of the House of Representatives, eighty four dollars, and twenty eight dollars for services rendered as Mail Carrier to the Engrossing Department; for the incidental or contingent fund of the House of Representatives, eight hundred and fifty dollars, if so much be necessary, to be paid on certificates drawn by the Speaker of the House of Representatives and attested by the Clerk of the House of Representatives, on accounts audited by the Committee on Contingent Accounts and passed by the House of Representatives: Provided, That no part of said contingent fund be used in giving extra pay to any of the eployees of the House of Representatives.
