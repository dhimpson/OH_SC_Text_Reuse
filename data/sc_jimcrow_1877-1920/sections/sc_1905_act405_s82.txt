SEC. 84. Courts martial and courts of inquiry are authorized and empowered to preserve order during their sessions; and any person or persons who shall be guilty or disorderly, contemptuous or insolent behavior in, or use any insulting or contemptuous or indecorous language or expressions to or before any such court, in open court, intending to interrupt the proceedings or to impair the respect and authority of such courts, may be committed to the jail of the County in which said courts shall sit, for a time not exceeding twelve hours, by warrant under the hand and seal of the President of such court.
