SEC. 2. That the said town of Tirzah shall have all the rights. powers, and privileges, and be subject to all the provisions and limitations enumerated and contained in an Act entitled " An Act to provide for and regulate the incorporation of towns of 19 Stat., 174. less than one thousand inhabitants in this State, " approved 23d December, A. D. 1885, except where the same may be modified by this Act.
