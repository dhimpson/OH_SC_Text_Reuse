SECTION 1. That for the purpose of maintaining graded public schools in the Town of Timmonsville, and for promoting Area and their efficiency, the area embraced in all that part of Timmonsville Township, in the County of Darlington, lying between Sparrow Swamp and Lake Swamp, from the Northern boundary of the township to the Southern boundaries of the lands of the estate of McLendon, of Evander White and of the estate of R. B. Garner, now owned by C. W. Hewitt, including the Town of Timmonsville, is hereby declared to be a new school district, to A. D. 1886. be known as the School District of the Town of Timmonsville.
