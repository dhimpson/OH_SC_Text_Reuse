SECTION 3. Before entering upon the discharge of the Oaths of office duties of his office each Magistrate must take, in writing, the oath of office prescribed in the Constitution, and also the several additional oaths required to be taken by Trial Justices, before the Clerk of the Court of Common Pleas of the County, or, in case there be no such Clerk, before any one authorized to administer an oath, and must file the same with the Secretary of State.
