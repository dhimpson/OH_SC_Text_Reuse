SEC. 31. There shall be an Assistant Adjutant and Inspector Assistant AdGeneral, appointed and commissioned by the Governor, upon the recommendation of the Adjutant and Inspector General, who shall have the rank of Colonel, and whose salary shall be thirteen hundred and fifty dollars. He shall be, at the time of his appointment, an officer in the active service of the State. The Assistant Adjutant and Inspector General shall aid the Adjutant General, by the performance of such duties as may be assigned to him, and shall, in case of absence or inability of the Adjutant General, perform all or such portion of the duties of the Adjutant General as the latter may expressly delegate to him.
