SEC. 5. The said election shall, in all other respects, be held as Result of other elections under the general laws of this State, and the same regulations apply thereto in so far as is consistent with this Act. If the result of said election be declared by the Election Commissioners of said County to be against the issue of the coupon bonds, nothing further shall be done. If said election. shall be declared for the issue of the bonds, then the details in connection therewith shall be left to some succeeding session of the General Assembly of this State, but no coupon bonds shall be issued until the General Assembly shall hereafter prescribe the details of said issue, which shall not be prior to the 1st day of April, 1908. The proceeds arising from the sale of said coupon bonds shall be equally distributed among the townships in said County under a Commissioner to be provided therefor so as to give as many people as practicable benefit thereof. Approved the 15th day of February, A. D. 1907.
