SEC. 4. That for the purpose of paying the interest accruing Tax levy. on said bonds, and the principal when due, the Auditor of Lee County shall levy annually such tax on all the taxable property, real and personal, in said School District, as will yield a sum sufficient for said purposes, in addition to the tax now levied, for the purpose of erecting a public school building in said district, and the amount so levied shall be collected by the Treasurer of Lee County, as State, County and school taxes are collected, to be held and applied by him solely to the payment of the said interest or principal, if any be due, and shall be used for no other purpose.
