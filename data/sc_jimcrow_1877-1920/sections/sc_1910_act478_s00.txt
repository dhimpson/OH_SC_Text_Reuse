AN ACT TO SUBMIT TO THE QUALIFIED VOTERS OF GREENVILLE COUNTY THE QUESTION OF THE ASSUMPTION BY SAID COUNTY OF THE BONDED DEBT OF DUNKLIN AND OAKLAWN TOWNSHIPS, AND TO SUBMIT TO THE QUALI-FIED VOTERS OF LAURENS COUNTY THE QUESTION OF THE ASSUMPTION BY SAID COUNTY OF THE BONDED DEBT OF SULLIVAN TOWNSHIP, IN LAURENS COUNTY. State of South Carolina, That the question of the assumption by the County of Greenville of the bonded debt of Dunklin and Oaklawn Townships, in said county, issued in pursuance of the Act of 1906, XXIV Statutes at Large, 309, be submitted to the qualified voters of Greenville County at the general election to be held in 1910.
