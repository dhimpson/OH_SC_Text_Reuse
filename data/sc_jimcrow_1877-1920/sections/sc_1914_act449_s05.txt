§ 4. Compensation of Treasurer. - The City Treasurer shall receive as compensation for his services such reasonable sum, not exceeding one hundred and fifty dollars, as the Board may determine, and before entering upon his duties as Treasurer, he shall give an official bond, in the penal sum of two thousand dollars, to the said Board, with two good sureties, conditioned for the faithful performance of his duties. 26 - A #436 (p.848) 848 STATUTES AT LARGE
