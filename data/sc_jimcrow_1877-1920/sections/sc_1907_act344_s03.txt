SEC. 4. The County Commissioners of Spartanburg County are hereby authorized and required to furnish to the Election Commissioners of said County, in ample time before said election, a sufficient number of ballots, both for and against the issuing of the said coupon bonds. On one set of ballots shall be printed the words, " For Public Road Improvements Coupon Bonds, " and on the other set of ballots shall be printed the words, " Agaiust Public Road Improvement Coupon Bonds, " and the County Commissioners are authorized and required to pay the expenses of said printing out of the general County Funds.
