AN ACT SUBMITTING THE QUESTION OF THE REPEAL OF A CERTAIN ACT APPROVED DECEMBER 24, 1891, AND KNOWN AS ACT NO. 988, AND FOUND IN VOLUME XX, AT PAGE 1488, WHEREBY CERTAIN TERRITORY IN WILLIAMSBURG COUNTY IS EXEMPT FROM THE OPERATION OF THE GENERAL STOCK LAW DURING A CERTAIN PERIOD OF EACH YEAR, TO A SPECIAL ELECTION TO BE HELD IN SAID TERRITORY. Whereas, under and by a certain Act Approved December 24, 1891, and known as Act No. 988, and found in Volume XX, at page 1488, certain territory in Williamsburg County is exempt from the operation of the general stock law of the State from the 15th day of December of each year, until the 15th day of March following in each year.
