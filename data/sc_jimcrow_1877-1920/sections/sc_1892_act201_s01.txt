SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Bonds author - School District Number One (1) of the County of Kershaw is hereby permitted and authorized to issue ten thousand dollars ($ 10,000,00) of bonds, or a less amount, for the purpose of erecting and furnishing suitable school buildings in said School Rate of interDistrict, said bonds to bear interest at a rate not exceeding seven per centum (7 %) per annum: Provided, That a majority of the qualified voters of said school district shall be in favor of such issue, as expressed at an election held for that purpose.
