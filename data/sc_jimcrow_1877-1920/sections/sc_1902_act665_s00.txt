"AN ACT TO FURTHER AMEND THE ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT IN RICHLAND COUNTY, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A LOCAL TAX THEREIN,"" APPROVED DECEMBER 24, 1880, AS AMENDED BY ACTS APPROVED DECEMBER 17, 1881, DECEMBER 21, 1883, AND DECEMBER 23, 1893, WITH REFERENCE TO THE ELECTION OF SCHOOL COMMISSIONERS.
