AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION 3 OF AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF LAURENS TO ISSUE BONDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF ERECTING OR REPAIRING A SCHOOL BUILDING AND EQUIPPING THE SAME, AND TO REFUND BONDS HERETOFORE ISSUED,"" APPROVED THE 19TH DAY OF FEBRUARY, A. D. 1907, SO THAT THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF LAURENS MAY USE THE PROCEEDS FROM THE SALE OF BONDS HERETOFORE ISSUED UNDER THE SAID ACT FOR THE PURCHASE OF A NEW LOT, AND ERECTING A BUILDING THEREON.
