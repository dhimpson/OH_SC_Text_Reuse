SEC. 8. That it shall be the duty of the County Treasurer to place all monies belonging to the sinking funds hereinbefore provided for on deposit in some savings bank in this State, to be designated in writing jointly by the said Board of Trustees and the County Auditor, and all interest accruing thereon shall be likewise kept on deposit in such savings bank. That when the right of redemption shall arise, at the expiration of the period of twenty years, as fixed by this Act, the said County Treasurer shall be authorized and he is hereby directed, at the written request of the said Board of Trustees, through its Chairman and Secretary, to redeem the said bonds, or so many thereof as the sinking fund may be sufficient to pay; and the remainder of said issue, if any, may be redeemed from time to time thereafter at the option of said Board of Trustees, in such amounts as may be deemed expedient by them, and the Board may designate the bonds to be so redeemed.
