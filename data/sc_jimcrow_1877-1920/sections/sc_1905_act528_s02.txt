Section 7. The Auditor of Newberry County shall annually Tax levy. levy on the taxable property of said School District a tax of three - fourths of a mill for the purpose of creating a sinking fund for the payment of said bonds, which tax shall be collected by the Treasurer of Newberry County and shall be held by him for the purpose aforesaid: Provided, however, That said Trustees at the end of every five years shall have the right to order such sinking fund then accumulated paid pro rata on the bonds then outstanding or may buy and cancel so many of said bonds from time to time as said sinking fund will purchase: Provided, further, That the County Treasurer shall deposit said sinking fund at interest in such bank or banks as said Trustees may from time to time direct, until the same may be required by them for payment on, or investment in, said bonds as herein provided.
