SEC. 6. Said commission shall as soon as convenient, after appointment and commission, elect one of their number to act as chairman and treasurer, and another to act as secretary. The Compensaone elected to act as chairman and treasurer shall receive an tion thereof. amount sufficient to cover his actual expense only, not to exceed the sum of five dollars per day, and the other two the sum of three dollars per day, for not exceeding fifty days in any one year. The chairman and treasurer shall give bond in some approved surety company to the county in the sum of twenty thousand dollars, conditioned for the faithful performance of his duty, the cost of which shall be paid by the commission out of the funds herein provided. The commissioners herein provided for shall be appointed as soon as convenient after the passage of this Act.
