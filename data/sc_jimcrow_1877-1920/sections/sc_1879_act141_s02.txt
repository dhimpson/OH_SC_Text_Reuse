SEC. 2. That the sum of six thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated for the support of the Health Department of the State, namely: 1. For maintaining quarantine, one thousand dollars. 2. For the salary of the Health Officer of Charleston, one thousand dollars. 3. For the salary of the Health Officer of Hilton Head, six hundred dollars. 4. For the salary of the Health Officer of St. Helena, six hundred dollars. 5. For the salary of the Health Officer of Georgetown, four hundred dollars. 6. For the salary of the Keeper of the Lazaretto, four hundred dollars. 7. For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of the Act to Lazaretto. establish a State Board of Health, two thousand dollars, to be paid upon application of the Chairman of the Executive Committee, upon the warrant of the Comptroller General.
