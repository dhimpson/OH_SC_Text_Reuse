SEC. 3. That for the purpose of determining the amount of such aid it shall be the duty of the County Commissioners of the County proposing to make the same, or the proper authorities of the city or town proposing to extend aid, upon the application of a majority of the freehold voters owning real estate in such County, town, or city, specifying the amount of such aid therein, to submit to the qualified voters of such County, city, or town, as the case may be, the question of aid or no aid to said company; that said County Commissioners, or the proper authorities of such town or city, as the case may be, shall have power to order an election, specifying the time, place, and purpose of the election, and to appoint three managers at each election precinct, who shall, without compensation, hold and Form of ballots. conduct said election; at which election the ballots shall have written or printed thereon either the word " Aid " or the words " No Aid, " the said County Commissioners, or the proper authorities of said city or town, having first by resolution fixed the amount of aid according to the request of the commission Notice of elecsubmitted to them. Notice of such resolution shall be given in one newspaper published in the County, city, or town proposing to aid for six weeks next previous to the election, and at such election only those voters of the said County, city, or town, as the case may be, who are qualified electors therein shall be entitled to vote, provided that registration shall not be required as a qualification for voting at such election. It shall be the duty of the managers to count the votes at their respective preCount of the cincts and make their returns to the County Commissioners or the authorities of said town or city, as the case may be, who shall receive and tabulate the same and declare the result, Declaration of which result shall be certified to as correct by said County Commissioners, or city or town authorities, and filed in their respective offices. Contests in such elections shall be heard and determined by the County Commissioners or city or town authorities, as the case may be.
