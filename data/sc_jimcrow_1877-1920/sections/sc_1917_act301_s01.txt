Section 1. James M. Sill to Be Placed on Pension Roll of Richland County. Be it therefore enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That the Board of Pensions for Richland county be, and the same is hereby, authorized to enter upon the pension rolls of Richland county, State, the name of Jas. M. Sill, and that he be authorized to receive the pension awarded the class to which he would properly belong.
