SEC. 4. That the said bonds shall be sold by the Board of Trustees at either public or private sale, as in their judgment may be best; but no such sale shall be made at less than par and accrued interest. The proceeds of such sale shall be paid over by the said Board of Trustees to the County Treasurer of the County of Georgetown, who shall keep a separate account thereof and shall hold the same subject to the orders of the said Board, and pay them out upon warrants duly issued by said Board in like manner as other public school funds belonging to said district. The County Treasurer shall be liable upon his official bond to the said school district, or to any party interested, for the non - performance of his duty in respect to said fund in the same manner and to the same extent and under like penalty as to non - performance of his duties in reference to other public monies entrusted to his keeping: Provided, however, That the said Board of Trustees may, in their discretion, deposit the said funds in the usual depository for State or County funds, in the name of the said school district, subject to their own order, and disburse the same directly by check or draft thereon, signed by the Chairman and countersigned by the Secretary of the Board: Provided, further, That before the said Board shall exercise the power of deposit and direct disbursement of said funds herein granted to it, each of said Trustees shall enter into bond in the sum of five thousand dollars with security, to be approved by the County Board of Commissioners, conditioned for the faithful performance of their official duties. The premium, which may be paid to any surety or guarantee company for furnishing said official bonds, and all other expenses which may be in the issue and sale of the bonds authorized by this Act, shall be paid by the said Trustees from the proceeds of the sale of such bonds or from any other fund of said school district. And the said Board of Trustees shall apply and expend all of said funds exclusively for the purposes provided in this Act.
