Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the territory embraced within the corporate limits of the city of Anderson, in said State, be, and the same is hereby, declared a separate school district, and that it be vested with such corporate powers as may be necessary to Incorporation carry out the purpose of its organization, as declared by this Act.
