SEC. 10. Each Dispenser shall be a qualified elector of this State and a resident of the County in which the Dispensary is of dispensers. located, who has never pleaded guilty or been adjudged guilty of violating any law relating to intoxicating liquors, who is not a keeper of a restaurant or place of public amusement, and is not addicted to the use of intoxicating liquors as a beverage.
