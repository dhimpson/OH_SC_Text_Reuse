§ 2. Tax Levied for Building Fund.—That a tax of three (3) mills is hereby levied for a period of five years, on all real and personal property of said district, beginning with the year 1917, for the purpose of accumulating a fund for erecting a school building for said district, to be used and expended in accordance with the provisions of Act Number 594 of the Acts of 1916, and the County Board of Education for said county is hereby authorized to borrow money on said levy, as provided in said Act.
