Sec. 13. That the County Board of Commissioners in each Civil Code, of the several Counties of this State shall levy a tax of three mills on the dollar, upon all taxable property of their respective Counties for the support of public schools in their respective Counties, which shall be collected at the same time and by the same officer as the other taxes for this year, and shall be held in the Counties Treasuries of the respective Counties and paid out exclusively for the support of public schools, as provided by law.
