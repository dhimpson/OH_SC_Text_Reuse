SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That General levy a tax of five mills, exclusive of the public school tax hereinafter for State purprovided for, upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property of this State, be, and the same is hereby, levied for the purpose of meeting appropriations to defray the current expenses of the Government for the fiscal year commencing November 1st, 1893, and to meet such other indebtedness as has been, or shall be, provided for in the several Acts and Joint Resolutions passed by this General Assembly at the regular session of 1893 providing for the same.
