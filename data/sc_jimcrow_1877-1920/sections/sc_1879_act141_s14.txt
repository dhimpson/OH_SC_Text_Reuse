SEC. 14. The unexpended portion of the amount of the appropriation of eleven thousand dollars made at the last session of this General Assembly to the Penitentiary for building dam and opening canal be retained by the Board of Directors and applied to the support and maintenance of the institution. And the said Board of Directors are further authorized to retain for the use of the Penitentiary all amounts received by them from the hire or labor of convicts during the current fiscal year, and the Treasurer of the State is authorized and instructed to pay back to said Board of Directors the amount paid into the Treasury for convict labor during the last fiscal year, viz., $ 3,354.01, unless already appropriated.
