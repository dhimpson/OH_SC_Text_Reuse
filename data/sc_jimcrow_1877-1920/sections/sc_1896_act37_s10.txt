Section 10. The said Town Council shall have power to regulate sales at auction within the limits of said town and to grant licenses to auctioneers: Provided, That nothing herein shall extend to sales by or for the Sheriff, Clerk of Court, Judge of Probate, Coroners, executors and administrators, assignees, or by any other person under the order of any Court, Magistrate or other inferior Court, or by the owner or owners of the property sold.
