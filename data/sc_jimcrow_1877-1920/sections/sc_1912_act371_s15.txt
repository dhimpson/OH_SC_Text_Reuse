SEC. 15. That said Court shall not be established till an election has been held, at which a majority of the qualified how conelectors of the county vote to establish it. That such election may be held if on or before the 1st of October, 1912, one - third of the qualified registered electors in the county file a petition with the Clerk of Court, praying for such election, in which event the election shall be held at the following general election in November; at which a separate box shall be provided, and ballots, upon which shall be written or printed the words: " Shall a Civil and Criminal Court for Barnwell county be established ? " with answers " Yes " and " No; " and if a majority vote " Yes, " then the said Court shall be established in accordance with the provisions of this Act and the office of Magistrate at Barnwell shall be abolished. The election for the Judicial Magistrates for the said Civil and Criminal Court shall be held on the first Tuesday in March, 1913, which election shall be ordered by the Clerk of Court and conducted under the rules and regulations now applying to general elections, and the Magistrate elected shall open the Court on the first Monday in April, 1913.
