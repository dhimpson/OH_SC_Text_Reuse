SEC. 4. That the sum of three thousand four hundred dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated to pay the expenses of the Engrossing Department of the General Assembly from the twenty seventh day of November, 1888, inclusive, to the close of the session, as follows: For the pay of D. R. Duncan, J. L. Orr, H. H. Newton, and T. M. Gilland, from the twenty seventh day of November up to and including the fifth day of December, each, five dollars per day; for the pay of John S. Wilson, O. L. Shumpert, M. F. Ansel, and J. M. Johnson from the sixth day of December to the close of the session, cach, five dollars per day; for the pay of W. St. Julien Jervey, W. Perry Murphy, J. E. McDonald, and P. H. Nelson, from the twenty seventh day of November to the close of the session, each, five dollars per day, Solicitors (in the Engrossing Department) office of the Attorney General; Pay of Clerks. for the pay of John W. Smoot, L. S. Carson, R. H. Colcock, T. S. Bonham, J. G. Capers, F. F. Covington, R. W. Memminger, Jr., T. B. Butler, from the twenty-seventh day of November to the close of the session, for the pay of J. H. Montgomery from the twenty seventh day of November to the seventh day of December inclusive, and for the pay of A. Levi from the eighth day of December to the close of the session, Clerks (in the Engrossing Department) office of the Attorney General, each, five dollars per day. To pay the mileage of the Circuit Solicitors as follows: W. pay St. Julien Jervey, twenty six dollars; H. H. Newton, twentyfive dollars; P. H. Nelson, thirteen dollars; D. R. Duncan, twenty dollars; James L. Orr, twenty eight 60-100 dollars; T. M. Gilland, nineteen dollars; J. E. McDonald, seven dollars; W. Perry Murphy, thirty dollars; John S. Wilson, twelve 20-100 dollars; J. M. Johnson, twenty 60-100 dollars; O. L. Shumpert, nine 40-100 dollars; M. F. Ansel, twenty eight 80-100 dollars; for the pay of James W. Williams, Porter (in the Engrossing Department) office of the Attorney General, fifty six dollars; for the pay of Edward J. Jones, Flag Keeper, fifty six dollars; Special assist for the pay of Melton Chupp, Temporary Special Assistant to the Keeper of the State House, fifty six dollars, to be paid on the joint certificate of the two Houses, signed by the presiding officers thereof and attested by the Clerks; for the incidental Contingent Or contingent expenses of the Engrossing Department, two hundred and fifty dollars, if so much be necessary, to be paid on certificates drawn by the Speaker of the House of Representatives and attested by the Clerk of the House of Representatives, on accounts audited by the Committee on Contingent Accounts and passed by the House of Representatives.
