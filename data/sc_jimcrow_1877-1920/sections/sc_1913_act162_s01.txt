Section 1. Bond Election in Richland County for Jail. — Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an election shall be held in the county of Richland on the first Tuesday in April, 1913, at which election the question of an issue of fifty thousand ($ 50,000) dollars of twenty - year coupon bonds, bearing interest at five per cent., payable semiannually, at such times as shall be designated by the Supervisors and Commissioners hereinafter provided for, shall be submitted to the qualified electors of said county.
