SEC. 4. That the County Treasurer shall collect said tax at the time of collecting the State and County taxes, and it shall be a surer to collect lien upon all property until paid, as is provided for in the case of State and County taxes; and the proceeds arising from said tax shall be paid out by the County Treasurer on warrants from the School Trustees of said district, countersigned by the County School Commissioner, and he shall be liable to all penalties prescribed elsewhere for non - performance of duty.
