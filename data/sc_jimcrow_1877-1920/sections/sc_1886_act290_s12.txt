SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That that portion of Barnwell County lying within the following Area of School boundary lines, to wit: A line beginning on the edge of Saltkehatchie River, at a point one mile above the upper bridge (where Augusta Road crosses) and running straight to D. S. Hair's water grist mill; thence along the road leading from said mill Eastward to the Blackville public road to said public road; thence along a straight line to Woodward's Junction pump, on the Barnwell R. R.; thence along a straight line to the Orangeburg road to the point where the ditch leading into " Jordan Branch crosses said road; thence along said ditch to where it flows into said branch; thence along said branch to where it empties into Toby's Creek; thence along said creek to where same empties into Saltkehatchie River; thence up said river, on the Eastern bank, to the point of beginning, one mile above the upper bridge over said river, as aforesaid, be, and is hereby, constituted a new school district, to be known as the " Barnwell Graded School District. "
