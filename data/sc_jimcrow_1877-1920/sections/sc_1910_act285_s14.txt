Sec. 15. Every association transacting business under this Act shall file with the Insurance Commissioner a duly certified copy of all amendments of or additions to its constitution and laws within ninety days after the enactment of the same. Printed copies of the constitution and laws and of additions or amendments thereto, certified by the secretary or corresponding officer of the association, shall be prima facie evidence of the legal adoption thereof. Sec. 16. Every association transacting business in this State shall annually, on or before the 31st day of March, file with the Insurance Commissioner, in such form as he shall prescribe, a statement, under oath, of its president or secretary or corresponding officers, of its condition and standing on the 31st day of December next preceding and of its transactions for the year ending on said 31st day of December.
