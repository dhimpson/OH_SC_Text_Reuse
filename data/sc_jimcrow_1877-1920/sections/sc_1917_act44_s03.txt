§ 3. The Treasurer of the State is hereby created and appointed custodian of all funds coming to the State from the United States under this Act, and the State Treasurer shall be responsible on his bond for the correct and proper handling of such funds. All moneys appropriated by the State or paid into the State Treasury from the United States for the purpose of this Act shall be paid out upon the order of the State Board of Education, duly countersigned and approved by the Secretary of the State Board of Education, and itemized vouchers shall be filed with the Comptroller General as in the case of other funds.
