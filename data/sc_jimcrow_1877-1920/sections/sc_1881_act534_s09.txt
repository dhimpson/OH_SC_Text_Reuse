SEC. 9. That the Mayor and Aldermen to be elected as above directed shall, before they enter upon the duties of their re-spective offices, take the oath prescribed by the Constitution of this State, and in addition thereto take the following oath: " As Oath of office Mayor (or Alderman, as the case may be,) of the town of Newof Mayor and Aldermen. berry, I will equally and impartially, to the best of my skill and judgment, exercise and discharge the trust reposed in me, and will endeavor to carry into effect the purposes for which I have been elected. So help me God. "
