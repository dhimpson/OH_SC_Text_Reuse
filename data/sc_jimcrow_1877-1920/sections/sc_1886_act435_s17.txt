SEC. 6. That on the 2d day of April, A. D. 1888, the State Treasurer shall ascertain the amount (face value) of such bonds and " Deficiency " bonds and certificates of stock, if any, which may remain outstanding and unexchanged, and he shall thereupon cause to be prepared for signing and sealing, in accordance with the provisions of Section 2 of this Act, an amount (face value) of the bonds in denominations of one thousand dollars and five hundred dollars of the class the issue of which is provided for in the said Section 2, and an amount (face value) in a certificate or certificates of stock of the class the issue of which is provided for in the said Section 2, (so arranging that said certificate or certificates shall be of the smallest amount possible,) which amount of bonds and certificates shall be equal to the amount of " Deficiency " bonds and certificates of stock ascertained as aforesaid to be then outstanding and unexchanged. That thereupon the said State Treasurer shall, by public advertisement for four weeks in one newspaper published in the cities of Columbia and Charleston, S. C., in the city of New York, N. Y., and in the city of London, England, respectively, offer to receive bids for the purchase in cash at not less than par of the entire lot of said bonds and certificates of stock hereinabove in this Section directed to be prepared for issue, said bids to be submitted to the State Treasurer at Columbia, S. C., on or before the twenty fifth day of May, A.D. 1888. That said bonds and certificates of stock shall be sold and delivered to the highest bidder or bidders therefor as aforesaid, and the same shall be paid for in cash on or before the fifteenth day of June, A. D. 1888, and should the same not be so paid for on or before the date last named, the said bid or bids shall forthwith be rejected by the State Treasurer. That the said bonds and certificates of stock shall be signed and sealed, ready for issue, in exact accordance with the provisions of Section 2 of this Act; but said signing and sealing shall be made and done at the time of the settlement with the State Treasurer for the said purchase, if a sale be made as aforesaid, and not before.
