Section 1. Limit of Special School Tax in Hendersonville School District, of Colleton County - Election. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: That Hendersonville School District, in Colleton county, be, and the same is hereby, authorized to supplement any special, or other tax for school purposes, by an additional tax of four (4) mills: Provided, That the question of the levying of said tax be first submitted to the qualified electors, in the manner now provided by law, and that a majority of said electors, voting at said election, vote in favor of the said increase
