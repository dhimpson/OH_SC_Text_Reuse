SEC. 2. The said Thornwell Orphanage shall be maintained May mainas a home and school for the support and education of white school, children of respectable parentage and deprived of the father by death. Exceptions to this rule may be made only on specific order of the board of trustees. The board of trustees of the Thornwell Orphanage, under said name, shall have a common seal, with power to change, alter and amend the same as often as said board may deem expedient.
