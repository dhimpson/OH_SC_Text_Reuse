SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That an Act entitled " An Act to charter the South Bound Railroad A. A. 1882, 17 Stat., 949, Company, " approved the 9th February, A. D. 1882, be, and the amended. " same is hereby, amended by adding thereto the following provision: That the South Bound Railroad Company shall have the Extension of power of extending and constructing its line of road either from Carolina a point within or near the limits of the City of Sumter or from a point within or near the limits of the City of Columbia, in the State of South Carolina, to the Northern boundary line between that State and North Carolina to some point on said boundary line leading in the direction of Monroe, in the State of North Carolina.
