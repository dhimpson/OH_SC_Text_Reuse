SEC. 21. All alcoholic liquors in possession of any person liquors may be for unlawful use shall be seized without warrant, and if no seized; limitations on actions action to recover same is begun within thirty days from such seizure, or if such action be begun and the judgment of the Court be adverse to the plaintiff, then such liquors shall be forfeited to the County in which same is seized, if there be a Dispensary in said County, and disposed of as the County Dispensary Board may deem best; but if there be no Dispensary therein, such liquors shall be destroyed publicly by the Sheriff of the County.
