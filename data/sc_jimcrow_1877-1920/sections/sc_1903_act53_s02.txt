Section 2159. The provisions of Section 2158 to Section 2162 shall not apply to nurses on trains, nor to narrow gauge roads or branch lines, nor roads under forty miles in length, or to relief trains, in case of accident, or to through vestibule trains not intended or used for local travel, nor to regular freight trains with a passenger coach attached for local travel, nor to officers or guards transporting prisoners, nor to prisoners or lunatics being so transported: Provided, That all railroads operated by steam under forty miles in length shall furnish separate apartments for white and colored passengers: Provided, further, That where said railroads under forty miles in length operate both a daily passenger train and a freight train, with or without a coach attached, said railroad shall be required to furnish separate apartments for white and colored passengers only on the said passenger trains.
