Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the Town Council of Barnwell shall have power, and are hereby authorized, to issue bonds of said town, on or before the first day of February, 1895, to the amount of two thousand and five hundred dollars, for the purpose of paying off the judgment against said town and completing and equipping the Opera House, said bonds to be issued in such denomination as said Town Council may deem best, and to be made payable twenty years from the date of said bonds, with interest payable semi-annually at the rate of not more than eight per cent. per annum, and the coupons for said interest, when due, to be receivable in payments of all taxes levied by or due to said town.
