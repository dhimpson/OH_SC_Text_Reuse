SEC. 5. That the Supervisors of Registration shall each Compensareceive for their services, during the year 1908, the sum of pervisors. two hundred dollars, to be paid by the State quarterly, as other State officers are paid: except in the Counties of Anderson, Charleston, Greenville, Sumter, Orangeburg, Richland, where they shall each receive two hundred and fifty dollars for the year 1908: Provided, That in Spartanburg County they shall receive this year three hundred dollars, to be paid as above provided; that, after the year 1908, the Supervisors of Registration shall each receive fifty dollars per annum in off years and one hundred dollars in general election years: except in the above named Counties, where they shall each receive seventy - five dollars per annum in off years, and in general election years they shall receive two hundred dollars each per annum, including the County of Spartanburg.
