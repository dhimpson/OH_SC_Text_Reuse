SEC. 6. That the said Board of Trustees are authorized and empowered to sell said bonds at their par value, and shall deposit all money coming to them from such sale in bank to the credit of said Board, to be drawn on checks signed by a majority of said Board, and they shall use the said money exclusively for the purpose set out in Section 1 of this Act, and should any of said bonds be not used they shall be cancelled by the said Board of Trustees.
