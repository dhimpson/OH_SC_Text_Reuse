SEC. 21. That the appropriation for the interest on consol and deficiency bonds and stock certificates herein before provided for shall be paid out of the moneys already in the Treasury for the payment of interest on the public debt of the State now remaining unfunded, and the State Treasurer is hereby authorized and required to apply such money, any law to the contrary notwithstanding, and out of such other funds as shall be realized from taxation for this purpose, under an Act to raise supplies for the fiscal year commencing on the 1st of November, 1879.
