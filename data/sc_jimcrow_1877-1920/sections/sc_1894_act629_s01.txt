Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the Comptroller - General be, and he hereby is, empowered and directed to place upon the list of those in the County of Kershaw entitled to pensions as the widows of Confederate soldiers the name of Mrs. E. M. Capel, widow of the late W. H. Capel, an ex - Confederate soldier, who recently died from the effects of wounds received during the war, and who at the time of his death was a pensioner. Sec. 2. That all back pensions due to her late husband and now in the hands of the Clerk of the Court for Kershaw County be paid over to her, upon her receipting therefor.
