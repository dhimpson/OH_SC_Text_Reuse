Section 2. The said town shall be governed by an Intendant and six (6) Wardens, who shall be citizens of the State, twenty - one (21) years of age, and shall have resided at least twelve (12) months within the corporate limits of the town immediately preceding the day of their election. The said Intendant and Wardens shall be known and called the " Town Council of Summerville, " and shall be elected at the same time on the second (2d) Wednesday in July of every second year, and shall hold office for the term of two years, and until their successors shall have been duly elected and qualified. The next election shall take place on the second Next election. Wednesday in July, A. D. 1895, and the present Intendant and Wardens and other officers shall hold office until their successors are elected and qualified. The Intendant and three (3) of the Wardens, or any four (4) of the Wardens, shall constitute a quorum for the purpose of transacting business or exercising the powers herein granted to the " Town Council of Summerville, " or the said Intendant and Wardens.
