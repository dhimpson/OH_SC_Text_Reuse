SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the 801, amended. State of South Carolina: That an Act entitled " An Act to create a new school district within the township of Blackville in Barnwell County to be known as ' The Blackville School District, ' and to authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, approved 23rd December, A. D. 1890, and all Acts amending the same, " be and the same are hereby amended in the following particulars: That is to say, that on and after the passage of this Act, there shall be elected by the freehold taxpayers and voters of said school district five trustees to take the place of the existing Board of Trustees which election shall take place on the Tuesday after the second Monday in May, and the five persons receiving the highest number of votes shall be the trustees and constitute the Board of Trustees for said " The Blackville School District. "
