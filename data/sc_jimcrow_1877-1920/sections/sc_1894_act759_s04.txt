Section 4. For the purpose of conducting the elections for members of the Town Council, it shall be the duty of the Governor, at least sixty (60) days before the next election, and at least sixty (60) days before each succeeding appoint Comelection, to appoint three (3) electors, who shall constitute a Board of Commissioners of Election for the town of Summerville, who shall hold their office, unless removed by the Governor, until sixty (60) days before the next election for members of the Town Council of said town, and until their successors are duly appointed and qualified. The Commissioners of Election shall appoint three (3) Managers of Election, who shall on the first Wednesday in May immediately preceding the second Wednesday in July appointed for holding such election, for two consecutive days, at or near the depot of the South Carolina and Georgia Railroad Company, open lists for the registration of the qualified voters of the town who are not entitled to vote upon a property qualification, between the hours of seven A. M. and twelve M., and again between the hours of three P. M. and six P. M. The after taking the oath of office prescribed by the Constitution of the State, Section 30, Article II., before any person qualified to administer an oath, shall administer the same to the two other Managers. The Managers shall administer to every person offering to register the following oath: " You do solemnly swear that you are duly qualified to vote Oath for regisfor members of the Legislature of the State of South Carolina, and that you have resided within the corporate limits of this town for the four months last past, and that it is your intention to continue to reside within the corporate limits until the day for holding the ensuing election, and that you have truly stated your name and place of residence; so help you God. " Upon the Managers being satisfied of such person's right to register, they shall duly register his name and place of residence, and issue to him a certificate of his registration, which certificate shall state the name of the voter, the date of registration, his place of residence, and shall be signed by the Chairman of the Board of Managers and by their Clerk. The registration list, when completed, shall List. be duly sealed up and delivered by the said Managers to the Commissioners of Election, together with all books and papers that may pertain to such registration, and the same shall be kept by the said Commissioners, unopened, and shall be so returned to the said Managers on the day of the said election.
