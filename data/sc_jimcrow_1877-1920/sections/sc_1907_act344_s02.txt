SEC. 2. The Managers of said Election shall serve without compensation, and the Managers appointed shall have authority and power to appoint substitute Managers to act in their stead. SEC. 3. The nature of the coupon bonds voted upon shall be as follows: ($ 200,000) two hundred thousand dollars, in coupon bonds, to be issued in denominations to be fixed by the General Assembly hereafter, to bear interest from their date at the rate of four per cent. per annum, payable semi - annually on the 1st day of April and October in each year after the issue. One - half of said coupon bonds shall become due and payable twenty years after their date and the other one - half thirty years after their date.
