SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That no greater interest than seven (7) per cent. per annum shall be charged, taken, agreed upon or allowed upon any contract arising in this State for the hiring, lending or use of money or other commodity, either way of straight interest, discount or otherwise, except upon written contracts, wherein, by express agreement, a rate of interest not exceeding eight per cent. may be charged.
