SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That all bonds hereafter issued or bonds sold, or to be hereafter issued or sold, by the Trustees of any taxation. School District, or School Districts, pursuant to the vote of the majority of the qualified voters of such School District, or School Districts, voting at an election heretofore or hereafter held for the erection of buildings, for equipment, for maintaining public schools in such district or districts, or for paying indebtedness of such district or districts, shall be exempt from all taxation for State, County, municipal or school purposes.
