Section 1. Act (1914, XXIII Stats., 567) Amended - Election of School Trustees in Greenville County. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That " An Act to Amend Section 1752, Volume I, Code of Laws, 1912, so as to provide for the election of Trustees in Spartanburg county, " approved February 25th, 1914, be, and the same hereby is, amended by adding at the end of said section the following: " Provided, that the provisions of this Act which relate to Spartanburg county shall also apply and be of full force and effect in Greenville county; Provided, further, That the first election of School Trustees shall be held on the first Tuesday in July, 1918, " so that said section, when so amended, shall read as follows:
