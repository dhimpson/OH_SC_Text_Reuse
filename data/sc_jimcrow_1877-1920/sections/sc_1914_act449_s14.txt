§ 13. Other Acts Repealed. - That all Acts or part of Acts. heretofore enacted by the General Assembly as amendments to the Act entitled " An Act to amend an Act entitled ' An Act to provide #438 (p.850) 850 STATUTES AT LARGE for the establishment of a new school district in the county of Darlington, and to authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, ' " approved December 24th, 1883, and approved January 4th, 1904, be, and they are hereby, repealed.
