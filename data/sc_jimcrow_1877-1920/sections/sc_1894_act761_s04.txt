Sec. 4. That the election for Mayor and Aldermen hereinbefore provided for shall be held, at such place as may be designated by the City Council, from nine o'clock A. M. until four o'clock P. M., and that at least ten days ' notice thereof be given by publication in a newspaper at Abbeville; that such election shall be conducted by three Managers to be appointed by the City Council, one of whom shall be Chairman and another clerk, and all of whom shall, before entering on their duties, take an oath to fairly and impartially conduct the election; that the said Managers, or any of them, shall administer to every person offering to vote at Oath for voter. the election an oath that he is duly qualified to vote at such election according to the Constitution and laws of this State, and has not voted before at this election; that said Managers shall keep a correct list of the names of the persons voting and of those offering to vote at such election, shall count the ballots, cast up the result and proclaim the election and file the lists and results with the Clerk of Council, who shall forthwith notify in writing the persons who have received a plurality of the ballots cast; that in the event no candidate should receive a plurality of the votes cast, or in the event of a vacancy in office caused by death, resignation or otherwise, another election shall be ordered in like manner as the first, but one registration in any one year shall be sufficient; that in said elections all male citizens over the age of twenty - one years who have been domiciled in the State one year and in the city sixty days next before the election, and who have been registered as hereinafter provided, and who are not disqualified by law, shall be entitled to vote.
