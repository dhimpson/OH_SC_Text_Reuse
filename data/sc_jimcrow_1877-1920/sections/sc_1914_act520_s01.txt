Section 1. Trustees of School District No. 7g, Greenville County, Authorized to Convey Certain Lot of Land. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the Trustees of School District No. 7g, Greenville county, South Carolina, or their successors in office, are hereby vested and #515 (p.927) OF SOUTH CAROLINA. 927 empowered to sell and convey said lot and pass a good fee simple title to purchaser thereof. Approved the 6th day of February, A. D. 1914.
