§ 15. Board May Establish and Maintain Free Libraries.— That in addition to the rights and privileges hereinbefore granted, the Board of School Commissioners of said school district is hereby authorized and empowered to accept or establish, upon such terms as they deem right, any public library or libraries, and if accepted, to support and maintain the same from funds hereinafter provided for; and after accepted or established, said library or libraries shall be managed and controlled by said Board of School Commissioners.
