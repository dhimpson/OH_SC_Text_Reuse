Sec. 5. That the said Board of Trustees are authorized and empowered to sell or hypothecate said bonds at their par value, and shall deposit all moneys coming to them from such sales or hypothecation in bank to the credit of said Board, to be drawn on checks signed by a majority of said Board; and they shall use the said moneys exclusively for the purposes set out in Section I of this Act, and should any of said bonds be not used they shall be cancelled by the said Board of Trustees. Sec. 6. This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
