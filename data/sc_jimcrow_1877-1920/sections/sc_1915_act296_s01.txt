Section 1. Improvement of Roads - Election - Tax. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State,Section 1. Improvement of Roads - Election - Tax. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That the Trustees of Higgins School District of Saluda county are hereby authorized to levy for the year 1916 on all the personal and real property returned in said district a special tax of ten mills on the dollar of said district, to be used by the Trustees of said district in improving the roads in said district, subject, however, to the following provisions: That the said Board of Trustees shall, upon the petition of one - third of the qualified voters, submit the question of " Special tax " or " No special tax " to the qualified electors residing within said district, and they shall have power to order an election specifying the time, place and purpose of the same some time during the year 1915, and to make all necessary rules and regulations for governing the same. The said Board of Trustees shall appoint three managers at such election, who shall, without compensation, hold and conduct the same, at which said election the ballots shall have written or printed thereon either the words " Special tax " or " No specil tax, " and of which election notice shall be given by the Chairman of the Board of Trustees, as now constituted, not less than ten days before the same is held in one or more of the newspapers of the city of Saluda.
