AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE CONTINUANCE OF PENSIONS TO CERTAIN WIDOWS OF SOLDIERS AND SAILORS WHO HAVE BEEN DUTY ADMITTED UPON THE PENSION ROLLS. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the widow of any soldier or sailor of this State dying after his application for pension has been duly approved shall be entitled to participate in the distribution of pension money in the same manner as her husband would have been entitled to if alive, under the same conditions as to residence, property and income now provided by law with reference to the widows of soldiers and sailors who lost their life while in the service of the State in the war between the States.
