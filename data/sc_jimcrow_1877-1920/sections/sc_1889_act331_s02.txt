SEC. 2. That the voters of the said school district who return real or personal property for taxation are hereby authorized to Local school levy and collect an annual tax not to exceed three mills, to supplement the Constitutional two mill tax, and for said purposes the Trustees of said school district, upon the written request of five taxpayers of said school district, at any time before the first Monday in January in each year, the call for which meeting shall be advertised in some newspaper published in said County once a week for two weeks, and published by posting the same in three conspicuous places in said school district before said. Powers of meeting. When assembled the meeting of taxpayers shall have power to elect a Chairman and Secretary, adjourn from time to time, and levy a special tax for the ensuing year, not exceeding three mills, for such school purposes, as a majority of those present shall determine, and no tax so voted shall be repealed at any repealed. subsequent meeting.
