SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the School district of the town of Greenwood is hereby permitted and authorized to issue eight thousand dollars of six or seven per cent. bonds for the purpose of paying for school property and building one or more public school buildings in the town of Greenwood: Provided, That a majority of the qualified A. D. 1891. voters of said School District shall be in favor of such issue, as expressed at an election held for that purpose.
