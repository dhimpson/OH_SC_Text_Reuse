SEC. 6. They shall have power to fix the school age of scholars Age of scholars. of the said district who shall receive benefit of such funds, and adopt text books for use in said schools; to charge an incidental fee of not more than one dollar per scholar; said fee to be paid before the scholar shall be admitted; such fee to go to the general expense funds of the schools, and such amounts that may be left over from incidental expenses shall be applied to extend the free term of the schools; to charge and collect supplementary Supplemental tuition fees when necessary for each pupil, according to grade, to supplement the general funds of the schools.
