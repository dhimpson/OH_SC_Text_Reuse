§ 2. Conduct of Election.—That the said School Commissioners of the School District of the City of Columbia shall order an election upon the said question to be held at such place, or places. in the School District as may be designated by the said School Commissioners, and shall give notice of such election for ten days in a newspaper published in said District, which notice shall designate the time and place, or places, at which the election will be held, and the said School Commissioners shall appoint the managers of said election, receive the returns, and declare the result. § 3. Form of Ballot—That the School Commissioners shall have printed and provided for the use of the voters a sufficient number of ballots, which shall be placed at the voting place, or places, on which shall be printed the question to be submitted and the words “Yes” and “No,” so that the voter may indicate his vote by striking out one or the other of the words on said ballot, and the word not so stricken out to be counted. § 4. Assessment of Tax.—That if a majority of the votes cast at such an election shall be in favor of levying the said tax, the School Commissioners shall certify the fact to the County Auditor who shall thereupon annually assess said tax on the taxable property of said School District and enter the same upon the tax duplicates, and the County Treasurer shall thereupon collect said taxes at the same time and in the same manner as other taxes. § 5. Disbursement of Proceeds.—That the County Treasurer shall keep a separate account of said taxes and shall pay all moneys realized and collected on account of same to the School Commissioners for the School District of the City of Columbia, to be used for the purposes aforesaid. § 6. School Commissioners May Borrow Money.—That the School Commissioners of said School District are authorized to borrow money on account of said taxes, if same shall be voted for the purposes aforesaid, and to pledge said taxes for the payment of such money as they may borrow. § 7. Immediately Effective—That this act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
