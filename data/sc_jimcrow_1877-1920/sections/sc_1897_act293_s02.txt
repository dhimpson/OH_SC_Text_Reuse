SECTION 2. It shall be the duty of the County Auditor and the Township Board of Commissioners, on or before the first Tuesday of March in each year, to diligently seek for and discover all property, both real and personal, in his County missioners to subject to taxation and not previously returned or listed with him; and it shall be his duty to list the same for taxation, giving the valuation thereof, with the name of the owner or person to whom it is taxable.
