SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That a new School District is hereby established in the County of Oconee, to be known as " Coneross School District, " and shall be embraced in the following described area: taking the dwelling house of T. D. Alexander as the central point, draw lines extending two miles North, South, East and West. Let lines be drawn at right angles to the extremities of these lines and be extended till they intersect each other, and the area thus included shall constitute the said School District.
