SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the area embraced in a square, the sides of which are three miles Area of School in length, running North, South, East and West, and the centre whereof is Gordon's Hotel, at the centre of the town of Rock Hill, be, and the same is hereby, created a special School District in said County, to be known as the Rock Hill School District, and shall be a body politic and corporate, with such rights, privileges and liabilities as are provided for School Districts by 16 Stat., 571. the general school law of South Carolina.
