AN ACT TO AMEND SECTIONS 3 AND 7 OF AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT IN LANCASTER COUNTY AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF SAID SCHOOL TAX THEREIN, APPROVED DECEMBER 23D, 1891, BY INCREASING THE AMOUNT OF LEVY ALLOWED AND PROVIDING FOR THE MANNER OF ELECTING MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES AND THEIR TENURE OF OFFICE, AND TO PROVIDE FOR FREE TUITION AND PROHIBITING ANY CHARGE FOR INCIDENTAL EXPENSES.
