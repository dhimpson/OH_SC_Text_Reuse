Sec. 24. That the said Town Council shall have full power to erect a powder magazine, and to compel every person holding more than one hundred pounds of powder at any one time to store the same therein, and to make regulations for rates of storage therein. They shall also have power to prohibit absolutely the keeping of dynamite in any quantity whatsoever within the corporate limits of said town, except as permitted by the Town Council for special purpose in said town by ordinance. That the said Town Council is hereby authorized and empowered to establish and keep up one or more public scales for weighing cotton and other articles sold by weight in said town, and to appoint one or more public weighers, who shall give good bond, to be approved by the Intendant, and who shall be sworn by said Intendant faithfully to perform the duties of said office, and who shall be removable for misconduct or incompetency by the said Town Council; and the said Town Council are hereby authorized to assess a sum not exceeding ten (10) cents on each bale of cotton, and a proportionate sum on other articles weighed, to be paid by the seller or buyer, as may be agreed upon between them, for the use of said town. That the public scales established in pursuance of this Act shall be the standard to which all others in said town shall conform; and the Town Council may establish by ordinance such rules and regulations as may be deemed necessary to effectually carry out the provisions of this Section.
