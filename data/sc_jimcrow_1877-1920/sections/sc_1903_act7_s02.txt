SEC. 2. That the Supervisors of Registration for Edgefield registration County are hereby directed to make out two additional books for such precinct, and to transfer thereto the names of all qualified electors of Edgefield County authorized by law to vote at the same, and also to make out and deliver new certificates to such electors upon the surrender of those held by them. Approved the 23d day of February, A. D. 1903.
