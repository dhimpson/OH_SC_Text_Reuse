SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Room for Confederate State of South Carolina: That a room on Senate side of third souvenirs and floor on the right at the top of stairway of the State Capitol be and is hereby set apart for Confederate Souvenirs and Relics, and the Daughters of the Confederacy are hereby authorized to control said room, and use the same for said purpose. Approved the 21st day of February, A. D. 1901. -----------------------------------------------------------------
