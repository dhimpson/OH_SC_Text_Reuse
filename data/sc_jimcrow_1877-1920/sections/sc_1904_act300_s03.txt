Sec. 3. (1) Health Department—That the following sums, if so much be necessary, he, and the same are hereby, appropriated to meet the expenses of the Health Department, as follows: (1) Charleston—The salary of the Quarantine Officer at harleston, sixteen hundred and fifty dollars; for the expenses of maintaining the Quarantine Station, Charleston one thousand dollars. at. (2) St. Helena—For the salary of the Quarantine Officer St. Helena, seven hundred dollars; for the expenses of mainining the Quarantine Station at St. Helena, two hundred dolars; for a new wharf and for repairs to buildings, one hunred dollars, if so much be necessary. (3) Port Royal—For the salary of the Quarantine Officer at Port Royal, seven hundred dollars; for the expenses of the Quarantine Station at Port Royal, three hundred dollars; for the salary of the Keeper of the Hospital Building at Port Royal, one hundred and seventy-five dollars; for repairs to buildings, one hundred and twenty-five dollars, if so much be necessary (4) Georgetown—For the salary of the Quarantine Officer at Georgetown, four hundred and fifty dollars; for the expenses of the Quarantine Station at Georgetown, one hundred and fifty dollars; for insurance for 1902, 1903 and 1904, sixty dollars and seventy-five cents; for new roof and repairs, three hundred dollars, if so much be necessary; for deficit 1903 $12.48. (5) Lazaretto—For the salary of the Keeper of the LazaTetto, three hundred dollars. (6) Board of Health—For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of an Act establishing a State Board of Health twenty-two hundred dollars, if so much be necessary; for Clerk hire for Secretary of the Board, five hundred dollars Provided, That out of the twenty-two hundred dollars herein appropriated, the sum of six hundred dollars be paid to the State Company for the printing of reports of 1901 and 1902, now unpaid for: Provided, That the State Board of Health shall not incur any expenses for the printing of extra reports (7) For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of an Act quarantining the State against contagious and infectious diseases, eight thousand dollars; this fund to be expended under the supervision and by the consent of the Governor Provided, however, That the Governor be, and he is hereby, authorized, in case of emergencies demanding it, to borrow seven thousand dollars additional, if so much be necessary
