Section 1. Election to Decide Charter Repeal for Bluffton Provided.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That the Intendant and Wardens of the Town of Bluffton be and they are hereby required within ten days after the approval of this Act to order an election to be held as are other elections in said town, for the purpose of submitting to the qualified electors of said town the question of the repeal of the charter of incorporation of said town. § 2. Reregistration of Electors Required.—For the purpose of providing for a full and fair expression of the will of the people said Intendant and Wardens are required to provide for a reregistration of all the qualified electors within the corporate limits of said town in the manner provided by law and to hold said election not earlier than six, nor later than eight weeks after the date of ordering said election. § 3. Ballots—How Marked.—At said election the qualified electors shall cast ballots on which shall be printed “FOR AGAINST repeal of Charter,” which ballots shall be provided by said Intendant and Wardens for all voters. All those desiring to vote for repeal of the charter shall cross out the word “AGAINST” and all those desiring to vote to retain the charter shall cross out the word “FOR” before depositing their ballots. § 4. Procedure After Election—How Municipality May Be Wound Up.—If a majority of the votes cast at said election be against the repeal of the charter the Intendant-Wardens shall certify the result of said election the Legislative Delegation from Beaufort County and proceed with the Town Government as if no election had been held. If a majority of the votes cast at said election shall be in favor of the repeal of said charter then the said Intendant and Wardens shall certify the same to the Legislative Delegation from Beaufort County and shall forthwith cease to exercise any of the powers of municipal officers except for the purpose of winding up the affairs of said town which they shall do in the following manner: They shall OS cause to be paid to each municipal officer his salary at the rate existing at the time of the passage of this Act, up to ten days after said election; they shall pay up all outstanding indebtedness of said town; they shall advertise for sale, by posting notices for at least six days in three or more prominent places in said town, all the personal property of said town except the books of account and records and documents, and sell the said personal property at public outcry for cash to the highest bidder and shall then disburse all funds remaining in manner following, to wit: By paying back to each licensee of said town the unearned proportion of the license money paid by him and then pay back to the taxpayers of said town, their agents or legal representatives all taxes paid by them during the last tax paying period, and if the fund is insufficient for that purpose then the same must be paid in the proportion that such fund bears to the whole amount of taxes collected. They shall then turn over to the County Commissioners of Beaufort County the books of account, records and documents of said town and all remaining cash, and the wharf and all real estate of said town, to be by said County Commissioners held as custodians until such further disposition may be made thereof by the General Assembly as it may determine. § 5. Charter Repeal Effective May 6, 1916, Unless Electors Vote Otherwise—County Commissioners to Wind Up Affairs if Municipal Officers Fail to Do So.—The Charter of the Town of Bluffton is hereby repealed to take effect on the 6th day of May, A. D. 1916, unless the provisions of Sections 1, 2 and 3 of this Act are complied with; but if said provisions of this Act are complied with the charter is repealed to take effect on said date if the result of the election shall be in favor of the repeal of said charter but not otherwise. If the provisions of Sections 1, 2 and 3 of this Act are not complied with the said Intendant and Wardens are hereby prohibited from drawing out any of the funds of said town, as salary or otherwise, and the duties imposed upon them by Section 4 of this Act for the purpose of winding up the affairs of said town shall be performed by the County Commissioners of the County of Beaufort or by some person named by them for that purpose, whose compensation shall be fixed by the said County Commissioners and paid out of the municipal funds of said town.
