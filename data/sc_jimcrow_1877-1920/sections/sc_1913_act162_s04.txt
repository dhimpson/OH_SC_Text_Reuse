§ 4. Signature of Bonds. - That said bonds shall be signed by the Supervisor and members of the Board of County Commissioners, who shall affix their official seal thereto, and shall be in the sums of five hundred ($ 500) dollars each. The said Supervisor and Board of County Commissioners shall also sign the coupons of the said bonds in the same manner as the said bonds are signed: Provided, That their names may be lithographed on the said coupons.
