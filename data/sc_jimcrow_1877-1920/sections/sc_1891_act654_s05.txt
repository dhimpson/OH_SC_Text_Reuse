SEC. 5. That it shall be the duty of the Chairman and Secreary of said public meeting, within one week after said meeting collection of tax has been held, to notify the Chairman of the Board of Trustees for said School District and the Auditor of said County of the amount of the tax thus levied and how it has been appropriated; and the County Auditor shall at once assess such tax on all real and personal property returned in said School District, and the County Treasurer shall collect the same with the State and County taxes; and such tax shall be a lien upon all property Lien for taxes. until paid, and defaulting taxpayers shall be liable to like process and penalties as defaulters for State and County taxes.
