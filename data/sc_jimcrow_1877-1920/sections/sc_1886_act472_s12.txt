SEC. 5. That the said Act be further amended by adding thereto an additional Section to be known as Section 22, to wit: " SECTION 22. The said City Council of Anderson are hereby authorized and empowered to purchase a suitable tract of land for cemetery purposes for the use of the white inhabitants of Anderson and its vicinity, which tract of land shall be situated near the incorporate limits of said city and shall be known as and styled the Silver Brook Cemetery, and for this purpose the purchase of the tract of land by said City Council outside of the city limits for cemetery purposes and known and styled as above, and all acts done by them pursuant to such purchase, be, and the same are hereby, validated and confirmed as completely and effectually as if the same had been fully and expressly authorized herein before such purchase. The said City Council are also hereby authorized and empowered to purchase a suitable Cemetery for tract of land for cemetery purposes for the use of the colored inhabitants of Anderson and its vicinity, which tract of land shall be situated outside and near the incorporate limits of said city and shall be known as the Mount Tabor Cemetery. ' The City Council shall have the right to regulate interments in said cemeteries, and to this end may lay them off in plats and sell the same on such conditions and subject to such regulations as Powers over they may prescribe, and may permit interment without charge under such regulations as they may prescribe. The said City Council may add to and extend either or both of said cemeteries by purchase from adjoining land owners whenever in their judgment it may be necessary or desirable to do so, and may from time to time expend such sums as they may think proper in adorning and beautifying the grounds of said cemeteries. The City Council shall have full power and control over said cemeteries for the protection and preservation of the same, and may establish by ordinance such rules and regulations for the government thereof as they may deem proper: Provided, They do not conflict with the laws of the land; and to enforce the same and punish any violation thereof the said City Council may provide a police system for said cemeteries, and shall have in all respects the same powers and authority in controlling and governing the same as is now or may hereafter be given them in the city of Anderson, of which incorporation the said cemeteries Offenses comare hereby made a part. All offenses committed in said cemeteries shall be tried and punished in the same manner as offenses against the other ordinances of the said city. "
