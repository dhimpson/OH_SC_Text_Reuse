SEC. 7. The Auditor of Newberry County shall annually levy on the taxable property of said school district a tax of threefourths of a mill for the purpose of creating a sinking fund for the payment of said bonds, which tax shall be collected by the Treasurer of Newberry County and shall be held by him for the purpose aforesaid: Provided, however, That said Trustees at the end of every five years shall have the right to order such sinking fund then accumulated paid pro rata on the bonds then. outstanding.
