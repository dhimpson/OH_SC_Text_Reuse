§ 10. Use of Interest on Deposits - Vehicle License Tax. —All interest accruing on such funds as are deposited by the Highway Commission in the banks of Willow township shall be paid by said banks to the Treasurer of Orangeburg county every six months, to be computed from the date of original deposit, and the interest so received shall be applied by the County Treasurer to the payment of the interest on the bonds herein authorized, in so far as it will pay said interest. Further, the Clerk of said Court shall furnish, on or before the first day of May, 1919, and on the first day of January of each year thereafter, to the County Auditor a list of all buggies and wagons used for pleasure or the transportation of merchandise or passengers, giving full information as filed in his office; and upon such vehicles shall be assessed and collected as taxes are collected a special license, in addition to other existing property tax, the amount of one dollar on buggies and on one - horse wagons, one dollar and fifty cents on two - horse wagons and log carts. The revenue from said license tax shall be applied to the repairing and upkeep of the roads herein provided for, and should there be any surplus, the same shall be applied to the sinking fund for the retirement of the said bonds.
