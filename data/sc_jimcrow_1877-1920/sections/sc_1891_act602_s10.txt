SEC. 12. It shall be the duty of each and every taxpayer of property. whose property may be partly within and partly without said. School District, when he makes his return for State and County taxes, to plainly and separately specify in such returns under oath the position or positions of such property, whether real or personal, subject to taxation within said District; and in case such taxpayer shall have made his or her return before such School District is laid off, it shall be his or her duty to make Amended reanother or amended return in accordance herewith; and it shall be the duty of the County Auditor to enter the tax belonging To be entered to said School District upon the tax duplicate in a separate column, so that the County Treasurer may conveniently keep the same separated from the other taxes.
