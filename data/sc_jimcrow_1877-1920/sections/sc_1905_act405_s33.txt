SEC. 34. The Quartermaster General, or other officer detailed ficer of U. S. by the Commander - in - Chief, shall act as disbursing officer of the funds allotted by the United States for the support or use of the militia of this State. He shall receipt to the proper officer of the United States for all such funds and shall expend the same under the orders of the Commander - in - Chief, in accordance with the laws of the United States. He shall render such reports and returns of such funds as may be required by the proper authorities of the United States and shall make an annual report of the expenditures to the Commander - in - Chief.
