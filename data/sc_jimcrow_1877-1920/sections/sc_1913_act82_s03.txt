§ 3. Ballots—Those favoring exemption shall cast a ballot on which shall be printed or written the words, “For exemption from general stock law;” those opposing shall cast a ballot on which shall be printed or written the words, “Against exemption from general stock law.” If a majority of votes cast shall be in favor of exemption then said territory shall be exempt, but if a majority of votes cast shall be against exemption then said territory shall remain under the general stock law, as at present.
