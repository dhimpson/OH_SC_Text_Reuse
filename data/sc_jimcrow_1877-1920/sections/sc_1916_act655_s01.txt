Section 1. Election for Transfer of School District—Conduct of Election.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That upon petition being filed with the County Board of Education for the County of Orangeburg by twenty qualified electors residing in school District No. 2, of the County of Orangeburg, it shall be the duty of the said County Board of Education to order an election in said school district upon the question of the transfer and annexation of school district No. 2 with Special School District No. 70, known as the Elloree School District, and the said County Board of Education shall designate the time and place of holding such election, shall appoint managers to conduct the same, and shall give two weeks public notice thereof; the said managers shall conduct the said election as general elections are conducted in this State, and all qualified electors residing in said school district No. 2 shall be entitled to vote; and the said managers shall forthwith make returns of such election to the County Board of Education. § 2. Annexation of District——That in the event a majority of the qualified electors, voting in said election shall vote in favor of the transfer and annexation of School District No. 2, of the County of Orangeburg, to said School District No. 70, known as the Elloree School District, it shall be the duty of the County Board of Education, for the County of Orangeburg, to pass an order directing that said school district No. 2, be transferred and annexed to the said school district No. 70 in which event the said school district No. 2 shall then compose a part of school district No. 70, with all the rights, powers, privileges and liabilities now or hereafter imposed by law upon said school district No. 70 known as the Elloree School District. § 3. Immediately Effective—This Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
