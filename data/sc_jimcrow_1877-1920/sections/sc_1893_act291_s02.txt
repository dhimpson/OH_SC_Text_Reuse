SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Establishment for the purpose of establishing and maintaining graded public schools in any city, incorporated town or village in this State, such city, incorporated town or village desiring to establish and maintain the same, and to receive the benefits of this Act, are hereby constituted and declared to be separate school districts. SEC. 2. that no tax so levied shall be repealed at any subsequent meeting; that within ten (10) days Notification to after said meeting the chairman thereof shall furnish the Board of Trustees of such school district and the County Auditor with the amount so levied, and the Auditor shall enter the same in his tax duplicate, and he shall annually each year thereafter Entry on duenter said amount in his tax duplicates until the same is increased, decreased or repealed by said taxpayers at a meeting, Change in tax called for that purpose and he is notified the same has been increased, decreased or repealed, and if increased or decreased he shall annually enter it as before, which meeting shall be called and notice given in the same way and manner as is herein provided for the calling of meetings to make the levy and the giving of the notice that it has been made, and the County Treasurer Collection of shall collect the same as other County and State taxes;
