Every such company, corporation or association, shall keep books keep accurate books of account of all business done by them on fire and lightning insurance within the limits of such incorporated cities or towns, and in case any fraud or dishonesty in said returns made by such company, corporation or association, as provided for in Section 1 of this Act, be apparent, it shall be the duty of the Comptroller General to investigate such returns and collect the amount which he shall find to be due. SEC. 4. Every fire insurance company who shall neglect to violating pro keep such books of account as aforesaid, or shall fail or neglect to report or pay over any of the money due on premiums as aforesaid, at the times and in the manner specified in the preceding Section of this Act, or shall be found upon examination to have made a false return of business done by them, shall for each offense forfeit three hundred dollars, for the use and benefit of such fire department of such city or town, to be recovered in a civil action in the name of the city or town.
