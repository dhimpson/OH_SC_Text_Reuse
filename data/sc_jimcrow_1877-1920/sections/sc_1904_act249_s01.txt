Section 2158. All railroads and steam ferries, and railroad Railroad companies to companies engaged in this State as common carriers of passenfurnish separgers for hire, shall furnish separate coaches or cabins for the white and accommodation of white and colored passengers: Provided, Equal accommodations shall be supplied to all persons, without distinction of race, color or previous condition, in such coaches or cabins: Provided, further, That all first class coaches and cabins shall be provided with a compartment at each end of such coaches or cabins; said compartments shall be provided with seats for three or more persons; on one compartment shall be printed the words, " For Females, " and on the other, " For Males. "
