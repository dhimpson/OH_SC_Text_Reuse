§ 9. Issue of Bonds. - If the election is in favor of issuing said. bonds the said trustees shall set aside $ 35,000 of said issue and shall exchange the same for the $ 35,000 of the school bonds issued by the city of Anderson for school purposes in said district, in which case the school bonds issued by the said city of Anderson, and all coupons. thereon remaining unpaid, shall be cancelled and destroyed by the Board of Trustees and the said Council of the city of Anderson. In case the owner or owners of the said city bonds heretofore issued for school purposes are unwilling or refuse to make the change, then said School Trustees shall hold the $ 35,000 worth of bonds, or as many as may be necessary, and deposit them in some safety deposit strong box for the purpose of taking up the city bonds when they #392 (p.360) 360 STATUTES AT LARGE mature, and at any time previous thereto, and that any exchange or substitution can be had therefor. That it shall be the duty of said trustees to issue and sell the balance of said bonds at such times as the necessity may arise to the best advantage possible, and the funds derived from the sale of said bonds shall be used for the purpose of improving the present school property, acquiring additional property and erecting a building or buildings for school purposes, and said funds are not to be used for any other purpose.
