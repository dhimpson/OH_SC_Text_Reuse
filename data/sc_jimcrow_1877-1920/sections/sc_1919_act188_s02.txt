Hampton County. Section 1. That there is hereby levied on all property in Hampton county for ordinary county purposes, ten (10) mills; no tax for road purposes shall be levied except a commutation road tax of three ($3.00) dollars, to be assessed and collected from each male citizen between the ages of twenty-one and fifty-five years. No one shall be exempt from the payment of said commutation tax except persons totally disabled. That all commutation taxes shall be expended on roads and bridges from townships and towns in which same is collected, and that part of commutation tax collected from citizens of incorporated town by County Treasurer, shall be turned over to Town Treasurer of respective towns: Provided, That the town authorities of the towns of Brunson, Hampton, Varnville and Estill be empowered to collect the commutation tax from residents within their respective corporate limits. The Treasurer of the county is authorized and directed to pay to the Sheriff three hundred ($300.00) dollars for traveling expenses, when in the discharge of his duties; that the Sheriff shall receive forty cents per day for dieting prisoners. The sum of $750.00 is hereby appropriated for the work of the Home Demonstration Agent. That the Superintendent of Education shall receive not to exceed $500.00 for traveling expenses; the Treasurer shall pay not more than $312.50 for Vital Statistics, if so much be necessary. From the funds in his hand the Treasurer is hereby authorized and directed to pay to the County Supervisor, in addition to the salary provided by law, a sum not exceeding $500.00 for traveling expenses; the Treasurer is hereby directed to pay to the Clerk of the County Board of Commissioners the sum of $40.00 per month, and to the Clerk of Court for the salary of such office help as he sees fit to employ, the sum of $300.00 per year; and to the County Treasurer for Clerk hire the sum of $120.00 per year, and to the County Auditor for traveling expenses during the ensuing year, the sum of $120.00; and to Mrs. H. E. Thomas, former Home Demonstration Agent, for refund of traveling expenses, the sum of $75.00; and to the Clerk of Court the sum of $250.00 for the purpose of havin indexed the judgments and abstracts of jud ents for Hampton county. The sum of three hundred and sixty-five dollars is hereby appropriated for the State Tuberculosis Camp: Provided, That in order to meet the requirements of the terms of an Act of the General Assembly of the State, approved February 20th, 1919, known as Act No. 163 of the Acts of 1917, and the Act of Congress referred to therein, so as to acquire the benefits thereof, the Supervisor and County Commissioners of Hampton county are hereby authorized and empowered to appropriate and use for such purpose any unexpended balances which may remain in the treasury of such county for any purposes, including the appropriation for the ensuing year which may not have been expended for the purpose for which the same may have been appropriated, and the said Board may also appropriate for said purpose all funds derived from the motor vehicle licenses and paid over to said county under existing laws; and said Board may likewise, in its discretion, use the chain gang of said county to supplement any fund made available under the terms of the said Act; and the said Board may also appropriate the commutation road tax for such purpose: Provided, further, That after the application of all funds derived under the provisions of the preceding proviso, should there be a deficiency with which to supplement the amount available for said county of Hampton under the terms of the said Act, then the Supervisor and County Commissioners for Hampton county are hereby authorized and empowered to borrow in the name of the said county such sum of money on the best terms possible as may be necessary to acquire and obtain the total amount available from the Federal Government under the terms of said Act, which shall be used in accordance therewith: Provided, further, That in order to raise a sum sufficient to repay the amount so borrowed, the Auditor of Hampton county is authorized, upon authority from the Supervisor and County Commissioners, or a majority thereof, to levy, and the Treasurer of Hampton county is authorized and directed to collect a tax of not exceeding ten (10) mills upon all the taxable property in Hampton county.
