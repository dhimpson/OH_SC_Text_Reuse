SEC. 5. The Board of Trustees of said school district shall of be composed of seven members, to be elected by the qualified voters of the said school district. The mayor of the city of Rock Hill, twenty days after the approval of the Act by the Governor, shall order an election under the rules prescribed for holding a municipal election, to be held the second Tuesday in April, 1910, for the election of the seven members, three of whom shall serve until the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill in 1916; two of whom shall serve until the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill in 1914, and two of whom shall serve until the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill in 1912, their respective terms to be chosen by lot in the presence of the Mayor and Board of Aldermen of the city of Rock Hill, within three days after their election, when they shall proceed to qualify and organize by the election of one from their number as a chairman, and one as a secretary, and other such officers as they may deem necessary A. D. 1910. and proper; subsequent election to fill vacancies caused by the expiration of the above terms heretofore provided for shall be held every two years, the term thereof to be six years, at the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill, in the manner and under the rules prescribed for holding said municipal elections: Provided, The present Board of Trustees of said school district shall continue in office until their successors shall have been elected and qualified, at which time their books, records, accounts, etc., shall be turned over to their successors. SEC. 6. That the said Board of Trustees, in addition to the duties and powers now provided by the general school law for duties of trustees of school districts, shall have the following duties and powers: 1. To select and purchase suitable grounds or accept donations thereof, and erect buildings thereon, or sell the same when in their judgment proper, and provide such other improvements, equipment and supplies as may be necessary for the use and conduct of the public schools of said school district. 2. To elect and dismiss the superintendents and teachers of said school district, prescribe their duties and terms of office, to fix their salaries, and to cause the examination of teachers whenever, in their opinion, the same shall be necessary, except the Winthrop Training School, over which they shall have no supervision, but the Winthrop authorities are hereby required to report the enrollment in the Training School to the trustees of the Rock Hill School District. Also to pre- scribe, in conjunction with the superintendent, the course of study to be pursued and the text - books to be used in said. schools, using the text - books prescribed by the State Board of Education as far as practicable, to make rules and regulations for the government of the same, and to prescribe and regulate the terms and conditions upon which parties residing without the limits of said school district may be admitted into the public schools thereof; that no pupil of lawful school age residing within the limits of the said school district, and not otherwise debarred, shall be charged any tuition, incidental or other fee whatsoever. 3. To determine and direct the manner in which the special tax hereinbefore provided for and the constitutional poll and three - mill tax, and the dog tax, provided by law, shall be expended in maintaining the public schools of said district, and to disburse the same upon their warrants drawn by their regularly elected or appointed officers upon the County Treasurer as aforesaid.
