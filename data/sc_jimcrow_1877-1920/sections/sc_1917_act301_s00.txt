AN ACT TO ADMIT JAS. M. SILL TO BE PLACED UPON THE PENSION ROLL OF RICHLAND COUNTY, AND TO BE ADMITTED AS A MEMBER OF THE CONFEDERATE INFIRMARY. Whereas, Jas. M. Sill, a citizen of the State, was for many years during the War Between the States, engaged as an employee of the Confederate States Government, but was not reg: 1larly enlisted as a Confederate soldier, but served the Confederate Government for several years in the capacity of guard; and, Whereas, The said Jas. M. Sill has filed his petition asking that the Board of Pensions for Richland county be permitted to place his name upon the pension roll as other Confederate soldiers, and that the Commission of the Confederate Infirmary be authorized to admit him as an inmate of the Confederate Infirmary, as other Confederate soldiers.
