SEC. 2. The said Civil and Criminal Court shall have criminal jurisdiction in all cases wherein the penalty does not exceed five hundred ($ 500) dollars fine, or imprisonment for twelve months, or both, in the discretion of the Court, and in all other actions heretofore cognizable within the said territorial limits of said county of Barnwell, wherein the amount sued for or the value of the property claimed, exclusive of A. D. 1912. costs, does not exceed one thousand dollars; but such jurisdicdiction shall not extend to cases where the title to real estate is in question, nor to cases in chancery, and shall be concurrent with the Courts of Common Pleas therein, in matters within the jurisdiction of the Court herein established.
