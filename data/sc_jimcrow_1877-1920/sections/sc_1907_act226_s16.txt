SEC. 16. The Dispenser shall sell only in a room fronting a shall be sold. public street, without pictures, mirrors, or any ornamentations whatever, inside or outside, and without any screen, curtain, or other device for preventing the passing public from fully viewing what may be transpiring within; nor shall any restaurant, pool or billiard room, or any other form of public amusement, be run or conducted in the building in which said room is situated, nor shall any opening communicating with such be permitted.
