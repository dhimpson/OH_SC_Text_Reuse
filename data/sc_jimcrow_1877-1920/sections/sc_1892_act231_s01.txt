SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That an Act entitled " An Act to create a special school district within the township of Seneca, in Oconee County, to be known as the 19 stat., 1072, Seneca Graded School District, and to authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, " approved December 24th, 1887, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.
