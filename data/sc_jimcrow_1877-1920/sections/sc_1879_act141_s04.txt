SEC. 4. That the sum of two thousand one hundred dollars be, University. and the same is hereby, appropriated for the South Carolina University, to wit: For the payment of the insurance on the University, one thousand dollars, to be paid on the application of the Board of Trustees upon the warrant of the Comptroller General. For the salary of the Librarian of the South Carolina University, five hundred dollars. For repairs to University buildings, six hundred dollars. PENAL AND CHARITABLE INSTITUTIONS.
