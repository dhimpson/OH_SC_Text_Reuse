SEC. 3. That the said association shall have power to provide for and regulate, by its constitution and by laws, a system of self endowment and aid to its members during life, and, after death, relief to their beneficiaries; the endowment and relief to be conferred to be created by assessments on its members. The principal office of said association shall be located at Greenville, S. C., with power to locate divisions and local offices at such time and place as its interest may demand.
