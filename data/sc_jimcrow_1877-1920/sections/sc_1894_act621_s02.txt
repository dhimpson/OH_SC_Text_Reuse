Sec. 2. That it shall be the duty of the City Council of the said city of Anderson, within ten days after the approval of this Act, to order that an election be held at the Court House in the said city of Anderson, and to appoint three Managers to conduct the same, of which election at least ten days ' notice shall be given by advertisement in the newspapers of said city of Anderson, at which election shall be submitted to the qualified voters resident in said city, who Qualification of return real or personal property within the limits thereof for taxation, the question of voting a tax of two mills on the dollar, to be assessed upon all taxable property within said city. Those voting for said tax shall deposit a ballot with the following words either written or printed thereon: " School Tax, Yes. " Those voting against said tax shall deposit a ballot with the following words either written or printed thereon: " School Tax, No. "
