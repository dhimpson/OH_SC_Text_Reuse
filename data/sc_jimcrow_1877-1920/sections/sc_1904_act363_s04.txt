SEC. 4. That said Association be, and it is hereby, empowered, Powers as to to divide, subdivide, improve and exchange any and all lands etc. which it may purchase; to act as agent or trustee of any land owner or citizen of this State in the sale or leasing of his lands, and to procure immigrants, settlers, tenants or laborers for him; to issue bonds, execute mortgages and enter into contracts as fully to all intents and purposes as a private person, not inconsistent with the Constitutions and laws aforesaid.
