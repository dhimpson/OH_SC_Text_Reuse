SEC. 6. That the said Board of School Commissioners shall have power to assess upon each scholar, as supplementary sessed. tuition fees, such sum or sums as may be necessary to meet the expenses of said schools: Provided, That should the parent, guardian or other person with whom such scholar resides make application to the Board of School Commissioners for said district, stating that such parent, guardian or other person with whom such scholar resides is not able to pay tuition, then such scholar shall be admitted free.
