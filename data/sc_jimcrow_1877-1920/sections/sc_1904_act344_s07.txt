SEC. 7. That for the purpose of paying annual interest upon Tax levy. the said bonds, and of establishing a sinking fund for the retirement of the same at maturity or at the period of redemption, it shall be the duty of the County Auditor of said Georgetown County, or of such other officers as may be charged by law with the levy and assessment of taxes, to levy annually such a tax upon all the taxable real and personal property in the said school district as shall be sufficient to meet the said interest, and also to raise a sum equal to one - fortieth (1-40) of the principal of said bonds, to be held as a sinking fund for that purpose. The amount so levied shall be collected by the County Treasurer of said County in like manner as State, County and school taxes are collected, and shall be applied by him solely to the payment of the said interest or of the principal of said bonds, respectively, and shall be used for no other purpose whatsoever.
