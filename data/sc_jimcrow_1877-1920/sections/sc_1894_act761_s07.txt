Sec. 7. That the said City Council shall annually, during the month of August, appoint three citizens of the said city to assess in that month the value of all the taxable property therein, and the said appraisers shall ascertain from Assessments. the taxpayers in person the true value of all their real and personal and mixed estates situated in said city, and enter the same in a book in separate columns, and it shall be signed by the appraisers and returned to the Clerk of Council forthwith; but the taxpayers shall have thirty days from the return of said assessment in which to complain thereof to the City Council, upon the entering of which the Council may lessen any assessment upon good cause shown. That upon the values so ascertained the City Council shall, during the month of October, levy a tax for corporate purposes not to exceed in any year five mills on the dollar, payable in advance to the Clerk and Treasurer of the Council during the months of November and December in each year: Provided, That the provisions of this Section shall not interfere with the levy and collection of the taxes for the town of Abbeville for the present year during the months of February and March next, but shall go into effect immediately thereafter; and all property upon which such taxes shall be levied shall be subject to a first lien for the payment thereof, except for debts due to the State; and upon failure of any person to pay such tax, the party in fault shall be subject to the penalties and remedies provided, or which may hereafter be provided, by law for failure to pay State and County taxes, except that the execution for the collection thereof shall be signed and sealed by the Clerk and Treasurer of the Council and directed to a police officer of the said city.
