§ 12. Disposition of Tax Levy. - That the Treasurer, when he has collected the taxes for interest and sinking funds herein provided, shall turn same over to the County Sinking Fund Commissioners who shall give a receipt for same showing what funds said money shall be applied to and said County Sinking Fund shall pay all interest on said bonds when due and invest the funds so arising so as to increase same each year and to pay the bonds when they fall due.
