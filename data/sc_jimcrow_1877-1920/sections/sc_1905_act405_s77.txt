SEC. 79. Courts martial may sentence an officer convicted Sentence. by them to be punished according to the nature and degree of the offense, and according to military usage; but in time of peace such punishment shall not extend further than cashiering the officer convicted, and disqualifying him from holding any office in the militia of this State, and imposing a fine not exceeding one hundred dollars. Officers cashiered by sentence of a court martial shall be precluded thereby from again volunteering into the military service of the State, except the sentence be remitted by the Commander - in - Chief.
