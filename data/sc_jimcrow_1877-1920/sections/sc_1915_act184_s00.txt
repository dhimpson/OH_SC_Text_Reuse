AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ELECTION OF SIX TRUSTEES FOR SCHOOL DIS- TRICT NO. 8, IN CALHOUN COUNTY. AN ACT to Provide for the Election of Six Trustees for School District No. 8, in Calhoun County. Section 1. Election of Trustees—Term of Office.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, On the OF SOUTH CAROLINA 377 third Tuesday in April, 1915, the qualified electors of School District No. 8, of Calhoun county, shall elect from among their number six Trustees for said Schol District as hereinafter provided: Two of said Trustees shall serve for two years, two for four years, and two for six years, the six Trustees receiving the highest number of ballots to decide by lot the term of office of each, whether for two, four or six years, at their first meeting after such election, and on the third Tuesday of April every two years thereafter, there shall be two Trustees elected as successors to those whose terms of office shall have expired § 2. Notice of Election—Managers.—The County Superintendent of Education shall give at least two weeks’ notice of such election in some newspaper of the widest circulation in said School District, and shall appoint three managers of said election who shall certify to the Superintendent of Education the result of said election: Provided, That no irregularity in the notice or appointment of managers shall in any way defeat the election if held on said dates § 3. Duties of Trustees—That said Trustees shall perform all the duties, and exercise all the powers, now conferred by law upon School Trustees within said district § 4. Inconsistent Acts Repealed.—That all Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed § 5. Immediately Effective.—That this Act shall go into effect immediately upon approval. Approved
