AN ACT TO EXTEND THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, AND 8 OF AN ACT ENTITLED AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS TO SUBMIT TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF THEIR SEVERAL COUNTIES A PROPOSITION TO ALTER THE FENCE LAWS, AND TO PROVIDE FOR EFFECTUATING THE SAME, TO TOWNSHIPS SIX AND EIGHT OF FAIRFIELD COUNTY, WITHOUT AN ELECTION.
