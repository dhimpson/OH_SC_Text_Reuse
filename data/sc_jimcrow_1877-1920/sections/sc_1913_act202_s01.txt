§ 5. Bond Election—Proviso.—That in case the trustees in charge of the aforesaid graded school fail to negotiate the mortgage authorized as aforesaid within any reasonable time after the passage of this Act, and shall certify the same to the trustees of said Aiken School District No. 1, then, and in that event, said Trustees of Aiken School District No. 1 be, and they are hereby. authorized and empowered, after giving public notice once a week for four weeks in one of the newspapers published in the city of Aiken, to call an election of the qualified electors of said Aiken School District No. 1 to vote upon the question of the issuing, by said Aiken School District No. 1, of thirteen thousand dollars of bonds of said district, to be used for the purpose of the erection of said additional building for said graded school; that the said Trustees of said Aiken School District are authorized and empowered to appoint the managers for said election and the time and place for holding of the same, which said election shall be held under the rules and regulation of the Constitution and Statutes of the State of South Carolina in regard to General Elections, except that the production of a registration certificate by the registration officers of Aiken county for the precinct of Aiken shall be sufficient without the use of the registration book for said precinct, but that the elector must reside within the territory of said School District No. 1, and that the said trustees must furnish for the use of the voters in said election an equal number ofballots, on one set of which shall be printed the words, “For the Issuing of Bonds,” and on the other set the words, ‘Against the Issuing of Bonds,” and a sufficient number of ballots shall be placed at the usual place of voting on the day of election. That the managers as aforesaid to be appointed shall conduct the said election, and at the close thereof shall canvass the vote and make return thereof in writing to the trustees aforesaid. who shall act thereon, and certify to the same to the Clerk of Court of Aiken county for filing in his office. § 6. Bond Issue.—That if at said election a majority of the votes declare for the issuing of bonds, the said Board of Trustees of the said School District shall issue the same up to the amount of $13,000, if so much be necessary, to run for a period of not more than forty years, with the right to redeem the same after twenty years; said bonds to draw not greater than seven per cent. per annum, but to be issued at a lower rate if they can be sold at par; and the said trustees are authorized to sell said bonds and appropriate the proceeds of said sale along with the Directors of the Aiken Institute with the funds as specified as aforesaid for the election of the additional school building on the lot as aforesaid to be a part of the graded school property in the city of Aiken. § 7. Bonds.—That the said bonds and the coupons thereof shall be signed by the Chairman of the Board of Trustees and countersigned by the Secretary of the Board of Trustees of said Aiken School District No. 1: Provided, That the signatures of the said officers on said coupons may be lithographed and that the said lithographing shall be sufficient signing of the bonds and coupons. § 8. Annual Tax Levy to Pay Interest.—That upon the order of the trustees and their successors in office, it shall be the duty of the county officers, charged with the assessment and collection of taxes of Aiken county, to levy and collect annually from the property of said school district a sum sufficient to pay the interest on said school bonds; and also a sum to provide for a sinking fund for the payment of the said sum when due, the amount of said taxes to be designated and certified to said trustees, and said Board of Trustees to pay the said coupons as they mature. § 8. Exempt from Taxation.—That the bonds issued under this Act shall be exempt from taxation—State, county and municipal. § 10. Effective Immediately.—That all Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are here, repealed; and that this Act shall take effect +mmediately upon approval by the Governor. Approved
