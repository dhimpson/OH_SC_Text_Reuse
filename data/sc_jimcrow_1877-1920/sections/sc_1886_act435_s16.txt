Government and other like purposes, a tax of one fourth of one mill, or such portion thereof as may be necessary, upon the entire taxable property of the State to meet the semi annual payment of the interest and the redemption of the principal of the bonds and certificates of stock the issue of which is hereinabove provided for; and this pledge is hereby declared to be a contract between the State of South Carolina and every holder Contract made of said bonds and certificates of stock, which pledge shall be by the State. expressed on the face of said bonds and certificates of stock.
