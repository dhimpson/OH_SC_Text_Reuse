§ 4. Tax Levy—Disbursement.—If a majority of the ballots cast at such election be in favor of said annual levy, said Trustees shall make return thereof to the County Auditor of Lancaster County, under oath, stating the true number of ballots cast in favor and against the same, which return shall become a permanent record of his office, and said Auditor and his successors in office be, and are hereby authorized and required, annually thereafter, to levy upon all the property returned for taxation within said school district, one-half of one mill on the dollar, and the County Treasurer collect the same, as a special fund for the maintenance of a public library therein, to be paid to the Trustees, or other authorized officers of such public library, upon their warrant.
