Section 5. The provisions of this Act shall not apply to the Counties of Charleston, Laurens, Berkeley, Chester, Spartanburg, Aiken, Horry, Edgefield, Georgetown, Richland, Saluda, Beaufort, Lancaster, Abbeville, York, Newberry, Kershaw, Lexington, Oconee, nor to the town of Liberty in Pickens County, or to Anderson Township in Anderson County.
