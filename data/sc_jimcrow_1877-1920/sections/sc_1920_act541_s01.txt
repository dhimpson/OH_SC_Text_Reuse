Section 1. Act (1893, XXI Stats. 480) Amended - Name of Winthrop College Changed. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: That Section 3 of an Act entitled " An Act to amend Sections 2, 7 and 14 of an Act entitled ' An Act to establish a normal and industrial college in the State of South Carolina for the education of white girls, ' approved December 23d, A. D. 1891, so as to provide for the election and term of local Trustees, and to authorize the State to incur certain expenses, " approved December 23d, A. D. 1893, be amended, and the same is hereby amended by striking out the words " The Winthrop Normal and Industrial College of South Carolina " wherever the same occurs in said section and inserting in lieu thereof the words " Winthrop College (The South Carolina College for Women), " so that the said college shall be known and called hereafter " Winthrop College (The South Carolina College for Women). " And that the change of name of the said institution as herein set forth shall in no way invalidate any securities issued in aid of the same.
