Section 1. County Government of Aiken County.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, The county government of Aiken county shall be administered by three Commissioners, to be known as the “County Commissioners of Aiken County,” each of whom shall hold office for a term of six years (except as hereinafter provided for those first elected to such office), unless sooner removed for incapacity, neglect, or other sufficient cause, by the Governor.
