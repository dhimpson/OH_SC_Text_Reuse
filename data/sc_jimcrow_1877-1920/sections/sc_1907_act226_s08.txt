SEC. 8. It shall be the duty of the said Board to cause an liquors to be analysis of the liquors in stock to be made by some person competent to determine whether any of said liquors are adulterated or impure, to the end that no impure liquors shall be sold by Dispensaries. If upon analysis it shall be determined that such liquors are adulterated or impure, the County Dispensary Board may retain the price thereof from the seller, or if they have been paid for, the said Board shall not allow said liquors to be sold, and may, in the name of the State, institute an action against the seller for the recovery of the amount so paid.
