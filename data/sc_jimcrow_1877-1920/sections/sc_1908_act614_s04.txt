SEC. 4. That the County Auditor and the County Treasurer for Laurens County are hereby required and directed to levy and collect, annually, a sufficient tax upon the taxable property in said School District to pay the interest on the said bonds; the said levy to be in addition to the levy already required by the Acts of 1891 and 1907 to pay the interest on the bonds already issued by the said School District. Any surplus remaining after the payment of the annual interest shall be applied, under the direction of the said Board of Trustees, to the payment of the principal of said bonds.
