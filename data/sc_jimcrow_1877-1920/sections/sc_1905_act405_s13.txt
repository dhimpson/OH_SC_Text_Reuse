SEC. 13. The uniform of the active militia shall be the same Uniform. as the service uniform - olive drab and khaki - now prescribed, or may hereafter be prescribed, for the army of the United States, except that the letters U. S. on said uniform shall be replaced by the letters S. C.: Provided, That officers may, when not on duty, wear the prescribed dress and full dress uniforms of the army of the United States. The change from the present blue dress uniform to the olive drab uniform shall go into effect in each organization whenever it is found practicable to supply the enlisted men of such organization with the olive drab uniform.
