SEC. 4. That if a majority of the votes cast at said election tees with ref shall be for issuing of said bonds, then the said trustees shall issue the same, to run for a period of not more than forty years, with the right to redeem the same after twenty years; and the said trustees shall sell said bonds for not less than par, and appropriate the proceeds of the said sale to the purpose of purchasing a lot, if a lot be needed, and erecting school buildings thereon, as said trustees shall deem best for school. purposes; and the said bonds shall constitute a first lien on the property purchased and improved, or on the improvements made from the proceeds of the sale thereof.
