AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED "AN ACT TO REGULATE THE HOLDING OF ELECTIONS FOR THE COMMISSION FORM OF GOVERNMENT IN CITIES OF OVER FOUR THOUSAND INHABITANTS, AND TO PROVIDE FOR THE ADOPTION OF SAID FORM OF GOVERNMENT IN CITIES OF OVER TEN THOUSAND AND LESS THAN TWENTY THOUSAND INHABITANTS, AND CITIES OF OVER FIFTY THOUSAND AND LESS THAN ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND INHABITANTS, AND IN CERTAIN CITIES NAMED HEREIN," BY ADDING A PROVISO WITH REFERENCE TO THE CITY OF FLORENCE TO THE END OF SECTIONS 8, 12, 13, 14, 17, 18, 21, 26 AND 29, RESPECTIVELY.
