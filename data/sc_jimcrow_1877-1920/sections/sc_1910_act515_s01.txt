SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the County to State of South Carolina, That the County Board of Commissioners of Laurens County are hereby authorized and directed to pay to Calvin Harper (colored) the sum of twenty - five dol-lars per annum as a recognition of his services to the Confederacy as body - guard to Capt. William D. Farley and Gen. Hugh S. Farley, in the War Between the States.
