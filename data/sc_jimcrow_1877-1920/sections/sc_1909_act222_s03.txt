SEC. 3. That said board shall have power to purchase, hold, Powers of secure, enjoy, possess and retain to itself, in perpetuity or for any term of years, any tenements or hereditaments or any other property of any nature whatsoever, or to sell or alien the same as the board may see fit, and by its said name to sue and to be sued, plead and be impleaded in a court of competent jurisdiction in this State, and to make such rules and by - laws for its own government as are not repugnant to the laws of the land and for the order and good government and management thereof as may be thought necessary and expedient.
