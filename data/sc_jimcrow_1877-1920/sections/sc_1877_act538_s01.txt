Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That for the purpose of building a railroad from the town of Spartanburg, in this State, to the city of Augusta, in the State of Georgia, by the way of Laurens Court House, in the County of Laurens, Ninety-six, in the County of Abbeville, and Edgefield Court House, in the County of Edgefield, the Counties along the line of said railroad and such other Counties in this State as may avail themselves, in the manner hereinafter provided, of the privileges of this Act, may issue bonds payable twelve years from date, with annual interest of not more than seven per cent., payable semi-annually, for such amount as by a vote of said Counties respectively may be determined. And for the purpose of redeeming said bonds the County Commissioners of the respective Counties shall, and are hereby required to, levy an annual tax on the taxable property within their Counties of at least sufficient to pay the annual interest and five per cent. of the principal of the bonds issued.
