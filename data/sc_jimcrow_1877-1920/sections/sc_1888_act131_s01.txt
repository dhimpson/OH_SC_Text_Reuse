SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Section 1 of an Act entitled " An Act to creat a special school A. A. 1887, 1. district within the Township of Seneca in Oconee County, to be amended. known as the Seneca Graded School District, and authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, " approved December 27th, A. D. 1887, be amended so as to read as follows: SECTION 1. That that portion of Oconee County in Seneca School district Township in the form of a regular octagon, having for its centre the intersection of the Richmond and Danville and the Blue Ridge Railroads, and formed upon two lines extending respectively due North and South and East and West from said centre, be, and the same is hereby, constituted a new school district, to be known as the " Seneca Graded School District. " The said Name. territory includes the town of Seneca, and extends two and a half miles from said centre to extreme points of boundary as represented on a map of said district made by J. W. Harper day of July, 1887.
