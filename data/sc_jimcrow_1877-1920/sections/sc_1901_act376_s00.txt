AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORA-TION OF TOWNS OF NOT LESS THAN ONE THOUSAND, NOR MORE THAN FIVE THOUSAND INHABITANTS,"" APPROVED MARCH 5TH, 1896, SO AS TO DELEGATE TO CERTAIN TOWNS THE POWER TO AUTHORIZE ERECTIONS OF OBSTACLES IN, UNDER OR UPON ITS STREETS AND ROADS, AND FURTHER AS TO FIRST ELECTION OF OFFICERS AFTER ACCEPTING CHARTER UNDER THE GENERAL LAW.
