§ 4. Seal Authorized—Proviso.—That the said Board of Trustees are hereby authorized to adopt and make a seal for their official use in the execution of said bonds and like important papers; that said bonds shall be signed by the Chairman and countersigned by the Secretary and Treasurer of the Board of Trustees of said district, and the official seal of said board attached thereto: Provided, That the signatures of said officers may be lithographed, or printed or engraved on the coupons attached to said bonds, and such signatures shall be a sufficient signing thereof.
