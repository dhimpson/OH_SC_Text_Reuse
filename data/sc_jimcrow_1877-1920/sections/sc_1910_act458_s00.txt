AN ACT TO FURTHER AMEND THE ACT ENTITLED “AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT IN RICHLAND COUNTY, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A SCHOOL TAX THEREIN, APPROVED DECEMBER 24, 1880, AS AMENDED BY ACTS APPROVED DECEMBER 17, 1881, DECEMBER 21, 1883, DECEMBER 23, 1893, AND FEBRUARY 20, 1902 BY AUTHORIZING THE SCHOOL COMMISSIONERS TO BORROW MONEY BY MORTGAGING LANDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF ERECT-ING SCHOOL BUILDINGS.
