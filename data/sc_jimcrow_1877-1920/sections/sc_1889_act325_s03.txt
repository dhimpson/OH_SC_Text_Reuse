Sec. 3. That every male citizen of this State of the age of twenty-one years who shall be the owner of a lot within the corporate limits of said town with a dwelling house thereon, and having an estate in fee simple in said lot previous to and at the time of the election for any member or members of the said Town Council, shall be entitled to vote at such election; and all male inhabitants of the town who have resided within the corporate limits thereof for sixty days next immediately preceding such election and who are qualified to vote for members of the General Assembly shall be entitled to vote upon delivering up their certificates of registration to the Managers of Election.
