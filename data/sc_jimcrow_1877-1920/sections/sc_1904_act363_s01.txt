SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the ciation, char State of South Carolina, That A. J. Mathison, E. J. Watson, J. C. Hemphill, T. H. Rainsford, W. O. Hammond, C. C. Langston, Knox Livingston, E. W. Dobbs, W. B. Moore, E. S. Addison, John Scott, D. A. Spivey, F. A. Burroughs, J. H. David, Wm. Goldsmith, and others, the members of the Executive Committee of the South Carolina Immigration Association, and their successors, be, and they are hereby, made and created a body politic and corporate, under the name and style of the South Carolina Immigration Association, for the purpose of encouraging immigration and securing and bringing to the several Counties of this State white settlers and laborers, and generally to conduct, do and perform whatsoever may be proper and necessary to accomplish the purposes of the Association, not inconsistent with the Constitutions and laws of the United States and of this State.
