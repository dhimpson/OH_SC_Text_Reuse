SECTION 8. That the sum of two hundred and eighty - three thousand seven hundred and forty - four 03-100 dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated to pay the interest on the valid debt of the State which shall mature first day of July, 1898, and on the first day of January, 1899, and for unpaid interest payable in prior years: Provided, That payment by the State Treasurer of the semi - annual interest on the valid debt of the State which matured on the first day of January, 1898, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed and validated.
