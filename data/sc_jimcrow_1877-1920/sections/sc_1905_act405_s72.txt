SEC. 73. Any person belonging to the military forces of this State, going to or returning from any parade, drill or meeting, which he may be appointed by law to attend, shall, together with his conveyance and the military property of the State, be allowed to pass free through all toll - gates and over all tollbridges and ferries. The constitution and jurisdiction of courts martial, the form and manner in which the proceedings of military courts. shall be conducted and recorded, and the forms of oaths and affirmations taken in the administration of military law by such courts, the limits of punishment and the proceeding in revision shall be governed by the Articles of War and the law and procedure of the courts martial of the United States, except as hereinafter modified. No officer or enlisted man shall be tried except on written charges and specifications. The arraignment of the accused, the proceedings, trial and record, shall, in all respects, conform to the forms and requirements of the United States law and practice of courts martial, except as herein otherwise provided.
