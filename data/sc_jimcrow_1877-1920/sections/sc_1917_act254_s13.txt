Sec. 13. That all Acts or parts of Acts heretofore enacted by the General Assembly as amendments to the Act entitled " An Act to amend an Act entitled ' An Act to provide for the establishment of a new school district in the county of Darlington, and to authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, ' ” approved December 24, 1883, and approved January 4, 1904, be, and they are hereby, repealed.
