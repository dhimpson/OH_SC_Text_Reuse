Section 1.,Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That all soldiers and sailors of the Confederate States, who enlisted from this State and who were honorably discharged from such service, shall hereafter be exempt from the charge of any license for the carrying on of any business or profession within this State, or any city, town or village therein: Provided, That such soldiers and sailors shall file with the Clerk of the Court of the County in which he resides, the proper evidence of his service in the Confederate war: Provided, further, That no partnership shall exist in any such business or profession, with any person not a bona fide soldier or sailor of the said Confederate States.
