SEC. 3. That said corporation have and is hereby given full power to keep up, conduct and maintain such college and to teach and to cause to be taught therein those branches of learning A. D. 1909. which are usually taught in colleges for girls and women in this State and the United States and to confer such degree of graduation, honors, and distinctions as it may think proper upon its students, and such honorary degrees on other persons as may think proper.
