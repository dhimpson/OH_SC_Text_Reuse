SECTION 8. 1. That the following sums, if so much be necessary, be, and the same are hereby, appropriated for miscellaneous expenses, namely; 2. For the civil contingent fund, one thousand dollars, paid on the order of the Governor, upon the warrants of the gent fund. Comptroller General 3. For the aid of the State Agricultural and Mechanical Society of South Carolina, two thousand five hundred dollars, to be paid on the application of the President of said society, upon the warrant of the Comptroller General: Provided, That no gambling, chance or raffling games shall be permitted upon the grounds occupied by said society. 4. For the purposes of an Act entitled " An Act to provide for the payment of water used in the public institutions of the State located in Columbia, " approved February 9th, 1882, one thousand dollars, to be paid on the warrants of the Comptroller General, issued in accordance with the provisions of said Act. 5. To pay the expenses of the Committee appointed to examine the books of the State Treasurer for the fiscal year ending October 31st, 1887, one hundred and twenty five dollars, if so much be necessary 6. For defraying the expenses of the continuance of the consolidation of the bonded debt of the State, as provided by law, one thousand dollars, if so much be necessary, the same to be paid on the application of the State Treasurer, upon the warrant of the Comptroller General. 7. To pay the claims passed by the General Assembly at its regular session of 1887, eleven hundred dollars. 8. To pay for the public printing of this fiscal year, fifteen thousand dollars, if so much be necessary. 9. To pay the cost of providing artificial limbs for the soldiers of this State who lost legs or arms, or who were permanently disabled in their legs or arms, one thousand dollars, if so much be necessary, to be paid in accordance with the provisions of an Act entitled " An Act to provide artificial limbs for all soldiers of the State who lost their legs or arms or who have been permanently disabled, & c., " approved December 17th, 1881. 10. For the salaries of the Railroad Commissioners, six thousand three hundred dollars; for the Clerk of the Railroad Commissioners, twelve hundred dollars, and three hundred and fifty dollars to pay the contingent expenses of the office for the year 1888, if so much be necessary, to be advanced by the State until the same shall have been collected from the railroad companies of this State in the manner prescribed by law, and when collected the same shall be replaced in the State Treasury. 11.To cover the amounts appropriated by the several Acts and Joint Resolutions passed by the General Assembly at its regular session of 1887, ten thousand dollars, if so much be necessary. 12. For the salaries of the Supervisors of Registration, seven thousand dollars, that is to say, to pay the Supervisors of Registration for each County in the State, except the County of Charleston, the sum of two hundred dollars for the services to be rendered during the fiscal year commencing November 1, 1887, and to the Supervisor of Registration for Charleston County the sum of four hundred dollars for services to be rendered during the same period, said amounts to be paid on the first day of June, 1888, out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated. 13. For the transportation of convicts to the Penitentiary from the several Counties of this State, five thousand dollars, if so much be necessary, to be paid on the application of the Superintendent of the Penitentiary, approved by the Board of Directors, upon the warrants of the Comptroller General. 15. For the payment of the rent of the store occupied as the Supreme Court Library while repairs were being done to said Library room in State House, being for four months from May 18th, 1887, to September 18th, 1887, at $ 25 per month, one hundred dollars, to be paid on the warrant of the Comptroller General, upon the application of the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court. 16. For repairs and improvements to the Governor's mansion, Governor's and for insurance and lighting, fifteen hundred dollars, if so much be necessary, to be paid on the order of the Governor, upon the warrant of the Comptroller General. 17. For the completion of the repairs of the State House, thirty five thousand dollars, if so much be necessary, in addition to the balance reported as unexpended, which balance is hereby reappropriated for this purpose: Provided, This amount is expended under the personal superintendence of a competent architect, who shall reside in the city of Columbia, in this State, during the progress of the work. 18. For paying for gas for State House, seven hundred dollars, if so much be necessary. 19. To pay for fuel for the purpose of heating the State House, thirteen hundred dollars, if so much be necessary. 20. For the pay of Commissioners and Managers of Election, fifteen thousand dollars. 21. To pay for advertising notices of election, two thousand dollars: Provided, That all notices of election published in the newspapers throughout the State shall be printed unleaded and in type not larger than brevier, and no more shall be charged or paid for any such notices than one dollar per square for the first insertion, and fifty cents per square for the second or each subsequent insertion. 22. For the pay of Messengers of Election, twelve hundred dollars. 23. To pay for books, blanks and stationery for general election, five hundred dollars. 24. To pay for books and certificates of registration, five hundred dollars. 25. To pay the cost of placing upon the Palmetto Monument, which stands on the State House grounds, the names of such members of the Palmetto Regiment as have been heretofore omitted through inadvertence or otherwise, the sum of one hundred dollars, if so much be necessary, and the Adjutant and Inspector General of the State is hereby directed to have this work done, and the cost thereof, as above provided for, shall be paid on the warrant of the Comptroller General upon the order of the said Adjutant and Inspector General. 26. To pay the account of M. H. Berry for thirty (30) dozen. brace arm chairs ordered for the galleries of the Senate Chamber and Hall of the House of Representatives, at twelve dollars and fifty cents per dozen, three hundred and seventy five dollars, to be paid on the warrant of the Comptroller General upon the order of the Governor. 27. For the pay of the Joint Committee appointed to report upon the best policy for the future provision of the colored insane, eighty one 20-100 dollars; that is to say, to J. A. Sligh of the Senate, two days ' per diem, ten dollars, and mileage, nine dollars; to G. R. Dean of the House, two days ' per diem, ten dollars, and mileage, twenty one 20-100 dollars; and to J. F. Ficken of the House, one day's per diem, five dollars, and mileage, twenty six dollars. 28. To provide for the pensions to Confederate soldiers and others, as authorized by the Act of the General Assembly at its present session, the sum of fifty thousand dollars, if so much be necessary, to be paid to the order of the applicant upon the warrant of the Comptroller General: Provided, That the Governor be, and he is hereby, authorized to borrow from time to time such amounts as may be necessary to meet this appropriation. 29. To pay the claims of the Commissioners and Managers of Election of Edgefield County for election held for Probate Judge of said County, December 6th, 1887, two hundred and fiftyeight dollars and ninety cents.
