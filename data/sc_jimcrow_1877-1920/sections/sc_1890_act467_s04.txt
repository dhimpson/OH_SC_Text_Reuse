SEC. 2. That the said Act be further amended by adding thereto the following Section, to be designated as Section 13 of said Act, viz.: That so much of the capital stock of any bank, banking assoed in bonds exciation, or other moneyed corporation located within this State, as is or shall be invested in the bonds and stock, or either of them, the issue of which is provided for in the said Act, shall be exempt from all State, County, township, municipal, or other taxation.
