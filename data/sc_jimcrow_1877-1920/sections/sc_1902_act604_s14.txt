Sec. 16. It shall be unlawful for any officer authorized to make contracts, to be paid from the money levies provided in a misdemeanor. this Act, to make any contract or contracts in excess of the $ 377a. money provided to be raised by said levies, and any officer or officers convicted of violating the provisions of this section shall be punished as for a misdemeanor. All contracts made in violation of this section shall be void.
