SEC. 2. Lake City township transferred and annexed to Lake City Florence county under this Act shall be known as Lake City township, and those portions of Lee and Sumter townships of Williamsburg county transferred and annexed to Florence county under this Act shall be known as Lee township, and those parts of Lake and Ridge townships hereby annexed to Florence county shall be known as Lake township. And that portion of Lake township which, by above description of official survey, is left in Williamsburg county, shall be incorporated in, and known as, Indian township by an extension of the line between Ridge township and Indian township to Lynches River. That all of the townships herein formed are hereby declared to be bodies corporate, with such powers, duties, liabilities and incidents as now or may hereafter be provided by law for said townships, as herein named.
