Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Section 259 of the General Statutes of this State, relating to the compensation of the members of the State Board of Equalization, be amended by striking out the word “ten” on the third line thereof and inserting the word “five” in lieu thereof. So that said Section as amended shall read as follows: Each member of the State Board of Equalization shall receive three dollars per day for each day he shall be employed in performing the duties enjoined upon him, and five cents per mile for traveling to, and the same for returning from, the seat of government, to be computed by the most usually traveled route, and paid out of the State Treasury, on the warrant of the Comptroller General.
