AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED “AN ACT TO ESTABLISH A NEW SCHOOU DISTRICT IN A PORTION OF THE TOWNSHIPS OF SCUFFLETOWN, YOUNG AND LAURENS, IN THE COUNTY OF LAURENS, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A SCHOOL TAX THEREIN”. APPROVED DECEMBER 24TH, 1891, SO AS TO FIX THE AREA EMBRACED IN AND TO NAME SAID SCHOOL DISTRICT.
