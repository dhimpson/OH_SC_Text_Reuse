Austin, Abner B. Wright, John B. Hyde, William S. Agnew and William R. White, and their associates, who have formed an association under the name of the Atlantic SelfEndowment and Benevolent Association of America, and their successors, shall be, and they are hereby declared to be, a body Incorporation. politic and corporate, under the name and style of the Atlantic Name. Self Endowment and Benevolent Association of America.
