SEC. 7. That the State Board of Education, as now constiState Board tuted, shall constitute the State High School Board, with full of Education authority to prescribe all such regulations as may not be inconHigh School sistent with this Act. The State High School Board shall provide rules for the apportionment and disbursement of the State aid to the high schools, giving due recognition to the number of years of the high school work, to the number of the courses of study offered, to the enrollments of pupils, the amount of industrial or commercial training given, and to such other matters of local merit as may appear to the board proper after tion. a careful examination of each high school: Provided, That not more than $ 500 may be given to a two - year high school, nor more than $ 600 may be given to a three - year high school, nor more than $ 700 may be given to a four - year high school: Provided, further, That an additional $ 100 may be given to a township high school, or to a high school embracing as many as six common school districts: Provided, further, That the board may give additional aid for industrial and commercial courses: Provided, further, That the board may give to any high school levying a special high school school tax of not less than one mill, twenty - five dollars for each and every common school district composing that high school unit: Provided, further, That no high school shall receive aid unless it has at least twenty - five high school pupils and two teachers in the high school department: Provided, further, That the board shall give aid to a rural high school with but fifteen high school pupils and one teacher: Provided, further, That no County shall receive more than five per cent. of the annual appropriation provided for under this Act.
