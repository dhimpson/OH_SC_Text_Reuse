AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE SCHOOL TRUSTEES OF THE AIKEN SCHOOL TO ERECT A NEW SCHOOL BUILDING AND TO PROVIDE FOR RAISING FUNDS FOR THE SAME. Whereas, The Aiken Institute, the graded school of School District No. 1, of Aiken county, is controlled by a Board of Trustees composed of the Directors of the Aiken Institute. a corporation, and the trustees of said school district, and supported by the school funds of said school district, and tuition fees, according to certain Acts of the General Assembly of South Carolina passed during the year 1890 and 1891; and, Whereas, It is necessary to accommodate the increased number of scholars in said school district that another school building should be erected: Section 1. Trustees of Aiken School Authorized to Erect New Building.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the Trustees of Aiken School District No. 1, which embraces the corporate limits of the city of Aiken. be. and they are hereby, authorized and empowered and directed to appropriate five thousand dollars out of the surplus fund of said school district, which they have on hand during the year 1912, towards the erection of such needed additional school building, to be used along with two thousand dollars now held by the Trustees of the Aiken Institute for the erection of said building. § 2. May Execute Mortgage to Raise Funds.—That an additional school building be erected upon the lot owned by the Aiken Institute at such place on said lot and of such size and dimensions as shall be fixed by the Board of Trustees governing said Aiken Institute; and inasmuch as it is estimated that said needed building will cost at least twenty thousand dollars, and there are no other school funds available than the aforesaid amounts, the stockholders of said corporation, the Aiken Institute, by a majority vote in a meeting called for said purpose, are hereby authorized and empowered to empower the trustees who manage said school to mortgage said lot, together with the building now thereon and the other building to be built for the purpose of raising so much of the additional fund as may be needed; and said authority is given, notwithstanding the fact that the deeds upon which said property are held prescribe that the property shall be held for school purposes only, and to the extent of said mortgage given for said purpose, only said property is hereby declared (if said stockholders so direct) to be free from said limitations for said purpose. § 3. Payment of Mortgage—How.—That in order to provide for the payment of said mortgage so authorized as aforesaid, the School Trustees of said Graded School District No. 1, of Aiken County, be, and they are hereby, authorized and empowered and required to pay upon said mortgage from year to year any surplus that may be to their credit as School Trustees in the treasury of Aiken county; and the Treasurer of said county shall recognize and pay their warrant therefor. § 4. City Council May Release Property from Lien.—That the City Council of Aiken by a majority vote be, and they are hereby. authorized and empowered to release the property of the said Aiken Institute, which is to be covered by the aforesaid mortgage, so as to allow the lien of said mortgage from any claim which they may be advised that they may have, because of bonds previously issued by the said city of Aiken under Section 4 of Act No. 771 of the General Assembly of South Carolina, approved December 16, 1891, and entitled “An Act to authorize the city of Aiken to issue bonds in aid of the Aiken Institute.”
