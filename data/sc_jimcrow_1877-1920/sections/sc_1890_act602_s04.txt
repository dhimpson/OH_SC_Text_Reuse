SEC. 4. That the said Board of Trustees, in addition to the duties and powers prescribed by law for Trustees of School Districts, shall have the power, and it shall be their duty, to determine the studies and class books to be used in the schools of said District; to elect and dismiss the teachers; to fix their salaries; to prescribe their duties, and to make rules for the government of the schools, and to take in charge and keep in School property. Order all buildings and other property belonging to or used for carrying on the schools of the District herein created.
