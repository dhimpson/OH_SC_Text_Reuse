SEC. 2. That annually hereafter two of the members of the Two trustees to Board of School Commissioners for said school district, such retire every year, two to be determined by lot, if necessary, shall retire from office and shall be ineligible for re - election for the next ensuing year. The vacancy thus created, and any other vacancy in said Board which may for any cause, at any time, occur, shall be filled by election by the remaining members of such Board.
