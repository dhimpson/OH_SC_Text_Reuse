All qualified electors residing in such township shall be allowed to vote in such election. The form of ballots shall be: " For........ Mills Additional Road Tax " or " Against........ Mills Additional Road Tax. " The Township Commissioners or Managers shall certify the result of such election to the County Supervisor and County Auditor. If a majority of the votes be for the additional tax, the County Auditor shall levy the same, and the County Treasurer shall collect it at the same time and manner as State and county taxes and place it to the credit of the township, and shall not be paid out except upon claims from said township allowed by the Township Board of Commissioners and marked " Special Road Fund......
