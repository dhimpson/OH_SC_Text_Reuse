AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE TRANSFER AND ANNEXATION OF A PORTION OF FAIRFIELD COUNTY TO RICHLAND COUNTY, AND TO ALTER THE COUNTY LINES OF SAID COUNTIES TO CONFORM THERETO. Whereas, At an election duly ordered and held in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution and laws of this State, on the question whether the county lines of Fairfield county and Richland county should be so altered so as to transfer and annex to Richland county a certain portion of Fairfield county, more than two - thirds. of the vote cast in said election were in favor of such transfer and annexation; and, Whereas, All of the conditions required by the Constitution and laws of this State were and have been complied with:
