SEC. 3. The Chief Game Warden shall have charge of the Warden force, and exercise supervision over the enforcement and duties. of the laws. He shall be empowered to make contracts with Wardens: Provided, That no Warden shall be paid in excess of the twelve hundred dollars per annum, nor be allowed more than six hundred dollars for traveling expenses. And he shall have the power to dismiss the Wardens at his discretion.
