SEC. 13. That the said Mayor and Aldermen shall be vested, jointly and severally, with jurisdiction in all criminal cases that in criminal cases shall occur within the corporate limits of said town, equal to that now vested by law in Trial Justices in this State. But it is provided that at some hour during the morning of each day, Daily court the Mayor, or acting Mayor, of said town, shall hold a Court to dispose of such cases as may be ready for trial or other consideration.
