SEC. 15. That in anticipation of the taxes hereinbefore levied, Power of Go: the Governor and the State Treasurer and the Comptroller to borrow General be, and they are hereby, empowered to borrow, on the credit of the State, so much money from time to time as may be needed to meet promptly at maturity the interest which shall mature on the first day of July, 1907, and on the first day of January, 1908, on the valid debt of the State, and to pay the current expenses of the State government for the present fiscal year, and for pensions: Provided, That the sum so borrowed A. D. 1907. shall not exceed five hundred thousand dollars.
