§ 11. State Hospital Commission. Item 1. Interest on Sinking Fund loan made to State Hospital Commission to April 1, 1913.... $ 8,232 75 In the event Sinking Fund Commission should find it necessary to recall said loan before the next session of the General Assembly, the State Hospital Commission is hereby authorized and empowered to borrow the sum of $ 159,659.72 to meet said loan at a rate of interest not exceeding five per cent. per annum and the credit of the State is hereby pledged for the payment of the same; and further, the General Assembly at its next session shall provide for the payment of said loan and interest.
