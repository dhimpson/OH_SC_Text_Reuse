§ 4. May Levy Tax. - That in addition to the rights and privileges hereinbefore granted the said school district shall have the power to levy and collect the tax on the assessed value of all real and personal property returned in said school district, not exceeding six mills on the dollar, subject to the following provisions: That the said Board of Trustees shall at any time previous to the first day of July, submit to the qualified voters in said school district an election to be held for that purpose after at least three weeks ' notice, which notice shall be duly advertised as provided in the case of trustees, the question of " special tax " or " no special tax. " It shall be the duty of the managers within one week after said election to count the votes and declare the result as in other elections, which result must be certified in writing by the Chairman of the Board of Managers to the Board of Trustees, and if a majority of the votes shall be in favor of the special tax the Chairman of the Board of Trustees shall certify to the Auditor of the county the tax thus levied, and the Auditor shall at once and thereafter from year to year until some other election shall have been held whereby the said special tax shall be repealed or changed, assess such tax on all real and personal property returned in said school district, and the County Treasurer shall collect said taxes the same as for State and county taxes, and such taxes shall be a lien on all property and all defaulting taxpayers, to be subjected to like process as that of State and county taxes.
