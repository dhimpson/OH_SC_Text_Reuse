Section 1. Board of County Commissioners for Chester County —Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That in January 1917, and every two years thereafter, there shall be two County Commissioners appointed for Chester County by the Governor upon the recommendation of the Legislative delegation, whose term of office shall be two years and until their successors are appointed and qualified, and the said two Commissioners, together with the County Supervisor, who shall be elected as now provided by law, shall constitute the Board of County Commissioners for Chester County, and shall exercise all the Jurisdiction and powers, and perform all the duties and obligations as are vested in and conferred upon the Board of County Commissioners by the existing laws of the State, except as herein qualified or altered § 2. Oath and Bond.—That said two County Commissioners before exercising the duties of their office shall take the oath of office required of all officers, and shall give bond in the sum of one thousand dollars for the faithful performance of their duties, to be approved by and filed with the Clerk of Court § 3. Per Diem and Mileage—That said two commissioners shall each be paid a per diem of two and a half dollars for not more than thirty days in any one ear, and six cents per mile one way for all mileage actually traveled in attending the meetings of the said board or their actual duties. § 4. Meetings—Claims—Supplies.—That said County Commissioners shall hold monthly meetings, on the first Mondays of each month at the Court House, and such other meetings as may be necessary for the conduct of the business of the County in their judgment; that said board shall have charge and control of all finances of the County affairs, approving and paying claims, and the making of all contracts for supplies for the County and chaingang, and the bridges, and all contracts for any County liability; and all affairs of the county now vested by law in the Board of County Commissioners. § 5. Supervisor to Have Charge of Chain-gang.—That the County Supervisor shall have charge of the chaingang and be responsible therefor and he shall distribute its work among the main public roads of the several townships as impartially as practicable; but the Supervisor shall consult and advise with the Commissioners in respect to the work and management of the chaingang. § 6. Township Road Supervisors.—That an act amending Sections Two, and Five of an Act to abolish the office of Township Commissioners in Chester County and providing eight Commissioners for Chester County, being Act No. 314 of the Laws of 1912, be, and the same is hereby repealed; and that the Township Road Supervisors shall be appointed as now provided by the Act, to provide for the improvement of the public roads in Chester County, approved February 15th, 1910. § 7. Repair of Roads—Working of Roads.—That the said Board of County Commissioners in the exercise of their jurisdiction and powers connected with the working and repairs of the public roads, as provided in section one of the said Act to provide for the improvement of the public roads, in Chester County and to levy a property and commutation tax therefor approved February 15th, 1910, and also under the General law of the State shall be specifically charged with the duty of having the main public roads that cross two or more Townships properly repaired and kept in good condition so far as the said special road tax can be proportionately used thereon. The said board of County Commissioners may employ an engineer to lay off or re-locate any of the. said public roads when in their judgment it is necessary and for such time as they may deem expedient, and the said County Commissioners in co-operation with respective Township Road Supervisor may contract for the repairing and working of the public roads of any: section or sections thereof in any of said Townships by private persons or companies if in their judgment the same shall be for the public interest. § 8. Inconsistent Acts Repealed—All Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act, be, and the same are hereby repealed. Acts, 1912, XXVII Stats., 555; 1907, XXV Stats., 648.
