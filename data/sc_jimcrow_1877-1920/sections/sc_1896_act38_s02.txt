Section 2. Any town or city may reduce its corporate limits in the following manner: Whenever a petition is presented to the Town Council signed by a majority of the resident freeholders of said town asking for the reduction of he corporate limits of said town, then said Council shall rder an election after not less than ten days’ public advertisement; such advertisement shall describe the territory that is proposed to be cut off. At said election should a majority of the qualified electors vote in favor of the release of the territory, then said Council shall issue its ordinance declaring the territory no longer a portion of said town, and shall so notify the Secretary of State, furnishing him at the same time with the new boundaries of said town.
