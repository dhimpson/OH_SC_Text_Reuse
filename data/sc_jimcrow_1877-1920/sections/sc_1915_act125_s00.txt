AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND EMPOWER ALL INCORPORATED TOWNS AND CITIES IN THIS STATE TO ADOPT ORDINANCES FOR THE SEGREGATION OF THE RACES. AN ACT to Authorize and Empower All Incorporated Towns and Cities in This State to Adopt Ordinances for the Segre-. gation of the Races. Section 1. Segregation of Races.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That municipal corporations of this State may provide by reasonable and suitable ordinances for the segregation of the races in their respective municipalities.
