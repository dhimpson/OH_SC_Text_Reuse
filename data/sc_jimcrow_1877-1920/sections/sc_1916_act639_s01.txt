Section 1. Bond Issue—Election.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That the School Trustees of School District No. 14 in Newberry County, embracing the town of Prosperity are hereby authorized and required to issue coupon bonds to the amount of Thirty-Five Hundred Dollars, payable in eight years, for the purpose of enlarging and further equipping the School House in said School District, provided that a majority of the qualified electors of said School OU District shall vote in favor of such issue at an election to be held hereafter at such time as muy be fixed by the said Trustees. § 2. Conduct of Election.—That the Trustees of said School District are hereby required to order an election on the question of the issue of said bonds and to give three weeks previous notice in one or more of the newspapers published in the county of Newberry, or in three or more public places in said School District of the time of such election, and they are further authorized to appoint managers and to prescribe the form of ballots and to receive the returns and to declare the result of such election. § 3. Denomination of Bonds—Signature—Non-Taxable.—That said School District is hereby declared a body corporate, for the purpose of executing and issuing said bonds and other powers herein given in case their issue be authorized by said election. The said bonds shall be issued in such denominations as the Trustees of said School District shall deem best, and shall be payable five years from the date of their issue, with interest payable annually, at a rate not to exceed six per cent. per annum. The said bonds and coupons shall be signed. by the Chairman of the Board of Trustees of said School District and countersigned by its secretary, and their coupons shall be receivable in payment of any taxes in said school district. The said bonds shall be exempt from taxation for State, County or municipal purposes. § 4. Tax Levy—Sinking Fund.—That for the purpose of paying the interest accruing on said bonds, and for the purpose of creating a sinking fund for the payment of the principal of said bonds, the County Auditor of Newberry County, or such other officer who may be charged with the assessment of taxes, shall annually levy a tax of two mills on all taxable real or personal property in said school district, and the amount so levied shall be collected by the County Treasurer of Newberry County as State and county taxes are collected, to be held and applied by him for the purpose aforesaid, and shall be used for no other purpose whatsoever. The surplus remaining after the payment of the annual interest on said bonds shall be a sinking fund for the payment of the principal of said bonds and shall be kept on deposit, at interest, by County Treasurer in the banks of the said school district. § 5. Deposit of Proceeds—How to Be Used.—That the said Board of Trustees are authorized and empowered to sell said bonds at their par value, and shall deposit all money coming to them from such sale in bank to the credit of said Board, and they shall use the said money exclusively for the purpose set out in Section 1 of this Act, and should any of said bonds be not used they shall be cancelled by the said Board of Trustees. § 6. Disposition of Surplus.—That any surplus remaining after the payment of said bonds be transferred from the Sinking Fund to the current funds of the school district, to be used for the maintenance of the schools therein. § 7. Immediately Effective —This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
