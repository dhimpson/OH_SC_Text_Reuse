SECTION 1. That for the purpose of maintaining public schools in the city of Spartanburg, the County Board of Examiners of Spartanburg County be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to lay off a separate school district, having Spartanburg Court House as the centre, and embracing an area which shall be described by the radius of a circle of one and a half miles in length A. D. 1884. from said Court House.
