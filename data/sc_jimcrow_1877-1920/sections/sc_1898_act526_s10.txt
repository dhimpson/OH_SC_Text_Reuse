SECTION 10. That the moneys herein before set apart to be used as contingent funds, and for other purposes, for the various officers for the State government shall be duly accounted for, and such officers shall make a detailed statement of the disposition made thereof to the General Assembly, at its next regular session: Provided, That no officer authorized to make contracts or draw funds from said appropriation shall expend, or make contracts for expending, more than has been specified for any purpose by this Act.
