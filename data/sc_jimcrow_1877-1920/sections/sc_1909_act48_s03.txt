SEC. 4. That on the first Tuesday in October, 1909, the auditing board of Williamsburg county shall order an election, to be held within thirty days from the date of said order, upon the question of said exemption, and said order shall be published for two weeks in some newspaper published in said county, and shall designate the date and purpose of such election, the manner of voting, the persons qualified to vote, three voting places in the territory in this Act described, and shall appoint three managers of election for each said voting place; said managers shall take the oath required of managers of general elections, shall serve without compensation, and at least one of each set of managers shall be in favor of said exemption, and one shall be opposed to said exemption, if there be any opposed in said territory. All persons who are qualified electors and resident freeholders of the territory described herein shall be qualified to vote at said election. Said election shall be conducted in the same manner as provided for general elections, no special size or form of ballot shall be required, but each ballot shall have plainly written or printed thereon the words, " For exemption " or " Against exemption. " The managers shall make re-turn of said election to said auditing board within three days, A. D. 1909. which board shall, within three days thereafter, canvass the vote cast and declare the result of said election, and if a majority of the votes cast at said election be found and declared to be " Against exemption, " that then, and in that case, immediately upon such declaration this Act shall be repealed, and if a majority of the votes cast in said election be found and declared to be " For exemption, " that then, and in that case, this Act shall remain in full force and effect.
