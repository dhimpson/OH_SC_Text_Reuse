Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, The State hereby accepts the provisions of an Act of Congress entitled: “To provide for the promotion of vocational education; to provide for co-operation with the States in the promotion of such education in agriculture, the trades, industries and home economics; to provide for co-operation with the States in the preparation of teachers of vocational subjects, and to authorize the appropriation of money and to regulate its expenditure,” and hereby designates and constitutes the State Board of Education as the South Carolina State Board of Vocational Training, to co-operate with the United States Government in putting such law into operation.
