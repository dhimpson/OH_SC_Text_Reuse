SEC. 6. That said corporation shall be managed, governed and controlled by a board of trustees consisting of not less than fifteen persons, or such larger number not exceeding twentyfive, as shall be determined by the convention of the Baptist denomination in South Carolina, a majority of whom shall constitute a quorum to do business, and that such trustees shall be elected by the convention of the Baptist denomination in South Carolina at its next session, one - fifth of whom shall be elected to hold office for one year; one - fifth for two years; one - fifth for three years; one - fifth for four years, and one - fifth for five years, the successors to those whose term of office has expired by limitation to be elected by said convention every year at the regular session thereof, and when so elected shall hold office for five years, unless sooner removed by death, resignation or otherwise. Said convention shall by election fill all vacancies caused by death, resignation or otherwise, but the term of any trustee or trustees elected to fill such vacancy or vacancies shall expire when the term of the trustee or trustees to fill whose places he or they may have been elected, would have expired.
