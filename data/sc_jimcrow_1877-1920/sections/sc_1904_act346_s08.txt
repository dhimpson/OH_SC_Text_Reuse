Sec. 9. The said Board of Trustees are hereby authorized to adopt and make a seal for their official use in the execution of the said bonds, and like important papers; that the said bonds shall be a first lien on all property purchased proved with the proceeds thereof, and said bonds shall not be taxable for municipal or public school purposes, other than the Constitutional three mills tax, and the coupons upon said bonds shall be receivable for all taxes in said School District which shall be levied for educational purposes.
