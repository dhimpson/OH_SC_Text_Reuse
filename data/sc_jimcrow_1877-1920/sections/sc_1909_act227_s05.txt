SEC. 5. That in addition to the foregoing powers said corporation shall have the following powers: (a) To make contracts, to loan money, to acquire, receive, hold and enjoy property, both real and personal, and transfer and alien the same, possessing the same powers in these respects as individuals now enjoy. (b) To sue and be sued by its corporate name. (c) To have a common seal and alter the same at pleasure. (d) To make by - laws and rules and regulations deemed expedient for its management not inconsistent with the laws of this State or of the United States. (e) To borrow money for the purpose of carrying out its object and purposes; to make notes, bonds and other evidences of debt, and to secure the payment of its obligations by mortgage or deed of trust on all or any of its property or franchises, both real and personal.
