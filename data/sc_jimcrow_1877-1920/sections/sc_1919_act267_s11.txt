§ 11. Elections in Other Townships Upon Petition. - That any of the other townships of said county, upon a petition signed by one - third of the qualified electors of said township, shall be entitled to hold an election upon the question of the issuing of coupon bonds of such township for the permanent building and improvement of the public roads of said township. Such petition shall be addressed. to the Chairman of the County Board of Commissioners of the said county and shall state the amount of bonds the petitioners desire to be issued. Upon the filing of such petition the said Chairman of the County Board of Commissioners shall proceed to order an election to be held in said township, under this issue, at which election the ballots shall be as provided for in Section 2 of this Act. Should a majority of the ballots cast in such election be in favor of issuing said bonds, the Commissioners, who shall be appointed as provided for herein in the case of the Commissioners for Willow township, shall, in so far as possible, be governed and controlled by the provisions of this Act relating to the conduct, the duties and methods. of procedure of the Commissioners for Willow township. The expenses of all elections ordered hereunder shall be paid out of the general funds of the county.
