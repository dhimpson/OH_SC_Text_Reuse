Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That a tax of two and three-quarter mills, exclusive of the public school tax hereinafter provided for, upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property of this State be, and the same is hereby, levied for the purpose of meeting appropriations to defray the current expenses of the government for the fiscal year commencing November 1 1878, and to meet such other indebtedness as have been or shall be provided for in the several Acts enacted by the present session of the General Assembly providing for the same.
