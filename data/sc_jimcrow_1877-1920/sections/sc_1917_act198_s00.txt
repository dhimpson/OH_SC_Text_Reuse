AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT IN RELATION TO THE INSURANCE OF PUBLIC SCHOOL BUILDINGS, WHETHER THE SAME BE HELD AND OPERATED UNDER THE GENERAL SCHOOL LAWS OR UNDER LAWS APPLICABLE TO SPECIAL SCHOOL DISTRICTS, AND TO PROVIDE WHAT PORTION OF SAID INSURANCE SHALL BE OBTAINED FROM AND CARRIED BY THE SINKING FUND COMMISSION,"" APPROVED 29TH FEBRUARY, 1916, BY ADDING THERETO A SEC- TION AUTHORIZING THE SAID COMMISSION TO CARRY MORE OF SAID INSURANCE AND TO REINSURE A PART OF ITS LIABILITY THERE- UNDER, AND PROVIDING HOW THE PREMIUMS THEREFOR SHALL BE PAID TO THE SAID COMMISSION.
