SEC. 5. That G. A. Viyanska, R. S. Link and T. G. White Trustees apbe, and the same are hereby, appointed Trustees, and they or any two of them shall represent the stock of the city of Abbeville, in all meetings of the said railway company, which may be subscribed under the said election. And in case of vacancy from any cause in said Board of Trustees, the City Council of Abbeville be, and are hereby, authorized to appoint a successor or successors to fill such vacancy.
