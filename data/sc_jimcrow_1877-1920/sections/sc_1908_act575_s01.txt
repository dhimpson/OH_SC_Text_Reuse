SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Hunter State of South Carolina, That in addition to the power the trict in AnTrustees of Hunter School District of Anderson County now have as to local taxes therein, they are hereby authorized to special levy annually, if more than one levy be necessary, on all the real and personal property returned in said district a special local tax not exceeding two mills on the dollar of said district for the years 1908 and 1909 to complete the schoolhouse in the town of Pendleton, subject to the following provisions: That the said Board of Trustees shall, as soon as possible Question to thereafter, submit the question of " Special Tax " or " No Speto electors. cial Tax, " to the owners of both real and personal property, who are qualified electors residing within said district, and they shall have power to order an election specifying the time, place A. D. 1908. and purpose of the same, and to make all necessary rules and regulations for governing the same. The said Board of Trustees shall appoint three managers at such election, who shall, without compensation, hold and conduct the same, at which said election the ballots shall have written or printed thereon either the words " Special Tax " or " No Special Tax, " and of which election notice shall be given by the Chairman of the Board of Trustees, as now constituted, not less than ten days before the same is held, in one or more of the newspapers of the city of Anderson.
