D. 1909. not more than twenty years from the date of their issue, and Terms of the other half shall become due and payable not more than bonds; spethirty years from date of issue. For the purpose of paying funds thereinterest on said bonds and of creating a sinking fund with from. which to redeem same at their maturity, there shall be levied each year a sufficient tax. It shall be the duty of the county treasurer to collect said tax, keeping a separate and accurate account of same, and, upon application by the treasurer of said commission to turn the same over to him as collected, whose receipt shall be a sufficient discharge of the county treasurer for the amount, or amounts, so paid. It shall be the duty of the public highway commission, by its warrant upon the treasurer, to pay the interest on said bonds as it becomes due and also to redeem the bonds themselves as they fall due, out of the moneys so turned over by the county treasurer. If, at any time, said commission shall have any moneys undisposed of or on hand for any purpose, they shall invest the same safely and securely or deposit the same in some bank or banks at as high a rate of interest as it can obtain. It shall in no case be the duty of the purchaser or purchasers of any of said bonds to see that the funds are properly expended. Said bonds shall be exempt from State, county, school or municipal taxation.
