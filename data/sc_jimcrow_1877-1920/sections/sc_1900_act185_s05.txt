SEC. 5. That the same form of pleading and the same rules of procedure, practice and evidence shall obtain in the said practice in, to County Court as is provided by law for the conduct and trial of cases, civil and criminal, in the Circuit Courts: " Provided, In all criminal cases wherein the punishment does not exceed a fine of one hundred dollars and imprisonment for thirty days, the same shall be tried without presentment by a grand jury on information filed by the County Solicitor. "
