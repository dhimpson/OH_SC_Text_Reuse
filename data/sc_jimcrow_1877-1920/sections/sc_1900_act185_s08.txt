SEC. 8. That in the selection of a jury for the trial of crimijurors in Counnal cases in said County Court, the accused, when charged with ty Courts. a misdemeanor, shall be entitled to peremptory challenges not exceeding three, and the State two; and in the trial of cases of felony, the accused shall be entitled to peremptory challenges not exceeding five, and the State three. In cases where there are two or more persons jointly indicted and so tried, the accused shall be jointly entitled to six peremptory challenges in cases of misdemeanor and eight peremptory challenges in cases of felony, and no more.
