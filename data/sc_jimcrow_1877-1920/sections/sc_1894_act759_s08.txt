Section 8. In case a vacancy occurs in the office of Intendant, by death, resignation or otherwise, an election to fill such vacancy shall be held by the appointment of the Wardens, or Warden if there be only one, ten days ' notice being given of the time when and the place where a registration of the votes will be taken, and that such election will be held twenty days thereafter. Said election to be conducted under the regulations hereinbefore provided for the regular election of members of the Town Council. In case a vacancy occurs in the office of any one or more of the Wardens, by death, resignation or otherwise, the vacancy or vacancies shall be filled by the remaining members of the Town Council.
