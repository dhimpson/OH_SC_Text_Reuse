Sec. 11. That the Comptroller General shall not issue his warrant on the Treasurer in payment of any account, whether contingent, traveling expenses, stamps, or other appropriations, unless a verified itemized account is rendered showing conformity with the requirements of this Act. Sec. 12. That at the close of the fiscal year, except for the payment of the interest on the valid public debt of the State, the Comptroller General be, and he is hereby, directed to write off his books, any balances on appropriations herein, except where warrants have actually been approved and are outstanding and all balances shall be covered into the Treasury. Src. 13. That this Act shall take effect from and immediately after its approval.
