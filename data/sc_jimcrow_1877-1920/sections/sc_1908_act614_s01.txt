SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the trict of town State of South Carolina, That the Board of Trustees of the Laurens School District of the Town of Laurens are hereby authorized and empowered to submit to the qualified voters resident in said School District, at an election to be held for that purpose, as hereinafter provided for, the question of issuing additional bonds, not to exceed the amount of ten thousand dollars, bearing not exceeding five and one - half per cent. interest, and payable in thirty years, and the levy of such annual tax as may be necessary to pay the interest on said bonds.
