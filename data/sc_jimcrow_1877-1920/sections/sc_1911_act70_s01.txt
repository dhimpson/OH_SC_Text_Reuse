SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That from and after the first day of License reJuly, 1911, it shall be unlawful for any persons to contract matrimony. matrimony within this State without first procuring a license as is hereinafter provided; and it shall likewise be unlawful for any one, whomsoever, to perform the marriage ceremony for any such persons without said persons first delivering to the party performing said marriage ceremony a license as is hereinafter provided, duly authorizing said persons to contract matrimony. Any officer or person performing the marriage ceremony without the production of such license shall, on conviction thereof, be punished by a fine of not more than one hundred ($ 100) dollars nor less than twenty - five ($ 25) dollars, or imprisonment not more than thirty days nor less than ten.
