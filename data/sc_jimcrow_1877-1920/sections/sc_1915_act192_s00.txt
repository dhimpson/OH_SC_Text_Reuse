AN ACT TO ELECT TRUSTEES OF SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 18, IN LEXINGTON COUNTY. AN ACT to Elect Trustees of School District No. 18, in Lexington County. e Section 1. Election of Trustees—Term of Office.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, There shall be an election held in School District No. 18, in Lexington county, on the second Tuesday in May, 1915, for the purpose of electing three Trustees for the public schools of said district; at which election only qualified electors living in said district shall be allowed to vote. The three persons receiving the highest number of votes at said election shall be declared to be the Trustees of said school district, whose terms of office shall be as follows, to OF SOUTH CAROLINA. 385 wit: One for the term of one year, one for the term of two years, and one for the term of three years. § 2. Terms of Office Decided by Lot.—The three persons elected as set forth in Section 1 shall decide, by lot, which one shall be entitled to terms of one, two and three years. § 3. Election of Successor—Term of Office.—There shall be an election held for the purpose of electing a successor éo the Trustees whose term expires on the last day of May in each year, following the election provided for in Section 1 of this Act, on the second ‘Tuesday in May of each succeeding year, whose term of office shall be three years and until his successor is elected and qualified. § 4. General Election Law.—The elections provided for in this Act shall be held under the laws now provided for the holding of general elections of this State. § 5. Inconsistent Acts Repealed—That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed. Approved
