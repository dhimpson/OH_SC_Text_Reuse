SECTION 5. (1) That the following sums, if so much be necessary, be, and the same are hereby, appropriated to meet the expenses of the South Carolina University, South Carolina namely: (2) For the support of the schools in the South Carolina South Carolina College in Columbia, twenty - five thousand dollars, for all College. purposes, including ordinary repairs, general expenses, improving of buildings and sanitary arrangements, and the library and salary of librarian, to be paid upon the application of the Board of Trustees, on the warrant of the Comptroller General: Provided, That suitable courses of study are provided in said institution for the deserving of both sexes: Provided, further, That nothing contained herein shall interfere with the students of the normal department. WINTHROP NORMAL COLLEGE. For the support of Winthrop Normal and Industrial College of South Carolina, including running expenses, equipment and permanent improvements, thirty thousand dollars; that the sum of five thousand four hundred and fiftysix dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated for the scholarships provided by law for the Winthrop Normal College of South Carolina; for draining marsh and other sanitary regulations and extension of sewerage system, three thousand dollars, if so much be necessary. SOUTH CAROLINA MILITARY ACADEMY. For the support of the beneficiary cadets of the South Carolina Military Academy, twenty thousand dollars, seven hundred and fifty dollars for repairs, equipment and library; and twelve hundred dollars for insurance for five years, to be paid on the warrant of the Comptroller General, issued upon the requisition of the Chairman of the Board of Visitors of said Academy. THE COLORED STATE INDUSTRIAL, NORMAL AND MECHANICAL COLLEGE. For the Colored State Industrial, Normal and Mechanical College at Orangeburg, for maintenance, new buildings and equipment, ten thousand dollars, to be paid on the application of the Board of Trustees on the warrant of the Comptroller General.
