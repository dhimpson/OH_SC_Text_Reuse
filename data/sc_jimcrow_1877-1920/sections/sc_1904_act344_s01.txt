SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the of Georgetown State of South Carolina, That Winyah Indigo School District, of Georgetown County, is hereby authorized and empowered to issue coupon bonds to the amount of thirty - five thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may, in the judgment of the Board of Trustees of said district, be necessary, for the purpose of purchasing grounds and erecting, furnishing and equipping a public school building, to be located in the city of Georgetown, in said district, and of altering and repairing any existing public school building or buildings therein: Provided, That the question of issuing said bonds shall first be determined at a special election to be held for that purpose, as hereinafter provided, in which a majority of the qualified electors voting thereat shall vote in favor of such issue.
