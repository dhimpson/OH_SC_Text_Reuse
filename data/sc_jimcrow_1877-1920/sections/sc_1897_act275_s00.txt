AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT TO FIX THE TIMES FOR HOLDING THE COURTS IN THE SECOND CIRCUIT. Sscrion 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Section 1 of an Act of the General Assembly of this State entitled “An Act to fix the times for holding the Courts in the Second Circuit,” approved the 9th day of March, 1896, be so amended that subdivision 4 of said first Section of said Act have stricken out of it the words “in September” and have inserted in lieu thereof the words “in October,” so that the subdivision of said Section when amended shall read: The Court of General Sessions at Aiken, for the County of Aiken, on the third Monday in October, and the Court of Common Pleas at the same place on Wednesday following the said Monday. Sxction 2. That Section 5 of said Act be stricken out and that the following Section, to be known as Section 5, be inserted in lieu thereof: “That if, there be three weeks for the Winter term of the Court at Aiken, three separate sets of petit jurors, one for each week, be drawn according to law, but that this chang shall not affect the drawing of jurors for the Winter term of 1897.”
