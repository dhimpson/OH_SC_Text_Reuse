SEC. 5. The public highway commission shall be composed of three citizens of the county of Spartanburg, to be appointed highway and commissioned by the Governor on the recommendation of commission. the Senator and members of the House of Representatives of said county, and no member thereof shall hold any public office except that of notary public. One of said commissioners shall reside in the city of Spartanburg, one outside of the city of Spartanburg and north of the main line of the Southern Railway, and one outside of the city of Spartanburg and south of the main line of the Southern Railway.
