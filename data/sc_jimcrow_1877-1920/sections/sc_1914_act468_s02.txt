§ 2. Management By Trustees and Episcopal Bishop. — The corporation shall be managed and controlled by a Board consisting of twelve (12) Trustees, and the Bishop of the Protestant Episcopal Church for the Diocese of South Carolina, for the time being, who shall be ex officio Chairman of the said Board, and entitled to one vote in like manner as the other trustees. The trustees shall be elected by the Diocesan Council of the said church, and shall serve for such term as said Diocesan Council shall determine. The Board of Trustees may make by - laws for the regulation and governance of the corporation, and in the name of the corporation may purchase, sell, convey, mortgage or release, acquire or alien property, real and personal, as it may deem advisable for the welfare of the corporation; and all property heretofore acquired by the said corporation shall be held by the corporation under its new name.
