SEC. 4. That the money collected from said tax levy and the Constitutional poll and two mill tax to which said District is entitled under the general provisions of the law, shall be held by the County Treasurer and paid out on warrants drawn by the Trustees of said School District, and said Treasurer shall be Liability of liable to said School District for the non performance of his duty in respect to said money in the same manner and to the same extent and under like penalties as for the non performance of his duties in reference to State and County taxes.
