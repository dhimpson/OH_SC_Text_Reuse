SEC. 2. That the money arising from the sale or hypothecation of said bonds shall be used exclusively for the purpose of erecting or purchasing, with the necessary grounds, within the incorporate limits of said town, a school building or buildings, and furnishing the same, improving the grounds thereof, and in erecting thereon a teacher's residence, and to these ends shall be How expended. expended under the direction and control of said Town Council and the Trustees of the Rock Hill School District on such grounds within said town as said Trustees may select: Provided, That the title to such property shall be made to the town property to be of Rock Hill, subject to the condition, however, that the same shall be always used for educational purposes, and under the exclusive management and control of the Board of Trustees of the Rock Hill School District.
