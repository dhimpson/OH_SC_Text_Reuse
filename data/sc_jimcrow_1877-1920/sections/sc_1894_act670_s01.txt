Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That from and after the passage of this Act all citizens of this State having resided twelve months within the State and six months within the town of Gaffney City shall be deemed, and are hereby declared to be, a body politic and corporate, and the said town shall be known and called by the name of Gaffney City, and its corporate limits shall extend one (1) mile in every direction from the centre of said town, which centre shall be at the intersection of Race and Johnson streets.
