§ 10. Use of Funds - Township Commissioners Abolished.— That in the event any township or townships vote bonds as herein provided all monies thereafter collected as now provided by law shall be turned over to the said Highway Commission, who shall receipt for the same, and shall use the same for the construction, up - keep, and maintenance of the roads and bridges within said township; and all the powers now conferred by law upon the Township Commissioners for said township shall cease and determine, and the officer of Township Commissioner shall be abolished and the duties and authority shall devolve upon the chairman of the said Highway Commission, and he shall be the member of the County Board of Commissioners from his township. Provided, That the provisions of this Act shall not affect the office or salaries of the Township Commissioners in said townships, who were elected in 1914 for a term of four years, at a salary of $ 200 each per year, but said Township Commissioners shall hold their office and enjoy the salaries and emoluments until the term to which they were elected shall expire.
