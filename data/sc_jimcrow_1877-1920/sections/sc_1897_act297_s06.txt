SECTION 7. Magistrates, in their discretion, sentencing Magistrates prisoners to labor on the public highways may commit them chain - gang directly to the officer in charge of persons so laboring upon the public highways or other public works. But one committal and discharge fee shall be charged by any Sheriff of the State for such prisoners when incarcerated in the County jail after sentence. All costs and fines collected by Magistrates in criminal cases shall be paid over forthwith to the County Treasurers of the respective Counties.
