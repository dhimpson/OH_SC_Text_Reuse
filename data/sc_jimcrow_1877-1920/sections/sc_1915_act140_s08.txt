§ 8. City May Abandon Form of Government - ProcedureFlorence. That Section 26 of said Act be amended so as to read as follows: " Section 26. Any city, after operating for six years under the provisions of this article, may abandon the form of government herein provided and accept the provisions of the general law of the State applicable to it before the adoption of this form of government, by procedure of petition and election on the question of abandonment in the manner provided hereinabove for adopting this form of government: Provided, That the city of Florence may abandon the commission form of government at the end of four years by an election held for that purpose. The time of holding such election shall be the first Tuesday in March of the year following the fourth year after the election in which such form of government was first adopted, and that any succeeding election shall be held on the same date in the respective four - year periods thereafter occurring. Before any election shall be held for the purpose of abandoning such form of government a petition to that end must be filed with the Governor. This petition (1) must be signed by registered qualified electors equal in number to at least twenty - five per cent. of the entire vote cast at the last primary election; (2) and must be accompanied by a certificate from the Supervisor of Registration of said city showing that all of the signers thereof are resident, registered, qualified electors of said city. Upon the filing of such petition with the Governor he shall at once notify the mayor of the city thereof and said mayor shall then by proclamation submit to a vote of the qualified registered electors of said city the question of abandoning the form of government herein provided, such proclamation to be submitted at least four months before the date herein fixed for the holding of such election. The question shall be submitted by the mayor in his proclamation in the following form, to wit: ' For the abandonment of commission form of government ' and ' Against the abandonment of commission form of government. ' In case a proclamation is issued for an election on the question of abandonment of the commission form of government, it shall be the duty of the city Supervisor of Registration to open the books of registration in said city on the first Monday in December for that year and keep the same open for registration of all persons every day, except Sundays, at least five hours per day until thirty days before the election. It shall also be the duty of the County Supervisor of Registration to keep the county books of registration open for like times and a like period, and they shall be paid for such additional service as provided in Section 2 of this Act. "
