§ 8. Election of Bond Issue. That the Board of Trustees of the school district of the city of Anderson is hereby empowered to submit to the qualified voters residing in said school district, on an election to be held for that purpose after at least three weeks ' notice published in some newspaper published in the county of Anderson, the question of authorizing an issue of one hundred thousand dollars not more than five per cent. bonds, interest payable semiannually, July 1st and January 1st of each year, payable in forty years from the first day of July, 1913, to be issued in $ 1,000 each and numbered from one to 100, inclusive. Said Board of Trustees shall appoint managers to receive the returns and declare the result of said election and shall issue and sell said bonds as the necessity may arise for the proceeds thereof in the event said election shall result in favor thereof.
