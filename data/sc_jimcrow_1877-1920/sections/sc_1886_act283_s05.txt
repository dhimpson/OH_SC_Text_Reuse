SEC. 2. That the said association shall have power to make a constitution and by laws, and alter the same, not repugnant to the Constitution and laws of the land; shall have succession of officers and members according to its constitution and by laws; may have, use and keep a common seal, and alter the same at will; to sue and be sued, plead and be impleaded, in any Court in this State; to have and enjoy every right incident to incorporations; and is empowered to purchase, possess, retain and Property use all such property, real and personal, as it may now possess, or be entitled to, or which shall hereafter be given, bequeathed to, purchased, entrusted or in any manner be acquired by it,and to sell, alien, loan or in any way transfer and contract concerning the same or any part thereof, not inconsistent with the law of the land: Provided, That the amount of property so held shall not exceed two hundred and fifty thousand dollars at any one time over and above its debts and liabilities.
