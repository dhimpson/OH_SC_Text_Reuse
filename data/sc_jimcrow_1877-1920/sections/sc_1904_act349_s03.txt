SEC. 3. That said Board of Trustees, in addition to the duties and responsibilities now provided by law for Trustees. of school districts, shall have the following powers and duties: 1. To elect and dismiss superintendents and teachers of schools of the said district, prescribe their duties, terms of their office, and to fix salaries, and to cause examination of said teachers to be made whenever they may deem it necssary; also, to determine the class books and studies to be used in said school, and to make rules and regulations for the government of said school. 2. To determine the manner in which the tax heretofore authorized (and the constitutional and poll tax and other school funds that may come into their hands), shall be expended in maintaining said school. 3. To fill all vacancies occurring in said Board of Trustees by death, resignation, removal or otherwise during their term of office. 4. If so authorized by the said taxpayers ' meeting, to charge and collect a supplementary tuition, not exceeding fifty cents per month, or an admission fee, not exceeding two dollars, for attendance in said school: Provided, nevertheless, That no child shall be debarred from attending said school through the inability of his or her parents or guardian to pay said fee or tuition: Provided, further, That in case in any year no such meeting shall be held, or in case at any meeting no action shall be taken in regard to the levy and collection of the special tax in said School District, it shall, nevertheless, be the duty of the County Auditor of Florence County to assess and levy a special school tax of two and one - half mills, in addition to the constitutional school tax on real and personal property returned in said School District, and the same shall be a lien until paid on all property in said district, and the County Treasurer or proper officer shall collect the same with the State and County taxes, in the same manner and subject to the same penalties as are now provided by law for collection of State and County taxes.
