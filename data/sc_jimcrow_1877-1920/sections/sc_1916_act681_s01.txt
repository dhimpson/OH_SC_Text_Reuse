Section 1. Act Repealed. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: That an Act entitled " An Act to Require a License Fee for Itinerant and Nonresident and Nontaxpaying Dealers, Peddlers or Venders in Union County, and to provide for the Collection of the License Fees and Penalties, and to Provide a Punishment for Failure to Pay or Avoiding the Payment of said License Fees, " approved the 9th day of March, A. D. 1915, be and the same is hereby, repealed.
