§ 43. One Mill School Tax Fund - Provision to Borrow Funds. -That in anticipation of a one (1) mill levy and the taxes derived therefrom as provided by Act of the General Assembly of South Carolina passed in 1913, entitled " An Act to levy and collect one (1) mill State tax for the free public school and to provide for the distribution of the same, the Governor, State Treasurer and the Comptroller General be, and they are hereby, empowered to borrow on the credit of the State so much money as from time to time as may be needed to pay for the objects and purposes set forth in the Act above referred to: Provided, The sum so borrowed shall not exceed one hundred and fifty thousand ($ 150,000) dollars.
