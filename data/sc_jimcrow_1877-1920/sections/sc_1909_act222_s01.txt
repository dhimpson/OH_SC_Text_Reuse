SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to amend an Act to incorporate the Board of Visitors of the Orphanage Thornwell Orphanage, and to authorize the Clinton Presbyterian Church to transfer certain property to the same, " approved January 8, 1895, be amended by striking out all of the said Act and inserting in lieu thereof the following: That the said board of trustees of the Thornwell Orphanage shall be created a body politic and corporate, and shall be known hereafter as the board of trustees of the Thornwell Orphanage, and shall be constituted as follows, viz.: of seven members duly appointed by, or from within the bounds of the Synod of South Carolina, five from the Synod of Georgia and three from the Synod of Florida, connected with the General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in the United States. This body, duly met, shall add to their number, by election, twelve ministers, elders or deacons, resident in the town of Clinton, connected with the said synods. To these local members they may commit under the name of local board the administration of the affairs between the annual sessions of the body, under such instructions and regulations as they may adopt. The board so constituted shall be a perpetual body, having due succession of officers and members; all vacancies in their body made by death, removal or resignation, being filled as above provided. In case said synods should fail or refuse to fill such vacancies, the same shall be filled by the remaining members of the board. This board so constituted may select and appoint advisory members, consisting of one person from each one of the othe synods of the Presbyterian Church in the United States, to serve under such regulations and with such right and privileges as may be agreed upon.
