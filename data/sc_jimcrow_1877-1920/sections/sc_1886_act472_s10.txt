SEC. 3. That the following Section be added to the said Act, to be known as Section 20, to wit: " SECTION 20. In cases of contest or protest as to any municipal or special election held under this charter or by virtue of the incorporation herein created, the Mayor and Aldermen composing the City Council shall be authorized and empowered to hear and determine the same subject to the right of appeal to the Circuit Court by any party in interest who may feel himself aggrieved thereby: Provided, Notice and grounds of such appeal be served upon the City Council and the opposing party within five days after the announcement of such determination.
