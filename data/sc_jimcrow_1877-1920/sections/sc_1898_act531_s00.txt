AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO CREATE A SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT FROM PORTIONS OF AIKEN AND ORANGEBURG COUNTIES, IN- CLUDING THE TOWN OF SALLY IN AIKEN COUNTY."". SecTion 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled “An Act to amend an Act entitled ‘An Act to create a separate school district from portions of Aiken and Orangeburg Counties, including the town of Sally, in Aiken County,’ ” approved Dec. 22nd, A. D. 1892, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out of Section 1 of said Act, beginning with and including the word “that,” at the beginning of line eight. down to and including the word “mentioned” on line eleven. so that said section when amended shall read as follows: That an Act entitled “An Act to create a seperate school district from portions of Aiken and Orangeburg Counties. including the town of Sally, in Aiken County,” be, and same is hereby, amended so as to exclude from the said school district therein mentioned the portion of Orangeburg County therein included, and to confine said school district exclusively to the portion of the County of Aiken therein described.
