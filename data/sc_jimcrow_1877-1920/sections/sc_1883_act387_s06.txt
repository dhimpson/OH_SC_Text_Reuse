Sec. 7, That for convenience in assessing and collecting such tax the County Commissioners of Marion County are hereby authorized (if necessary) to lay off a new Township, with such name and number as they may select, to be continuous in extent with such school district. The assessment for the fiscal or scholastic year beginning November Ist, 1883, to be made by the County Auditor immediately after he shall have been notified by the said Chairman and Secretary of the amount of the tax so levied.
