SEC. 6. It shall be the duty of said Bank Examiner, at any Special Examtime, upon receiving the petition of stockholders representing one - fourth (4) of the capital stock of any bank which may be incorporated under the laws of this State to make forthwith a special examination of such bank and to file a report of the same with the State Treasurer, and for such special examination the said bank shall pay to the State Bank Examiner for the State Treasurer the same fees as is provided for one annual examination under this Act.
