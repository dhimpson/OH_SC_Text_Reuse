Section 1. Election to Exempt Portion of Berkeley from Stock Law.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the question of exemption, or no exemption from the operation of the general stock law of all that portion of Berkeley county, beginning at Wallace Smith’s place on the new road and up the Sand Ridge road to a point one-fourth mile below Singletary’s, on State road; thence across woods to a point one-fourth mile below Singletary’s on the old State road and on down the east side of the old State road to the present exemption line, near Budd’s place, be submitted to the qualified electors who are freeholders, residents within said territory, at an election to be held on the second Tuesday in May, 1913.
