SEC. 7. The duty of the controlling synods to and their responsibility for the institution shall consist in the appointment synods. of trustees, as in this charter provided. Whenever the said trustees shall, in the judgment of the synods, fail in the discharge of their duties, it shall be the duty of the synods to remove these trustees and to appoint others. The responsibility for the general government of the home shall rest upon the trustees, so appointed, who shall annually report their conduct of the affairs of the home and schools to the controlling synods.
