That the Board of Directors of the State Penitenconvicts. tiary are hereby directed to pay into the Treasury of the State, at the end of each three months or within five days thereafter, all amounts received by them from the hire of convicts and from other sources, after paying the necessary expenses of the said institution and all other disbursements authorized by law; the said amount to be paid into the Treasury and to be held subject to the warrants of the Comptroller General, to pay amounts appropriated by the General Assembly in the same manner as the other funds in the Treasury; and the Board of Directors are hereby instructed in hiring out convicts to receive as compensation for the services of said convicts only lawful money of the United States.
