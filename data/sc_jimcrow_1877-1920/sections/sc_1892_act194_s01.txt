SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That John G. Williams, J. M. Bostick, J. T. Morrison, W. V. Lanier, W. Incorporation. H. Mauldin, John Lawton, E. W. Peeples, A. McB. Speaks, W. O. Thomson, A. C. Mekins, F. Jones, G. W. Morrall and R. T.
