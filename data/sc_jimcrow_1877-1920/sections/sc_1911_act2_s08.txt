SEC. 7. That no person shall be appointed to the office of Qualifications the State Bank Examiner, unless he be an expert accountant Examiner. and shall have had practical experience in the banking business, nor shall he qualify as such examiner until he shall have taken the oath provided for in the Constitution, and shall have filed in the office of the State Treasurer a bond, in the sum of fifteen thousand dollars, with sufficient surety to be approved by the State Treasurer, and conditioned for the faithful performance of his duties.
