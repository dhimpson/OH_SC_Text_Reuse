Section 23. Every male citizen of this State and of the United States of the age of twenty - one years and upwards, cipal elections. having all the qualifications mentioned in the first Section of this Act, and who has resided within the corporate limits of any incorporated city or town in this State for four months previous to any municipal election, and has paid all taxes due and collectible for the preceding fiscal year, and who has been registered as hereinafter required, shall be entitled to vote at all municipal elections of his city or town after the general election in the year 1896.
