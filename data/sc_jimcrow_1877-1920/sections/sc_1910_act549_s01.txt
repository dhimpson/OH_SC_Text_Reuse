SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Monument State of South Carolina, That the commission charged with Confederacy the erection of a monument to the heroism, fidelity and fortitude of the women of South Carolina during the war between the Confederate States and the United States be, and they are grounds. hereby, empowered to locate said monument on the State House grounds or on any of the approaches to the said grounds as in their judgment is most appropriate and suitable, and where permission can be obtained.
