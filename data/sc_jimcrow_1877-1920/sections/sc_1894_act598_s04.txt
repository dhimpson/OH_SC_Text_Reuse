Section 2. That J. J. Miller, J. B. Barron and J. R. Comer, and their successors in office, be, and they are hereby, constituted and appointed a Board of Trustees for said school district, and they shall each hold said office of Trustees for a period of two years, when their successors shall be elected at Term. a meeting called for that purpose, who shall likewise hold said office for the period of two years, which is hereby fixed as the term of office for Trustees in said school district: Provided, That said Board of Trustees may fill any vacancy Proviso. occurring between the regular times of election. Approved December 24th, A. D. 1894.
