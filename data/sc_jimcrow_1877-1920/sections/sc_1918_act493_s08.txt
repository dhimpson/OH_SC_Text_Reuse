§ 8. Claims—Accounts—Statements.—That the aforesaid Commissioners shall be charged with the duty of approving any claims against the county of Aiken, and no claims against said county, shall be paid unless approved by a majority of the said Commissioners. The said Commissioners shall keep proper books of account, and other proper expenditures, and shall publish each month a statement of such expenditures in one of the newspapers published in said county.
