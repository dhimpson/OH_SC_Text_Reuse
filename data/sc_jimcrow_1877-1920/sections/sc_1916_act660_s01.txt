Section 1. Election for School Tax Levy.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That the School Commissioners for the School District of the City of Columbia, County of Richland, and State, be, and they are hereby, authorized and empowered to submit to the qualified electors of said School District not later than March Sixteenth, A. D., 1916, the question of levying an annual tax of five mills on the taxable property in said School District, to be used for the current expenses incurred for the support and maintenance of the schools in said District.
