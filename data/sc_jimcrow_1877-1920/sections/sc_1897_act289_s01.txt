SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Act 1896, XXII, State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to define in what manner towns and cities in South Carolina may increase or diminish their corporate limits, " approved 28th February, 1896, be, and said Act is hereby, amended Amended. as follows: In Section 1 of said Act between the word " annex " and the word " praying " insert the words " or by a majority of the qualified electors of such town or city, " and strike out all between the word " thereof " and the word " if " and insert in lieu thereof the following words: " in a box provided for that purpose, and the qualified electors of the territory proposed to be annexed shall vote in a separate box provided at each poll in the town for that purpose, " and strike out from and including the word " at " down to and including the word " annexation, " and insert in lieu thereof the words: " cast by the qualified electors of the town and of the territory proposed to be annexed, each aggregated separately, shall be each in favor of annexation, then the Council shall publish, " & c., and by striking out the word " resident " between " the " and " freeholders, " and by striking out in Section 1 " or by a majority of the qualified electors of said town or city, and by inserting after the word “ box ” and before the word " provided " the words " to be, " and by striking out after the word " provided " " at each poll in the town " and insert in lieu thereof the words " for that purpose within the territory proposed to be annexed, " and by adding after the word " annexation " the words " or if neither give a majority against annexation.
