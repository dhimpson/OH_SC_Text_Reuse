SEC. 12. The above bonds shall not be issued and nothing shall be done towards their issue until the same have been apbond issue. proved by a majority of the qualified electors of said county at a special election to be held for the purpose on the 24th day of August, 1909. The commissioners of election for said county shall appoint the managers and receive and tabulate the votes as in other elections. They shall make their report in writing in duplicate, one copy to be filed with the clerk of court of Spartanburg county, and the other with the Secretary of State, and said duplicate copies shall be kept on perpetual record in both of said offices. The managers of election shall serve without any compensation; and the managers appointed shall have the power and authority to appoint substitute managers to act in their stead. The county board of commissioners of said county is authorized and required to furnish to the election commissioners of said county, in ample time before said election, a sufficient number of ballots for and against said coupon bond issue. On one set of the bonds shall be printed the words, " For public highway improvement bonds, " and on the other set shall be printed the words, " Against public highway improvement bonds. " And the said commissioners are authorized to pay the expense of said printing out of the general county funds. In all other respects, said election shall be held as other elections, under the general laws of this State, and the same regulations apply thereto, in so far as are consistent with this Act. If said election shall be declared for the issue of the bonds, then the provisions made in former sections of this Act be carried out. Said commission shall not be allowed any salary until the results of the election is disclosed. If said election is declared against the issue of the bonds the term of office of said commissioner shall cease.
