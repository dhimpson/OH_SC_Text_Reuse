Sec. 4, XXIII. SEC. 2. That Section 4 of said Act be amended by inserting Stats., 213, on line 5 of said Section, between the words " trustees " and powers of Sy- " and " the following words " and may change the name and remove said Orphanage to such locality as it may select " so that said section, when so amended shall read as follows: Section 4. That said Associate Reform Presbyterian Synod of the South may whenever they assume control as herein provided, elect a Board of Trustees to succeed the Board of Trustees above named, may change the name and remove said Orphanage to such locality as they may select and may prescribe the number and tenure of office of such Trustees, and may prescribe such rules and regulations for the government of said Orphanage as it may deem advisable: Provided, The same be not inconsistent with the laws of this State or of the United States; and any action of said Synod herein authorized, before the final passage of this Act, is hereby ratified and confirmed.
