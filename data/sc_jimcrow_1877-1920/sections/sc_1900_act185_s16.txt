SEC. 16. That the Clerk of the Circuit Court shall be ex officio Clerk of officio Clerk of the County Court, and shall keep such calenCounty Court. dars, minutes and records of the said County Court, and the causes therein pending, and attend and perform such duties as the Clerk thereof, as is required of him by law as Clerk of the Circuit Court. That for services performed as Clerk of the County Court he shall receive the same compensation as if performed by him as Clerk of the Circuit Court.
