§ 7. Proceeds of Sale of Bonds.—That, as soon as said bonds are sold, the proceeds of said sale shall be deposited in three or more of the banks in Abbeville county, as the Commission may determine, upon such terms and conditions as the Commission and the banks may agree upon, and shall only be drawn out upon checks signed by said Commission, or a majority of its members, for the purposes contemplated in this Act: Provided, That should said Commission desire to lend any of said funds on short time loans, before it is needed to use in the building of roads, it shall have the right to do so, taking proper security therefor. All interest on such loans to be added to the sinking fund, for the ultimate retirement of said bonds.
