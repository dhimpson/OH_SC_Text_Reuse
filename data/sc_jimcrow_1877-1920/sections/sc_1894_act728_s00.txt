AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED " AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT IN MARION COUNTY, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A LOCAL TAX THEREIN, " APPROVED DECEMBER 24TH, A. D. 1886. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Section 3 of an Act entitled " An Act to provide for the establishment of a new school district in Marion County, and to authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, " approved December 24th, A. D. 1886, be, and said Section 19 Stat., 734. 3 is hereby, amended by striking out the words " two and one - half " from the fourth line of the printed Act and by inserting in lieu thereof the word " three and one - half " between the word " exceeding " and the word " mills.
