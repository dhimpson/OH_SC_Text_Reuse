SEC. 2. That R. E. Dennis, A. C. Durant, J. L. Parrott, R. Board of TrusE. Carnes and W. R. Law, and their successors in office, be, and they are hereby, constituted and appointed a Board of Trustees for said School District, and they shall each hold said office of Trustees for the term of two years and until their successors are appointed; and the County School Examiners of Sumter County shall fill all vacancies that may occur in said Vacancies. Board of Trustees from death, resignation, removal from the bounds of the School District or otherwise, and shall also appoint the School Trustees for said School District every two years thereafter: Provided, That no person shall be eligible to the office of Trustee who is not a patron of said school.
