§ 7. Charter in Perpetuity—The charter herein granted shall be held in perpetuity unless said corporation shall be dissolved which said dissolution may be brought about by a two-thirds vote of the said stockholders, in a meeting called for that purpose, of which thirty days’ notice shall be given to the said stockholders, in writing, by mail or in person: Provided, That at such meeting, and in all meetings of the said stockholders, the vote shall be taken by stock, each stockholder being entitled to one vote for each share of stock represented by him or her
