SEC. 2. That the said Trial Justice is authorized and empowered to appoint and employ a person to act as Constable, who shall receive a salary of two hundred and fifty dollars, payable Salary. quarterly by the County Treasurer, upon the warrant of the County Commissioners, and the said Constable shall not receive any other sums of money for such service rendered by him in any pensation in criminal case under such employment or appointment, except as herein provided.
