Section 1. Bonds May Be Issued—Election.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That the trustees of School District No. 40 of Lancaster County, consisting of portions of Lancaster and Kershaw Counties, be, and they are hereby authorized and empowered to issue and sell ae coupon bonds of said school district payable to bearer in such denominations and amounts as they may deem necessary, not to exceed Three Thousand ($3,000.00) Dollars, bearing interest ae _ at not exceeding six per cent. per annum, payable annually, or semi-annually, and at such times as they may deem best: Provided, That the question of issuing bonds authorized herein shall first be submitted to the qualified electors of said school district at an election to be held, to determine whether said bonds shall be issued or not. § 2. Election to Be Ordered—Notice.—That for the purpose of determining the issue of said bonds the said trustees shall order and election to be held in the town of Kershaw in said School District, in which election only qualified electors residing in said district shall be allowed to vote, and said Trustees shall.give at least ten days notice of the time and place of said election by publication in a newspaper published in the town of Kershaw, shall appoint the managers, receive the returns and declare the results. § 3. Form of Ballot—The Trustees shall have printed for the use of the voters in said election an equal number of ballots which shall be placed at the voting place or places, on one set of which shall be printed the words “For Bonds,” and on the other set the words “Against Bonds.” § 4. Issue of Bonds—Object—Tax Ievy.—If a majority of the votes cast at said election shall be for bonds the Trustees shall issue said bonds, which shall run for a period of twenty years from the date of issue and shall sell the same at not less than par, the proceeds of which shall be used by said Trustees to install and equip a modern heating plant in the Graded School building in the said town of Kershaw, and the said bonds and the coupons of same shall constitute a lien upon the property owned by said school district, and it is hereby made the duty of the County Officer charged with the assessment and collection of taxes to levy and collect annually from all the property, real and personal, within the limits of said school district, a sum sufficient to pay the interest on said bonds and all other bonds existing against said school district, and also a sum sufficient to provide a sinking fund for the payment of said bonds when due and the coupons thereof shall be receivable for school taxes upon property within said district. § 5. How Bonds Signed.—That all bonds issued under and in pursuance of this act shall be signed by the Trustees of the School District: Provided, that signatures of said Trustees shall be lithographed or engraved upon the coupons attached to said bonds and such lithographed or engraved signatures shall be sufficient signing. § 6. May Cancel Existing Indebtedness. — That the said Trustees are hereby authorized to use Three Thousand ($3,000.00) Dollars, of the Sinking Fund now on hand belonging to said District for the payment and cancellation of any bonds now existing as valid bonded indebtedness against said School District, and the Treasurers of Lancaster and Kershaw Counties are hereby directed to pay said: sum of Three Thousand ($3,000.00) Dollars, upon the warrant or order of said Board of Trustees. § 7. Immediately Effective — Inconsistent Acts Repealed.— That this Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval, and all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.
