SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Separate for the purpose of maintaining public schools in the City of in Greenville Greenville, the County Board of Examiners of Greenville County be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to lay off a separate School District, embracing the territory included in the corporate limits of said city.
