SEC. 2. That the objects and purposes of said corporation Purposes shall be to keep up, maintain and conduct a college in the city of Greenville, in the State of South Carolina, for the education of girls and women, in which shall be taught all those branches of learning which are usually taught in colleges for girls and women in this State and the United States, and to confer such degree of graduation, honor and distinction as it may think proper upon its students.
