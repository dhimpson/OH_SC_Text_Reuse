Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That an Act entitled ‘An Act to renew and amend the charter of the town of Chester,” approved March 26th, A. D. 1869, be amended as follows, to wit: Strike out Section 9 of said Act and insert the following, to be known as Section 9: “ That the said Town Council of Chester shall have power to arrest any person or persons who shall be guilty of disorderly conduct in said town to the annoyance of the citizens thereof, and to punish such disorderly persons by sentencing them to pay a fine not exceeding fifty dollars and to confinement in the jail or guard house not exceeding thirty days, and to labor on the streets of said town not exceeding thirty days. That either one or two of said sentences may be imposed at one and the same time, or in the alternative, in the discretion of the Council: Provided, That any person sentenced to labor upon the streets of said town shall have the privilege of commuting said sentence by paying therefor at the rate of fifty cents per day for each day embraced in said sentence. In case any person shall be sentenced to labor on the streets of said town and shall fail or refuse to commute said sentence, as above provided, the said Town Council shall have power and authority to use all such appliances and means, by means of a chain gang or otherwise, as may be necessary to carry out sentence and to cause such labor to be performed. In case any fine is imposed and not paid, the said Town Council shall have power to issue executions and collect the same by levy and sale of the property of the defaulter. It shall be the duty of the Marshal of, the police of the town to arrest all disorderly persons, and to call to his assistance the posse comitatus, if necessary; and upon failure to perform said duty, he shall be fined in a sum not exceeding twenty dollars for each and every offense.” Sec. 2. That Section 10 of said Act be amended by inserting after the word “town,” in the fifth line, the following: “They shall have power to appoint, as often as may be necessary, three or more persons to list and assess for taxation the real and personal property of said town: Provided, That before beginning their duties said persons shall be sworn faithfully, honestly and fairly to assess all the property of said town, and shall be authorized to require returns, under oath, of all property listed for taxation, the same as is now, or may hereafter be, required of persons making returns of property for taxation to the State: Provided, That said Town Council shall have power to alter and amend and equalize said assessments and returns.” Sec. 3. That Section 12 of said Act shall be known as Section 13, and the following shall be known as Section 12, to wit: “That the said Town Council of Chester shall have power to issue bonds of said town to an amount not exceeding five thousand (5,000) dollars to fund the floating indebtedness of said town; said bonds to be made payable at such time or times as the said Town Council may deem best, with interest payable annually at a rate not exceeding seven per cent. per annum.
