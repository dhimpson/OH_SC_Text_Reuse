SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to provide for the election of Trustees for School District No. 16, in the County of Florence, " approved 15th February, A. D. 1901, be amended by striking out all after the enacting words, and by inserting the following in lieu thereof: That the Board of Trustees of School District No. 16, in the County, to call County of Florence, shall call a meeting of the taxpayers of the district, to be held not later than July 1st each year: Provided, That no person shall be allowed to participate or vote at such meeting who is not a bona fide taxpayer, and a qualified elector within such district.
