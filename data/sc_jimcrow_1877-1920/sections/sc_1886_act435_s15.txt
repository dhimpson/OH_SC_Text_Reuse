SEC. 4. That all coupons of the said bonds and all interest Receivable for orders of said certificates of stock, the issue of which bonds and certificates is hereinabove provided for, shall be received in payment of all taxes which shall become payable to the State during the year in which such coupons and interest orders shall become payable, except for the tax levied for the support of the Except school public schools, and the fact that said coupons are so receivable shall be expressed on the face of said coupons.
