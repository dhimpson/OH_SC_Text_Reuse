SEC. 5. That said railroad company shall have full power and authority to connect with or cross over all other railroads on its Rights of way. proposed line, and shall have every right, privilege, and power necessary for the purpose of acquiring such lands or rights of way as may be required for the location or construction by any route the directors of said railroad may agree upon, or for the erection or location of depots, warehouses, stations, or other necessary establishments, or for extending or altering the same, and the said railroad shall have the benefit of every process or proceedings, and be subject to all the restrictions now provided by law in such cases.
