SEC. 2. That upon the written petition of one - fourth of the freeholders of said school district, filed with the said Trustees, they shall make an order for the holding of such an election, fixing the time and place for holding the same, appointing the managers (who shall be three resident freeholders and qualified electors of said district), and making all other proper provisions for the conduct thereof. At such election only duly qualified electors residing in said district shall be entitled to vote. The said Trustees shall publish, at least twenty days prior to the holding of said election, in one or more newspapers published in the city of Georgetown, due notice of the time and place of holding the same, and of the objects thereof, in which shall be specified the amount of bonds proposed to be issued, the time when the same shall become payable and the rate of interest thereon. The Trustees shall have printed, for the use of the voters in said election, an equal number of ballots for and against said issue, on one set of which shall be printed the words, " For the issuing of bonds, " and on the other set the words, " Against the issuing of bonds; " and a sufficient number of such ballots shall be placed at the following place or places in said district on the day of election. The said managers shall conduct the election, and at the close thereof shall count the votes and make a return of the result thereof in writing to the Board of School Trustees aforesaid. The said Trustees shall canvas the returns and declare the result; and if a majority of the ballots cast at such election shall be in favor of the issue of said bonds, the said Board of Trustees shall proceed to issue and dispose of the same as hereinafter provided.
