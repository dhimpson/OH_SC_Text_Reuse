SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the trict No. State of South Carolina, That Special School District No. 18, County, may embracing the town of Loris, in Horry County, is hereby make special authorized and empowered to levy and collect a special tax on the assessed value of all real and personal property returned in said school district, not exceeding seven mills on the dollar, subject to the following provisions: The school trustees of said school district shall, at any time previous to the thirtieth day of June, 1910, upon the written request of ten property holders, issue a call for a public meeting, after two weeks ' notice, to all those citizens who return real or personal property in said school district, and such notice shall be published in one newspaper or posted in two public places in said district for at least two weeks before such meeting, and shall specify the time, place and object of said meeting. A list of all citizens who return real or personal property in said school district shall be prepared and certified by the County Auditor, who shall send said list to said meeting, and in case a majority of citizens, as above defined, shall vote at said meeting in favor of said tax, then same shall be levied and collected as now provided by law for the levying, collecting and paying out of taxes in special school districts.
