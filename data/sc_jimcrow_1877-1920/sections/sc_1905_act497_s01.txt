SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Inspector Gen State of South Carolina, That after the approval of this Act by names to Con the Governor, the Adjutant and Inspector General, or the Clerk of the Historical Commission, be, and is hereby, authorized and empowered to add to any of the Confederate Rolls, in his custody, such names as shall appear by proper proof under oaths to have been omitted therefrom by oversight or otherwise; the proof furnished shall in each case be filed with and become a part of said Rolls.
