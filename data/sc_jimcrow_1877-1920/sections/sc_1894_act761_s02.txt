Sec. 2. That the said city is hereby divided into four wards, as follows: The territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Southwest and a line drawn along the centre of Washington and Cambridge streets on the Southeast shall be known as Ward One; the territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Washington and Cambridge streets on the Northwest and a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Southwest shall be known as Ward Two; the territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Northeast and a line drawn along the centre of Pinckney and Ferry streets on the Northwest shall be known as Ward Three; the territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Pinckney and Ferry streets on the Southeast and a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Northeast shall be known as Ward Four.
