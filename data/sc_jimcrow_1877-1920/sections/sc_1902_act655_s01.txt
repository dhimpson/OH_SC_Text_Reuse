Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the School DisState of South Carolina: That School District No. 70, emElloree, Orangeburg Co., bracing the Town of Elloree, in the County of Orangeburg, to issue bonds; is hereby authorized and required to issue coupon bonds to the amount of three thousand dollars for the purpose of purchasing grounds, erecting and maintaining buildings, furnishing and equipping a public school house, to be located in said school district: Provided, That a majority of the qualified electors of said school district voting shall be in favor of such issue, as may be expressed at an election hereafter to be held for that purpose.
