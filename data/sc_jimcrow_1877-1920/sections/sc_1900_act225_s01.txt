SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, The sum of at least one hundred thousand dollars shall be annually appropriated to pay the pensions provided for by this Act, and in case the same, or such amount diers and sailas shall be appropriated, shall be insufficient, then the amount so appropriated shall be distributed proportionately among those legally entitled to receive the same: Provided, That those pensioners described in subdivision (a), section 4, herein, shall have been first paid in full.
