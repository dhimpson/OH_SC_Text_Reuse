§ 3. Board of Trustees——That all the powers given said corporation shall be, and they are hereby, lodged in a Board of Trustees, consisting of the persons named in Section 1 of this Act. and such other persons as may be substituted for them, or any of them, or chosen to succeed them, or any of them, in the manner hereinafter provided. That three of the members of the said Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of one year, or until their successors are elected; that two of the members of the said Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of two years, or until their successors are elected; that two of the members of the said Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of three years, or until their successors are elected, and that two of the members of the Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of four years, or until their successors are elected; that the said trustees, named in this Act, shall determine by lot which of them shall hold for one year, two years, three years and four years, and thereafter there shall be held annually, at such time and place as may be fixed by the by-laws, an election, at which the successors of the said trustees so named in Section 1 of this Act, shall be elected.
