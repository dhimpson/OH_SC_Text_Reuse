Sec. 3. The said trustees shall have printed, for the use of the voters in said election, two sets of ballots, which shall be placed, an equal number of each, at the polling place, on one set of which shall be printed the words, “For the issuing of bonds,” and on the other set of which shall be printed the words, “Against the issuing of bonds.” If a majority of the votes cast at said election shall be for the issuing of the coupon bonds provided for in Section one (1) hereof, the said trustees may issue said bonds, or such amount not exceeding five thousand ($5,000) dollars, as they may deem requisite for the purposes set forth in Section one (1) of this Act, payable to bearer, to run for a period of not exceeding thirty years from the date of issue, bearing interest not exceeding six (6) per centum per annum, payable semi-annually. Any bonds executed and not issued shall be cancelled.
