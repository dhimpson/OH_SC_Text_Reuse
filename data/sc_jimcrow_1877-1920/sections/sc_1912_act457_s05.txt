SEC. 5. That it shall be the duty of the County SuperintendA. D. 1912. ent of Education of Lexington county to forthwith furnish to County Suthe County Superintendent of Education of Richland county perintendent a statement of amounts of disbursements and balances on hand to furnish for each of the school districts or portions thereof in the terrischool funds. tory annexed to Richland county under this Act, together with such further information as may be necessary for the proper continuation of the public schools located in such territory; and the County Superintendent of Education of Lexington county shall apportion and distribute to the various school districts or portions thereof in said territory their lawful share of all school funds collected, or to be collected, from the taxes of the year 1910, and from any State or county dispensary funds, to which they may now or hereafter be entitled to under the law, and as soon as possible he shall furnish a duplicate statement thereof to the County Superintendent of Education of Richland county. In case it shall be necessary for the County Superintendent of Education of Richland county to visit the county seat of Lexington county, for the purpose of adjusting any matter or matters referred to in this section or pertaining to his duties towards the public schools, he shall be allowed compensation therefor, for not exceeding five days, at the rate of three dollars per day and his necessary expenses. The Superintendent of Education of Lexington county shall be paid the sum of twenty - five dollars by Richland county upon satisfactory compliance with the provisions of this section.
