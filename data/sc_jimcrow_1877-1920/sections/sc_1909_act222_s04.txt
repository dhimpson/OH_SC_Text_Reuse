SEC. 4. The said board is authorized hereby to maintain such May mainschool or schools of a collegiate, normal and technical character tain schools. as may be found necessary, and may under the name of the Thornwell College for Orphans and the Thornwell Technical Institute and the Thornwell Home and School, confer such diplomas and degrees as are usual in colleges. It is hereby permitted to the president of the board of trustees of the Thornwell College for Orphans to submit their course of study to the approval of the State Board of Education, and to conform to the course of study required by the said board to secure the degree of Licentiate of Instruction, and when conformed to by students, the said degree shall be conferred upon them as a license to teach in free schools of the State, subject to the approval of the State Superintendent of Education.
