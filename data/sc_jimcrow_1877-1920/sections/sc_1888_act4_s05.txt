SEC. 5. 1. That the following sums, if so much be necessary, be, and the same are hereby, appropriated for miscellaneous expenses, namely: 2. For the pay of extra clerical services in the Engrossing Department, one hundred and sixty dollars, to be paid on the joint certificates of the presiding officers of the two Houses, signed by the presiding officers thereof and attested by the Clerks, upon the application of the Attorney General. 3. For preparing for the printer, indexing and making marginal notes of the Acts and Joint Resolutions of the regular session of 1888, to be done under the supervision of the State Reporter, two hundred and fifty dollars. 4. For the payment of the deficiency in the appropriation for the mileage of the General Assembly for the regular session of eighteen hundred and eighty seven, one hundred and five dollars.
