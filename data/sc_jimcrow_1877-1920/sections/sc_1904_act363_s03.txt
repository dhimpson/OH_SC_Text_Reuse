SEC. 3. That in addition to the usual officers of a corporation, said Association may immediately upon the approval of this Act elect one or more of its members to be known and designated as Agent or Agents of the South Carolina Immigration Association, who shall hold his office for the term of four years and until his successor is elected and qualified, who shall perform such duties as usually devolves upon such officers in procuring and obtaining immigrants and settlers, and who shall to the extent of the means at his command, obtain and disseminate information relative to the advantages of soil and climate, and the natural resources and industrial opportunities offered in this State to capitalist and immigrants, and persons seeking homes; to act as a medium between land owner and proposed immigrants and settler, and otherwise to do and perform such other labors and duties as may be imposed upon him by the bylaws of the Association.
