SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the to erect monuState of South Carolina, That a Commisison is hereby appointed, to consist of his Excellency the Governor of the State, the Adjutant and Inspector General, and the Lieutenant General commanding the Department of the Army of Northern Virginia, United Confederate Veterans, whose duty it shall be, and who are hereby authorized and required, to proceed at once and cause the monument erected by the State upon the battlefield of Chickamauga to be repaired and put in substantial and becoming form, after such design as said Commission may decide upon.
