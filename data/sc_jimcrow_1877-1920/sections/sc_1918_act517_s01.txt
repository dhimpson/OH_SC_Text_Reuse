Section 1. Board of Trustees Industrial School to Be State Board of Correctional Administration. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Caro lina, That the Board of Trustees of the South Carolina Industrial School are hereby declared to be the State Board of Correctional Administration. $ 2. Powers of Board. — That the said State Board of Correc tional Administration shall succeed to all the duties and powers now vested in the Board of Trustees of the South Carolina Industrial School consistent with the terms of this Act.
