Sec. 7. That all taxes assessed herein shall be due and payable in two equal installments as follows: The first installment shall be due and payable from the Ist day of May to the Ist day of June, 1879, and the second installment shall be due and payable from the 15th day of September to the 30th day of October, 1879: Provided, That it shall be, and is hereby, left to the option of any person either to pay the amount of the first installment at the time first mentioned or to pay the whole amount at the time of the payment of the second installment thereof: Provided, further, That if any person or persons shall fail or refuse to pay his or her taxes due under the first installment, he, she or they shall be charged with a penalty of five per centum on the first installment, which penalty shall be added; and the several County Treasurers shal! collect the same in the manner prescribed by law, and give receipts therefor to the several parties paying the same,.in which the real estate paid on shall be briefly described, and the value of the personal property paid on shall be stated, together with the time such taxes are paid and the amount of same: Provided, further, That in the County of Hampton the whole of the two mill tax levied for the building of a court house and jail shall be due and payable at the time mentioned for the payment of the first installment of the State and County tax; and in case any person or persons shall fail or refuse to pay the same at the time mentioned, he, she or they shall be charged with a penalty of twenty per cent. on the amount so delinquent, to be collected by the County Treasurer as hereinafter prescribed: Provided, Nothing herein contained shall operate to prevent any taxpayer from paying the whole amount of his tax at the time the first installment is payable.
