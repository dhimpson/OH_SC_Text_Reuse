For the incidental or contingent expenses of the Engrossing Department, two hundred dollars, if so much be necessary, to be paid on certificates drawn by the Speaker of the House of Representatives and attested by the Clerk of the House of Representatives, on accounts audited by the Committee on Accounts of the House of Representatives and passed by the House of Representatives.
