Section 1. Greenwood Educational Association Incorporated.— Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina (a Concurrent Resoution, allowing this Bill to be introduced, having been passed by a two-thirds vote of each House, as required by the Constitution), That H. V. R. Schrader, F. N. K. Bailey, A. F. McKissick, D. A. G. Ouzts, W. T. Jones. C. C. Featherstone, S. C. Hodges, W. J. Snead and Kenneth Baker, of Greenwood, S. C., trustees, and their successors in office, be, and are hereby, created and constituted a body politic and corporate, under the name and style of “Greenwood Educational Associ7 ation,’ and as such corporation and in, by and under the said name shall have the right to contract and be contracted with, to sue and be sued and in any manner whatsoever, to acquire, hold and dispose of any and all kinds of property, real, personal and mixed, and to lease or mortgage the same; to have and use a common seal, to make such by-laws, rules and regulations as may be thought proper and, generally, to have all such rights, powers and privileges as are usually incident to corporations of like character and as now are or hereafter may be conferred by gencral laws upon corporations organized for educational purposes and as may not be repugnant to the Constitution and laws of this State or of the United States.
