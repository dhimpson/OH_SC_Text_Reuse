SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That upon the payment of five thousand eight hundred and thirty-four and 78-100 dollars ($5,834.78) by the bondsmen of J. T. Patterson, former Treasurer of Edgefield County, into the treasury of Edgefield County, within thirty days from the approval by the Governor of this Act, of which sum eight hundred thirty-four and 78-100 dollars ($834.78) shall be turned into the State treasury, the said bondsmen of former Treasurer J. P. Patterson be immediately relieved from any, liability on the bonds of said former County Treasurer by reason of any shortage in said County Treasurer’s office.
