Line No. 9, thence runs N. 25 degrees W. 1.10 miles, cornering at ford of McCord's creek, on Abbeville and Hodges road, about one - half (2) mile south of Charles Botts. Line No. 10, thence runs N. 70 degrees W. 1.02 miles, passing 150 yards north of Patrick Roches ' and cornering on public road. 50 yards south of Maud Thomas's (colored) and twenty feet north of small bridge.
