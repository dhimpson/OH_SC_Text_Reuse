SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the School DisState of South Carolina, That from and after the approval of Kershaw this Act there shall be elected a board of trustees of school county, to elect board district No. 1 of Kershaw county, including the town of Camden. Said board to consist of three members as now provided by law, who shall be qualified electors residing in said district and shall hold office for the term of two years and until their successors are elected and qualified.
