Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That in the administration of the assets of a decedent, mortgages shall not be entitled to a priority over rents, debts by specialty or debts by simple contract, except as to the particular parts of the estate affected by the liens of such mortgages. That after the property covered by the liens is exhausted the grade of the demand shall be determined by the nature of the instrument which the mortgage was given to secure.
