Section 6. Said Commission shall have full power and authority to make such rules and regulations as they may deem necessary with the proper performance of their duties. They shall meet, confer with, and report to the delegation from Greenwood county every two months, and from time to time, as may be necessary, with reference to the prosecution of said work and the discharge of their duties, as herein provided, and the said Commission shall annually, at the close of each year, make a full report to the Grand Jury of Greenwood county of its transactions during the year then closed, including an itemized statement of the money received and disbursed, and for what purpose, which said report shall be transmitted by said Grand Jury to the Circuit Judge presiding at the next succeeding term of Court for said county after said report has been made to said Grand Jury. It shall be the duty of the Grand Jury of said county to make an annual examination of the books, papers, accounts and work of said Commission as they are now required by law to make of the county officers of said county.
