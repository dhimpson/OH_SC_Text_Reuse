§ 11. County Treasurer to Make Annual Statement of Taxes Collected, etc. - That on or by the first day of May of the year 1914, and annually thereafter, it shall be the duty of the County Treasurer to make a statement to the Board of Trustees of the said school district of the city of Anderson, showing the amount of money he has collected on said taxes, and shall turn over to the said Board of Trustees any sum remaining in his hands after the payment of the interest due on said bonds; and it shall be the duty of the Board of Trustees as soon as practicable thereafter to invest said money in some safe security or securities to be approved by them for the benefit of the sinking fund provided for within the terms of this Act, and to keep the same invested as far as possible, until the maturity of said bonds or until they shall be redeemed in accordance with law, and said trustees shall have the right, in case any sums so held by them should be too small for the purpose of investments in approved securities, to deposit the same in some banking institution in the saving department at the best rates of interest obtainable until such time as the amount so obtained shall be practicable for permanent investments at a better rate of interest.
