Section 3. That in addition to the rights and privileges Act creating new School hereinbefore granted, the said school district shall have power District in to levy and collect a special tax on the assessed value of all County real and personal property returned in said school district, not authorizing exceeding three mills on the dollar, subject to the following provisions: The School Trustees of said school district shall, at any time previous to the thirtieth day of June, 1903, upon the written request of ten property holders, issue a call for a public meeting, after two weeks notice, of all those citizens who return real or personal property in said school district, and such notice shall be published in one newspaper or posted in two public places in said district for at least two weeks before such meeting, and shall specify the time, place and object of said meeting. A list of all citizens who return real or personal property in said school district shall be prepared and certified by the County Auditor, who shall send said list to said meeting.
