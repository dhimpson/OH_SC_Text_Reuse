§ 3. Duties of Trustees. - That the trustees herein provided for shall have exclusive control of the free and graded schools in said. district and shall be vested with all powers of the School Trustees under the general school law. They may organize schools, purchase or lease school property, erect school buildings, elect superintendents and teachers, and fix their salaries, prescribe the textbooks to be used and the rate of tuition to be paid by pupils not entitled to the benefit of the free school funds of said district; and make rules and regulations for the control of the schools and the school property not repugnant to law, and shall have the power to assess upon each scholar such sum or sums as may be necessary to meet the incidental expenses of the said school, not to exceed the sum of $ 2 each for one school year: Provided, That should the parent, guardian or other #390 (p.358) 358 STATUTES AT LARGE person with whom such pupil resides make application to the Board of Trustees of the said district, stating under oath that such parent, guardian or other person with whom such scholars resides is not able to pay said incidental fee, then such pupil shall be admitted without paying same.
