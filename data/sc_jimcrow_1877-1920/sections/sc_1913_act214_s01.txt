Section 1. Bond Issue for School District No. 52, in Newberry County.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That for the purpose of enlarging and equipping the school building of School District No. 52, of Newberry county, the trustees of said school district are hereby authorized and empowered to issue coupon bonds to the amount of five thousand ($5,000) dollars, if so much be necessary, for said purpose, payable in twenty years and bearing interest at not exceeding six per centum per annum, payable semiannually: Provided, That at an election to be held hereafter a majority of the qualified electors of said school district voting shall be in favor of the issue of said school bonds. § 2. Notice of Election—That the trustees of said school district are hereby required to give two weeks’ previous nqtice by publication in one or more of the newspapers published in Newberry county, and by posting at three public places in said school district, of the time and place of such election. § 38. Denominations of Bonds, etc.—That the said bonds shall be issued in such denominations as the trustees may fix, and shall be signed by the trustees, but the signatures of the trustees may be lithographed on the coupons attached to said bonds. Said bonds shall be exempt from taxation for State, county and municipal purposes. The coupons attached to said bonds shall be receivable in payment of any taxes due said school district. § 4. Annual Tax Levy to Pay Interest, etc.—That for the purpose of paying the interest on said bonds and providing a sinking fund for the payment of the principal of said bonds, the Auditor of Newberry county, or such other officer as may be charged with the assessment of taxes, shall annually levy a tax of threefourths (34) of one mill on all the taxable property in said school district, and the tax so levied shall be collected by the County Treasurer of Newberry county as State and county taxes are collected, to be held and applied by him for said purposes only, viz., to the payment of the interest on said bonds, and the surplus after paying the interest each year as a sinking fund for the payment of the principal of said bonds, which surplus shall be deposited at interest by the County Treasurer under the direction of the trustees of said school district. § 5. Proceeds of Bonds.—That the trustees of said school district shall use said bonds or the proceeds of the sale thereof at not less than par value for the purposes mentioned in Section 1 of this Act, any surplus to be turned into the general funds of said school district and used for maintaining and equipping the schools of the district. § 6. Bonds to Be Registered in Office County Superintendent Education.—Said bonds shall be registered in the office of the County Superintendent of Education. § 7. Immediately Effective—This Act shall go into effect immediately. * * * This Act was presented to the Governor the 21st day of February, A. D, 1913, and was not returned by him to the House in which it originated within three days, the General Assembly being in session.—Code Commissioner. No. 215. AN ACT Relating to Newberry School District. Section 1. Election for Newberry School District—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the Trustees of Newberry School District are hereby authorized and empowered to submit to the qualified electors of said school district not later than July 15th, 1913, the question of levying an additional tax of one mill on the taxable property in said school district, to be used for improvements and repairs. § 2. Trustees to Order Election.—Said trustees, if they deem it necessary to hold an election on this question, shall order said election on not less than full four weeks’ notice thereof, published in the newspapers of said school district, stating the time and place of voting. They shall appoint three managers to conduct the election, prescribe the form of ballots, receive the written returns from the managers showing the votes cast for and against the levy of said tax and declare the result of the election. § 3. Assessment of Tax.—In case the majority of the votes cast is in favor of the said tax levy, the trustees shall certify the fact to the County Auditor, who shall thereupon assess said tax on the taxable property of said district and enter the same upon the tax duplicate. The County Treasurer shall thereupon collect said tax at the same time and in the same manner as other taxes. § 4. Account of Tax.—The County Treasurer shall keep a separate account of said tax and pay out the same on the warrants of the trustees issued for the purposes aforesaid. § 5. Levy for 1918 and 1914.—Said tax, if voted, shall be levied for the fiscal years 1913 and 1914, only any surplus of said tax, after providing for the improvements and repairs contemplated by the trustees, may be used by them for the current maintenance of the schools in said district. § 6. Trustees May Borrow Money.—The trustees of said district are authorized to borrow money on account of said tax, if voted for the purpose of making improvements and repairs and to pledge said tax for the payment of such money as they may borrow.
