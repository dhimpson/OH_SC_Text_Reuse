SEC. 22. Upon affidavit, which may be on information and search belief, to the effect that contraband liquor is being unlawfully be issued. concealed, kept or stored in any place, a search warrant may be issued by any Magistrate of the County, empowering any officer or person who may be deputized to enter the said place by day or night and to search the said premises for the purpose of seizing the said contraband liquors therein concealed, kept or stored, which said liquor, when so seized, shall be disposed of as hereinbefore provided for the disposition of unlawful liquors: Provided, That no dwelling house shall be searched in the night time.
