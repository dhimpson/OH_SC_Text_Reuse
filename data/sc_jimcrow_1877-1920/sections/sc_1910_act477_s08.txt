SEC. 8. The said commissioners shall have power and authority, and it shall be its duty to lay out, survey, change, alter, open and make public highways in Horry County, and for any of said purposes it is hereby given full power and authority: Provided, That the County Supervisor shall be ex officio a member of said board, and have a vote thereon upon all questions of improving the highways in the county under consideration. Should there be a tie vote upon any such questions, then it shall be the duty of the Clerk of the Court of said county to cast the deciding vote, which shall be final.
