Section 1. Bond Issue by Wexford School District No. 30, Chesterfield County, Declared Void - Tax Levy to Go to Building Fund. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That the said bond issue above set out is hereby declared null and void, the trustees of said school district are relieved from issuing same, and that the interest and sinking fund derived from a tax levy placed for raising same in reference to said proposed bonds, be set aside as a part of the building fund for erecting a school building in said district.
