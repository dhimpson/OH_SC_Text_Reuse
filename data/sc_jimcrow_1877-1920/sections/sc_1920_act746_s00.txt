AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED "AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ISSUE OF BONDS BY SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 1, OF CHESTER COUNTY, SUBJECT TO THE APPROVAL OF THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF SAID DISTRICT," APPROVED THE 14TH DAY OF MARCH, A. D. 1919, TO PROVIDE FOR AN INCREASE IN THE AMOUNT FOR WHICH BONDS MAY BE ISSUED.
