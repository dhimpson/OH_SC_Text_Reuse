Sec. 2. It shall be the duty of the managers, within one week after said election, to count the votes and declare the result, as in other elections, which result must be certified in writing by the chairman of the Board of Managers to the said Board of Trustees, and if a majority of the votes cast shall be in favor of the special tax, the chairman of the Board of Trustees shall certify to the Auditor of the county the tax thus. levied, and the Auditor shall at once and thereafter from year to year assess such tax on all real and personal property returned in said school district, and the County Treasurer shall collect the same with the State and county taxes, and such tax shall be a lien on all property, and all defaulting taxpayers shall be liable to like process as for State and county taxes.
