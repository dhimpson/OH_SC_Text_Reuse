SEC. 11. That the amounts appropriated in the several preceding Manner of Sections of this Act for salaries and clerical services shall be payable drawing said monthly, and shall be paid upon the warrants of the Comptroller General; and the amounts appropriated for contingent funds and printing, as shall be required, shall be paid upon the warrants of the Comptroller General, on the application of the various officers entitled to the same: Provided, That the amounts and vouchers upon which said applications are made shall be filed with the Comptroller General before issuing his warrants on the State Treasurer for the That the amount required by the various offices for the purchase of stamps, the Comptroller General is hereby authorized to issue his warrant to the said officers for the same.
