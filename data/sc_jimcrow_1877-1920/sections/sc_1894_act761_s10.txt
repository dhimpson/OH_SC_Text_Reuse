Sec. 10. That the said City Council is hereby invested Jurisdiction with ample jurisdiction over the streets, alleys and sidewalks within the corporate limits; to extend or close, widen or narrow, lay out, grade, curb, pave and otherwise improve streets, alleys and sidewalks; to build bridges in and culverts under said streets; to prevent or remove any structure or obstruction or encroachment over or in or under said streets, alleys or sidewalks, and may permit the planting of shade trees along said streets; to prevent any company or persons from occupying with its or their works, or any appurtenances thereof, the streets, alleys or sidewalks of the city without the consent of Council; to prevent the cumbering of streets, sidewalks, alleys, lanes or bridges in the city in any manner whatever; and for the purpose of opening new streets or of widening or changing existing streets, the Condemnation. City Council may condemn lands or buildings, or parts thereof, but just compensation therefor shall first be made to the owner, and if the City Council and such owner fail to agree upon the amount of such compensation the same may be fixed as follows, to wit: The City Council shall nominate two citizens of the corporation, the owner of the property shall nominate two other citizens of the corporation, and these four shall nominate a fifth other citizen of the corporation, and these five citizens shall constitute a commission to examine the locus in quo, to take testimony and assess the compensation, after having first been sworn to decide justly and impartially in the premises; and they shall file their decision with the Clerk of the Circuit Court for the County of Abbeville within ten days from the time of their appointment, and it shall be final unless either party appeals therefrom to the Court of Common Pleas for said County by serving on the other party within five days after the filing of such decision a notice of appeal; and in that case the issue of compensation shall be submitted to a jury at the term of the Circuit Court next thereafter ensuing. In the event the property owner refuses to nominate two commissioners or fails to do so within five days after he is notified to do so, then they shall be nominated by the Clerk of the Circuit Court for the County of Abbeville, and the fifth commissioner shall be chosen as before. The said commissioners shall be entitled to receive one dollar each for their services, to be paid by the City Council, but the costs of appeal shall be paid by the losing party. That for the purpose of keeping the streets, alleys, sidewalks, bridges, drains and culverts in repair, or for laying out and constructing new streets, all the male citizens of said corporation between the ages of sixteen and fifty - five years shall render to the City Council personal service therein for at least five days in each year, at such time as they may be called on by the corporate authorities; but such services may be commuted by the payment of two and 50-100 dollars to compound tax. the Treasurer of the city at the time of the annual payment of taxes, herein before provided for, to be expended for like purposes; and in the event any person liable to perform such service shall refuse or fail to render the same, after notice of the time and place to serve, the said City Council or the Mayor shall have the power to try such person for such offense, and if found guilty to sentence him to as much as thirty days ' imprisonment or thirty days ' labor on the streets or to pay a fine of as much as thirty dollars, to be collected as other fines are collected. Members of the City Council shall be exempt from such duty, and all citizens of Privileges of said corporation shall be exempt from road duty without the corporation. That whenever any new street is to be laid out or opened, a street graded or paved, a culvert built or any other public improvement constructed, the City Council shall determine what proportion, if any, of the cost Cost of streets. thereof shall be paid out of the city treasury and what proportion by the owners of real estate to be benefited thereby; but no such public improvement shall be made, to be defrayed in whole or in part by local assessment, until it has been requested to be undertaken by at least a majority of the owners of the property to be assessed for such improvement; and such assessments shall be collected in the same manner as is the cost of the construction of sidewalks. That the said City Council may require all persons owning and occupying a lot or lots on any street or alley in the said city to construct and keep in good repair sidewalks abutting such lot or lots, and the Council may designate the width of such sidewalks and the manner of their construction; and in the event the owner shall refuse or neglect to comply with such requirement or regulation, the City Council may repair or construct the said sidewalks and recover from the owner the cost thereof in a Court of competent jurisdiction; but the contract for such repairs or construction must first have been let out to the lowest bidder after notice by one publication in a newspaper in said city.
