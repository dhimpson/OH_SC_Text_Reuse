Sec. 9. That persons charged with the violation of law or the ordinances of said city shall undergo trial before the Trials. Mayor or before the Council, as the defendant may elect, and with or without the intervention of a jury; that the Mayor or Council may require the attendance of witnesses and require them to give testimony upon the trial of any cause then pending, and shall be governed by the same rules of procedure and evidence as are now or may hereafter be prescribed for the government of the Courts of Trial Justices; and the party charged with an offense shall be cited to trial by the service upon him of a summons under the Summons. hand of the Mayor, unless the offense is committed in full view of an officer, wherein shall be specified the offense charged and the time and place of trial and wherein the City Council shall be plaintiff; that the said Council shall keep a book wherein shall be entered the names of parties Docket. charged with the violation of law or ordinances, the date of trial and the judgment in such case; that fines so imposed may be collected by an execution issued under the hand and seal of the Clerk and Treasurer, tested by the corporate seal and directed to a policeman of the corpora- tion, who shall seize such property of the defendant as shall be subject to levy and sale and sell in a public place the same to satisfy the judgment, after two weeks ' advertisement on the court house door.
