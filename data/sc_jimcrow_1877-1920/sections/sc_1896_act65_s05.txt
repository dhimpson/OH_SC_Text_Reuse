Section 6. That the Board of Trustees of the Colored Normal, Industrial, Agricultural and Mechanical College ef South Carolina shall have, and are hereby given, full and ample power to do and to perform any and all acts whatsoever necessary to effect a complete and final separation of the interests of the State from those of Claflin University, and, if found necessary to protect or promote the interests of the State, the authority here given shall authorize said Trustees to sell, purchase or exchange real estate.
