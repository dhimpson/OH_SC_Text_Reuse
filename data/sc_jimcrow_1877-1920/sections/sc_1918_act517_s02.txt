§ 3. Appointment and Term of Office of Board — Superin tendents and Employees. — That the said State Board of Correc tional Administration shall consist of five members, appointed by the Governor, with the advice and consent of the Senate, whose terms of office shall be five years each, and shall be so designated that the term of one member shall expire each year, subject to removal by the Governor for cause, which Board shall have charge of the South Carolina Industrial School and of the State Reformatory for Negro Boys as hereinafter provided. The said State Board of Correctional Administration shall have exclusive power to appoint, and, in its discretion, remove the Superintendent of the South Carolina Indus trial School and the Superintendent of the State Reformatory for Negro Boys, and each of the said Superintendents shall have the power, in his discretion, to appoint and remove all other officers and employees of the South Carolina Industrial School and the State Reformatory for Negro Boys, respectively, subject to the approval of the State Board of Correctional Administration: Provided, That the said State Board of Correctional Administration may, in its dis cretion, appoint one Superintendent for both the South Carolina Industrial Schools and the State Reformatory for Negro Boys. The members of the said State Board of Correctional Administra tion, appointed in accordance with the provisions of this Act, shall be appointed upon the expiration of the terms of the present mem bers of the Board of Trustees of the South Carolina Industrial School, whose terms of office shall not be affected by the new method of appointment.
