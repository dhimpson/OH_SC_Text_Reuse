SEC. 2. That immediately after the said election the said trustees shall organize as a Board by electing one of their number Chairman and another Secretary, and shall determine by lot or otherwise who of their number shall serve for the term of one year, who for the term of two years, who for the term of three years, who for the term of four years, and who for the term of five years, and make a record of the same and the respective terms of the members of the said Board of Trustees Election and shall be in accordance therewith, and until their successors are elected. And there shall be annually an election in the same manner on the same day for one member of said Board of Trustees who shall hold his office for the term of five years, and until his successor is elected.
