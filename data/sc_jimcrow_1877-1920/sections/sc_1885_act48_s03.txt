SECTION 2. That said town shall be governed by an Intendant and four Wardens, who shall be citizens of the United States and of this State, and shall have been residents of the said town for sixty days immediately preceding the election, and who shall be elected by the qualified voters of the said town on the first When elected. Monday in March, A. D. 1886, and every year thereafter on the first Monday in March, ten days ' public notice thereof being previously given; and all male inhabitants of the age of twenty - one years, citizens of the State, who shall have resided in said town for sixty days immediately preceding the election shall be entitled to vote for said Intendant and Wardens.
