SEC. 14. No sale or delivery permitted under this Act shall be made on Sunday, on a general or primary election day, on a legal holiday, or between sunset and sunrise of any day, nor shall it be lawful for Dispensaries to ship liquors or beverages into a County where there is no Dispensary; nor shall any common carrier transport such liquors or beverages from a County having a Dispensary into a County where there is no Dispensary.
