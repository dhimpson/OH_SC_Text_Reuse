SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That from and after the passage of this Act it shall be unlawful for any white Intermarriage man to intermarry with any woman of either the Indian or negro races, or any mulatto, mestizoe or half breed, or for any white woman to intermarry with any person other than a white man, or for any mulatto, half breed, negro, Indian or mestizoe to intermarry with a white woman, and any such marriage, or attempted marriage, shall be utterly null and void and of none effect.
