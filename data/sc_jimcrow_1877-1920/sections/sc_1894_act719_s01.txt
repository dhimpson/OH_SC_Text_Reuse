Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That for the purpose of maintaining public schools in the town of Waterloo, in Laurens County, the County Board of Examiners of Laurens County be, and they are hereby, auSchool district thorized and required to lay off a separate school district, the limits of which shall extend four miles square, taking as a central point the present depot of the Port Royal and Western Carolina Railroad, so that the limits thereof shall form a square.
