SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That for the purpose of maintaining graded public schools in the city School district of Sumter and promoting their efficiency, the area embraced in the corporate limits of the city of Sumter is hereby declared to be a new school district, to be known as the School District of Name. the City of Sumter.
