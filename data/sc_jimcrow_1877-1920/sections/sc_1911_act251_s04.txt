SEC. 4. That if a majority of the votes cast at said election shall be for issuing of said bonds, then the said trustees shall Proceeds to be issue the same, to run for a period of not more than twenty years; and the said trustees shall sell said bonds for not less than par, and appropriate the proceeds of the said sale to the purpose of erecting school buildings in said district, or other school purposes, as said trustees shall deem best for the educational interest of said district; and the said bonds shall constitute a lien on the property of said school district.
