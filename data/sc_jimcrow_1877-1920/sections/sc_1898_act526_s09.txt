SECTION 9. That the amount specified in the preceding sections of this Act for salaries and clerical services shall be shall be paid. paid in monthly installments, unless otherwise provided for, and shall be paid upon the warrants of the Comptroller General on the application of the various officers entitled to the same: Provided, That the accounts and vouchers upon which such applications are made shall be filed with the Comptroller General before issuing his warrants on the State Treasurer for the same, and that for the purchase of stamps the Comptroller General is hereby authorized to issue his warrant to such officers.
