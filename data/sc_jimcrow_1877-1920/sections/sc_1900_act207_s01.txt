SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Sec. 34 of Act State of South Carolina, That section 34 of an Act entitled " An Act to declare the free school law of the State, " approved March 9, 1896, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out said section, and inserting in lieu thereof the following, to be known as section 34: How school districts may school tax.
