SEC. 9. The Road Commission as herein provided for shall continue in office for four years, at which time their term shall expire. If it shall be made to appear to the satisfaction of the Supervisor that it is necessary or desirable to continue the said Road Commission for any township at the expiration of the term herein provided for, he shall order another election for the successors to the said Road Comimssion, who shall be elected, qualified and shall have the same powers and duties as are herein prescribed.
