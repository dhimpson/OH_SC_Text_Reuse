SEC. 3. That immediately upon the approval of this Act by the Governor, he shall appoint some competent person magisappoint magistrate for the territory herein annexed, whose salary shall be nexed terrifour hundred dollars per annum, with the power to appoint a constable, whose salary shall be three hundred dollars. The magistrate so appointed shall have such further rights, duties and powers, and be subject to such restrictions as are now provided by law for the magistrates for Florence county, and shall continue in office until his successor is appointed and duly qualified, at such time and in such manner as the other magistrates for Florence county.
