Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, Whenever two or more sections of an old County or Counties desiring to be incorporated into a new County shall file with the Governor a petition signed by one-third of the qualified electors residing within the area of each section of an old County proposed to be cut off to form a new County, setting forth the boundaries of the proposed new County, the proposed name, the number of inhabitants, the area, the taxable property as shown by the last tax returns, and that the proposed lines for the new County do not run nearer than eight miles of any court house building then established.
