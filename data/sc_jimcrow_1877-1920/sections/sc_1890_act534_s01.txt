SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the title of an Act entitled " An Act to create a School District of that portion of Barnwell County lying within the corporate limits of the Town of Williston " be, and the same is hereby, amended by adding the words " and territory adjacent thereto, ' Title changed. so that said title when so amended shall read as follows: " An Act to create a School District of that portion of Barnwell County lying within the corporate limits of the Town of Williston and territory adjacent thereto. "
