SEC. 11. The said school district, in addition to the powers the corporahereinbefore provided for, shall have, and is hereby endowed with, the powers of a corporation, to issue the said bonds, and, Description of when issued, they shall be signed by the chairman of the board of trustees for said school district, shall run for a period of thirty years, their coupons shall be receivable for all taxes in said school district, and they shall constitute a first lien upon all property which may be improved from their proceeds.
