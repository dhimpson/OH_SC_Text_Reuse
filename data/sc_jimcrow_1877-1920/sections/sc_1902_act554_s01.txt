SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the artificial limbs State of South Carolina: That the sum of (2,000) two thousand dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and is hereby, appropriated to defray the expenses of the repair of artificial limbs heretofore donated to citizens of this State who lost a leg or an arm, or who became permanently disabled in a leg or in an arm during military service in the war between the States.
