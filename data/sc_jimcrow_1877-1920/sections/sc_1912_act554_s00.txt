AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED "AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ISSUE OF TOWNSHIP BONDS FOR ROAD IMPROVEMENT IN OCONEE COUNTY. Approved the 15th DAY OF FEBRUARY, 1910, BY ADDING A SECTION TO BE KNOWN AS SECTION 10, RELATING TO SPECIAL ROAD TAX LEVY FOR TOWNSHIPS, AND BY CHANGING SECTION 10 TO SECTION 11.
