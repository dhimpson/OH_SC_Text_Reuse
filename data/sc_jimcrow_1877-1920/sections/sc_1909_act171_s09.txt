SEC. 9. Said commission shall not at any time or place expend any money upon any highway unless the parties whose propEasements erties touch or abut upon said highway shall, without exed to county. pense or cost to the county, convey in writing all easements and rights of way necessary over and along same: Provided, This restriction shall not interfere with the right of the commission to improve any portions of said highway that do not touch or abut upon the property owner refusing to give the right of way. The commission shall have full power to employ competent engineer or engineers, who shall be paid such compensation as the commission may fix out of the funds herein provided. Said commission shall file with the county supervisor four times each year a full statement of their receipts and disbursements.
