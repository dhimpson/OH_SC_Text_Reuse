Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: That for the purpose of extending the length of the sessions of the St. George’s Graded School, and’ of paying the debt upon the school building, the School Trustees of George District, number five, in Dorchester County, are authorized and empowered to charge and collect a matriculation fee not exceeding two dollars in each year of each scholar attending said school: Provided, nevertheless, That no child shall be debarred from attending said Graded School through the inability of his or her parents or guardian to pay said matriculation fee.
