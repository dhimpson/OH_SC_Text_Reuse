Sec. 3. That the said Town Council shall semi-annually, at such time and place as they shall fix and determine, pay the interest due on said bonds, and for this purpose the said Town Council, in addition to the powers already conferred, shall be, and they hereby are, authorized and required to levy such tax upon the real and personal property assessed for taxation in the said town as may be deemed necessary to pay the interest on said bonds which may become due in each and every year, until the whole of said bonds be paid.
