SECTION 1. Be tt enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That a new township to be known as Limestone township be, and the is hereby, formed and created in the county of Orangeburg out of Caw Caw and portions of Elizabeth and Orange townships, with the following boundaries: Beginning at that point on the boundary line between Caw Caw and Elizabeth townships where the boundary line of Calhoun county deflects from said township line, thence following the boundary line of Calhoun county to the run of Saddler’s Swamp, thence down to the run of Caw Caw Swamp, thence down to the Edisto River, thence up the Edisto River to the mouth of Lang Branch, thence up the run of Long Branch to a point at or near its source, and thence a straight line to the point of beginning. The supervisor of Orangeburg county is hereby authorized and directed to have the last mentioned line located. The said township shall have all the rights, powers, liabilities and incidents, now or hereafter, fixed by law upon the other townships of said county.
