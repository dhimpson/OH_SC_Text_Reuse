Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the State Treasurer be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to receive from the holders thereof, who shall surrender the and stock for same on or before the first day of April, A. D. 1888, all coupon bonds and all certificates of stock commonly known as " Deficiency " Bonds and " Deficiency " Stock, issued under the proVisions of a Joint Resolution entitled " A Joint Resolution providing a mode of ascertaining the debt of the State, and of liquidating and settling the same, " approved 22nd March, 1878, and under all Acts and Joint Resolutions relating thereto; and the State Treasurer shall immediately thereupon, in exchange for and in lieu of said bonds and certificates of stock so surrendered, issue to said holders other coupon bonds and certificates of stock of equal face value with those so surrendered, and of the kind, class and description the issue of which is provided. for in Section 2 of this Act; or the State Treasurer may issue to such holders as shall require it all bonds or all stock, or part bonds and part stock, of said authorized issue in making such both. exchange; and, further, at the time of such surrender the State Treasurer shall anticipate and pay in cash all coupons to mature Anticipation of on such surrendered bonds and all interest to mature on such surrendered certificates of stock, being the interest which shall mature on the first day of July, A. D. 1888. That the State Treasurer shall by advertisement made for sixty days prior to the first day of April, A. C., respectively, give notice of the provisions made in this Act for the said exchange, and for the anticipation of the payment of said interest.
