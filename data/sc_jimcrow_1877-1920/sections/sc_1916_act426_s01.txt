Section 1. Act Providing for Rural Policemen in Marlboro County Amended - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: That an Act entitled " An Act to Amend an Act entitled ' An Act to Provide for Rural Policemen for Marlboro County ' approved 13th Day of February, 1911, now embodied in Sections 1267, 1268, 1269, 1270, 1271 and 1272 of the Code of Laws of South Carolina, 1912, Volume I, " approved the 12th day of February, 1915, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the word " three " on line 7 of Section 1267 of said Act, and by inserting in lieu thereof the word " four; " by striking out the words " one hundred ” on line 2 of Section 1268 of said Act, and by inserting in lieu thereof the words " eighty five; " and by adding another section to be designated as Section 1272c, to read as follows: " The rural policemen are hereby authorized to serve civil papers for Magistrates, and to collect delinquent taxes in Marlboro County, and for such work shall be allowed to receive in addition to the salary hereinbefore provided, the regular costs for serving such papers in Magistrate's courts: Provided, Such rural policemen shall make and file with the Clerk of Court verified itemized statements of all sums of money collected by them monthly, such statements to show the amount collected, and shall specify the claim on which same has been collected, " so that said Act when so amended shall read as follows:
