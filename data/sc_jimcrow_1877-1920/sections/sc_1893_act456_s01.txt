SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Area of school all that territory lying within the following boundary, to wit: Starting at Knotts ' Mills, and thence by Bull Swamp and up Third Branch to Redmond's Mill, then up Pine Plain road till it intersects the Platt Springs Township line, and along said line till it crosses the Jones road, then down said road to Daniel Jefcoat's field, thence on to Knotts ' Mill in a straight line, be, and Creation and the same is hereby, created as a separate school district in said County, to be known as Swansea School District.
