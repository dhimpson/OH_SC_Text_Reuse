SEC 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That for the School purpose of maintaining public schools in the city of Spartanburg count be, the County Board of Examiners of Spartanburg County be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to lay off a separate School District, having Spartanburg Court House as the centre, and embracing an area of not less than three miles square.
