SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Magistrates to State of South Carolina, The Governor shall have authorbe appointed by the Governor by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Magistrates in each County of the State, who shall hold their offices for the term of two years, and until their successors are appointed and qualified. The number of Magistrates to be appointed for each County and their territorial jurisdiction shall be the same as heretofore prescribed by law for Trial Justices in the respective Counties of the State, except as hereinafter otherwise provided.
