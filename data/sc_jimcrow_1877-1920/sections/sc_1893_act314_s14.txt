SEC. 15. That in anticipation of the collection of the taxes herein before levied, the Governor and the State Treasurer be, and they are hereby, empowered to borrow on the credit of the State so much money as may be needed to meet promptly at maturity the interest which shall mature during the year 1894 on the valid debt of the State and to pay the current expenses of the State Government for the present fiscal year: Provided, That the sum so borrowed shall in no event exceed the amount of three hundred thousand ($ 300,000) dollars.
