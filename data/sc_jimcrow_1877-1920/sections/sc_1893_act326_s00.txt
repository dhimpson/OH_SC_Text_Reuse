AN ACT TO FURTHER PROVIDE ADDITIONAL LIMBS FOR ALL SOLDIERS OR SAILORS OF THE STATE WHO LOST A LEG, EYE OR ARM DURING MILITARY SERVICE IN THE YEARS 1861, 1862, 1863, 1864 AND 1865, IN THE WAR BETWEEN THE STATES, WHO ARE NOW LIVING. SEcTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authcrity of the same, That all soldiers or sailors of the State who lost a leg, eye or arm during military service of the years 1861, 1862, 1863, 1864 and 1865 in the war between the States, who are now living, whose income is not sufficient to give them a comfortable support, and who have heretofore received from the State an artificial leg, eye or arm, or who are entitled to receive the same, or who may have elected to take in money the price of said artificial leg, eye or arm, be, and they are hereby, allowed an additional leg, eye or arm, or the price of same in money, if they shall so elect, upon their complying with all the provisions and conditions of the law now of force in regard to the same: Provided, That those soldiers or sailors who did not receive under the provisions of the Act approved 24th December, 1892, an additional artificial eye, arm or leg, or the price thereof in money, shall be first provided for under the provisions of this Act: Provided, further, That nothing herein contained shall be so construed as to prevent said soldiers or sailors from also receiving their share of the annual amount appropriated for the pensions to Confederate soldiers and others as authorized by the Act of the General Assembly entitled “‘An Act to provide for the relief of certain soldiers, sailors and widows of soldiers or sailors of the late war between the States,” approved December 24th, A. D. 188%, or by Acts amendatory thereto.
