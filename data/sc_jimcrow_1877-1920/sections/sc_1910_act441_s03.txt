SEC. 3. That the said trustees shall have printed, for the use of the voters in said election, an equal number of ballots, on one set of which shall be printed the words: " For the issuing of bonds, " and on the other set, the words: " Against the issuing of bonds, " and a sufficient number of said ballots shall be placed at the voting place on the day of the election. That the said managers shall conduct the said election, and, at the close thereof, shall canvass the vote and make a return of the result thereof, in writing, to the Board of School Trustees aforesaid.
