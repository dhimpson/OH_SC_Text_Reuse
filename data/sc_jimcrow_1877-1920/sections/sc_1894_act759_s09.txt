Sec. 6. That the Town Council of the town of Summerville, in this State, be, and the same is hereby, authorized, Encroachments in cases where any person or persons have encroached by remedied. means of buildings, fences or other structures upon the streets or roads or premises claimed to be part and parcel of the streets or roads of the said town, in its discretion, to treat and arrange with said party or parties in regard to the same, with power to agree that the portion encroached upon or any part thereof shall be and become the property of the encroaching party or parties, upon such terms and for such considerations as may be mutually agreed upon; and also in its discretion to arrange with any such party or parties for the proper location of the line and lines between the streets aforesaid and private property, and on such arrangements being made, and said lines duly established, the same shall be the true lines, notwithstanding that what was or was claimed to be a part of any street may thus become a part of private premises: Provided, however, That in any Proviso. and all cases where no such arrangement shall be proposed or completed, and until any such arrangement shall be agreed upon and completed, nothing herein contained shall be construed to prejudice the State, or any person or persons, or the said Town Council, in respect to any and all rights and remedies and the exercise thereof regarding obstructions of or encroachments upon the streets or roads of the said town.
