SEC. 2. That J. M. Wheeler, J. P. Bowers and J. H. Hunter are hereby appointed to serve as an Advisory Board with the said Trustees who shall serve in the event of the bonds being voted, and shall have equal power with said Trustees, and shall serve as said Advisory Board until the funds therein voted shall have been exhausted. Any vacancy arising in said Advisory Board shall be filled by the said Board of Trustees and remaining members of said Advisory Board.
