SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That that portion of Newberry County lying within the following boundary lines, to wit: Beginning at a point on the line dividing the Counties of Newberry and Lexington near the residence of John A. Boland; and from thence North of the residence of George Shealy; and from thence North of the residence of Calvin Metts; and from thence North of the residence of Michael Bowers; and from thence in a straight line to Camping Creek; thence along Camping Creek to Lexington County line; thence along Lexington County line to beginning near residence of John A. Boland, Creation and be, and is hereby, constituted a new school district, to be known as Wheeland Graded School District.
