Sec. 5. That it shall be the duty of the Chairman and secretary of said public meeting, within one week after said meeting has been held, to notify the Chairman of the Board of Trustees for said school district and the Auditor of said County of the amount of said tax thus levied and how it has been appropriated, and the County Auditor shall at once Assess ment. assess such tax on all real and personal property returned And collection in said school district, and the County Treasurer shall collect the same with the State and County taxes, and such tax shall be a lien on all property until paid, and the defaulting taxpayers shall be liable to like process and penalty as defaulters for State and County taxes.
