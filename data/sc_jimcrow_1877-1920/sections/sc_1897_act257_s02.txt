That for the full and just accounting for said money and books, and all his acts and dealings relating thereto, the County Superintendent of Education shall be responsible on his official bond. Secrion 5. That no tax or license, either State, County or municipal, shall be imposed on or required of the County Superintendent of Education on account of the purchasing and furnishing of said books to the pupils as aforesaid. Section 6. That this Act shall be a general Act, and apply to all the Counties of the State, from and after its approval. Section 7. That all Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent herewith be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
