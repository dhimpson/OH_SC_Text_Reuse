SEC. 3. That to provide for a sinking fund for said bonds and sinking fund. for the interest on said bonds, the said Board of School Commissioners of said school district shall annually assess and levy a tax of one mill upon all real and personal property in said school district, and the Treasurer of the city of Florence shall collect said tax at the time and in the manner provided for collecting the city taxes, and for this purpose shall be a part of the body corporate of said school district.
