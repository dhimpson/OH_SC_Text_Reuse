Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the provisions of Sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of an Act entitled “An Act to authorize County Commissioners to submit to the qualified electors of their several Counties a proposition to alter the fence laws and to provide for effectuating the same approved June 7, 187 together with all amendments to said Act shall apply without an election to a certain portion of Orangeburg County, being portions of the township of Amelia and Pine Grove, included within the following lines, to wit A line commencing on Congarce River at a point where the boundary line between the lands of Carroll and A. D. Goodwyn strike same above Fort Motte, and running in a direction about southeast along the boundary lines of lands of Goodwyn, Legare and Rast; thence through lands of McKenzie, Laws, and Ham, and Ryan to bounda line of Peterkin, thence along the line of Ham and Ryan to lands of Deveaux, passing through lands of Deveaux to line of Wienges, thence in an uwsterly direction to the Whetstone place, thence northeasterly to lands of Deveaux thence easterly following lines of lands of Darby, Fairey, Buyck and the Bradenburgs, thence along the line of estate of Taber including lands of Mrs. T. J. Goodwyn, Karick and Crosswell striking the Santee River at a point where lands of Crosswell and Riser join. Src. 2. That this Act shall go into effect on the first day of January, 1881 Approved
