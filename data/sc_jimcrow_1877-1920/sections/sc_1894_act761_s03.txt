Sec. 3. That the said city shall be governed by a Mayor Mayor and and eight Aldermen, who shall be citizens of the United Aldermen. States, qualified electors of said city, who shall have been domiciled in the State one year and in the city sixty days next before their election, and shall continue in office for two years from the day of their election, and until their successors are elected and qualified; that the Mayor and one Election and Alderman for each ward shall be elected during one year, and one Alderman for each ward the next year, and so on in each alternate year; that the present Intendant and four Wardens of the town of Abbeville shall be the Mayor and four of the Aldermen of the city of Abbeville until the regular city election, which shall be held on the second Tuesday in April, 1896, and that one Alderman from each ward shall be elected on the second Tuesday in April, 1895, as hereinafter provided, and that thereafter the regular city election shall be held on the second Tuesday in April of each year; that the Mayor and Aldermen shall take the oath of office prescribed by the Constitution of this State before entering on the discharge of their official duties, and a majority of Quorum. the Council shall constitute a quorum to transact business; that the Mayor shall be paid for his services such compensaMayor's salary. tion as the City Council by ordinance fix, which shall not be diminished during his term of office; that in case the Mayor be temporarily absent or unable to attend to the duties of his office, from sickness or other cause, the Aldermen shall have power to elect any one of their number to serve as Mayor pro tempore; that the City Council shall hold stated Mayor pro tem. meetings at least once in each month, and such other meetings as it may desire; that the Mayor shall nominate at the commencement of his term, subject to the approval of the City Council by vote, such committees to transact the public business as the Council may by ordinance determine. That the City Council shall elect a Clerk and Treasurer, a Superintendent of Streets, such policemen as may be necessary, and such other officers as may be necessary to transact the business of the city; and it may by ordinance prescribe the duties of said officers and may fix the salaries of the same and require bonds of them for the faithful performance of their duty; that the policemen shall take the oath prescribed by law for Constables, and shall have all the powers and be charged with all the liabilities now or hereafter incident to the office of Constable; and they may arrest and commit to the guard house of the city for speedy trial any person who, within the corporate limits, may be engaged in a breach of the peace, riotous or disorderly conduct, open obscenity, public drunkenness, or any conduct grossly indecent or dangerous to the citizens of said city. All of said officers shall be liable to removal at the pleasure of the Council. That the Clerk of Council shall annually, on the first day of April, make out and publish on the court house door in said city, and file in the office of Council, an itemized statement of the receipts and disbursements of the city during the preceding year; and when a new Council has been elected and sworn in, then the outgoing Council shall forthwith turn over to it all the records, property and funds belonging to the said city, and a failure to do so without reasonable excuse for twenty - four hours after a demand, in writing, so to do shall subject the members of the outgoing Council to a fine of five hundred dollars, to be recovered by the City Council for the use of said city in any Court of competent jurisdiction.
