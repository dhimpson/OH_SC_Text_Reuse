§ 3. Terms of Bonds - Execution. That the said school district be, and is hereby, authorized and empowered to issue coupon #770 (p.1380) 1380 STATUTES AT LARGE bonds in the sum of twenty - five thousand dollars, of the denomination of one thousand dollars each, bearing interest at not more than six per centum from date of issue, and running for a series of twenty - five years, one bond and the annual interest on the whole issue outstanding to be paid each year until the whole amount of principal and interest shall be paid in full; said bonds and interest coupons to be signed by the Chairman of the Board of Trustees of said school district and countersigned by the Clerk of said Board: Provided, That the signature of said officers may be lithographed upon the bonds and coupons of said bonds, and such lithographing shall be sufficient signing of said bonds and coupons of said bonds.
