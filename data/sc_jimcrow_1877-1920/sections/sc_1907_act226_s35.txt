SEC. 36. Any person, firm or corporation, now engaged Regulations under license in the manufacture and sale of alcoholic liquors the manufacor beers, in Counties which have not heretofore voted upon the ture of liquors. question of " Dispensary " or " No Dispensary, " is hereby, permitted and licensed, upon compliance with the following provisions, to continue such manufacture and sale until an election be held as herein provided, resulting in the prohibition of such manufacture, unless such license be sooner revoked by the General Assembly. Such license is hereby granted upon payment annually, in advance, to the County Dispensary Board in such County of a license fee graduated as follows: For a distillery using from three to fifty bushels of grain per day, five hundred dollars; for a distillery using from fifty to one hundred bushels of grain per day, one thousand dollars; for a distillery using from one hundred to two hundred bushels per day, fifteen hundred dollars; for a distillery using from two hundred to six hundred bushels per day, twenty - five hundred dollars; and for a distillery using over six hundred bushels per day, five thousand dollars; and for breweries and bottling establishments, manufacturing or bottling lager and rice beer, or other intoxicating or malt beverages, the following sum, to wit: Such establishment using from one to ten barrels per day, five hundred dollars; from ten to twenty barrels per day, fifteen hundred dollars; from twenty to forty barrels per day, three thousand dollars; and for those using more than forty barrels per day, five thousand dollars. Such licensee shall file with said Board a bond to the State for the use of the County in a sum of not less than five thousand dollars nor more than twenty thousand dollars, to be fixed by the County Dispensary Board, said bond to be based upon the size of the establishment, with good and sufficient surety, conditioned upon the faithful compliance with the law: Provided, That no license is hereby granted to any person, firm or corporation other than those now engaged in such manufacture and sale in Counties wherein. Dispensaries are now established and in operation, or to the successor or successors of any such person, firm or corporation, approved by the County Dispensary Board; and no license shall hereafter be granted except in such Counties, and ony to manufacture and sell in one city therein of at least twenty thousand inhabitants: Provided, further, That it shall not be lawful for such manufacturers to sell such liquors and beverages except in quantities and in the manner prescribed in the Constitution: Provided, further, That such manufacturers shall not sell such liquors and beverages to any person, firm or corporation within the limits of this State, except to County Dispensary Boards. In the application for a permit or license to manufacture liquors County Board and beverages, or beer, the applicant shall give the County Dispensary Board full power, upon any violation of this Act, to seize and take possession of any machinery or product on hand A. D. 1907. at the distillery or place where such applicant may manufacture such liquors or such beers, and shall authorize the said Board to pay the United States Government tax upon the same and dispose thereof as provided herein for contraband goods. The license fees herein provided shall be divided as provided for Dispensary profits in Sections 18 and 34 of this Act; and the term manufacturer, wherever used in this Act, shall include bottling establishments for malt liquors, and the brewer of beer may sell to such bottling establishments.
