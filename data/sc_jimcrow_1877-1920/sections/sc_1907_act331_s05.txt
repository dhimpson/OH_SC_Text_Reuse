SEC. 5. That said bonds and the coupons thereto attached shall be signed by the Chairman and countersigned by the Secretary and Treasurer of the Board of Trustees of said School District: Provided, That the signature of said officers may be lithographed upon the coupons attached to said bonds, and such lithographed signatures shall be a sufficient signing thereof. Approved the 20th day of February, A. D. 1907.
