AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF LAURENS TO ISSUE BONDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF ERECTING OR REPAIRING A SCHOOL BUILDING AND EQUIPPING THE SAME, AND TO REFUND BONDS HERETOFORE ISSUED.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the Board of Trustees of the School District of the town of Laurens are hereby authorized and empowered to submit to the qualified voters resident in said School District, at an election to be held for that purpose, as hereinafter provided for, the question of issuing additional bonds, not to exceed the amount of thirty thousand dollars, bearing not exceeding five and one - half per cent. interest, and payable in thirty years, and the levy of such annual tax as may be necessary to pay the interest on said bonds.
SEC. 2. An election shall be held in said School District for said purpose upon the filing of a petition with said Board of Trustees, signed by a majority of the freeholders residing within said School District; the said election to be ordered by the said Board of Trustees when the said petition is duly filed with it. Twenty days ' notice of the time, place and purpose of said election shall be given by advertising the same in one of the newspapers published within the said School District. At said election only those shall be allowed to vote who are duly qualified to vote under the Constitution and Laws of the said State. Those persons voting in favor of the issuing of said bonds shall vote a written or printed ballot, upon which the words " For the Issuing of Bonds " are written or printed thereon; and those opposed to the issuing of said bonds shall vote a similar ballot, upon which the words " Against the Issuing of Bonds " are written or printed thereon. The managers for said election shall be appointed by the said Board of Trustees, and the polls shall be open from 9 o'clock in the forenoon until 4 o'clock in the afternoon. After the said polls are closed the result of said election shall be ascertained by the said managers and certified to the said Board of Trustees, who shall declare the result thereof. If the election shall result in a majority of persons, voting at said election, voting in favor of the issuing of said bonds, then bonds, not exceeding in amount the sum of thirty thousand dollars, shall be executed and sold by the said Board of Trustees. The said bonds shall be signed by the Chairman and the Secretary of the said Board. The said bonds shall be issued in such denominations as may be agreed upon by the said Board, and shall bear interest at a rate not exceeding five and one - half per cent. per annum, which interest shall be payable semi - annually, in January and July of each year: Provided, That said bonds shall not be sold for less than par value.
SEC. 3. That the proceeds realized from the sale of the said bonds shall be used by the said Board of Trustees in erecting a suitable school building upon the lot already owned by the Board of Trustees, or in remodeling and changing the old building that is thereon situate, and in providing suitable furniture and apparatus for the same.
SEC. 4. That the County Auditor and the County Treasurer for Laurens County are hereby required and directed to levy and collect, annually, a sufficient tax upon the taxable property in said School District to pay the interest on the said bonds. The said levy to be in addition to the levy already required by the Act of 1891, to pay the interest on the bonds already issued by the said School District. Any surplus remaining A. D. 1907. after the payment of the annual interest shall be applied, under the direction of the said Board of Trustees, to the payment of the principal of said bonds.
SEC. 5. That upon the maturity of the bonds, amounting to Trustees ten thousand dollars, heretofore issued, under the provisions of thorized an Act entitled " An Act to authorize the Board of Trustees turing bonds. of the School District of the town of Laurens to submit to the qualified voters the question of issuing bonds for the use of said School District, " approved 24th day of December,.1891. the Board of Trustees of the School District of the town of Laurens is hereby authorized and directed to take up and refund the said bonds heretofore issued, by issuing new bonds of the said School District; the said new bonds to bear interest at a sum not exceeding five and one - half per cent. per annum, and to mature in thirty years from that date. That the said Board of School Trustees is hereby, also, authorized and empowered to take up and refund the said old bonds before they mature, provided the holders thereof consent, by issuing new bonds for said amount, the said bonds to run for a period of thirty years and to bear interest at a sum not exceeding five and onehalf per cent. per annum.
SEC. 6. That the said bonds so issued shall be free and exempt from all taxation.
SEC. 7. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
