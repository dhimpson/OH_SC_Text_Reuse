SEC. 2. Any person who shall violate the first Section of this Act or any one of the provisions thereof shall be guilty of a misdemeanor, and, on conviction thereof, shall be punished by a fine of not less than five hundred dollars or imprisonment for not less than twelve months, or both, in the discretion of the Court.
