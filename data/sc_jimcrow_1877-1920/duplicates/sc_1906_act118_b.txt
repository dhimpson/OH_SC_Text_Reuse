AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE SCHOOL TRUSTEES OF SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 20, OF LEXINGTON COUNTY, TO ESTABLISH AND PROVIDE FOR TWO WHITE SCHOOLS IN SAID DISTRICT.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That from and after the passage of this Act, the School Trustees of School District No. 20, of Lexington County, shall be, and are hereby, authorized to establish and provide for two white schools in said District, as other such schools are established and provided for. Approved the 16th day of February, A. D. 1906.
