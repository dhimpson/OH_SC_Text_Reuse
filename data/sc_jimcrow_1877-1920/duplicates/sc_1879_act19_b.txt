AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT AUTHORIZING AND REQUIRING THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF LANCASTER AND CHESTERFIELD COUNTIES TO OPEN A CERTAIN PUBLIC ROAD.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That an Act approved March 18th, 1878, authorizing, empowering and requiring the County Commissioners of Lancaster and Chesterfield Counties to open a Public road. public road from B. F. Miller's, in Lancaster County, by White Plain, to William Moore's, in Chesterfield County, is hereby amended by striking out the word " require, " in the fifth line.
Approved December 18, 1879.
