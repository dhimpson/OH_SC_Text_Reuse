AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO CREATE A BOARD OF POLICE COMMISSIONERS FOR THE CITY OF GREENVILLE, IN THIS STATE,TO DEFINE ITS POWERS AND DUTIES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES,"" AP-PROVED 2D MARCH, A. D. 1899, BY PROVIDING FOR THEIR ELECTION BY THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS AND FIX THE TIME THEREFOR.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Act creating State of South Carolina, That Section 2.be stricken out and lice Commisinsert in lieu thereof the following:
" At the next regular election for the purpose of elecitng a Mayor and Aldermen for the City of Greenville there shall be elected by the qualified electors thereof five members of the Board of Police Commissioners, two of whom shall be elected for two years and three of whom shall be elected for four years, or until their successors shall be elected and qualify; at each subsequent regular city election successors to the members of said Board of Police Commissioners whose terms have expired shall be elected for a term of four years. "
SEC. 2. That Section 3 be amended to read as follows: " Section 3. That the members shall serve without compensation, and if any one of these, after being duly elected, shall refuse to qualify and serve on said Board, shall be subjected to a fine of twenty - five dollars, to be imposed by the Mayor and collected by the City Council. "
SEC. 3. That Section 4 be amended to read as follows:
" Section 4. No person shall be eligible to. election on said Board who is not a citizen of Greenville, and who has not resided in said city for one year preceding his election, nor shall any person be elected who at the time of his election holds any office of profit or trust under the national, State, municipal or County governments, except Notaries Public. "
SEC. 4. That said Act be further amended by striking out Section 5 and inserting the following therefor:
" Section 5. All vacancies in said Board caused by death, resignation, or otherwise, shall be filled by election by the City Council of Greenville until the succeeding regular election. "
SEC. 5. That Section 6 be amended so as to read as follows: " Section 6. That, at its first meeting, said Board shall proceed to elect from one of its own members a President, and also a Secretary, who shall keep the books of minutes provided for in this Act, and discharge such other clerical duties as may be required of him by the Board. ”
SEC. 6. That Section 8 be amended to read as follows:
" Section 8. That said Board shall meet not less than once every month, with the power of the President, upon request of two members of said Board, to call a meeting, and shall have their acts and doings recorded in a book to be kept for that purpose, which book shall be open to the inspection of all citi-zens, and shall make at least twice in each year a report of their A. D. 1907. operations to the City Council of Greenville. The City Council shall furnish said Board a permanent office, in the City Hall or elsewhere, and shall pay all of its legitimate expenses for stationery, printing, etc., upon vouchers signed by the Secretary, approved by the President. "
SEC. 7. That Section 9 be amended to read as follows:
" Section 9. That on or before the first Tuesday in October, Election 1907, and of each and every October thereafter, the City Counvates. cial of Greenville shall, by resolution or ordinance, fix the compensation and the number of officers and men of the police force of the city of Greenville for the ensuing year, the year to be reckoned from the first Tuesday in October until the first Tuesday of the following October of each and every year, and shall immediately transmit a certified account of their action to the Board of Police Commissioners. The Board of Police Commissioners shall, on the first Tuesday in October, 1907, and upon the first Tuesday in October in each and every year thereafter, proceed to elect by a majority viva voce vote the officers and privates provided for by the City Council, said officers and privates to be citizens of Greenville, to take the same oath now taken by the police of said city, and to hold their offices from that date, unless sooner removed by said Board. Said officers and privates shall be paid monthly by check, order or certificate upon the Mayor of said city (said check, order or certificate to be signed by the President and Secretary of the said Board), in accordance with the rate of compensation fixed by the City Council, and subject to such deductions for fines or lost time as may have been ordered by said Board. ”
SEC. 8. That Section 12 be amended to read as follows: " Section 12. That if any member of said Board shall become a candidate for, or shall accept or hold any office of profit or bers of Board. trust, either Federal, State, County or Municipal, except Notaries Public, such act shall at once operate as a forfeiture of his membership of said Board. It shall in such event be the duty of the Board, through its President and Secretary, to notify the City Council of any vacancy that may exist by reason of such forfeiture, or from any cause, and the City Council shall proceed to fill said vacancy as hereinbefore directed. "
