AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND EMPOWER THE SCHOOL TRUSTEES OF INDIAN FIELD SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 21, IN DORCHESTER COUNTY, AND OF SARDIS SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 12, IN FLORENCE COUNTY, TO CHARGE AND COLLECT A MATRICULATION FEE OF PUPILS ATTENDING THE INDIAN FIELD SCHOOL AND SARDIS SCHOOL.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That for the purpose of extending the length of the sessions of the Indian Fields school; and Sardis school, and increasing their usefulness, the School Trustees of Indian Fields School District Number Twenty-one, inDorchester County, and of Sardis School District No. 12, in Florlence County, are authorized and empowered to charge and collect a matriculation fee, not exceeding one dollar and fifty cents in each year, of each pupil attending said schools: Provided, nevertheless, That no child shall be debarred from attending said schools through the inability of his or her parents or guardians to pay said matriculation fee.
