AN ACT TO RECHARTER WHITE HALL FERRY, IN BEAUFORT No. 151. COUNTY, AND TO AMEND THE SAME.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That all the rights, privileges and immunities granted by the charter of White Hall Ferry, in Beaufort County, be, and the same are hereby, renewed for the the term of 17 years and vested in Peter Massey and York Heyward at the expiration of the term therein recited and granted; and that said Act, approved December 22, 1873, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out, on the tenth line, the words " ten cents " and insert the words " five cents. "
SEC. 2. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
Approved December 24, 1879.
