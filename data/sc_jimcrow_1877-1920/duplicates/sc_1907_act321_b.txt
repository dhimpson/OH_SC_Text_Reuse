AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED 'AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTAB-LISHMENT OF A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT IN LAURENS COUNTY, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A SCHOOL TAX THEREIN,'" APPROVED DECEMBER TWENTY - FOURTH, 1890, REGULATING THE ATTENDANCE OF CHILDREN AND WARDS OF NON - RESIDENTS OF THE CITY OF LAURENS.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the new school disState of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act torens County,
amend an Act to provide for the establishment of a new School District in Laurens County, and to authorize the levy and collection of a school tax therein, " approved December twentyfourth, 1890, be amended by striking out Section 8 thereof and inserting in lieu thereof the following:
Section 8. That the children and wards of non - residents of non - residents to said School District shall be required to pay such tuition and incidental fees as the Trustees of said School District may prescribe for the admission of the children and wards into said school: Provided, however, The said non - residents shall be entitled to a credit on said fees for the amount of the public school taxes paid by said non - residents, or by their children or wards, upon the property owned by them and located within the territorial limits of said School District.
SEC. 2. That this Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
