AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SUBMISSION TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF SPARTANBURG COUNTY THE QUESTION OF ISSUING ($200,000) TWO HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS, IN TWENTY AND THIRTY YEAR (4 PER CENT.) FOUR PER CENT. COUPON BONDS, FOR PERMANENT PUBLIC IMPROVEMENT.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the question of the issuing and bond issuett sale of ($ 200,000) two hundred thousand dollars ' worth of couSpartanburg pon bonds by Spartanburg County for permanent public road County. improvement, shall be submitted to the qualified electors of said County, at an election to be held for that purpose on the 20th day of August, 1907. The Commissioners of Election for said County shall appoint the Managers and receive and tabulate the returns as in other elections. They shall make their report in writing, in duplicate, one copy to be filed with the Clerk of the Court for Spartanburg County and the other with the Secretary of State, and said duplicate copies shall be kept on perpetual record in both of said offices.
SEC. 2. The Managers of said Election shall serve without compensation, and the Managers appointed shall have authority and power to appoint substitute Managers to act in their stead. SEC. 3. The nature of the coupon bonds voted upon shall be as follows: ($ 200,000) two hundred thousand dollars, in coupon bonds, to be issued in denominations to be fixed by the General Assembly hereafter, to bear interest from their date at the rate of four per cent. per annum, payable semi - annually on the 1st day of April and October in each year after the issue. One - half of said coupon bonds shall become due and payable twenty years after their date and the other one - half thirty years after their date.
SEC. 4. The County Commissioners of Spartanburg County are hereby authorized and required to furnish to the Election Commissioners of said County, in ample time before said election, a sufficient number of ballots, both for and against the issuing of the said coupon bonds. On one set of ballots shall be printed the words, " For Public Road Improvements Coupon Bonds, " and on the other set of ballots shall be printed the words, " Agaiust Public Road Improvement Coupon Bonds, " and the County Commissioners are authorized and required to pay the expenses of said printing out of the general County Funds.
SEC. 5. The said election shall, in all other respects, be held as Result of other elections under the general laws of this State, and the same regulations apply thereto in so far as is consistent with this Act. If the result of said election be declared by the Election Commissioners of said County to be against the issue of the coupon bonds, nothing further shall be done. If said election. shall be declared for the issue of the bonds, then the details in connection therewith shall be left to some succeeding session of the General Assembly of this State, but no coupon bonds shall be issued until the General Assembly shall hereafter prescribe the details of said issue, which shall not be prior to the 1st day of April, 1908. The proceeds arising from the sale of said coupon bonds shall be equally distributed among the townships in said County under a Commissioner to be provided therefor so as to give as many people as practicable benefit thereof. Approved the 15th day of February, A. D. 1907.
