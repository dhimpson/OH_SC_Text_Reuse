AN ACT TO EXTEND THE PROVISIONS OF SECTIONS 3, 4, 6, 7 AND 8 OF AN ACT ENTITLED " AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS TO SUBMIT TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF THEIR SEVERAL COUNTIES A PROPOSITION TO ALTER THE FENCE LAWS AND TO PROVIDE FOR EFFECTUATING THE SAME " TO THE UPPER PORTION OF UPPER TOWNSHIP, RICHLAND COUNTY, WITHOUT AN ELECTION.
A. D. 1879.
No. 110.

part of Rich-
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That the provisions of Sections 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8 of an Act entitled " An Act to authorize County Commissioners to submit to the qualified electors of their several Counties a proposition to alter the fence plied to upper laws and to provide for effectuating the same " shall apply without land County. an election to that portion of Upper Township, Richland County, adjoining Townships Nos. 9, 10 and 11 of Fairfield County, and separated from the lower part of said Upper Township, Richland County, by a line running from the mouth of Cedar Creek to a point near Mount Pleasant Church, and thence in a Northwesterly direction to the Fairfield line so as to include with Townships Nos. 9, 10 and 11, Fairfield County, the plantations of E. B. Turnipseed, H. E. Turnipseed, Anna Turnipseed, John H. Kinsler, J. H. Lever, E. Stanton, Jesse Taylor, R. B. Turnipseed, William McCants, N. J. Dubard, J. G. Lever, E. J. Lever, Mary A. Graddick, and all other plantations embraced within said line, shall, by mutual arrangement and agreement, build the necessary fence without tax or cost to the township.
SEC. 2. That this Act shall take effect and be in force from and after January 1st, 1880.
Approved December 23, 1879.
