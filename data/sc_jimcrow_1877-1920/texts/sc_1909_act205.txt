AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF ABBE-VILLE TO ISSUE ADDITIONAL BONDS AND LEVY A TAX FOR THE PAY-MENT OF SAID BONDS AND TO HAVE A SINKING FUND TO REDEEM THE SAME.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the board of trustees of the Abbeville school district of the town of Abbeville is hereby empowered trict on bond to submit to the qualified voters resident in said school disissue. trict at an election to be held for that purpose on petition of one - third of the qualified electors of said district, after at least ten days ' notice, the question of authorizing an issue of twenty thousand dollars of five per cent. bonds, payable in forty years. from the 1st day of July, 1909, to be of the denomination of five hundred dollars each and numbered from one to forty, inclusive. Said bonds to be redeemable at the option of the board of trustees after twenty years from the date of issuing, at the discretion of the board of trustees. Said board of trustees shall appoint the managers, receive the returns and declare the result of said election, and shall issue and sell said bonds in the event that said election shall result in favor thereof.

SEC. 3. That for the purpose of paying the interest on said bonds and the principal when due, it shall be the duty of the county auditor of the county of Abbeville, or such other officers as may be charged with the assessment of taxes, to compute annually the amount necessary to be raised, not exceeding two mills on the dollar, and to levy the same upon real estate and personal property in said school district, and the amount so A. D. 1909. levied shall be collected by the county treasurer of the county of Abbeville and held by him for the purpose of paying the said interest or the principal, if any be due, and for the purpose of providing a sinking fund for the ultimate retiring of said bonds, and shall be used for no other purpose whatsoever.

SEC. 4. That in case the election herein provided for should result in favor of issuing the said bonds, it shall be the duty of bonds. said trustees to issue and sell said bonds to the best advantage possible, and the funds derived from the sale of said bonds shall be used for the purpose of improving the present school property, acquiring additional property and erecting a building or buildings thereon for school purposes, and said funds shall not be ustu for any other purpose.
SEC. 5. That on or by the first day of March of the year 1910, and annually thereafter, it shall be the duty of the county by treasurer. treasurer to make a statement to the board of trustees of the school district of the town of Abbeville, showing the amount of money he has collected on said taxes, and shall turn over to said board of trustees any sum remaining in his hands after the payment of the interest due on said bonds, and it shall be the duty of the board of trustees as soon as practicable thereafter to invest said money in some safe security or securities to be approved by them for the benefit of the sinking fund provided for under the terms of this Act, and to keep the same invested as far as possible until the maturity of said bonds or until they shall be retired in accordance with law.
SEC. 6. That the board of trustees of the school district of the town of Abbeville are hereby authorized to turn over to fund. the sinking fund created by this Act and any surplus remaining in their hands after the full payment of the interest and principal of the issue bonds now outstanding.
SEC. 7. That the provisions of an Act entitled " An Act to provide for the establishment of a new school district in Abbeville county and to authorize the issuing of bonds by said school pealed. district and a local tax therein, " approved December 18, 1891. as far as not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, be, and are hereby, reenacted.
SEC. 8. That this Act shall be deemed a public Act and become of force immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
