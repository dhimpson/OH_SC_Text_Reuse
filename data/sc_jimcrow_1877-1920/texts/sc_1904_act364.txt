AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE VOORHEES INDUSTRIAL SCHOOL FOR COLORED YOUTHS.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Elizabeth E. Wright, Jessie C. chartered. Daisey, M. A. Menofee, of Bamberg County, R. C. Bedford, Beloil, Michigan, George W. Kelly, of Rockland, Massachusetts, and L. E. Stokes, of New York, and their associates, be. and they are hereby, declared a body politic and corporate under the name of the Voorhees Industrial School, and as such shall be entitled to all the rights and privileges of education and eleemosynary corporations as provided by law. That the capital stock of said corporation shall not exceed two hundred thousand ($ 200,000) dollars.
SEC. 2. That the number of Trustees shall not be less than six nor more than thirty, and their removal and election shall be controlled by such rules and by - laws as they may from time to time adopt, not inconsistent with the laws governing educational, industrial, eleemosynary corporations, and the said Voorhees Industrial School shall have the right and be authorized to receive under its care and tuition orphan children of the negro race, and bring them up and educate them and train them to at least one useful trade, and have control of said orphan children subject to the laws of South Carolina, with the right under its rules and by - laws to place them, or any of them, at other places or institutions to further carry out the education and training of said orphan children, or other children submitted to it as aforesaid.
SEC. 3. That this charter shall be perpetual under the name of Voorhees Industrial School, and subject at all times to the provisions granted by the Constitution and laws of this State. SEC. 4. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
