AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE AND PAY TO COL. M. P. TRIBBLE FIFTEEN HUNDRED DOLLARS FOR COMPILING CONFEDERATE RECORDS.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That fifteen hundred dollars be, and is hereby, appropriated for Col. M. P. Tribble for services rendered in compiling Confederate record, under appointment of his Excellency D. C. Heyward, pursuant to a provision in the " Legislation and Judicial Appropriation Act, " approved February 25, 1903, Part I., United States Statutes at Large, page 884, and the Comptroller General of this State be, and is hereby, authorized and required to draw his warrant in favor of Col. M. P. Tribble for fifteen hundred dollars, and the State Treasurer is hereby authorized and required to pay the same. Approved the 22d day of February, A. D. 1904.
