AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAW IN RELATION TO THE LOCATION AND NAMES OF VOTING PRECINCTS IN THIS STATE.
SEC. 1. Change of voting precincts in several Counties.
SEC. 2. Special Congressional box near Mount Pleasant.
SEC. 3. Change of registration certificates.
SEC. 4. Repealing clause.
SECTION 1. SEC. 2. That there shall be one new voting precinct established within the Seventh Congressional District at the brick Episcopal Church, on the Georgetown road, near Mount Pleasant, County of Berkeley, for the election of Presidential Electors and Congressman, and no voter not a resident of the town of Mount Voters shall vote for Presidential Electors or Congressman at the voting precinct in the town of Mount Pleasant.
SEC. 3. That the Supervisor of Registration is hereby authorized and directed to exchange the registration of such electors as may apply to him for that purpose, from other precincts to the precincts established by this Act, whenever it shall appear to him that the elector so applying resides within a reasonable distance of the same.
SEC. 4. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
