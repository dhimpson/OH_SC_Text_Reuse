AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE REDEMPTION OF THAT PART OF THE STATE DEBT KNOWN AS DEFICIENCY BONDS AND STOCKS BY THE ISSUE OF OTHER BONDS AND STOCKS, AND BY TEMPORARY LOAN, IF SUCH LOAN SHALL BECOME NECESSARY.

SEC. 2. Description of bonds.
SEC. 3. Registry of exchanges and reissue.
SEC. 4. Coupons receivable for all taxes except school taxes.
SEC. 5. Faith and credit of State pledged.
SEC. 6. New issue to be sold to highest bidder.
SEC. 7. Proceeds to be applied to unsurrendered deficiency securities.
SEC. 8. Appropriation to defray expenses.
SEC. 9. In absence of sale, loan to be made on deposit of bonds, & c. SEC. 10. Powers to Governor and State Treasurer.

SEC. 6. That on the 2d day of April, A. D. 1888, the State Treasurer shall ascertain the amount (face value) of such bonds and " Deficiency " bonds and certificates of stock, if any, which may remain outstanding and unexchanged, and he shall thereupon cause to be prepared for signing and sealing, in accordance with the provisions of Section 2 of this Act, an amount (face value) of the bonds in denominations of one thousand dollars and five hundred dollars of the class the issue of which is provided for in the said Section 2, and an amount (face value) in a certificate or certificates of stock of the class the issue of which is provided for in the said Section 2, (so arranging that said certificate or certificates shall be of the smallest amount possible,) which amount of bonds and certificates shall be equal to the amount of " Deficiency " bonds and certificates of stock ascertained as aforesaid to be then outstanding and unexchanged. That thereupon the said State Treasurer shall, by public advertisement for four weeks in one newspaper published in the cities of Columbia and Charleston, S. C., in the city of New York, N. Y., and in the city of London, England, respectively, offer to receive bids for the purchase in cash at not less than par of the entire lot of said bonds and certificates of stock hereinabove in this Section directed to be prepared for issue, said bids to be submitted to the State Treasurer at Columbia, S. C., on or before the twenty fifth day of May, A.D. 1888. That said bonds and certificates of stock shall be sold and delivered to the highest bidder or bidders therefor as aforesaid, and the same shall be paid for in cash on or before the fifteenth day of June, A. D. 1888, and should the same not be so paid for on or before the date last named, the said bid or bids shall forthwith be rejected by the State Treasurer. That the said bonds and certificates of stock shall be signed and sealed, ready for issue, in exact accordance with the provisions of Section 2 of this Act; but said signing and sealing shall be made and done at the time of the settlement with the State Treasurer for the said purchase, if a sale be made as aforesaid, and not before.
SEC. 7. That if said bonds and certificates of stock the sale of which is hereinabove authorized are sold and delivered by the State Treasurer in accordance with the provisions of Section 6 of this Act, the proceeds of the sale shall be kept separate and apart from the other cash funds of the State, and shall be used exclusively for the redemption of such of said " Deficiency " bonds and stocks as shall be presented for payment on and after their date of maturity, and the bonds and stocks so redeemed shall at the time of such redemption be cancelled and filed as provided in Section 3 hereof.
SEC. 8. That for the purpose of defraying the expenses to be incurred in carrying out the provisions of this Act the sum of three thousand dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, to be drawn on the warrant of the Comptroller General, to be approved by the Governor.
SEC. 9. That in the event that the State Treasurer shall, as he is hereinabove directed to do, offer to receive bids for said be used, as bonds and certificates of stock, and shall not receive therefor such a bid as he is authorized to accept, then he is hereby authorized and required with proper dispatch to cause to be signed and sealed as provided for in Section 2 hereof a sufficient amount of said bonds as may be necessary for the purpose of the pledge hereinbelow authorized to be made. That thereupon the Governor and the State Treasurer shall negotiate in the name of the State a loan at a rate of interest not to exceed seven per centum per annum for the period of eight months of an amount in cash sufficient to redeem on the first day of July, A. D. 1888, the then unexchanged outstanding " Deficiency " bonds and certificates of stock. That as a collateral security for the payment of the said loan the Governor and the State Treasurer are hereby authorized to lodge and pledge the said bonds received by them from the State Treasurer, provided they shall be accepted as such collateral security at a valuation not less than ninety cents on the dollar, and taken at said valuation the amount of such bonds to be pledged shall not exceed the sum of money which may be borrowed under the provisions of this Section.
SEC. 10. That the Governor and the State Treasurer are hereby authorized in their discretion to perform all and singular every act necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act not herein specifically given and which are not inconsistent with the provisions thereof.
SEC. 11. That said bonds and stocks shall be free from all Exempt from State, County or municipal taxes whatever.
SEC. 12. That if any officer of this State upon whom any duty is devolved under the provisions of this Act shall willfully neglect or refuse to perform said duty he shall be subject to indictment for a misdemeanor, and upon conviction thereof shall be fined in a sum not less than two thousand dollars, nor more than ten thousand dollars, at the discretion of the Court.
SEC. 13. That this Act shall take effect from and immediately after the date of its approval.
