AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE TOWN OF TIRZAH, IN YORK COUNTY.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That from and after the passage of this Act, all citizens of the United States and of this State, who have resided one year in this State, and sixty days in the town of Tirzah, in York County, shall be deemed, and are hereby declared to be, a body politic and corporate, and the said town shall be called and known by the name of Tirzah, and its corporate limits shall be Name and limits. one square mile with the Charleston, Cincinnati, and Chicago Depot in the centre thereof.
SEC. 2. That the said town of Tirzah shall have all the rights. powers, and privileges, and be subject to all the provisions and limitations enumerated and contained in an Act entitled " An Act to provide for and regulate the incorporation of towns of 19 Stat., 174. less than one thousand inhabitants in this State, " approved 23d December, A. D. 1885, except where the same may be modified by this Act.
SEC. 3. The Town Council of said town of Tirzah is hereby Sale of liquors prohibited from granting any license for the sale of spirituous, prohibited. malt, or intoxicating liquors in said town.
SEC. 4. This Act shall go into effect from the date of its approval and shall continue in force for a term of thirty years.
