AN ACT TO REGULATE THE DRAWING AND TERM OF SERVICE OF JURORS IN THE COUNTY OF SUMTER.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Jurors to be for the first or Spring and Fall terms of the Court of Common Pleas and General Sessions for the County of Sumter thirty - six jurors shall be drawn for each term, in the manner provided by law, to serve for the first week of each term, and a like number shall be so drawn to serve for the remainder of said terms respectively, and separate writs of venire shall issue for the jurors drawn as aforesaid: Provided, That whenever a jury shall be charged with a case such jury shall not be discharged by reason of anyTo serve until thing in this Act contained until a verdict shall have been found or a mistrial ordered in such case.
SEC. 2. That Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Repealing Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed to the extent of such inconsistency.
