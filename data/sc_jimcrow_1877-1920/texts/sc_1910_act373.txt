AN ACT MAKING IT A MISDEMEANOR FOR ANY PERSON TO DISPOSE OF THE CUSTODY OF A WHITE CHILD TO A NEGRO.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the MisdemeaState of South Carolina, That from and after the approval of nor to give perthis Act by the Governor it shall be unlawful for any parent,or relative or other white person in this State, having the control white child. or custody of any white child, by right of guardianship, natural § or acquired or otherwise, to dispose of, give or surrender such white child permanently into the custody, control, maintenance or support of a negro.
SEC. 2. Any person violating the provisions of Section 1 of this Act shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor, and on conviction shall be fined or imprisoned in the discretion of the presiding Judge: Provided, The provisions of this Act shall not be construed so as to prevent the offices of a negro in the family of any white person as a nurse.
