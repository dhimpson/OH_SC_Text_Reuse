AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND DIRECT THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF SPARTANBURG COUNTY TO OPEN AND CONSTRUCT A PUBLIC HIGHWAY FROM A POINT ON THE WHITE PLAINS ROAD NEAR BETHESBA CHURCH TO THE PUBLIC ROAD LEADING FROM TROUGH SHOALS TO SPARTANBURG COURT HOUSE.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Road to be the County Commissioners of Spartanburg County be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to open and construct a public road commencing at a point on the White Plains road Route. near Bethesba Church and about one - half of a mile from the Crockerford Bridge on Pacolet River; thence in a Southeastern direction, following the general course of the old road, through the lands of Miss R. F. Crocker, estate of Fulton Broon and lands of J. M. Lee, to the public highway leading from Trough Shoals to Spartanburg Court House, and intersecting said highway at a point about one - half mile above Pacolet Mills in said County. And for the purpose of carrying into effect the proRights and visions of this Act said County Commissioners are hereby invested with all the rights, powers and privileges of an Act entitled " An Act to further empower County Commissioners to open and 20 Stat., 282. establish public highways, " approved 17th December, 1889.
SEC. 2. That said County Commissioners are hereby further Expenditure authorized and directed, for the purpose of opening and constructing said public road, to expend such sum of money as in their judgment is necessary to complete said road and the bridges thereon.
