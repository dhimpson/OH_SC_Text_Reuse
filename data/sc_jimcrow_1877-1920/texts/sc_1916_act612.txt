AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND EMPOWER WINYAH INDIGO SCHOOL DISTRICT OF GEORGETOWN COUNTY TO ISSUE COUPON BONDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF ALTERING, ENLARGING AND EQUIPPING BUILDINGS FOR THE PUBLIC SCHOOLS OF SAID DISTRICT, AND TO PROVIDE FOR THE PAYMENT OF THE SAME.
Section 1. Bond Issue for School Purposes Authorized—Election Prerequisite——Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That Winyah Indigo School District, of Georgetown County, is hereby authorized and empowered to issue coupon bonds to the amount of fifteen thousand < dollars, or so much thereof as may, in the judgment of the Board of Trustees of said district, be necessary, for the purpose of altering, enlarging and equipping any existing public school building or buildings in the city of Georgetown, in said district: Provided, That the question of issuing said bonds shall first be determined at a special election to be held for that purpose, as hereinafter provided, in which a majority of the qualified electors voting thereat shall vote in favor of such issue.
§ 2. Election—Notice—Ballot—That upon the written petition of one-fourth of the resident freeholders of said school district, filed with the said Trustees, they shall make an order for the holding of such an election, fixing the time and place for holding the same, appointing the managers (who shall be three resident freeholders and qualified electors of said district), and making At such all other proper provisions for the conduct thereof. At such
election only duly qualified electors residing in said district shall be entitled to vote. The said Trustees shall publish, at least twenty days prior to the holding of said election, in one or more newspapers published in the city of Georgetown, due notice of the time and place of holding same, and of the objects thereof, in which shall be specified the amount of bonds proposed to be issued, the time when the same shall become payable and the rate of interest thereon. The Trustees shall have printed, for the use of the voters in said election, an equal number of ballots for and against said issue, on one set of which shall be printed the words, “For the issuing of bonds,” and on the other set the words, “Against the issuing of bonds,” and a sufficient number of such ballots shall be placed at the following place or places in said district on the day of election. The said managers shall conduct the election, and at the close thereof shall count the votes and make a return of the result thereof in writing to the Board of School Trustees aforesaid. The said Trustees shall canvass the returns and declare the result; and if a majority of the ballots cast at such election shall be in favor of the issue of said bonds, the said Board of Trustees shall proceed to issue and dispose of the same as hereinafter provided. § 3. Bonds—Denominations—Interest—Are Lien.—That the said bonds shall be of such denominations as may be determined by the said Trustees, shall bear interest at a rate not exceeding five per centum per annum, payable either annually or semiannually, and the principal thereof shall be due and payable thirty years from and after the date thereof with the right of redemption after twenty years from said date. Both principal and interest of said bonds shall be payable in any legal tender money of the United States. Each of said bonds and interest coupons attached thereto shall be signed by the Chairman of the Board of Trustees of said school district, and countersigned by the Secretary of said Board: Provided, That the signature of said officers may be lithographed in facsimile upon the bonds and coupons, and such lithographing shall be a sufficient proof of the execution thereof under the terms of this Act. The said bonds when duly issued in conformity to the provisions of this Act, shall, in the hands of any bona fide holder for value, constitute a lien upon all property altered, enlarged and equipped with the proceeds of the sale thereof. § 4. Sale - Deposit - Disbursement - Bond - Duties. - That the said bonds shall be sold by the Board of Trustees at either pub-
lic or private sale, as in their judgment may be best; but no such sale shall be made at less than par and accrued interest. The proceeds of such sale shall be paid over by the said Board of Trustees to the County Treasurer of the County of Georgetown, who shall keep a separate account thereof and shall hold the same subject to the orders of the said Board, and pay them out upon warrants duly issued by said Board in like manner as other public school funds belonging to said district. The County Treasurer shall be liable upon his official bond to the said school district, or to any party interested, for the nonperformance of his duty in respect to said fund in the same manner and to the same extent and under like penalty as to nonperformance of his duties in reference to other public moneys entrusted to his keeping: Provided, however, That the said Board of Trustees may, in their discretion deposit the said funds in the usual depository for State or County funds, in the name of the said school district, subject to their own order, and disburse the same directly by check or draft thereon, signed by the Chairman and countersigned by the Secretary of the Board: Provided, further, That before the said Board shall exercise the power of deposit and direct disbursement of said funds herein granted to it, each of said Trustees shall enter into bond in the sum of two thousand five hundred dollars with security, to be approved by the County Board of Commissioners, conditioned for the faithful performance of their official duties. The premium, which may be paid to any surety or guarantee company for “i furnishing said official bonds, and all other expenses which may be in the issue and sale of the bonds authorized by this Act, shall be paid by the said Trustees from the proceeds of the sale of such bonds or from any other fund of said school district. And the said Board of Trustees shall apply and expend all of said funds exclusively for the purposes provided in this Act. § 5. Exempt from Taxation.—That the said bonds and the coupons thereto attached shall be exempt from taxation in this State. § 6. Disposition of Surplus.—That, if there shall remain any surplus funds from the proceeds of the sale of the said bonds after carrying out the purposes of this Act, and the payment of all expenses incident thereto, the same shall be covered into the county Treasury and shall constitute a part of the sinking
fund to be created, as hereinafter provided, for the retirement of the said bonds. § 7. Tax Levy—Sinking Fund.—That for the purpose of paying annual interest upon the said bonds, and of establishing a sinking fund for the retirement of the same at maturity or at the period of redemption, it shall be the duty of the County Auditor or of such other officers as may be charged by law with the levy and assessment of taxes, to levy annually such a tax upori all the taxable real and personal property in the said school district as shall be sufficient to meet the said interest, and also to raise a sum equal to one-fortieth (1-40) of the principal of said bonds, to be held as a sinking fund for that purpose. The amount so levied shall be collected by the County Treasurer of said County in like manner as State, County and school taxes are collected, and shall be applied by him solely to the payment of the said interest or of the principal of said bonds, respectively, and shall be used for no other purpose whatsoever. § 8. Place of Deposit—Redemption.—That it shall be the duty of the County Treasurer to place all monies belonging to the sinking funds hereinbefore provided for on deposit in some savings bank in this State, to be designated in writing jointly by the said Board of Trustees and the County Auditor, and all interest accruing thereon shall be likewise kept on deposit in such savings bank. That when the right of redemption shall arise, at the expiration of the period of twenty years, as fixed by this Act, the said County Treasurer shall be authorized and he is hereby directed, at the written request of the said Board of Trustees, through its Chairman and Secretary, to redeem the said bonds, or so many thereof as the sinking fund may be sufficient to pay; and the remainder of said issue, if any, may be redeemed from time to time thereafter at the option of said Board of Trustees, in such amounts as may be deemed expedient by them, and the Board may designate the bonds to be so 7 redeemed. § 9. Inconsistent Acts Repealed—That this Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval; and all other Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed.
