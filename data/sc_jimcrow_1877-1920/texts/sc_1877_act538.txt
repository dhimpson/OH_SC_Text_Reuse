AN ACT TO CHARTER THE SPARTANBURG AND AUGUSTA RAILROAD COMPANY
Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That for the purpose of building a railroad from the town of Spartanburg, in this State, to the city of Augusta, in the State of Georgia, by the way of Laurens Court House, in the County of Laurens, Ninety-six, in the County of Abbeville, and Edgefield Court House, in the County of Edgefield, the Counties along the line of said railroad and such other Counties in this State as may avail themselves, in the manner hereinafter provided, of the privileges of this Act, may issue bonds payable twelve years from date, with annual interest of not more than seven per cent., payable semi-annually, for such amount as by a vote of said Counties respectively may be determined. And for the purpose of redeeming said bonds the County Commissioners of the respective Counties shall, and are hereby required to, levy an annual tax on the taxable property within their Counties of at least sufficient to pay the annual interest and five per cent. of the principal of the bonds issued.
Sec. 2. That when not less than two hundred taxpayers of any County (except Abbeville) through which this railroad is to be built shall apply by written petition to the County Commissioners, then the said County Commissioners shall, after giving a notice of at least thirty days, submit to a vote of the County whether or not. said County will issue bonds as herein provided, and for what amount; and for the purpose of conducting said election the County Commissioners shall appoint three Managers for each polling place in the County, who shall serve without compensation and shall make their returns of said election to the County Commissioners of said County, who shall declare the result of said election; and any County may make an additional issue of bonds when authorized as this Section requires: Provided, That no-township or County shall subscribe to any railroad unless a majority of all the voters of such township or County shall have voted in favor of such subscription, the majority tv be determined by the votes at the last general election: Provided, further, That nothing in this Act shall apply to the townships of Rion and Washington, in the County of Edgefield. Sec. 3. That upon one or more of the Counties having by the vote aforesaid authorized the issue of not less than fifty thousand dollars in bonds for the purpose aforesaid, the County Commissioners of each of the Counties so subscribing shall, after thirty (30) days’ notice, order an election to be held in their respective Counties, said election to be held after the manner heretofore provided in Section 2 of this Act, for the purpose of choosing delegates to a convention to be ordered by the County Commissioners of the County authorizing the largest issue of bonds; each County shall be entitled to five delegates for each twenty-five thousand dollars of bonds authorized. Said convention shall assemble at the time and place ordered by said County Commissioners and shall proceed to elect a President and nine Directors, each County being entitled to at least one Director. The election of the President and Directors aforesaid shall be deemed and taken to be an organization of the company, and they are hereby declared to be a body politic and corporate under the name and style of the Spartanburg and Augusta Railroad Company; and said company shall have power to receive, own and transfer real and personal property, to have and use a common seal, and to pass such by-laws, not inconsistent with the Constitution and laws of the State, as may be necessary to carry out the object of the corporation; may sue and be sued, plead and be impleaded, answer and be answered, defend and be defended, in
Courts of record and any other place whatsoever; to have perpetual succession; to have and to exercise all the rights and privileges of all railroad corporations of a similar kind now existing, except the right to interfere with the chartered rights and privileges of such corporations relative to the building and construction of roads. Sec. 4. That in addition to the rights, privileges and franchises conferred upon this company by this Act, they shall also have authority and power, and they are hereby authorized and empowered, to build and construct a railroad from the town of Spartanburg, in this State, to the city of Augusta, in the State of Georgia, by the way of Laurens Court House, in the County of Laurens, Ninety-Six, in the County of Abbeville, and Edgefield Court House, in the County of Edgefield. Sec. 5. That upon the organization of the company as aforesaid the, President and Directors shall determine whether the amount of bonds directed and authorized to be issued by the County or Counties will warrant the commencement of the enterprise, and if they deem the amount sufficient they shall thereupon give written notice of their action to the County Commissioners of each of the Counties subscribing; and the County Commissioners of each of the Counties subscribing shall issue and turn over to the President and Directors the amount of bonds authorized by their respective Counties, which said bonds shall be issued for the purpose of raising the money for the construction of said road, by sale or otherwise, as the.President and Directors may determine; and to secure the payment of the said bonds, the road, as it is built, with its rolling stock and other property, shall stand pledged, and the said bonds shall have a first lien thereon, which may be enforced through the Courts of this State as in case of mortgages. Sec. 6. The annual tax authorized under the provisions of this Act shall be known as the Spartanburg and Augusta Railroad tax in each County, and shall be collected by the County Treasurers of their respective Counties at the’ same time and under the same regulations and penalties as the State and County taxes are collected therein; and upon the payment of the said railroad tax, the County Treasurers shall give a special receipt, which shall specify the amount of such payment of the taxpayer, and that it ia for the railroad tax; and whenever any taxpayer or holder of such special receipt shall have the sum of twenty-five dollars or more, the taxpayer or holder shall, upon presentation of such receipt or receipts to the proper officer of said railroad company, be entitled to receive the stock of said railroad company at the rate of one share for
each twenty-five dollars: Provided, That nothing herein contained shall authorize said railroad company to issue any share for a less amount than twenty-five dollars; and upon said receipt being converted into stock, the said taxpayer or holder shall become a stockholder to the extent of his said stock; and upon any taxpayer subscribing for and taking stock in said company, he shall be entitled to credit his annual railroad tax on his said stock until the same is exhausted; and the said’ County Treasurer is hereby required, upon the presentation of the certificate of stock of any taxpayer, to credit thereon his annual railroad tax from year to year until the said stock is exhausted. Sec. 7. The County Treasurer shall, within one month of the closing of the tax books of his County, turn over to the Treasurer of said railroad company all taxes collected by him on account of said railroad tax; and on his failure so to do shall be liable to the same penalties that attach to him as County Treasurer under similar circumstances; and the Treasurer of said railroad company shall immediately, under the direction of said company, apply said tax to the payment of the annual interest of said bonds and such proportion of the principal as may be redeemed. Sec. 8. The President and Directors may sell shares in said company, not exceeding one hundred thousand dollars, at twentyfive dollars per share, to be paid in labor and materials, upon such terms as the said President and Directors may determine; and any taxpayer taking stuck in this way may have his annual tax credited thereon as above provided in case of other subscribers; and that the said President and Directors may in their discretion receive subscriptions in unencumbered real estate at the assessed valuation upon the Auditor’s books of the County where such real estate is situate, the subscriber furnishing said railroad company with a plat of said real estate with the understanding that the person subscribing shall retain possession of euch real estate until the completion of said railroad. At the completion of said railroad any person so subscribing real estate shall have the right to redeem said subscription at the valuation at which it was subscribed, and upon failure so to do the said railroad company shall sell said real estate and pay over to the subscriber thereof the excess, if any, of the amount for which it is sold above the valuation at which said estate was received. The person so subscribing real estate shall pay the annual railroad tax thereon so long as he retains the possession thereof, but upon the sale or redemption thereof he shall be entitled to all annual credits of his said taxes on the amount of
his subscription then paid until the same is exhausted as above provided in other cases of subscription. Sec. 9. There shall be at least one annual meeting of said company, to be held at such place as may be designated by the Board of Directors, and such call meetings as the President and Directors may determine; the meetings shall be composed of delegates from each County, at least two for each twenty-five thousand dollars in bonds said County may have outstanding at the time, and such other parties as may have become stockholders by having their tax receipts converted into stock as herein above provided or by subscription. The members of the meeting shall vote according to the following scale, the delegates representing the County bonds voting by majority. Sec. 10. After the organization as herein provided, the officers of said company shall be elected at the annual conventions, and the salaries of the said officers fixed by said convention from time to time as the interest of the company may require. The President and Directors shall make a full report at least twice a year, and publish the same in one County paper in each County acting under this Act, which report shall contain the amount of bonds received, how expended, and the amount of shares sold. Sec. 12. Upon the organization of said railroad company as provided in Section 3 of this Act, the President and Directors may have surveys made of the different routes between the points named in Section 4 of this Act and upon said surveys may locate the line; and to meet the expense of said surveys the amount thereof shall be by the President and Directors proportioned between the said Counties and be collected as the first levy of the tax herein provided for. Sec. 13. That the County Commissioners of Abbeville shall not submit to the voters of said County the question of subscription to the capital stock of said company except upon the written application of one thousand freeholders. Sec. 14. That it shall be lawful for the township of Ninety-Six, in Abbeville County, to subscribe to the capital stock of said company such sum as a majority of the voters of said township may direct and authorize by an election held according to provisions of this Act. And the township of Ninety-Six is hereby created a body corporate and politic for the purposes of this Act.
