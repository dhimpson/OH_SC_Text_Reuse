Election—Rules Of.—Said election shall be held in said townships at the time above set forth in this Act, at which election all qualified electors shall be entitled to vote. The polls shall be opened at eight o’clock a. m. and closed at four o’clock p.m. Three weeks’ notice shall be given prior to said election to be held in each of said townships, by posting the same in three public places in the said townships, provided that where a newspaper is published in any of said townships said notice may be published in such newspaper for such township, said notice shall state the amount of bonds to be voted. The said County Board of Commissioners shall appoint three managers of said elections for each precinct in each of said townships, who shall be qualified electors, and said County Board of Commissioners shall prepare the ballots on which shall be printed “For Bonds” and “Against Bonds,” respectively, a sufficient number of each to be placed at each voting precinct. If a majority of the votes cast in any of said townships be “For Bonds” the bonds shall be issued for such township or townships voting for the issue of same, as hereinafter set forth.: § 3. When the proceeds of said bonds as may be issued under the provisions of this Act are received by the said Board of Public Works of such township as may issue bonds hereunder, the said Board of Public Works shall deposit such proceeds in the bank or banks of the township issuing said bonds, and in the event there is more than one bank in such township the said proceeds shall be deposited in equal amounts in such banks. In the event there is no bank in a township voting for the issue of bonds hereunder, the Board of Public Works of such township shall deposit the proceeds of such bonds in such banks as they may deem best; said Board of Public Works shall deposit said funds at not less than three per cent. § 4. Sale of Bonds.——The Board of Public Works of such township as may vote to issue bonds hereunder may sell all of said issue before proceeding with the road work for which said bonds are issued or said Board of Public Works may sell said bonds as the funds may be needed: Provided, Said bonds shall not be sold for less than par. Said bonds shall be sold upon at least two weeks’ advertisement in some newspaper published in the county of Chesterfield for competitive bids, and said Board of Public Works shall have the right to reject any and all bids. § 5. For Road Improvement Only.—That the proceeds of the bonds that any of said townships may issue hereunder shall be issued exclusively for road improvement in building and maintaining roads in such townships as may issue bonds hereunder, respectively, including actual expense incident to the carrying on of the work of the said Board: Provided, however, That sufficient funds shall be appropriated by the said Board from the proceeds of the sale of the first bonds, or from the sale of all the bonds, to pay the interest for the first ear on such bonds

In the event that a regular tax levy is made to pay the interest for the first year, such appropriation shall not be necessary to pay said interest. § 6. Tax—Sinking Fund.—That the Auditor of Chesterfield county shall cause a special tax to be levied annually upon all the property, real and personal, within the limits and boundary of such township as shall issue bonds under the provisions of this Act sufficient to pay the annual interest occurring on said bonds as they may be sold, and also to provide for a sinking fund which shall be sufficient to retire all bonds at maturity, and that the funds derived from said tax shall be turned over to the Board of Public Works of such township as may issue bonds under this Act as same is collected. That the sinking fund herein provided for shall be deposited with some banking institution or institutions in the county of Chesterfield at the best rate of interest obtainable. § 7. Board of Public Works—Term of Office—Vacancy.— That the Board of Public Works of such township-or townships as may vote to issue bonds hereunder, respectively, shall consist of three elective members, whose term of office shall be six years, and until his successor is duly elected and qualified, one member of such Board, after the first election herein provided for, to be elected every two years at an election to be held in said township upon at least two weeks’ notice. In case of a vacancy caused by death, resignation or removal or otherwise of a member of such Board, the Commissioners of Election of Chesterfield county shall call a special election at such time as may be deemed best by the said Commissioners, but within sixty days from the time such vacancy occurs, for the purpose of electing a member to fill such vacancy: Provided, That if the vacancy occurs within five months of the expiration of the term the Commissioners of Election may postpone said election until the regular time for electing a member, if, after consultation with the remaining members of the said Board of Public Works of such township, it appears that such postponement will not interfere with the proper administration of the affairs of such Board. At the election herein provided for one member of such Board shall be chosen for a full term of six years, a second member for four years, and the third member for two years, and shall hold office until their successors are duly elected and qualified,

Engineer—Salary—Location of Roads, Etc.—Tax.—That the said Board of Public Works of such townships as may issue bonds hereunder shall have power to employ an engineer at such salary and upon such terms as may be deemed proper, and shall have full authority to determine the location of roads to be built or of portions of roads which are to be improved, to determine the width and character of same, and shall also have power and the right to institute condemnation proceeding to obtain rights of way and road material, just as the County Board of Commissioners of said county are empowered to do under the law, and such Board or Boards may contract for building, improvement, maintenance or repair of roads; may purchase or lease machinery for use of road work, or do any and all things expedient or proper in the development and maintenance of roads in such townships as may issue bonds hereunder, respectively. The Auditor of Chesterfield county shall annually levy a tax of one mill on all the property of such township or townships, respectively, except the township of Cheraw, as may vote bonds under the provisions of this Act. The proceeds of said one mill tax levy shall be used by the Board of Public Works of such township exclusively for the upkeep and maintenance of such roads as may be built as a result of the issue of bonds, and said Board shall let out by contract to some competent person by sections the work of keeping in repair such roads, such contract to be let to the lowest responsible bidder, and such contractor shall be provided with a split log drag, or some similar device, and shall drag or work said road at least once per month in a satisfactory manner, same to be done under the supervision of said Board of Public Works. § 9.
