AN ACT RELATING TO THE APPOINTMENT OF SCHOOL TRUSTEES IN MARION COUNTY AND TO FIX THEIR TERM OF OFFICE.
Section 1. Appointment and Term of Office of School Trustees in Marion County—Proviso.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That the County Board of Education of Marion county shall, after the expiration of the term of office of the present School Trustees of the various school districts of Marion county, appoint three Trustees from each of said districts, whose terms of office shall be one, two and three years, respectively, the said County Board of Education to designate which Trustee shall serve for one year, which for two years, and which for three years, and thereafter annually during the month of June shall appoint one Trustee of each of the various sehool districts, who shall hold cffice for three years, so that the Board of Trustees of each district as thus constituted shall always have at least two experienced members: Provided, The provisions of this Act shall not apply to special and graded school districts created by special Acts.
§ 2. Vacancies.—In case of a vacancy on the Board of Trustees of any school district subject to the provisions of this Act, the same shall be filled by appointment by the County Board of Education for the unexpired term.
§ 3. Inconsistent Acts Repealed.—All Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
