AN ACT TO PROHIBIT THE UNLAWFUL MANUFACTURING, SELLING, USING OR PROCURING OF CONFEDERATE CROSSES OF HONOR, AND TO PROVIDE A PENALTY FOR SAME.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the wear or sell State of South Carolina, That it shall be unlawful for any perCrosses of Honson to wear as a badge, or expose upon their person, or to sell, or offer for sale, or otherwise dispose of or use, or to manufacture, any badge or insignia known as the Confederate Cross of Honor, in any other way than that prescribed by the rules and regulations made and adopted by the United Daughters of the Confederacy.
SEC. 2. That any violation of the provision of this Act shall be punished by a fine of not less than one hundred ($ 100.00) dollars nor more than five hundred ($ 500.00) dollars, or be imprisoned for not less than twelve (12) months nor more than five (5) years.
