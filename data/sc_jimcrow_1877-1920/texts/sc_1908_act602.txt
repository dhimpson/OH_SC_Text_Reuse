AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION 3 OF AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF LAURENS TO ISSUE BONDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF ERECTING OR REPAIRING A SCHOOL BUILDING AND EQUIPPING THE SAME, AND TO REFUND BONDS HERETOFORE ISSUED,"" APPROVED THE 19TH DAY OF FEBRUARY, A. D. 1907, SO THAT THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF LAURENS MAY USE THE PROCEEDS FROM THE SALE OF BONDS HERETOFORE ISSUED UNDER THE SAID ACT FOR THE PURCHASE OF A NEW LOT, AND ERECTING A BUILDING THEREON.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Section 3 of an Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Board of Trustees of the School District of the Town of Laurens to issue bonds for the purpose of erecting or repairing a school building and equipping the same and to refund bonds heretofore issued,” approved the 19th day of February, A. D. 1907, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out all of said Section and inserting in lieu thereof the following, which shall be known as Section 3:
Section 3. That the proceeds realized from the sale of the bonds already, or that may be hereafter issued hereunder shall be used by the said Board of Trustees in erecting a suitable school building or buildings upon any lot or lots now belonging to said school district or which may be hereafter purchased by said Board of Trustees of said school district, and in providing suitable furniture and apparatus for the same The said Trustees are hereby authorized to sell or dispose of any lot or lots they may now own. This Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
