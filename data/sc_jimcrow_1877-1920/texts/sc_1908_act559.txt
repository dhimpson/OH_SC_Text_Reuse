AN ACT TO RAISE SUPPLIES AND MAKE APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING 1908.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That a tax of five and one - half (51-2) for Sta te mills, exclusive of the public school tax hereinafter provided for, upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property of this State, be, and the same is hereby, levied for the purpose of meeting appropriations to defray the current expenses of the government for the fiscal year beginning January the 1st, 1908, and to meet such other indebtedness as has been, or shall be, provided for in the several Acts and Joint Resolutions passed by this General Assembly at the session of 1908, providing for the same.
