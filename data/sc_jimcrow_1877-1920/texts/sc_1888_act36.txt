AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED "AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE CHERAW AND CAMDEN SHORT LINE RAILROAD COMPANY," APPROVED DECEMBER 24TH, 1885
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That A. A. 1885, 25, Section five of said Act be amended by inserting after the word amended. " years " on the sixth line, the words " from the passage of this Act. " So that said Section, when amended, shall read as follows:
SECTION 5. This Act shall be deemed and taken to be a public Act, and shall continue in force for thirty years: Provided, That work under the same shall be commenced and the road graded from Hamlet in North Carolina to Camden in South Carolina within three years from its passage, and that the same be completed to Camden in five years from the passage of this Act; otherwise this charter shall be null and void.
SEC. 2. That any County, city, or town may aid in the construction of said road, by exchanging its bonds for a like amount of the first or second mortgage bonds of the said railroad company, said bonds to be payable in thirty years from date of issue.
SEC. 3. That for the purpose of determining the amount of such aid it shall be the duty of the County Commissioners of the County proposing to make the same, or the proper authorities of the city or town proposing to extend aid, upon the application of a majority of the freehold voters owning real estate in such County, town, or city, specifying the amount of such aid therein, to submit to the qualified voters of such County, city, or town, as the case may be, the question of aid or no aid to said company; that said County Commissioners, or the proper authorities of such town or city, as the case may be, shall have power to order an election, specifying the time, place, and purpose of the election, and to appoint three managers at each election precinct, who shall, without compensation, hold and Form of ballots. conduct said election; at which election the ballots shall have written or printed thereon either the word " Aid " or the words " No Aid, " the said County Commissioners, or the proper authorities of said city or town, having first by resolution fixed the amount of aid according to the request of the commission Notice of elecsubmitted to them. Notice of such resolution shall be given in one newspaper published in the County, city, or town proposing to aid for six weeks next previous to the election, and at such election only those voters of the said County, city, or town, as the case may be, who are qualified electors therein shall be entitled to vote, provided that registration shall not be required as a qualification for voting at such election. It shall be the duty of the managers to count the votes at their respective preCount of the cincts and make their returns to the County Commissioners or the authorities of said town or city, as the case may be, who shall receive and tabulate the same and declare the result, Declaration of which result shall be certified to as correct by said County Commissioners, or city or town authorities, and filed in their respective offices. Contests in such elections shall be heard and determined by the County Commissioners or city or town authorities, as the case may be. In case a majority of the ballots cast at such election shall have written or printed thereon " Aid, " Exchange of then the Chairman of the Board of County Commissioners, or the proper city or town authorities, shall be authorized and required to exchange of the County, city, or town bonds the sum which may have been fixed and named in the resolution of the said Board for a like amount of the first or second mortgage bonds of said railroad company, which exchange shall be made in six per cent. coupon bonds, as hereinbefore provided, with interest payable annually. SEC. 5. That said railroad company shall have full power and authority to connect with or cross over all other railroads on its Rights of way. proposed line, and shall have every right, privilege, and power necessary for the purpose of acquiring such lands or rights of way as may be required for the location or construction by any route the directors of said railroad may agree upon, or for the erection or location of depots, warehouses, stations, or other necessary establishments, or for extending or altering the same, and the said railroad shall have the benefit of every process or proceedings, and be subject to all the restrictions now provided by law in such cases.
SEC. 6. That the said company is authorized to purchase, lease, and consolidate with any railroad company now existing, or which may hereafter exist, under the laws of this State, or any other State, with which its lines may connect.
SEC. 7. That said company shall have power to mortgage all its franchises and property.
SEC. 8. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
