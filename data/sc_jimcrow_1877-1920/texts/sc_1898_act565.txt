AN ACT TO ALTER THE COUNTY LINE OF GREENWOOD COUNTY SO AS TO INCLUDE PORTIONS OF ABBEVILLE AND EDGEFIELD COUNTIES.
Whereas upon a petition of one-third of the qualified electors residing within portions of Indian Hill and Cedar Spring Townships of Abbeville County and of Hibler Town ship of Edgefield County, filed with the Governor on the 29th day of March, 1897, and upon a petition of one-third of the qualified electors residing within that portion of Cokesbury Township remaining in Abbeville County, filed with the Governor on the fifth day of November, 1897, in accordance with an Act entitled “An Act to provide for the formation of new counties and the changing of county lines and county seats and consolidation of counties, approved 9th March, 1896, asking for an election to alter the county line of Greenwood County, so as to include said portions of said townships and said remaining portion of said town ship of Cokesbury within the boundaries of Greenwood County;
And whereas on the 8th dav of May, 1897, and 30th day of December, 1897, respectively, the said elections were held pursuant to several proclamations by the Governor, dated he 20th April, 1897, and 10th November, 1897, and resulted in more than two-thirds of the votes of the qualified electors of said territories being cast in favor of the alteration of aid county line of Greenwood County; And whereas all other constitutional and statutory requirements relating to the altering of county lines have been complied with in full; now, therefore,
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the portions of Indian Hill and Cedar Spring Townships of Abbeville County Iving and being within the boundaries hereafter given shall from the Approval of this Act be included within and become a part of the County of Greenwood. The aforesaid bounda ries are as follows: Beginning on the line of Greenwood County, one hundred and fifty feet from Jordan’s old mill on Long Cane Creek, in Abbeville County, and running south thirteen degrees to a mile post on the public road leading from Troy to MeCormick, near George Lebert’s residence; thence south thirty-three degrees east to the Abbe ville and Edgefield County line, at the crossing of Rockr Creek; thence along said county line to Greenwood County line near Winter seat; thence along Greenwood County line to the starting point. containing twenty (20) square miles: also that portion of Hibler Township, in Edgefield County. described as follows All that portion of Hibler Township in Edvefield County, west of Cutfa Town Creek, containing sixteen and one-fourth (16 1-4) sqnare miles: Also that por tion of Cokesbury Township, in Abbeville County, de scribed as follows: Commencing on corner post on Green wood County line, near Douglass’ mill, and! running north west seven (7) degrees to corner post on Goose Creek and branch of Long Cane Creek; thence northeast fifty-five (55) deerces forty-five minutes to corner post on Turkey Creek; thence down Turkey Creek to centre of bridge on public road, known as River Road; thence northeast sixty-seven (67) degrees to corner on Saluda River; thence up middle stream of Saluda River to corner of Cokesbury and Donald's Township line; thence along said township dividing line to the point of its crossing Long Cane Creek; thence down Long Cane Creek to the point of crossing of Cokesbury and Long Cane Township line; thence down said township division line to point of contact with present Greenwood County line; thence down said county line to beginning point, near Douglass’ mill, containing twelve (12) square miles.
Section 2, That Greenwood County shall be bounded as follows: That is to say, beginning at the middle line of Saluda River at the northeast corner of Saluda County, thence along the line of Saluda County to its northwest corner on Mountain Creek, thence a straight line to the middle line of Shinburg Bridge on Cuffa Town Creek, thence down the middle line of Culfa Town Creek to its junction with Kfard Labor Creek, thence down the middle line of Stevens's Creek to the mouth of Rocky Creek, thence up the middle line of Rocky Creek to the Abbeville and Edgefield County line, thence north 23 degrees west to mile post on public road leading from Troy to McCormick near George Lebert's residence, thence north 13 degrees west to a corner post one hundred and fifty feet (150) south of Jordan’s old mill on Long Cane Creek, thence northeast 50 degrees two miles and three-quarters, thence northeast 42 degrees two miles, thence northeast 27 degrees 50 minutes two miles, thence northeast 13 degrees 20 minutes two miles, thence northwest 1 degree two miles, thence northwest 15 degrees 10 minutes two miles, thence northwest 29 degrees 20 minutes two miles, thence northwest 43 degrees 35 minutes two miles, thence northwest 57 degrees 45 minutes, one and onefifth miles, to post four hundred feet (400) north of Douglass’ Mill Bridge on Long Cane Creek, thenee north 7 degrees west to division line between Long Cane and Cokesbury Townships, thenee up said division line to its crossing of Long Cane Creek, thence up the middle line of Long Cane Creck to its crossing of the division line between Donald’s and Cokesbury Townships, thence along said Cokesbury and Donald’s Township division line to the middle line of Saluda River, thence down the middle line of Saluda River to the northeast corner of Saluda County, the point of beginning.
Section 3. The Governor is authorized and empowered to appoint a commission of three persons, one of whom shall be a resident of Abbeville County, one a resident of Edgefield County and one a resident of Greenwood County, yhich said commission shall divide and justly apportion etween the Counties of Edgefield and Greenwood the tawul and bona fide indebtedness of Edgefield County existing t the time of the approval of this Act, and also justly divide and apportion between the Counties of Abbeville and greenwood the lawful and bona fide indebtedness of Abbeville County existing at the time of the approval of this Act, having due regard to the amount of unpaid taxes due in each of the Counties of Abbeville and Edgefield, and that said County of Greenwood shall receive credit for its proportionate share of any money on hand in each of said counties not heretofore expended, and said commissioners shall leach receive from the County of Greenwood two dollars per day, not exceeding eight days. The portion of said indebtedness to be apportioned by said commission to Greenwood is the proportionate share of the territory added to Greenwood County by this Act.
Section 4. That this Act shall be deemed a public Act, shall take effect immediately upon its approval, and all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent therewith be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
