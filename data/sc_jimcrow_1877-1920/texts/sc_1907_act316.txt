AN ACT TO RAISE SUPPLIES AND MAKE APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING 1907.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That a tax of four and one - half mills, exclusive of the public school tax hereinafter provided for, upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property of this State, be, and the same is hereby, levied for the purpose of meeting appropriations to defray the current expenses of the government for the fiscal year beginning January the 1st, 1907, and to meet such other indebtedness as has been or shall be provided for in the several Acts and Joint Resolutions passed by this General Assembly at the session of 1907, providing for the
same.
