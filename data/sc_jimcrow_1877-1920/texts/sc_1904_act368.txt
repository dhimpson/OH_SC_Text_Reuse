AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR REPAIRING THE MONUMENT ERECTED BY THE STATE UPON THE BATTLE-FIELD OF CHICKAMAUGA.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the to erect monuState of South Carolina, That a Commisison is hereby appointed, to consist of his Excellency the Governor of the State, the Adjutant and Inspector General, and the Lieutenant General commanding the Department of the Army of Northern Virginia, United Confederate Veterans, whose duty it shall be, and who are hereby authorized and required, to proceed at once and cause the monument erected by the State upon the battlefield of Chickamauga to be repaired and put in substantial and becoming form, after such design as said Commission may decide upon.
SEC. 2. That the sum of twenty - five hundred dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and hereby is, appropriated for the purpose of said repairs, to be paid out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.
SEC. 3. That the Attorney General is hereby directed to institute such proceedings as he may deem expedient to recover upon the bond of the contractors who built said monument the
amount of damages to the State resulting from the defective work upon said monument and for any and all breaches of the condition of said bond; and the amounts so collected to be turned into the Treasury for the use of the State.
SEC. 4. That this Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
