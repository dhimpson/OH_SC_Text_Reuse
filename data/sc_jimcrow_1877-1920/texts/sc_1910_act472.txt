AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT IN THE COUNTY OF YORK, TO BE KNOWN AS ROCK HILL SCHOOL DISTRICT, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COL-LECTION OF A LOCAL TAX THEREIN, APPROVED DECEMBER 19, A. D. 1887,"" BY PRESCRIBING THE MANNER OF ELECTING TRUSTEES, THEIR POWERS AND DUTIES AND TERM OF OFFICE.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the 19, 1887, State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act for the establishment of a new school district in the County of York, to be known as the Rock Hill School District, and to authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, approved December 19th, 1887, " be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to read as follows: Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Area of State of South Carolina, That the area embraced in a square, Dis the sides of which are three miles in length, running north, south, east and west, and the center whereof is the center of the city of Rock Hill, be, and the same is hereby, created a special school district in said county and State, to be known as the Rock Hill School District, and that the same shall be a body politic and corporate, with such rights, powers, privileges, liabilities and immunities as are provided for school districts A. D. 1910. by the general school law of the State of South Carolina.
SEC. 2. That, in addition to the rights, powers, privileges, Rights and liabilities and immunities hereinbefore granted, the said school powers of district as to district shall have power to levy on all real and personal propspecial taxes. erty returned in said district a tax not exceeding four mills on the dollar, subject to the following provisions: The school trustees of said school district shall, at any time prior to the 25th day of December of each and every year, upon the written request of five property holders in said district, issue a call for a public meeting of all those citizens of said district and State who return real or personal property for taxation in said school district; and such notice shall be published in a newspaper published in the said school district, and posted in some public place in said school district for at least two weeks before the meeting is held, and notice specifying time, place and purpose of said meeting. When so assembled the persons answering the above description shall have power: First, to elect a chairman and secretary, adjourn from time to time, decide what tax, if any, in addition to the constitutional school tax apportioned to the schools of said school district, not to exceed the four - mill tax hereinbefore prescribed, shall be levied for the educational interests of said district, under the direction of the trustees thereof: Provided, That no tax thus levied shall be repealed at any subsequent meeting during the same fiscal year: Provided, further, That, if for any reason, said meeting shall not be called during any year, the tax levy of the previous year shall be collected and applied in the manner hereinbefore provided. Second, to fill any vacancy or vacancies that may exist in the Board of Trustees by reason of death, removal, resignation or otherwise, until the next regular municipal election, when such vacancy or vacancies shall be filled in the manner hereinafter provided for the election of trustees: Provided, further, That at all such meetings only those persons shall have power to vote who are qualified electors in said State, residing in said school district, and who return real or personal property for taxation within said school district.
SEC. 3. That it shall be the duty of the chairman and the secretary of said meeting, within one week after said meeting tax and levy. has been held, to notify the chairman of the Board of Trustees for said school district and Auditor of the said county of the amount of the tax thus levied; and the County Auditor shall at once assess such tax on all real and personal property returned in said school district, and the County Treasurer shall collect the same at the same time and in the same manner as the State and county taxes are collected; and such tax shall be a lien upon all the property upon which the same is levied until said tax is paid, and defaulting taxpayers shall be liable to like process and penalties as defaulters for State and county taxes.
SEC. 4. That the money collected from said tax levy, the How funds constitutional poll and three - mill tax, and the dog tax, to which paid out. said district is entitled under the general provisions of law, shall be held by the County Treasurer and paid out on warrants drawn by the trustees of the said school district, or their duly authorized officers, and said Treasurer shall be liable to said school district for the non - performance of his duty in respect to said money in the same manner and to same extent, and under like penalties as for the non - performance of his duties in reference to State and county taxes.
SEC. 5. The Board of Trustees of said school district shall of be composed of seven members, to be elected by the qualified voters of the said school district. The mayor of the city of Rock Hill, twenty days after the approval of the Act by the Governor, shall order an election under the rules prescribed for holding a municipal election, to be held the second Tuesday in April, 1910, for the election of the seven members, three of whom shall serve until the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill in 1916; two of whom shall serve until the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill in 1914, and two of whom shall serve until the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill in 1912, their respective terms to be chosen by lot in the presence of the Mayor and Board of Aldermen of the city of Rock Hill, within three days after their election, when they shall proceed to qualify and organize by the election of one from their number as a chairman, and one as a secretary, and other such officers as they may deem necessary A. D. 1910. and proper; subsequent election to fill vacancies caused by the expiration of the above terms heretofore provided for shall be held every two years, the term thereof to be six years, at the regular municipal election in the city of Rock Hill, in the manner and under the rules prescribed for holding said municipal elections: Provided, The present Board of Trustees of said school district shall continue in office until their successors shall have been elected and qualified, at which time their books, records, accounts, etc., shall be turned over to their successors. SEC. 6. That the said Board of Trustees, in addition to the duties and powers now provided by the general school law for duties of trustees of school districts, shall have the following duties and powers: 1. To select and purchase suitable grounds or accept donations thereof, and erect buildings thereon, or sell the same when in their judgment proper, and provide such other improvements, equipment and supplies as may be necessary for the use and conduct of the public schools of said school district. 2. To elect and dismiss the superintendents and teachers of said school district, prescribe their duties and terms of office, to fix their salaries, and to cause the examination of teachers whenever, in their opinion, the same shall be necessary, except the Winthrop Training School, over which they shall have no supervision, but the Winthrop authorities are hereby required to report the enrollment in the Training School to the trustees of the Rock Hill School District. Also to pre-
scribe, in conjunction with the superintendent, the course of study to be pursued and the text - books to be used in said. schools, using the text - books prescribed by the State Board of Education as far as practicable, to make rules and regulations for the government of the same, and to prescribe and regulate the terms and conditions upon which parties residing without the limits of said school district may be admitted into the public schools thereof; that no pupil of lawful school age residing within the limits of the said school district, and not otherwise debarred, shall be charged any tuition, incidental or other fee whatsoever. 3. To determine and direct the manner in which the special tax hereinbefore provided for and the constitutional poll and three - mill tax, and the dog tax, provided by law, shall be expended in maintaining the public schools of said district, and to disburse the same upon their warrants drawn by their regularly elected or appointed officers upon the County Treasurer as aforesaid.
SEC. 7. That the Board of Trustees shall make an annual Annual re report of the schools of said district to the State Superintendent of Education, on or before the first day of August of each year and every year, the same to be forwarded through the office of the County Sperintendent of Education of York County, which report shall contain such facts, statistics and other information as shall be required by law: Provided, further, That books and accounts of said board shall be annually audited by an expert accountant and published in the newspapers published in said district.
SEC. 8. That the County Superintendent of York County shall have over the said Rock Hill School District only such jurisdiction as is provided by law in reference to similar special school districts, and none other.
SEC. 9. That this Act shall take effect from and after the date of its approval, and that all Acts and parts of Acts in conflict or inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
