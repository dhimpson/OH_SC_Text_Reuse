AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE RE-ENROLLMENT AND REGISTRATION OF THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF BERKELEY COUNTY IN THE YEAR 1910, AND TO PROVIDE EXTRA COMPENSATION FOR THE SUPERVISORS OF REGISTRATION FOR SAID COUNTY, AND TO PROVIDE FOR ADDITIONAL REGISTRATION IN RICHLAND COUNTY IN SAID YEAR AND FOR THE EXPENSES THEREOF.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Supervisors of RegistraState of South Carolina, That the Supervisors of Registration tion in Berkeley for Berkeley County are required to re - enroll all the qualified to re - enroll electors for said county during the year 1910, and the Superin Richland visors of Registration for Richland County are required to County to allow additional registration of qualified electors as hereinafter registration.
SEC. 2. That the said Supervisors shall register all persons who may make application therefor, and who may be entitled thereto.

And the Supervisors of Registration for Richland County shall keep the books of registration open in the city of Columbia from 9 A. M. to 8 P. M. each day from and after the second day after the approval of this Act, and shall register qualified electors not already registered, or issue new certificates in case of application therefor by registered electors who may have lost their certificates, for six consecutive days. of which registration notice shall be given by advertisements in the daily papers published in the city of Columbia each day of such registration.
SEC. 4. That for the purposes of said re - rollment and registration, the County Supervisor of Berkeley County shall furnish the necessary books and pay to each of said Supervisors an extra compensation of fifty dollars, and also for the necessary advertisements; said extra compensation to be paid from the county funds, and shall be in addition to the compensation now allowed by law, and the Board of County Commissioners of Richland County shall, out of the ordinary county funds, pay to each of the Supervisors of Registration for said county for said six days of additional registration, three dollars per day, and also pay the necessary costs of advertisements as hereinabove required.
SEC. 5. That all Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
SEC. 6. That this Act shall be taken and construed to be an Act to be amendment to the general law providing for the registration of amendment electors, and an addition thereto (especially Section 179 of the law. Civil Code of 1902), being intended as a special provision in such general law.
SEC. 7. That this Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
