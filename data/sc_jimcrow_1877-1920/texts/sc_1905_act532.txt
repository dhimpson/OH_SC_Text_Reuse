AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND EMPOWER THE NORTH AUGUSTA SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 66, OF AIKEN COUNTY, IN THE STATE OF SOUTH CAROLINA, TO ISSUE BONDS FOR PURCHASE OF A LOT AND THE ERECTION AND EQUIPMENT OF A GRADED SCHOOL BUILDING THEREON; TO PROVIDE FOR A BUILDING COMMITTEE, AND TO PROVIDE MEANS FOR THE PAYMENT OF SAID BONDS AND THE INTEREST THEREON.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the District, No. State of South Carolina, That School District No. 66, embracCounty, to is ing the village of North Augusta, in the County of Aiken, is hereby authorized, empowered and required to issue coupon bonds in an amount not to exceed the sum of twelve thousand dollars, for the purpose of purchasing grounds and erecting, maintaining, furnishing and equipping a proper school house, to be located north of Jackson and east of Georgia avenues, in said district, upon grounds to which the district must have a good and legal title in fee simple: Provided, A majority of the qualified electors in the said district shall be in favor of such issue, as may be expressed at an election hereafter to be held for that purpose: And Provided, further, That at the said election five legal voters of said district are to be elected to act as a Building Committee, as is provided for in the following Sections.
SEC. 2. That the Trustees of the said district immediately Election. upon the passing of this Act are required to give fifteen days. notice in one or more of the newspapers published in Aiken County, of the time and place of such election, appoint managers, prescribe form of ballot, receive the returns and declare the election results.
SEC. 3. That the said School District be, and hereby is, auTerms of bonds thorized and empowered to issue coupon bonds in a sum not to exceed twelve thousand dollars of the denomination of six hundred dollars each, the bonds to be sold at face value and the interest not to exceed six per cent. per annum from the date of issue, and running for a series of twenty years, one bond and the annual interest on the whole issue outstanding to be paid each year until the whole amount of principal and interest shall be paid in full. That the said bonds and interest coupons are to be signed by the Building Commitee: Provided, That the signature of said Committee may be lithographed upon said coupons, and such lithographing shall be sufficient signing of the said coupons of said bonds.
SEC. 4. That the said bonds shall be sold by the said Building Sale of bonds. Committee, either at public or private sale, as in the judgment of said Committee may be best, and that the proceeds of the said bonds shall be paid over to the County Treasurer of the said County, and shall be kept separate by him subject to the order of a majority of said Building Committee, to whose order it shall be paid.
SEC. 5. That for the purpose of paying the said bonds and the interest thereon, so much as three mills on the dollar of the special tax which is now being levied upon the taxable property of the taxpayers of the said district is to be placed upon the duplicate of this County by the County Auditor, and collected by the County Treasurer, as other taxes are collected, and at the same time, and shall be kept separate by him and paid out upon the order of the School Trustees of the said School District, who are hereby required to issue their warrants in favor of the holders of the said bonds and coupons when due, approved by the County Auditor and School Superintendent, and the payment of said tax shall be enforced in the same manner as the payment of other taxes. Which annual tax shall continue until the General Assembly shall otherwise provide. And in case of the neglect or failure of the County Auditor or any other official to perform the duties herein required to be performed by him, or either of them, the holder or holders of any of said bonds may enforce them by mandamus, either in the Circuit or Supreme Court of the State.
SEC. 6. That the Building Committee, in addition to the mittee. power to them delegated in foregoing Sections, shall have full power and authority in selecting plans, employing and paying an architect, taking builder's bond, and in passing upon and receiving the building when properly constructed, and in all other matters that may be necessary to the full carrying out of this Act. That should a vacancy occur upon said Committee, that same shall be immediately filled by an appointment of a member by the Board of Education of Aiken County.
SEC. 7. That any balance of the proceeds of sad bonds which may remain after the erection and equipment of said building and of said tax remaining after the payment of the annual interest and coupons of the said bonds, shall be placed to the credit. of the said district, and may be used by the Trustees thereof for the payment of the teachers of the said School District, or for ordinary school purposes therein.
SEC. 8. This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
