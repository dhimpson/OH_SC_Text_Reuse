"AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE DIVISION OF THE CITY OF
COLUMBIA INTO FIVE WARDS AND FIXING THE NUMBER
OF ALDERMEN WHO, TOGETHER WITH THE MAYOR, SHALL
CONSTITUTE THE CITY COUNCIL OF SAID CITY"
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, [tt shall be the duty of the Mayor and Aldermen of the city of Columbia before thesnext regular municipal election in said city to divide the city into five wards, as follows: AL that portion of the city located between Blossom street and Gervais street to constitute Ward Onevall that portion of the city between Gervais street and Plain street to constitute Ward Two all that) portion between Plain street and Laurel street to constitute Ward Three; all that portion of the city between Laurel street and the Northern boundary of the city to constitute: Ward Four; and all that) portion of the city between Blossom street and the Southern boundary of the city to constitute Ward Five.
Srerion 2. At the first regular municipal eleetion, in April, 1898, and at all subsequent regular municipal eleetions, three Aldermen shall be eleeted from each of the five aforesaid wards, and said fifteen Aldermen, together with the Mayor, shall constitute the City Council of Columbia, nine or more of whom, including the Mayor, or Mayor pra tompore, shall constitute a quorum to do the business of the City Council.
Secrion 3. ALL Acts and parts of Vets inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
