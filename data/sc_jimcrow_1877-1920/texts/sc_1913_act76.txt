AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR TOWNSHIP COMMISSIONERS FOR CHEROKEE COUNTY, A CLERK FOR SAID BOARD, TO PRESCRIBE THEIR DUTIES, AND TO OTHERWISE PROVIDE FOR THE COUNTY GOVERNMENT OF SAID COUNTY.
Section 1. Township Commissioners for Cherokee County.— Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the county government of Cherokee county shall be administered by a Supervisor and Board of County Commissioners, consisting of one Commissioner from each township, to be known as Township Commissioners. § 2. County Supervisor.—That the County Supervisor shall be elected, give the same bond and receive the same salary as now provided by law for Cherokee county; he shall examine all claims against the county, see that they are properly itemized and sworn to, and shall submit the same to the board, with recommendations thereon; he shall let the contracts for bridges and public works, and advertise publicly for bids, where the amount to be expended is not over fifty dollars, subject to the approval of the board, and shall personally inspect, with the Township Commissioner of the township in which the work is being done, all work done in the county: Provided, That the consent of the Supervisor and Township Commissioner of the township wherein the work is to be done, shall only be necessary, where the amount does not exceed two hundred dollars, but where over this amount, the assent in writing of a majority of the whole Board of Commissioners shall be necessary, to make a valid contract, binding the county. § 3. Supervisor to Have Charge of Chain Gang.—That the Supervisor shall have charge of the chain gang, its guards and employees, with the co-operation and equal authority of the Township Commissioner, where the chain gang is doing work on the roads and bridges in the township where the Township Commissioner is situated, to direct the work to be done. He shall keep a journal in which shall be kept an itemized account of all expenses connected with and arising out of the operation of the chain gang, and likewise, a ledger account, showing the average, approximate expense of the chain gang per day, and the Board of County Commissioners shall distribute the chain gang and road levy throughout the county, in each township,
in amounts in proportion to the number of miles of public roads in the township, upon a mileage basis, as follows: Cherokee township, 135 miles; Limestone township, 185 miles; Morgan township, 110 miles; Gowdeysville township, 100 miles; White Plains and Draytonville townships, 82 miles each, and they shall file with the Clerk of Court of the county, quarterly, beginning with the first of April, 1918, an itemized statement showing the total amount of the chain gang and road levy so apportioned, amount of expenses for the quarter, where and how expended, and the amount on hand yet to be expended: Provided, The Supervisor shall have no right to vote upon any question when the Board of Commissioners are assemble to transact any business, unless they are equally divided, then he shall cast the deciding vote. § 4. Township Commissioners Appointed—How.—The Governor shall appoint and commission, upon the recommendation of the Legislative Delegation, one man in each township, to be known as the Township Commissioner, whose term of office shall be for two years, who shall give bond for five hundred dollars for the faithful performance of the official duties of his office; each Township Commissioner shall receive three dollars a day for not exceeding sixty days’ service actually rendered in each year, his claim for services to be itemized, sworn to and approved by the Board of Commissioners. § 5. Township Road Fund.—The township road fund shall consist of: (a) the sum apportioned of the chain gang levy to each township, as herein provided to each township; (b) the commutation tax for said township, which shall be added to the chain gang fund; (c) any special local tax levied in any township for improvements in the township. § 6. Jurisdiction of Township Commissioners.—Each 'Township Commissioner shall have exclusive jurisdiction and control over the township road fund for his township, by special levy, and shall spend the same for the maintenance and improvement of the public roads in his township, and for the construction and repair of such bridges as shall cost not more than fifty dollars. He shall personally inspect all work done under his jurisdiction, and shall vouch for and attest every claim against his township fund before it shall be submitted to the Supervisor and Board of Commissioners for approval. The said Board of Commissioners, with the Supervisor, shall constitute the County Board
of Commissioners, which board, or a majority, shall pass upon all claims made out against the county, and shall have power to reduce or reject any claim or claims that may be deemed illegal or unjust. § 7. Duty of Township Commissioners.—It shall be the duty of Township Commissioners to ascertain the names of all persons living in his township who are liable for road duty, and to file with the Supervisor an alphabetical list of the same. It shall be the duty of the Supervisor to compare this list with the commutation tax list filed by the County Treasurer and to report back to the Township Commissioners not later than April 1st of each year the names of all persons in his township who have not paid the commutation tax and are liable for road duty for that year. Each Township Commissioner shall summon out these road hands at such times as he thinks most beneficial to the public service, and shall, through his road foremen that he has authority to appoint, work them upon the road for three days each year. § 8. Organization.—_Immediately after the appointment of the Township Commissioners as herein provided, or as soon thereafter as is practicable, the Supervisors shall call a meeting for the purpose of organization and the County Board of Commissioners shall meet thereafter on the first Monday in each month, and a majority of said board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business, except as herein provided. The Supervisor may call a special meeting at any time, and must do so upon the written request of two members of the board, at which meeting all members of the board shall be present, unless providentially, or for other good reasons, prevented. § 9. Clerk to Be Appointed.—The Governor shall appoint and commission, upon the recommendation of a majority of the Legislative Delegation, a Clerk, who shall hold office for a term of two years, whose salary shall be $300; who shall give bond in the sum of $500 for the faithful performance of the duties of his office, and who shall be removed by the Governor upon a recommendation of a majority of the Legislative Delegation. It shall be the duty of the Clerk to remain in the office of the Supervisor and keep the same open all the time. It shall be his duty to keep all the books of the Supervisor and of the Board of County Commissioners, to keep a journal and ledger account of the chain gang expenses, carefully and particularly itemized, the
amount expended each week, month and quarter, the amount of the chain gang levy apportioned to each township and how and when expended, the amounts of special levy for each township and how and when expended, keeping the vouchers of the chain gang levy, commutation tax, and special levy in each township separate as to each township, that it may be seen and shown how the different amounts belong to each township has been expended. § 10. Township Commissioners May Swear Out Warrants.— It shall be the duty of the Township Commissioners to swear out warrants before a Magistrate against persons who shall fail or refuse to perform road duty after being summoned thereto. § 11. Duty to File Itemized Statement.—The said Board of Commissioners shall, at the end of each month, file with the Clerk of Court of said county for the inspection of the grand jury an itemized sworn statement in writing of all expenditures for the chain gang for road work, for the poorhouse farm and poor, and all other expenditures within their jurisdiction, showimg every item and its costs, from whom purchased, which statement shall at all times be open to inspection of the public. § 12. Misdemeanor to Violate Provisions.—Any County Commissioner, the Supervisor or other officer herein mentioned, who shall violate any of the provisions of this Act, shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor, and, upon conviction, shall be punished by fine of not less than one hundred dollars or to imprisonment not over thirty days, and, upon the recommendation to do so by a majority of the county delegation in the General Assembly, such Township Commissioner shall be removed from office by the Governor. § 13. All Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
