AN ACT RELATING TO SCHOOL TRUSTEES IN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 3, OF ORANGEBURG COUNTY.
Section 1. Election of Trustees.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That there shall be elected by the qualified electors residing in School District No. 3, known as the Four Holes School District, in the County of Orangeburg, three trustees who shall go into office at the expiration of the term of office of the present trustees. The three trustees so elected shall determine among themselves by lot their respective terms of office so that one trustee shall serve for one year, one for two years and one for three years and one trustee shall go out of office each year thereafter, and their successors shall be annually elected for a term of three years each. The County Board of Education shall annually order said election and give notice of such election for two weeks and shall appoint wt managers to manage such elections. The managers of said election shall conduct the same as general elections are conducted, and shall make returns thereof to the County Board of Education of Orangeburg County, who shall declare the result thereof; and it shall be the duty of the said County Board of Education to issue a certificate of election to the trustee or trustees so elected.
