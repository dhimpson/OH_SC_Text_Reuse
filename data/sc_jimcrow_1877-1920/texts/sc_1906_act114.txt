AN ACT TO PERMIT THE SCHOOL TRUSTEES OF FAIR FOREST SCHOOL DISTRICT AND OF BOILING SPRINGS SCHOOL DIS-TRICT NO. 54 OF SPARTANBURG COUNTY TO ERECT CERTAIN BUILDINGS FROM THE PUBLIC SCHOOL FUNDS OF SAID SCHOOL DISTRICT.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the School Trustees of Fair School Dist. Forest School District and of Boiling Springs School District tan burg Co., No. 54 of Spartanburg County are hereby authorized to spend a welling for such sum or sums of the public school fund remaining to their credit, or hereafter placed to their credit, as they may deem wise for the erection of a dwelling on the public school land in said district for the teacher or teachers of said school or schools. Approved the 17th day of February, A. D. 1906.
