AN ACT TO VALIDATE AN ELECTION HELD IN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 3 OF ABBEVILLE COUNTY, KNOWN AS LOWNDESVILLE SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 3, FOR THE PURPOSE OF ISSUING BONDS
TO ERECT A SCHOOL BUILDING IN SAID DISTRICT.
Whereas, an election was held in School District # 3 of Abbeville county, known as Lowndesville School District # 3, on the 20th day of June, 1914, for the purpose of determining the question whether said district would issue bonds for the purpose of erecting a school building, in said district, and
Whereas, the petition for said election filed with the trustees called for an election for eight thousand dollars in bonds, but the order for the election only ordered an election on the question of issuing six thousand five hundred dollars worth of bonds, which election resulted in the bonds being voted by fifty-four votes in favor thereof, and four votes against said issue of bonds, and Whereas, said bonds have been issued, and a tax levied and collected for three years for the purpose of paying the interest on said bonds, and providing a sinking fund to retire said bonds, and Whereas, said bonds were made payable at the Bank of Lowndesville, instead of at the Lowndesville Banking Company; Now therefore, for the purpose of validating said election, and said bonds, and for the purpose of fixing a place at which said bonds may be paid:
Section 1. Lowndesville School District No. 3 Election Validated.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That said election for the purpose of voting bonds in Lowndesvillé School District # 3, of Abbeville county, be, and the same is hereby, validated and confirmed in every respect.
§ 2. Where Bonds Payable—That said bonds are hereby made payable, either at the office of the Lowndesville Banking Company, or at the office of the County Treasurer, as the holders thereof may elect.
