AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE SCHOOL TRUSTEES OF EACH AND EVERY SCHOOL DISTRICT IN THE COUNTY OF RICHLAND TO ORDER AND HOLD AN ELECTION FOR LEVYING A SPECIAL TAX FOR SCHOOL PURPOSES.
Section 1. Election for Special School Tax Authorized.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That the School Trustees of each and every School District in the County of Richland, and State be, and they are hereby, authorized and empowered to submit to the qualified electors of their respective School Districts, not later than June Ist, A. D. 1916, the question of levying an annual tax on all the taxable property in said School District to be used for the current expenses incurred for the support and maintenance of the School in said district. § 2. Conduct of Election.—That the said School Trustees for each and every district shall order an election upon the said question to be held at such place, or places, in the School District as may be designated by the said School Trustees, and shall give notice of such election for ten days in some newspaper published in the County of Richland, and by posting a notice of such election at three prominent places in the School District, to be selected by the School Trustees, which notice shall designate the time and place or places, at which the election will be held, and the said School Trustees shall appoint the managers of said election, receive the returns, and declare the result.
§ 3. Form of Ballot—That the School Trustees shall have printed and provided for the use of the voters a sufficient number of ballots which shall be placed at the voting place, or places, on which shall be printed the question to be submitted and which shall state definitely the amount of the tax levy to be voted upon, which amount shall be determined by the said School Trustees, and the words “Yes” and “No,” so that the voter may indicate his vote by striking out one or the other of the words on said ballot, and the word not so stricken out to be counted. § 4. Tax Levy.—That if a majority of the votes cast at such an election shall be in favor of levying the said tax, the School Trustees shall certify the fact to the County Auditor who shall thereupon annually assess said tax on the taxable property of said School District and enter the same upon the tax duplicates, and the County ‘Treasurer shall thereupon collect said taxes at the same time and in the same manner as other taxes. § 5. Trustees to Receive Proceeds.—That the County Treasurer shall keep a separate account of said taxes and shall pay all moneys realized and collected on account of same to the School Trustees for the School District for which said tax has been levied, to be used for the purposes aforesaid. § 6. Trustees May Borrow Money.—That the School Trustees of any School District are authorized to borrow money on account of said taxes, if same shall be voted for the purposes aforesaid, and to pledge said taxes for the payment of such money as they may borrow. § 7. Immediately Effective—That this Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
