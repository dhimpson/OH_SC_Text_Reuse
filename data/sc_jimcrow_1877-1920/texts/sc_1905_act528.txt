AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO ENABLE AND AUTHORIZE SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 14, IN NEWBERRY COUNTY, TO ISSUE BONDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF BUILDING AND ERECTING A SCHOOL HOUSE THEREIN, AND TO PURCHASE A LOT THEREFOR,"" APPROVED THE 25TH DAY OF FEBRUARY, A. D. 1904, AND TO VALIDATE THE BONDS ISSUED UNDER AUTHORITY OF SAID ACT.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the School District State of South Carolina, That Section 7 of an Act entitled “ An berry County, Act to enable and authorize School District No. 14, in Newberry County, to issue bonds for the purpose of building and erecting a school house therein, and to purchase a lot therefor, " approved the 25th day of February, A. D. 1904, be amended by adding at the end of said Section the following words: " or may buy and cancel so many of said bonds from time to time as said sinking fund will purchase: Provided, further, That the County Treasurer shall deposit said sinking fund at interest in such bank or banks as said Trustees may from time to tine direct until the same may be required by them for payment on, or investment in, said bonds as herein permitted; " so that said Section, so amended, shall read as follows:
Section 7. The Auditor of Newberry County shall annually Tax levy. levy on the taxable property of said School District a tax of three - fourths of a mill for the purpose of creating a sinking fund for the payment of said bonds, which tax shall be collected by the Treasurer of Newberry County and shall be held by him for the purpose aforesaid: Provided, however, That said Trustees at the end of every five years shall have the right to order such sinking fund then accumulated paid pro rata on the bonds then outstanding or may buy and cancel so many of said bonds from time to time as said sinking fund will purchase: Provided, further, That the County Treasurer shall deposit said sinking fund at interest in such bank or banks as said Trustees may from time to time direct, until the same may be required by them for payment on, or investment in, said bonds as herein provided.
SEC. 2. That the bonds, aggregating five thousand dollars, issued by the School Trustees, under the terms of the aforesaid Act, be, and the same are hereby, validated and declared to be a debt of said School District, subject to all the provisons of said Act as amended by this Act.
