AN ACT TO INCORPORATE GREENWOOD EDUCATIONAL ASSOCIATION, OF GREENWOOD, S. C.
Section 1. Greenwood Educational Association Incorporated.— Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina (a Concurrent Resoution, allowing this Bill to be introduced, having been passed by a two-thirds vote of each House, as required by the Constitution), That H. V. R. Schrader, F. N. K. Bailey, A. F. McKissick, D. A. G. Ouzts, W. T. Jones. C. C. Featherstone, S. C. Hodges, W. J. Snead and Kenneth Baker, of Greenwood, S. C., trustees, and their successors in office, be, and are hereby, created and constituted a body politic and corporate, under the name and style of “Greenwood Educational Associ7 ation,’ and as such corporation and in, by and under the said name shall have the right to contract and be contracted with, to sue and be sued and in any manner whatsoever, to acquire, hold and dispose of any and all kinds of property, real, personal and mixed, and to lease or mortgage the same; to have and use a common seal, to make such by-laws, rules and regulations as may be thought proper and, generally, to have all such rights, powers and privileges as are usually incident to corporations of like character and as now are or hereafter may be conferred by gencral laws upon corporations organized for educational purposes and as may not be repugnant to the Constitution and laws of this State or of the United States.
§ 2. Power to Establish and Maintain School.—That the said corporation shall have, and it is hereby given, the power to establish, maintain and operate at or near the town of Greenwood, in the State of South Carolina, a high grade school or cvllege, for the liberal education of white boys and men, and for these purposes shall be authorized to do all such acts and make all such contracts as may be proper or necessary.
§ 3. Board of Trustees——That all the powers given said corporation shall be, and they are hereby, lodged in a Board of Trustees, consisting of the persons named in Section 1 of this Act. and such other persons as may be substituted for them, or any of them, or chosen to succeed them, or any of them, in the manner hereinafter provided. That three of the members of the said Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of one year, or until their successors are elected; that two of the members of the said Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of two years, or until their successors are elected; that two of the members of the said Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of three years, or until their successors are elected, and that two of the members of the Board of Trustees shall hold office for the term of four years, or until their successors are elected; that the said trustees, named in this Act, shall determine by lot which of them shall hold for one year, two years, three years and four years, and thereafter there shall be held annually, at such time and place as may be fixed by the by-laws, an election, at which the successors of the said trustees so named in Section 1 of this Act, shall be elected.
§ 4. Certificates of Stock.—That the said corporation shall issue to such person or persons as shall or may hereafter subscribe money or other property to said corporation for the purpose of establishing, improving or maintaining the same, certificates, showing the amount or amounts contributed by them, the said contributions or certificates to be divided into shares of fifty dollars each, and the said certificates to show the number of shares contributed by each person or persons; that the said contributors shall, for the purpose of electing the said trustees, be deemed to be stockholders in the said corporation and the said annual meeting, heretofore provided for, shall be composed of the said stockholders, and in the election of the said trustees each stockholder shall be entitled to one vote for each share of stock so held by him or her, and it shall take only a majority of the shares represented at the meeting to elect said trustees: Provided, That at said meeting the said stockholders may vote in person or by proxy, as now provided by law with reference to meetings of stockholders in other corporations: Provided, That the provision of this section shall not apply to donations of land or property for a site, or otherwise.
§ 5. Degrees.—That the said corporation shall have power and authority to confer and award all such distinctions, honors and degrees as are usually conferred or awarded by any of the colleges and universities of the United States. § 6. Property to Be Used for Educational Purposes.—That all of the property, real, personal or mixed, held by the said corporation shall be used only for educational purposes, at or near the town of Greenwood, in the State of South Carolina, and in the event that the said property should, for any reason, cease to be used for such educational purposes, at or near the said place, the said corporation shall be dissolved and all of the assets of the said corporation shall revert to the said stockholders, or their assignees, and the same shall be distributed by the said trustees to the said stockholders, or their assignees, in proportion to the several amounts owned by them.
§ 7. Charter in Perpetuity—The charter herein granted shall be held in perpetuity unless said corporation shall be dissolved which said dissolution may be brought about by a two-thirds vote of the said stockholders, in a meeting called for that purpose, of which thirty days’ notice shall be given to the said stockholders, in writing, by mail or in person: Provided, That at such meeting, and in all meetings of the said stockholders, the vote shall be taken by stock, each stockholder being entitled to one vote for each share of stock represented by him or her
§ 8. Exempt from Taxation.—That all of the property owned by the said corporation, real, personal or mixed, shall be used so long as the corporation exists, for educational purposes, and for said purposes only, and all of the said property of the said corporation, so long as it shall be used for educational purposes shall be exempt from the State, county and municipal taxes
§ 9. May Borrow Money.—That the said corporation shall, if a majority of its trustees deem it wise, have full power and authority to borrow money for the purpose of erecting or improving its buildings, or plant, and to pledge or mortgage its property, real or personal, to secure the payment of said money so borrowed.
§ 10. Immediately Effective-—That this Act shall be deemed and taken to be a public Act, and shall go into effect and continue of force from and immediately after the day of its approval.
