AN ACT TO ESTABLISH AN INFIRMARY FOR CONFEDERATE VETERANS.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Commission State of South Carolina, That a Commission consisting of five manage an members, three of whom shall be ex - Confederate soldiers or infirmary for Confederate sailors, be appointed by the Governor, who are hereby em-powered and required to establish and manage an infirmary for the infirm and destitute Confederate sailors and soldiers of the State, on what is known as the Bellevue place on Wallace land, now owned by the State. That said Commission shall prescribe rules regulating admisison to said infirmary: Provided, That two veterans shall be admitted from each County, on the recommendation of the County Pension Board: Provided, further, That in case any County Board fails to make such recommendation, the said Commission may fill the vacancy from the same or any other County.
SEC. 2. That the sum of twelve thousand dollars, if so much Appropriation. be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated to be used for the establishing and maintaining said infirmary.
SEC. 3. That the members of said Commission shall receive no compensation for their services, but shall be entitled to Members to actual expenses for attending the meetings thereof, to be paid receive upon the warrant of the Comptroller General out of any funds in the hands of the State Treasurer not otherwise appropriated.
