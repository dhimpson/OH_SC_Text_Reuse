AN ACT TO PROVIDE AN ANNUAL APPROPRIATION FOR THE HOLDING OF THE ANNUAL RE-UNIONS OF THE CONFED-ERATE VETERANS IN THIS STATE, AND TO CREATE A COM-MISSION TO DISBURSE THE SAME.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the propriation for State of South Carolina, That there shall annually be appropriated the sum of three thousand and five hundred dollars ($ 3,500) to defray the expenses of holding an annual re - union of the Confederate Veterans in this State, if so much be necessary.
SEC. 2. That the Commander and the Quartermaster - GenWho shall eral of the South Carolina Division of the United Confederate Veterans and one other member of said organization, who shall be named by the Governor, shall constitute a Commission to disburse the funds provided for in Section 1, of this Act, the same to be used for the purpose of paying for the accommodation of needy Confederate Veterans in attendance for said re - unions, and for such other purposes pertaining to the holding of the said re - unions as may be deemed proper and expedient by the said Commission.

SEC. 4. This Act shall take effect upon its approval by the Governor.
