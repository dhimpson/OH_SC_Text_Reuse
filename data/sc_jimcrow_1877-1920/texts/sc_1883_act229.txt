Sec. 5. That the sum of seventy-six thousand four hundred and eighty three and 33-100 dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated for miscellaneous expenses, namely:
1. To pay the claims passed by the General Assembly at its regular session of 1883, nine thousand dollars, if 30 much be necessary.
2. To cover the amounts appropriated by the several Acts and Joint Resolutions passed by the General Assembly at its regular session of 18833, five thousand dollars, if so much be necessary.
3, To pay for the public printing of this fiscal year, fifteen thousand dollars, if so much be necessary,
4, For preparing for the printer, indexing and making marginal notes of the Acts and Joint Resolutions of the regular session of 1883, to be done under the supervision of the Clerks of the Senate and House of Representatives, two hundred and fifty dollars.
5. To pay the per diem and mileage of the members of the Sinking Fund Commission who reside outside of the limits of Richland County, each of whom shall be entitled to reccive five dollars per day for each day actually engaged in attending the meetings of the said Commission and in going to and returning from the same, and g mileage of five cents per mile for each mile actually traveled, the same to be paid out of any funds in the possession of the Sinking Fund Commission.

For the salaries of Supervisors of Registration, thirteen thousand and nine hundred and thirty-three 33-100 dollars: Provided, That the said Supervisors shall be paid monthly as follows, that is to say: From the first day of November, 1883, to the 29th day of February, 1884, at the rate of two hundred dollars per annum; and from the first day of March, 1884, to the 31st day of October, 1884, at the rate of five hundred dollars per annum, except the Supervisors of Charleston, whose compensation from the first day of March to the thirty-first day of October, 1884, shall be at the rate of one thousind dollars per annum.

Sec. 6. That the President’ of the Senate and Speaker of the House of Representatives respectively shall furnish pay certificates for the amount of the per diem and mileage due to each member of the Senate and House of Representatives, and to each officer and employee of that branch of the General Assembly to which such officer and employee shall respectively belong, signed by the respective officers and properly attested by the Clerk of such branch of the General Assembly.
Sec. 7. That this Act shall take effect from and immediately after the date of its approval, and all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act be, and the same are, for the purposes of this Act, hereby repealed.
