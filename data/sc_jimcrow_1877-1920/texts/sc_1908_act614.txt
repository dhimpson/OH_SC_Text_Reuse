AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF LAURENS TO ISSUE ADDITIONAL COUPON BONDS FOR THE PURPOSE OF ERECTING A SCHOOL BUILDING AND EQUIPPING THE SAME,AND TO SELL ANY LOT OR LOTS NOW OWNED BY THE SAID SCHOOL DIS-TRICT, AND TO PURCHASE OTHER LOT OR LOTS, OR LANDS, FOR SCHOOL PURPOSES.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the trict of town State of South Carolina, That the Board of Trustees of the Laurens School District of the Town of Laurens are hereby authorized and empowered to submit to the qualified voters resident in said School District, at an election to be held for that purpose, as hereinafter provided for, the question of issuing additional bonds, not to exceed the amount of ten thousand dollars, bearing not exceeding five and one - half per cent. interest, and payable in thirty years, and the levy of such annual tax as may be necessary to pay the interest on said bonds.
SEC. 2. An election shall be held in said School District, for said purpose, upon the filing of a petition with said Board of Trustees, signed by a majority of the freeholders residing within said School District; the said election to be ordered by the said Board of Trustees when the said petition is duly filed with it. Twenty days ' notice of the time, place and purpose of said election shall be given by advertising the same in one of the newspapers published within the said School District. At said election only those shall be allowed to vote who are duly
qualified to vote under the Constitution and Laws of the said A. D. 1908. State. Those persons voting in favor of the issuing of said bonds shall vote a written or printed ballot, upon which the Ballots. words " For the Issuing of Bonds " are written or printed thereon; and those opposed to the issuing of said bonds shall vote a similar ballot, upon which the words " Against the Issuing of Bonds " are written or printed thereon. The managers for said election shall be appointed by the said Board of Trustees, and the polls shall be open from 9 o'clock in the forenoon until 4 o'clock in the afternoon. After the said polls are closed, the result of said election shall be ascertained by the said managers and certified to the said Board of Trustees, who shall declare the result thereof. If the election shall result in a majority of persons, voting at said election, voting in favor of the issuing of said bonds, then bonds, not exceeding in amount the sum of ten thousand dollars, shall be executed and sold by the said Board of Trustees. The said bonds shall be signed by the Chairman and the Secretary of said Board. The said bonds shall be issued in such denominations as may be agreed upon by the said Board, and shall bear interest at a rate not exceeding five and one - half per cent. per annum, which interest shall be payable semi - annually, in January and July of each year: Provided, That said bonds shall not be sold for less than par value.
SEC. 3. That the proceeds realized from the sale of said bonds shall be used, along with the proceeds of the sale of the bonds Use of issued under the Act of 1907, to purchase a lot or lots and erect a suitable school building or buildings thereon, and in providing suitable furniture and apparatus for the same.
SEC. 4. That the County Auditor and the County Treasurer for Laurens County are hereby required and directed to levy and collect, annually, a sufficient tax upon the taxable property in said School District to pay the interest on the said bonds; the said levy to be in addition to the levy already required by the Acts of 1891 and 1907 to pay the interest on the bonds already issued by the said School District. Any surplus remaining after the payment of the annual interest shall be applied, under the direction of the said Board of Trustees, to the payment of the principal of said bonds.
SEC. 5. That the said bonds so issued shall be free and exempt from all taxation for State, County and municipal purposes.
SEC. 6. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
SEC. 7. This Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
