AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR CREATING A HIGHWAY COMMISSION FOR ABBEVILLE COUNTY, DEFINE ITS POWERS AND DUTIES, AND TO PROVIDE FOR FUNDS TO CARRY OUT THE OBJECT OF SAID COMMISSION.
Section 1. Highway Commission for Abbeville County.—Le it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That there is hereby created in Abbeville county a commission, consisting of five men, who shall be known as the Highway Commission of Abbeville County, and who shall have such powers and duties as are given them under this Act, or that may hereafter be conferred upon them by law.
§ 2. Appointment.—The said Commissionefs shall be appointed by the Governor upon the recommendation of the Abbeville Delegation in the General Assembly.
§ 3. Terms of Office.—That the terms of said Highway Commissioners shall be for two years each, unless removed by the Governor upon the recommendation of Abbeville Delegation in the General Assembly.
§ 4. Jurisdiction That said Highway Commission shall have full jurisdiction over the roads and highways of Abbeville county, as far as in their judgment are necessary or proper for them to assume, for the purpose of building, laying out, widening and maintaining the roads of Abbeville county; and they are hereby empowered to take charge of such roads as in their judgment may be necessary or advisable by giving the Board of County Commissioners five days’ notice of their intention, and it shall be the duty of the Supervisor to turn over such roads as they may demand to the jurisdiction of said Highway Commission.
§ 5. Oath and Bond.—That said Highway Commission, when appointed according to the terms of this Act, shall take the oath of office provided by the Constitution of this State, and shall each give bond in the sum of five thousand dollars for the faithful performance of his duties as such Commissioner; said bond to be approved by the Clerk of the Circuit Court, both as to its form and sufficiency.
§ 6. Authorized to Issue Bonds.—That said Highway Commission is hereby authorized to issue bonds of the county of Abbeville to the amount of one hundred and eighty thousand ($180,000) dollars, for the purpose of raising money to carry out the objects of this Act; said bonds to be in the denomination of five hundred dollars each, to be payable in twenty years, and to bear a rate of interest not exceeding five per cent. per annum, and to be styled and called Highway Bonds of Abbeville County. Said Commission shall advertise said bonds for sale by publishing notices thereof in such newspapers as they may deem best calculated to secure bidders, and shall sell said bonds to the highest bidders, for cash, in such quantities as they may be able to get the best price for; said bonds when issued shall be exempt from State and county taxes, and the coupons shall be receivable for county taxes.
§ 7. Proceeds of Sale of Bonds.—That, as soon as said bonds are sold, the proceeds of said sale shall be deposited in three or more of the banks in Abbeville county, as the Commission may determine, upon such terms and conditions as the Commission and the banks may agree upon, and shall only be drawn out upon checks signed by said Commission, or a majority of its members, for the purposes contemplated in this Act: Provided, That should said Commission desire to lend any of said funds on short time loans, before it is needed to use in the building of roads, it shall have the right to do so, taking proper security therefor. All interest on such loans to be added to the sinking fund, for the ultimate retirement of said bonds.
§ 8. Proceeds—How Used.—That out of the proceeds of said bonds the said Commission shall use so much as may be necessary in building a system of good roads in Abbeville county, of a permanent and lasting character, and maintain the same in good condition.
§ 9. Civil Engineer.—That in order to carry out the objects of this Act the said Highway Commission shall employ a competent civil engineer, whose duty it shall be to make a survey of the roads of the county, which the said Commission desires to build or improve, and who shall superintend the building of such roads as he may be ordered to build by said Commission, and who shall be at all times subject to the orders of said Commision. He shall be paid such salary as may be determined by said Commission, not exceeding the sum of eighteen hundred ($1,800) dollars per year. That said engineer shall have the right to enter upon any lands in the county for the purpose of making surveys for roads, provided that he shall do no injury to the owner of the land, in the way of cutting timber or destroying crops, fences, or other property of the landowner.
§ 10. Annual Tax Levy for Interest and Sinking Fund.—That for the purpose of paying interest on said bonds, and providing a sinking fund for their retirements, there is hereby levied upon all the taxable property of Abbeville county a tax of two and one-half mills upon the dollar, which shall be due and payable at the same time and upon the same terms as other county taxes, and shall be listed by the Auditor and collected by the Treasurer of the county, as is provided by law for the other taxes of the county.
§ 11. Highway Commission Fund.—That out of the amount of taxes so collected the said Treasurer shall pay to the bondholders the interest on said bonds, as it shall become due, and the remainder of said taxes shall be set apart as the Highway Commission fund, and shall be drawn out only upon the warrants of said Highway Commission, signed by said Commission, or a majority thereof. That it shall be the duty of said Highway Commission to invest all moneys arising out of the taxes hereby levied, except that paid for interest on the bonds, and the salaries of the engineer and Commission, in some safe security, at as good rate of interest as possible: Provided, That the rate of interest shall not be less than seven per cent., for the purpose of a sinking fund to retire said bonds at maturity, and said sinking fund shall never be used for any purpose except that herein set forth, and shall be held as a trust fund, for the benefit of the holders of said highway bonds. In lending said money preference shall be given to real estate security in Abbeville county, wherever the same can be had on as good terms as other securities.
§ 12. Maintain Office.—That said Highway Commission shall maintain an office at the courthouse, which shall be kept open at all times for the transaction of business pertaining to said Commission, and shall be in charge of one of said Commissioners, who shall be elected clerk by said Commission at its first meeting, and who shall hold his office for such time as may be determined by said Commission. That said Commissioners shall each be paid a salary of two hundred dollars per year, except the secretary, who shall receive a salary of six hundred dollars per year; said salaries to be paid quarterly, upon the warrants of the Commission, out of the funds of said Commission. Said Commission shall meet at least once a month, and at such other times as they may call extra meetings, and shall be presided over by one of their number, to be elected by said Commission, and shall be designated as the chairman thereof. That all roads of the county, taken in charge by said Commission, shall be worked by contract, let to the lowest responsible bidder, who shall be required to give bond to the Commission for the faithful discharge of his contract and who shall work under the direction and control of the Road Engineer.
§ 13. May Condemn Lands.—That in case it becomes necessary to condemn any land for the purpose of building, repairing, changing, or in any manner working the roads and highways of this county, said Commission is hereby granted the right to condemn lands for said purpose, or for any of them, which land shall be condemned in the following manner, that is to say, the said Commissioners shall select six freeholders of the county, the landowner, whose lands it is desired to condemn, shall select six, and said twelve shall proceed to condemn the land required, and determine its value, and the amount so agreed upon shall be paid to the landowner within ten days after said condemnation proceedings are reported to the Commission out of the funds under the control of the Commission for that purpose. Should said twelve freeholders be equally divided in opinion, and thus fail to agree, they shall have the right to select another freeholder, and a majority of the said jury shall determine the compensation to be paid. If either party is dissatisfied with the amount awarded by the jury for the land required, then the Commission, or the landholder, shall have the right to appeal to the Court of Common Pleas, and the case shall be tried before a jury, upon issues to be submitted to them by the Court, and whose decision on all questions shall be final, unless set aside by the presiding Judge: Provided, however, That the appeal shall not stay the acquiring of the land by the Highway Commission, but the amount agreed on for the land to be deposited with the Clerk of the Court to await the ‘result of the appeal.
§ 14, Election to Be Held.—That the question of whether this Act shall become law or not shall be submitted to an election to be held by the voters of Abbeville county, on the second Tuesday in August, 1913: Provided, A petition, signed by one-third of the qualified electors of Abbeville county, be filed with the Supervisor of said county on or before the fifteenth day of June. 1913, asking for said election; which said election shall be held at the regular voting precincts of Abbeville county, under the same rules and regulations as the regular elections for State and county officers are held, the managers of said election to be appointed by the Board of County Commissioners of Abbeville county not less than two weeks nor more than four weeks before said election, and the returns from said election, consisting of the poll lists, ballots and returns of the managers, shall be filed with the Board of County Commissioners within three davs after said election; which board shall assemble and declare the result of said election in not less than one week from the date thereof. At such election the ballots shall be printed and furnished by the Board of County Commissioners to the managers of election. and there shall be printed on said ballots the following words. to wit: “In favor of bonds for roads,” “Opposed to bonds for roads,” and an equal number of said ballots shall be furnished of both kinds. If a majority of the legal and qualified voters of Abbeville county shall vote in favor of bonds for roads, then the above Act shall be and hecome the law. and shall go into effect on the first day of September, 1913, and the elections for said Commissioners shall be held, as provided by this Act, not later than September 15, 1913. Should a majority of the legal and qualified voters of Abbeville county vote against bonds for roads, then this Act shall be and void and of no force and effect whatever.
