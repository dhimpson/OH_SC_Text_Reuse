AN ACT TO REPEAL AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR RURAL POLICEMEN FOR SPARTANBURG COUNTY,"" AND TO SUBMIT THE REPEAL OF THE SAID ACT TO THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF SPARTANBURG COUNTY.
Section 1. Act (1912, XXVII Stats., 896) Repealed, Subject to Result of Election - Rural Police in Spartanburg County. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That an Act entitled " An Act to provide for Rural Policemen of Spartanburg county, " being Act No. 486 of the Acts of 1912, be, and the same is hereby, repealed: Provided, That this Act shall be submitted to the qualified electors of Spartanburg county for the purpose of voting on the same at an election to be held in said county on the second Tuesday in April, A. D. 1919, at which election, if a majority of the electors voting on the said question shall vote in favor of the repeal of the said Act, then the same shall stand repealed, to take effect on the first day of January, 1920, but should a majority of the electors voting on the said question at said election vote against the repeal of the said Act, the same shall stand as now provided by law.
§ 2. Regulations for Election. That at the said election there shall be printed and furnished by the Election Commissioners of Spartanburg county ballots which shall have printed thereon the following words: " To repeal an Act to provide for Rural Policemen of Spartanburg county - Yes, " and other ballots with the following words printed thereon: " To repeal an Act to provide for Rural Policemen of Spartanburg county - No. " The said election shall be held and conducted as other general elections are held in said county and all qualified electors in said county shall be entitled to vote in said election.
§ 3. Act Effective on Approval. - This Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor. Approved the 14th day of March, A. D. 1919.
