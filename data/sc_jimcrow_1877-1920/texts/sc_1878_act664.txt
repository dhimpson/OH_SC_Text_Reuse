AN ACT TO RAISE SUPPLIES AND MAKE APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING NOVEMBER 1, 1878.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That a tax of two and three-quarter mills, exclusive of the public school tax hereinafter provided for, upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property of this State be, and the same is hereby, levied for the purpose of meeting appropriations to defray the current expenses of the government for the fiscal year commencing November 1 1878, and to meet such other indebtedness as have been or shall be provided for in the several Acts enacted by the present session of the General Assembly providing for the same.
Sec. 2. That a tax not to exceed three mills upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property in each of the Counties of this State, except the Counties of Lancaster and Georgetown, which shall be four mills, and in the County of Aiken, which shall be three mills, be, and the same is hereby, levied for County purposes
for the fiscal year commencing November 1, 1878, except that in the following named Counties the additional tax shall be, and the same is hereby, levied in the manner and for the purposes herein named, that is to say: In the County of Abbeville, one-half of one mill for past indebtedness and three-fourths of one mill to be levied for public fence; in the County of Aiken, two and one-half mills, of which one and one-half mills shall be applied to the payment of the past due indebtedness of the County and one mill to the payment. Of past due school claims. That there be levied upon the taxable property of Charleston County an additional tax of three fourths of one mill, to be collected by the County Treasurer at the same time when the other taxes are collected, the proceeds whereof shall be appropriated as follows: The one-third thereof, if so much be necessary, to the amount required under the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for funding the debt of Charleston County, &c.,” and the other two-thirds to be appropriated to the payment of the deficiencies in the said County for the fiscal years 1877-78: Provided, That before any such deficiency be paid the claim therefor must be established before the Board of County Commissioners: Provided, also, That in case the amount so provided for this purpose does not equal said deficiency, the claims so established shall be paid pro rata. That the County Commissioners of Charleston County be, and they are heréby, authorized and required to levy a tax from all the taxable property of the said County sufficient to raise the sum of three thousand dollars, which tax shall be paid at the same time with other tuxes. The proceeds arising therefrom shall be held by the Treasurer of the said County subject to the draft of the Major General commanding the First Division, and shall by him be apportioned as follows: The one-tenth thereof to the use of the militia in said County without the city of Charleston; another tenth thereof to the use of the First Regiment of the National Guard, and the remaining eight-tenths to be equaily divided amovg the companies composing the Fourth Brigade, by companies. That the City Treasurer of the city of Charleston shall levy and collect, when the first installment of city taxes are paid, an additional tax of one-quarter of one mill on the dollar on all the taxable property in the said city and apply the proceeds of the said tax to the payment of the deficiencies for the fiscal year 18771878 of the school fund of the school district composed of the city of Charleston; and that the Board of School Commissioners of the city of Charleston account to the City Treasurer of the said city for the value of all bills of the Bank of the State apportioned to said Board from the amount received tendered in payment of taxes of the said fiscal year, first paying in full any sum remaining unpaid as such deficiency; and in the County of Charleston one mill of the three mill tax levied for County purposes shall be set apart and kept separate by the County Treasurer for the payment of Court expenses, to wit: Dieting prisoners, pay of jurors, Constables in attendance on the Court, witnesess in State cases, Clerk of Court and Sheriffs’ accounts; and the County Treasurer, in each of his reports to the County Commissioners of funds on hand, shall specify the amount in his hands for Court expenses, and the County Commissioners are hereby prohibited from drawing checks on the fund so created for any other purpose; the County Commissioners of Charleston County are hereby authorized and required to pay from any funds collected for the fiscal year 1878 any sums that remain due for making assessments for the fiscal year 1876; in the County of Richland, one mill for past indebtedness of the County, as provided by Act passed at the regular session of 1874-75; in the County of Chester, four-fifths of one mill for past due school claims of the County, the proceeds of which, with the school funds now in the County Treasury, shall be applied to the payment of the Commissioners for investigating school claims and to the satisfaction of the claims approved by them in the manner and to the amount proposed in their report made to the General Assembly; in the County of Chesterfield, three mills for the past indebtedness of the County, in accordance with a Joint Resolution p at this session; in the County of Clarendon, one mill for past indebtedness; in the County of Darlington, three mills for past indebtedness of the County; for the County of Colleton, one mill for deficiencies of 1877-1878; in the County of Edgefield, one-half of one mill for current expenses and one mill for deficiencies for the year 1877-78; in the County of Fairfield, one and one-half mills to build public fence; in the County of Hampton, two mills for building the Court house and jail provided for at the last session of the General Assembly; in the County of Kershaw, two mills for past indebtedness of the County; in the County of Lancaster, two mills for past indebtedness of said County, to be paid in such manner and upon such terms as the County Commissioners and the several creditors may agree; in the townships of Indian Land, Waxhaw and Cane Creek, together with that portion of Gill’s Creek township adjoining Cane Creek and lying West of the Concord road, in Lancaster County, six mills, if so much be necessary, to be applied to the payment of the expenses of erecting and mainotain‘ng the fences directed to be established; in the County of Laurens, one and one-fifth mills for the past indebtedness of said County as ascertained by the Commission to inquire into the actual bona fide indebtedness of said County and one mill for deficiencies of the County for the past fiscal year, one-half mill for building County fence; in the County of Marion, two mills for past indebtedness of the County, if so much be necessary, in accordance with an Act approved December 24th, 1875, to be applied first to payment of indebtedness prior to passage of said Act, and should any balance remain, to be applied to any past indebtedness in said County; in the County of Marlboro, two mills to pay the past indebtedness of Marlboro County found to be bona fide by a Commission appointed under an Act to ascertain the actual bona fide indebtedness of the various Counties in this State and to regulate the payment of the same, approved June 11, 1877, also one and a fourth mills to pay deficiencies for fiscal year ending October 31st, 1878; in the County of Newberry, one mill for the payment of the funded indebtedness of said County, as provided for by Acts of the General Assembly, and one-half mill for past due school claims to be audited and funded; in the County of Oconee, one mill for past indebtedness of the County; in the County of Orangeburg, one-half of one mill, and no more, for past indebtedness of the County, as now provided by law; in the County of Spartanburg, one mill to retire railroad bonds, and one-half of one mill for past indebtedness; in the County of Sumter, one mill for past indebtedness; in the Counties of Williamsburg and Georgetown, one and one-half mills for past indebtedness. Sec. 3. That all the proceeds of the taxes levied for and on account of the State, as specified herein, shall be deposited and kept by the State Treasurer in such bank or banks or place of special deposit in the cities or towns of this State as in the judgment or discretion of the Financial Board of the State shall afford sufficient protection to the interests of the State; and the State Treasurer shall publish in one of the newspapers published in each of the cities of Columbia and Charleston a monthly statement of all the moneys received by him, and the amount paid out, and to whom, and on account of what appropriation paid, as well as the balance of moneys on hand. Sec. 4. That the County Auditors and County Treasurers of the several Counties of this State are hereby required, under the direction and supervision of the Comptroller General, to make the collection of the taxes levied under and pursuant to the provisions of
this Act in the manner and at the time and under the conditions hereinafter to be provided; and they are hereby forbidden to collect any other tax except the taxes to meet the interest and retire the bonds issued by Counties in aid of railroads (and taxes to build fences under the statutes, and except also the special school tax authorized to be levied in School District No. 14, in the County of Fairfield,) whatsoever for the fiscal year, unless herein expressly authorized so to do; and any State or County officer who shall fail to comply with, or shall evade, or attempt to evade, the provisions of this Act shall he deemed guilty of a felony, and, upon conviction thereof, shall be punished by fine of not less than one thousand dollars nor more than five thousand dollars and by imprisonment in the Penitentiary for a period of not less than ohe year nor-more than five years. Sec. 5. There shall be assessed on all taxable polls in this State a tax of one dollar on each poll, the proceeds of which tax shall be applied solely to educational purposes. Every male citizen between the ages of twenty-one and sixty years, except those incapable of earning a support from being maimed, and except those who are now exempt by law, or from any other cause, shall be deemed taxable polls; and should any person fail or refuse to pay said poll tax, he shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor, and, on conviction thereof before any Trial Justice or other Court having jurisdiction of the same, shall be punished by a fine, which shall not exceed ten dollars, together with the cost of said suit, or by imprisonment in the County jail for a term not exceeding thirty days. In each school district of the County of Orangeburg having a school indebtedness past due there shall be levied and collected a special tax of one-half of one mill on the dollar of the value of the taxable property in such school district, if so much be necessary, to be applied to the payment of the past due indebteduess of the school districts wherein the same shall be collected. Sec. 6. That all taxes assessed and payable under this Act shall be paid in the following kinds of funds and no other: Gold and silver coin, United States currency and national bank notes; and for County taxes, jury and witness certificates in accordance with the Act passed at this session.
Sec. 7. That all taxes assessed herein shall be due and payable in two equal installments as follows: The first installment shall be due and payable from the Ist day of May to the Ist day of June, 1879, and the second installment shall be due and payable from the 15th day of September to the 30th day of October, 1879:
Provided, That it shall be, and is hereby, left to the option of any person either to pay the amount of the first installment at the time first mentioned or to pay the whole amount at the time of the payment of the second installment thereof: Provided, further, That if any person or persons shall fail or refuse to pay his or her taxes due under the first installment, he, she or they shall be charged with a penalty of five per centum on the first installment, which penalty shall be added; and the several County Treasurers shal! collect the same in the manner prescribed by law, and give receipts therefor to the several parties paying the same,.in which the real estate paid on shall be briefly described, and the value of the personal property paid on shall be stated, together with the time such taxes are paid and the amount of same: Provided, further, That in the County of Hampton the whole of the two mill tax levied for the building of a court house and jail shall be due and payable at the time mentioned for the payment of the first installment of the State and County tax; and in case any person or persons shall fail or refuse to pay the same at the time mentioned, he, she or they shall be charged with a penalty of twenty per cent. on the amount so delinquent, to be collected by the County Treasurer as hereinafter prescribed: Provided, Nothing herein contained shall operate to prevent any taxpayer from paying the whole amount of his tax at the time the first installment is payable.
Sec. 8. That the County Commissioners, immediately upon receipt of the tax duplicate for the year from the County Auditor, shall cause a notice to be inserted twice ia one daily newspaper published at the County seat of his County, and if no daily paper be published at such County seat, then in one weekly paper published in such County, and if no paper be published in the County, then such notice shall be given in such manner as the County Treasurer may direct, stating the rate per centum of the levy for State purposes and the rate per centum for all other purposes on the duplicate of the present fiscal year; and if any special levies have been made on the property of aschool or other district not affecting an entire County, the total rate of levies in such district shall also be stated in such notice.
Sec. 9. That when the taxes and assessments charged against any property or party on the duplicate for the present fiscal year shall not be paid on or before the first day of November, 1879, or when the remainder of such taxes and assessments shall not be paid on or by the said time, together with the penalty of five per centum as aforesaid, the County Treasurer shall proceed to collect the same, by distress or otherwise, as now prescribed by law, together with a penalty of fifteen per cent. on the amount so delinquent; and if the amount of such delinquent taxes, assessments and penalties shall not be paid on or before the fifteenth day of November, 1879, or be collected by distress or otherwise, the same shall be treated as the delinquent taxes on such real and personal property, and shall be collected by sale of such real and personal property as hereinafter prescribed.
Sec. 10. All personal property subject to taxation shall be liable to distress and sale for the payment of taxes and assessments hereunder; and at any time after any taxes or assessments shall become due, according to the provisions of this Act, the County Treasurer, by himself or deputy, may distrain sufficient personal property of the party against whom such taxes or assessments are charged, if the same can be found in his County, to pay the taxes or assessments so due, with any penalty charged or chargeable thereupon and the costs that may accrue, and shall immediately advertise the same in three of the most public places in the town or ward or district in which such property shall be distrained, stating the time and place in such town, ward or district when and where such property will be sold; and if the taxes, assessments and penalties for which such property was distrained, together with the cost of the proceeding, shall not be paid before the day appointed for such sale, (which shall not be less than ten nor more than twenty days after posting up such notices of sale,) such Treasurer, or his deputy, shall proceed, at the time and place mentioned in such notice, to sell such property, or so much thereof as may be necessary, at public vendue, to the highest bidder. And if such property, or a sufficient amount thereof, shall not be sold at the time and piace aforesaid, such Treasurer shall retain the same in his possession and advertise and offer the same for sale, in manner and form aforesaid, from time to time, until the same shall be sold.
Sec. 11. All real property returned delinquent by the County Treasurer shall be sold on the first Monday in February, 1880, after due advertisement as now provided by law, and thereafter from day to day until the whole amount thereof as included in the delinquent list; and except as in this Section provided the County Treasurer shall proceed in reference to the sale of such delinquent real estate according to the forms and with the conditions now prescribed and required by law: Provided, That the cost of such advertising shall not exceed twenty-five cents upon each parcel of land so advertised.
Sec. 12. The County Commissioners in each of the several Counties in this State shall levy a tax of two mills on the dollar upon all the taxable property of their respective Counties for the support of public schools in their respective Counties, which shall be collected at the same time and by the same officers as the other taxes for this year, and shall be held in the County treasuries of the respective Counties and paid out exclusively for the support of public schools as provided by law.
