AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ELECTION OF TRUSTEES FOR THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF SWANSEA, IN THE COUNTY OF LEXINGTON.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Lexington State of South Carolina, That the Board of Trustees of the County, to call meeting of taxSchool District of the town of Swansea, in the County of Lexington, shall call a meeting of the taxpayers of the district, to be held not later than July 1st of each year: Provided, That no person shall be allowed to participate or vote at such meeting who is not a bona fide taxpayer and a qualified elector within such district.
SEC. 2. That when the persons duly qualified under Section 1 of this Act shall have assembled in public meeting, they shall have power: First. To select a Chairman and Secretary, adjourn from time to time, decide what special tax, if any, shall be levied, and appropriate the same in such manner as they may think best for the educational interests of said School District: Provided, That no tax levied shall be repealed at any subsequent meeting within the same year. Second. They shall also have power to select a Board of six Trustees, the terms of office of two of whom shall be for three years, dating from the election A. D. 1905, of two of whom for two years, and of the remaining two of whom for one year from the same date; subsequent elections to be held every year thereafter for the term of three years, to fill the vacancies to be caused by expiration of the terms of two of the Board, so that four of each Board may continue in office after each election: Provided, That the appointment by the County Board of Examiners of W. B. Rast, J. L. Jumper and I. W. Hutto is hereby confirmed, and that J. Lee Brooker, P. M. Oliver and Chas. B. Dowling are hereby appointed by this Act, whose term of office shall begin on the 1st of July, 1904, and continue with the first three named Trustees until July, 1905, when there shall be an election for Trustees in said district as provided for in this Act.
SEC. 3. That said Board of Trustees, in addition to the duties and responsibilities now provided by law for Trustees of School Districts, shall have the following powers and duties: 1st. To elect and dismiss superintendents and teachers of schools of the said district, prescribe their duties, terms of their office, to fix salaries, and to cause examination of said teachers to be made whenever they may deem it necessary; also to determine the class of books and studies to be used in said school, and to make rules and regulations for the government of the same. 2. To determine the manner in which the tax heretofore authorized (and the Constitutional and poll tax and other school funds that may come into their hands), shall be expended in maintaining said school. 3. To fill all vacancies occurring in said Board of Trustees by death, resignation, removal or otherwise during the term of office. 4. If so authorized by the said taxpayers ' meeting to charge and collect a supplementary tuition fee not exceeding twenty - five cents per month, or an admission fee not exceeding two dollars, for attendance in said school: Provided, nevertheless, That no child shall be debarred from attending said school through the inability of his or her parents or guardian to pay said tuition or fee: Provided, further, That in any year no such meeting shall be held, or in case at any meeting no action shall be taken in regard to the levy and the collection of the special tax in said School District, it shall nevertheless be the duty of the County Auditor of Lexington County to assess and levy a special school tax of two mills, in addition to the Constitutional school tax, on real and personal property returned in said School District; and the same shall be a lien until paid on all property in said district. And the County Treasurer, or proper officer, shall collect the same with the State and County taxes, in the same manner and subject to the same penalties as are now provided by law for the collection of State and County taxes.
SEC. 4. That all Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed. Approved the 25th day of February, A. D. 1904.
