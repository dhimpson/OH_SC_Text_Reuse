AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ELECTION OF TRUSTEES OF SCHOOL DIS-TRICT NO. 1 OF KERSHAW COUNTY.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the School DisState of South Carolina, That from and after the approval of Kershaw this Act there shall be elected a board of trustees of school county, to elect board district No. 1 of Kershaw county, including the town of Camden. Said board to consist of three members as now provided by law, who shall be qualified electors residing in said district and shall hold office for the term of two years and until their successors are elected and qualified.
SEC. 2. The election herein provided for shall be conducted by three managers to be appointed by the county board of education of Kershaw county, and only the qualified electors residing in said district shall be allowed to vote therein. Said election to be governed by the same rules and regulations as now provided for general elections.
SEC. 3. The first election under the provisions of this Act shall be held on the first day of July, A. D. 1909, and on the first day of July every two years thereafter.
SEC. 4. The present board of trustees of said district shall continue in office until the first election to be held under this Act.
SEC. 5. All Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
