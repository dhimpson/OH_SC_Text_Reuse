AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED " AN ACT TO ESTABLISH A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT TO EMBRACE THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF THE TOWN OF LITTLE MOUNTAIN, IN NEWBERRY COUNTY, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A SCHOOL TAX THEREIN, " APPROVED DECEMBER 24TH, A. D. 1892.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That an Act entitled " An Act to establish a new school district to embrace the corporate limits of the town of Little Mountain, in amended. Newberry County, and to authorize the levy and collection of a school tax therein, " approved December 24th, A. D. 1892, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out Section 7 and inserting the following, to be known as Section 7:
SECTION 7. That the County Auditors of Newberry and Lex - Report by ington Counties shall report to the Treasurer of each County all tors. moneys arising from the school taxes which said school district shall be entitled to under the general provisions of law, and the said County Treasurer shall pay out the same upon the warrant Payment of of the Board of Trustees for said special district, countersigned by the respective School Commissioners of each County. And for the purpose of maintaining a school or schools in said school district for the present fiscal year, each of said Treasurers of the Counties of Newberry and Lexington shall pay to said Chairman of said Board of Trustees in like manner the school tax and poll tax which they, or either of them, have collected or may collect from persons or on property within said school district.
SEC. 2. That this Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
