AN ACT TO SECURE BETTER SCHOOL FACILITIES IN THE TOWN OF PENDLETON.
Whereas, the charter of the Trustees of the Pendleton Female Academy, located at Pendleton, in this State, has expired by limitation; and, whereas, the Board of Trustees now in office, namely: R. W. Simpson, N. M. Hunter, J. C. Stribling, P. H. E. Sloan, Jr., and R. E. Sloan were regularly elected, and now have control of the property of said Academy, which they now desire to sell and to turn the proceeds of sale over to the Trustees of the Pendleton Male Academy, which is also located in the town of Pendleton, for the purpose of securing better facilities for the public school now being conducted in the said Pendleton Male Academy; now, therefore,
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the School DisState of South Carolina, That R. W. Simpson, N. M. Hunter, J. C. Stribling, P. H. E. Sloan, Jr., and R. E. Sloan, now acting as Trustees of the Pendleton Female Academy, be, and they, or a majority of them, are hereby authorized to sell and in feesimple convey the real estate belonging to the Pendleton Female Academy, located in the said town of Pendleton, upon such terms as they may deem advisable, and to turn over the proceeds of sale to the Trustees of the Pendleton Male Academy, A. D. 1904. to be used by them in securing better school house facilities for the white children in the town of Pendleton and for such other white children as are, or may be, entitled by law to attend said school.
SEC. 2. And the better to secure the purpose in view, J. T. Hunter, J. J. Sitton, J. C. Stribling, Samuel McCrary and P. H. E. Sloan, Jr., the present Trustees of the Pendleton Male Academy, whose charter has also expired by limitation, be, and they are hereby, reincorporated under the name of the " Trustees of the Pendleton Academy, " and invested with all the rights and title in and to the real estate belonging to the Pendleton Male Academy, located in the town of Pendleton, and which they now have under their control. Upon the death, resignation or removal of any one of said Trustees, the survivors, or a majority thereof, shall elect a successor or successors from among the white taxpayers residing in the town of Pendleton. The said Trustees shall have the right to sue or be sued, to purchase real estate for the use of said Academy, and to sell and convey the real estate now belonging to or which may hereafter be purchased by the said Trustees of the Pendleton Academy, and shall have all the rights, privileges and responsibilities granted by the original or renewed charters of either or both of the academies, male or female, hereinbefore mentioned, as well as those now or that may hereafter be granted by general laws to any similar institution. And the Trustees of the Pendleton Academy are hereby authorized to receive the proceeds of sale of the real estate of the Pendleton Female Academy, and to use the same in enlarging, repairing or rebuilding, or in securing new school house facilities as aforesaid: Provided, That said Trustees shall always permit its property to be used as a school house and grounds for the white children, male or female, as aforesaid, and said school shall be under the control of the officials of the public schools as now or may hereafter be provided by law.
SEC. 3. The Trustees of Hunter School District may, if they deem it advisable, appropriate a portion of the funds collected in said district by special taxation in aid of securing such improved school house facilities.
