AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED "AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE CITY OF ANDERSON," APPROVED FEBRUARY 9TH, 1882.
SEC. 1. As to trial of offenders against the ordinances.
SEC. 2. Repeal of circus license.
SEC. 3. Contests and protests.
SEC. 4. Opening of streets.
SEC. 5. Cemeteries and their regulation.
SEC. 6. Change in numbering of Sections.
SEC. 7. Repealing clause.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That an Act entitled " An Act to incorporate the city of Anderson, " approved February 9th, 1882, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out Section six thereof and inserting the following in lieu thereof, to wit:
" SECTION 6. The said Mayor shall have power and authority to try all offenders against the ordinances of the said city in a summary manner, without a jury, upon their apprehension or arrest, or as soon thereafter as possible, unless such persons enter into good and sufficient surety, to be approved by the said Mayor, or, in his absence or inability to serve, by the Clerk of the City Council, to appear for trial within five days after such arrest, or at such other time as may be agreed upon, in which event the trial shall be deferred until that time. Whenever the said Mayor shall find a party tried before him guilty of violating an ordinance of the said city, he shall have power to impose in his discretion fine or imprisonment, in the alternative, not to exceed the limits prescribed for such violation. From all decisions of the said Mayor any party in interest feeling himself aggrieved shall have the right of appeal to the City Council: Provided, He give notice of such appeal immediately upon the sentence being pronounced, and enter surety to appear and defend before the City Council at a time to be specified in such undertaking, not less than five nor more than ten days after the date of such undertaking. In all cases appealed to the City Council the Mayor shall preside or some Alderman as herein before provided, and the Alderman shall sit as a jury to try the facts involved, and may also reverse, modify or affirm any or all of the rulings of the Mayor in the first trial of the case. In all cases tried before the Mayor or the City Council the party charged shall Summons in first be served with a summons, if he demand the same, citing him to such trial, in which shall be stated with accuracy the nature of the charge and the time and place of its alleged commission. "
SEC. 2. That Section eleven of said Act be amended by striking out the second proviso thereof, which is in the words following, to wit:
" Provided, That no license for any circus shall be granted for a less sum than three hundred dollars by the said City Council. "
SEC. 3. That the following Section be added to the said Act, to be known as Section 20, to wit:
" SECTION 20. In cases of contest or protest as to any municipal or special election held under this charter or by virtue of the incorporation herein created, the Mayor and Aldermen composing the City Council shall be authorized and empowered to hear and determine the same subject to the right of appeal to the Circuit Court by any party in interest who may feel himself aggrieved thereby: Provided, Notice and grounds of such appeal be served upon the City Council and the opposing party within five days after the announcement of such determination. No Mayor or Alderman interested personally in any way in the event of such contest or protest shall sit in the hearing thereof, and if a majority of the City Council shall be interested therein protests. the Judge of Probate for Anderson County shall hear and deterWho to hear. mine the same, subject to appeal from his decision as herein provided from the determination of the said City Council. "
SEC. 4. That the following be added to said Act, to be known as Section 21 thereof, to wit:
" SECTION 21. If at any time it shall become necessary or advantageous in the judgment of said City Council to open any street over lands whose owner or owners will not consent thereto, the said City Council shall have the right, and they are hereby empowered, to proceed to open such street in the same manner as County Commissioners are authorized to open public high streets. ways under like circumstances. "
SEC. 5. That the said Act be further amended by adding thereto an additional Section to be known as Section 22, to wit:
" SECTION 22. The said City Council of Anderson are hereby authorized and empowered to purchase a suitable tract of land for cemetery purposes for the use of the white inhabitants of Anderson and its vicinity, which tract of land shall be situated near the incorporate limits of said city and shall be known as and styled the Silver Brook Cemetery, and for this purpose the purchase of the tract of land by said City Council outside of the city limits for cemetery purposes and known and styled as above, and all acts done by them pursuant to such purchase, be, and the same are hereby, validated and confirmed as completely and effectually as if the same had been fully and expressly authorized herein before such purchase. The said City Council are also hereby authorized and empowered to purchase a suitable Cemetery for tract of land for cemetery purposes for the use of the colored inhabitants of Anderson and its vicinity, which tract of land shall be situated outside and near the incorporate limits of said city and shall be known as the Mount Tabor Cemetery. ' The City Council shall have the right to regulate interments in said cemeteries, and to this end may lay them off in plats and sell the same on such conditions and subject to such regulations as Powers over they may prescribe, and may permit interment without charge under such regulations as they may prescribe. The said City Council may add to and extend either or both of said cemeteries by purchase from adjoining land owners whenever in their judgment it may be necessary or desirable to do so, and may from time to time expend such sums as they may think proper in adorning and beautifying the grounds of said cemeteries. The City Council shall have full power and control over said cemeteries for the protection and preservation of the same, and may establish by ordinance such rules and regulations for the government thereof as they may deem proper: Provided, They do not conflict with the laws of the land; and to enforce the same and punish any violation thereof the said City Council may provide a police system for said cemeteries, and shall have in all respects the same powers and authority in controlling and governing the same as is now or may hereafter be given them in the city of Anderson, of which incorporation the said cemeteries Offenses comare hereby made a part. All offenses committed in said cemeteries shall be tried and punished in the same manner as offenses against the other ordinances of the said city. "
SEC. 6. That the said Act be further amended by changing the numbers of Sections 20, 21, 22 and 23 so that they shall be known respectively as Sections 23, 24, 25 and 26.
SEC. 7. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act be, and they are hereby, repealed so far as they conflict herewith.
