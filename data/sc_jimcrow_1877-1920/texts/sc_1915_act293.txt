AN ACT TO RECHARTER THE TOWN OF DARLINGTON.
AN ACT to Recharter the Town of Darlington. Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That all persons who are citizens of this State, and who now, or may hereafter, reside within the corporate limits of the town of Darlington, as hereinafter described, shall be deemed, and are hereby declared to be, a body politic and corporate, under the name of the “Town of Darlington.” § 2. That the boundaries or corporate limits of the town of Darlington shall be as follows, to wit: Beginning at a point on South Main street, otherwise known as the Florence Road, one and one-quarter (114) miles distant from the courthouse, and designated by the letter A on a map of the said Town of Darlington made by T. E. Wilson, C. E., under authority of the Town Council of said town, and running thence in a straight line to the point of intersection of the Sumter branch of the Atlantic Coast Line Railroad with Washington street, otherwise known as the Timmonsville Road, designated on the said map by the letter B; thence N. 1° 45’ E., along the northwestern
OF SOUTH CAROLINA. 517 margin of said Washington street a distance of four hundred and sixty (460) feet to a monument designated on said map by the letter C; thence N. 34° 50’ W., a distance of twenty-three hundred and sixty-seven (2,367) feet to a ditch, at a point designated on said map by the letter D; thence N. 34° 15’ W., along said ditch a distance of six hundred and twenty-two (622) feet to the public road leading from Darlington to Lamar, at a point designated on said map by the letter E; thence N. 26° 25’ W., to the run of Indian Branch, at a point designated on said map by the letter F; thence with the run of said Indian Branch to its intersection with Swift Creek; thence with the run of Swift Creek to the point where the Bennettsville branch of the Atlantic Coast Line Railroad crosses the same, designated on said map by the letter G, and thence in a straight line to the beginning point. That the Mayor and Aldermen of the said town shall divide the same into six wards, and shall prescribe the boundaries thereof by ordinance. § 3. That the said town of Darlington, by its corporate name, may sue and be sued, plead and be impleaded in any Court of competent jurisdiction, may contract and be contracted with, may purchase, own, hold, enjoy and possess, for the use of said town, in perpetuity, or for any term of years, any property, real, personal or mixed, may mortgage, sell, alien and convey the same at will, and shall have and keep a common seal. § 4. That the municipal powers of the town of Darlington shall be, and they are hereby, vested in a Mayor and six Aldermen, who shall be denominated the Mayor and Aldermen of the town of Darlington. That the said Mayor and Aldermen shall together constitute the Town Council of the town of Darlington, and, for the sake of brevity, are hereinafter designated as the Town Council. § 5. That the Mayor and Aldermen of said town shall be citizens of the United States and duly qualified electors under the Constitution and laws of the State of South Carolina, who shall have resided within the corporate limits of said town for at least six months next preceding election to such office. That one Alderman shall be elected from each ward separately by the electors of such ward and not by the electors at large of said town; but that the said Mayor shall be elected at large by the direct vote of the qualified electors of said town.

That the next election shall be held on the third Tuesday in May, 1915, and all subsequent elections shall be held every two years thereafter. The newly-elected Mayor and Aldermen shall assume office on the first day of June next after each election, except when the first day of June shall come on Sunday, in which event they shall assume office on the Monday following. All of the present town officials shall hold office until the first day of June, 1915, and until their successors are elected and qualified. At all elections for Mayor and Aldermen of said town all male inhabitants who for four months previous to the day of said election have resided within the corporate limits of said town, and who are duly qualified electors under the Constitution and laws of this State, shall be entitled to vote: Provided, however, That it shall be the duty of the Town Council to require a registration of all voters of said town as a prerequisite to voting at any election for Mayor and Aldermen thereof, and for this purpose full power is hereby vested in the said Town Council to prescribe by ordinance the time, place, manner and terms of such registration: Provided, further, That no new registration shall be required for any special election to be held for any purpose between the regular biennial elections hereinbefore provided for, and that only those persons who were duly qualified and registered at the last preceding regular election shall be entitled to vote at any special election. § 7. That at least twenty-one (21) days’ public notice of any regular or special election hereinbefore provided for shall be given before the day fixed for the holding of such election by publication in some newspaper published within the corporate limits of said town, or by posting copies thereof at three public places within the corporate limits of said town. That three managers of election shall be appointed by the said Town Council for each ward at least twenty-one (21) days before the day fixed for the holding of any such regular or special election for Mayor and Aldermen, or either of them, and that the names of such managers shall be published at the same time and in the same manner as the notice of such election hereinbefore provided for. That all such elections shall be held and conducted by said managers, who, before the polls are opened, shall be sworn by some officer authorized to administer oaths, to conduct such

That if for any reason the election of Mayor and Aldermen hereinbefore provided for shall not be held at the time hereirabefore appointed, it shall be the duty of the said Town Council to order an election to be held as soon as practicable thereafter and to cause due notice of the time and place of such election to be given as hereinbefore provided. The persons who shall be chosen at such election shall qualify and enter upon the duties of the offices to which they have been elected on the Monday following such election § 9. That the Mayor and Aldermen to be elected as above directed shall, before they enter upon the duties of their respective offices, take the oath prescribed by the Constitution of this State, and also the following oath, to wit: “As Mayor (or Aldermen) of the town of Darlington, I will equally, fairly and impartially, to the best of my ability and skill, exercise and discharge the trust reposed in me, and will use my best endeavors to preserve the peace and carry into effect according to law the purposes for which I have been elected. So help me, God.” § 10. That in case a vacancy shall occur in the office of Mayor or Alderman by death, resignation or otherwise an election to fill such vacancy shall be ordered by the Town Council, which election shall be held and conducted in the same manner and after the same notice as hereinbefore provided for regular elections. Should such vacancy occur within sixty days next preceding any regular election it shall be left to the discretion of the Town Council whether or not an election to fill such vacancy shall be held.

That the said Town Council is hereby authorized to enact such ordinances as may be necessary to carry the purposes of this section into full force and effect and to provide for the punishment of all delinquents thereunder. § 19. That the said Town Council shall, by ordinance duly enacted, establish a Municipal Court for the trial and determination of violations of the ordinances of the town of Darlington. § 20. That the said Municipal Court: shall be held by a Recorder, who shall be elected by the said Town Council. The Recorder shall hold his office for a term of four years and until his successor is elected and qualified. The salary of the Recorder shall be fixed by the Town Council, and shall be neither increased nor diminished during his term of office. Before entering upon the discharge of his duties he shall take and subscribe the usual. oath of office. § 21. That the general jurisdiction of, and proceedings in, said Municipal Court shall be the same now or hereafter to be prescribed by the laws of this State for Municipal Courts in towns or cities of the same class, except as herein otherwise provided, and the rules and practice therein shall be the same, so far as possible, as in Magistrates’ Courts of this State. It shall not, however, be necessary for the presiding officers of said Court to reduce to writing the testimony in any case tried before him, unless a demand to that effect be made by the defendant. § 22. That the said Municipal Court shall have jurisdiction to try and determine all cases arising under the ordinances of the town of Darlington.
