AN ACT TO REQUIRE THE CLERK OF THE COURT OF GENERAL SESSIONS TO ENTER UPON THE CALENDAR THE RACE TO WHICH THE INDICTED PERSON BELONGS.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Clerk of court State of South Carolina, That hereafter all the Clerks of the required to Court of General Sessions shall enter upon the calendar of said court the race to which each person indicted in said court belongs.
SEC. 2. That it shall be the duty of the Circuit Judges to enforce law. require the Clerks of Court to comply with the requirements of this Act.
