"AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO DEFINE IN WHAT MANNER TOWNS AND CITIES IN SOUTH CAROLINA MAY INCREASE OR DIMINISH THEIR CORPORATE
LIMITS,"" APPROVED 28TH FEBRUARY, 1896, AS TO THE PETITION AND AS TO VOTING, AND SO AS TO EXTEND THE LIMITS OF BENNETTSVILLE, IN MARLBORO COUNTY."
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Act 1896, XXII, State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to define in what manner towns and cities in South Carolina may increase or diminish their corporate limits, " approved 28th February, 1896, be, and said Act is hereby, amended Amended. as follows: In Section 1 of said Act between the word " annex " and the word " praying " insert the words " or by a majority of the qualified electors of such town or city, " and strike out all between the word " thereof " and the word " if " and insert in lieu thereof the following words: " in a box provided for that purpose, and the qualified electors of the territory proposed to be annexed shall vote in a separate box provided at each poll in the town for that purpose, " and strike out from and including the word " at " down to and including the word " annexation, " and insert in lieu thereof
the words: " cast by the qualified electors of the town and of the territory proposed to be annexed, each aggregated separately, shall be each in favor of annexation, then the Council shall publish, " & c., and by striking out the word " resident " between " the " and " freeholders, " and by striking out in Section 1 " or by a majority of the qualified electors of said town or city, and by inserting after the word “ box ” and before the word " provided " the words " to be, " and by striking out after the word " provided " " at each poll in the town " and insert in lieu thereof the words " for that purpose within the territory proposed to be annexed, " and by adding after the word " annexation " the words " or if neither give a majority against annexation. " So that said Section when so amended shall read as follows:
Section 1. Any Town or City Council shall have power of cities and to extend the corporate limits of said city or town in the following manner: A petition shall first be submitted to said Council by a majority of the freeholders of the territory which it is proposed to annex, praying that an election be ordered to see if such territory shall be included in said town. The said Town Council shall order an election after not less than ten days ' public advertisement. At such election the qualified electors of the municipality shall vote at the usual voting precincts thereof in a box provided for that purpose, and the qualified electors of the territory proposed to be annexed shall vote in a separate box to be provided for that purpose within the territory proposed to be annexed. If a majority of the votes cast by the qualified electors of the town and of the territory proposed to be annexed, each aggregated separately, shall be each in favor of annexation, or if neither give a majority against annexation, then the Council shall publish the result of said election and declare the annexed territory a part of said town: Provided, however, That if the property sought to be annexed belongs to a corporation only, it may be annexed on the petition of the stockholders of said corporation. Any town increasing its territory shall file a notice with the Secretary of State describing its new boundaries.
SECTION 2. That there be added to said Act another Section, to be designated as Section 4, which shall read as follows:
Section 4. That immediately after the approval of this Act the corporate limits of the town of Bennettsville, in Marlboro County, be,nd the same are hereby, extended so as to conform to the following lines on its Southern and Southwestern boundaries: Beginning at large sweet gum, corner on Darlington street on Western boundary, and running thence S. 490 E. 58.50 chains to post on East side of C. and D. Railroad track marking yard limits; thence N. 45º E. 44 chains to lightwood stake, corner between C. and D. Railroad terminal property and J. H. Hudson's land; across Main street, thence up said street to a point seventy yards South of the side track of the C. and D. Railroad, the corner lot of A. J. Mathewson; thence N. 660 E. 52.70 chains, thence N. 100 W. 16.55 chains to the Fayetteville road at the point where the Eastern limit now crosses same.
