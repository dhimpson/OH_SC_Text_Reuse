AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION 142 OF CRIMINAL CODE OF SOUTH CAROLINA, SO AS TO REQUIRE THE COUNTIES IN WHICH THE CASES THEREIN REFERRED TO ORIGINATE TO BEAR THE EXPENSE OF PAYING WITNESSES.

ACT 443:
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Section 142 of the Criminal Code of South Carolina be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the words " State Treasurer, " on line 17 of said Section, and inserting in lieu thereof the words " County Treasurer of the County in which said case originated; " so that said Section, when so amended, shall read as follows:
Section 142. In the case of any prisoner lawfully in the Penalty to officer from charge, custody or control of any officer, State, County or whom a prisoner is taken. municipal, being seized and taken from said officer through his negligence, permission or connivance, by a mob or other unlaw-ful assemblage of persons, and at their hands suffering bodily A. D. 1908. violence or death, the said officer shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor, and, upon true bill found, shall be deposed from To be prosehis office pending his trial, and, upon conviction, shall forfeit his office, and shall, unless pardoned by the Governor, be ineligible to hold any office of trust or profit within this State. It shall be the duty of the prosecuting attorney within whose Circuit or County the offense may be committed to forthwith institute a prosecution against said officer, who shall be tried in such County in the same Circuit, other than the one in which the offense was committed, as the Attorney General may elect. The fees and mileage of all material witnesses, both for the State and the defense, shall be paid by the County Treasurer of the County in which said case originated on a certificate issued by the Clerk and signed by the presiding Judge, showing the amount of said fee due the witness.
