AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND EMPOWER TRUSTEES OF CLEMSON AGRICUL-TURAL AND MECHANICAL COLLEGE TO PURCHASE ADJACENT LANDS AND PROVIDE MEANS OF PAYMENT FOR SAME.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the au State of South Carolina, That the trustees of Clemson Agricultural and Mechanical College of South Carolina be, and they are hereby, authorized and empowered to purchase land adjacent to said college, and to pay for same out of the general fund of said college or with any funds that may be available for such purpose: Provided, That the title to said property shall be taken in the name of the State of South Carolina.
