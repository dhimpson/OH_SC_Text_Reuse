AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND EMPOWER SCHOOL DISTRICT No. 52, NEWBERRY COUNTY, IN THE STATE OF SOUTH CAROLINA, TO ISSUE BONDS FOR PURCHASE OF A LOT AND THE ERECTION AND EQUIPMENT OF A SCHOOL BUILDING THEREON; TO PROVIDE FOR A BUILDING COMMITTEE AND TO PROVIDE FOR THE PAYMENT OF SAID BONDS AND THE INTEREST THEREON.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the School District State of South Carolina, That School District No. 52, embracing the village of Whitmire, in the County of Newberry, is hereby authorized, empowered and required to issue coupon bonds in an amount not to exceed the sum of six thousand dollars for the purpose of purchasing grounds and erecting, maintaining, furnishing and equipping a proper school house, to be located in said district, upon grounds to which the district must have a good and legal title in fee simple: Provided, A majority of the qualified electors in the said district shall be in favor of such issue, as may be expressed at an election hereafter to be held for that purpose: And Provided, further, That at the said election two legal voters of said district are to be elected, who, with the Trustees of said district, shall constitute a Building Committee, as provided for in the following Sections.
SEC. 2. That the Trustees of the said district, immediately Election. upon the passage of this Act, are required to give fifteen days notice in one or more of the newspapers published in Newberry County, of the time and place of such election, appoint managers, prescribe forms of ballot, receive the returns and declare the election results.
SEC. 3. That the said School District be, and hereby is, auTerms of bonds thorized and empowered to issue coupon bonds in a sum not to exceed six thousand dollars, of the denomination of three hundred dollars each, the bonds to be sold at face value and the interest not to exceed six per cent. per annum from the date of issue, and running for a series of twenty years, one bond and the annual interest on the whole issue outstanding to be paid each year until the whole amount of principal and interest shall be paid in full. That the said bonds and interest coupons are tobe signed by the said Building Committee: Provided, That the signature of said Committee may be lithographed upon said coupons, and such lithographing shall be sufficient signing of the said coupons of said bonds.
SEC. 4. That said bonds shall be sold by the said Building Committee, either at public or private sale, as in the judgment of said Committee may be best, and that the proceeds of the said bonds shall be paid over to the County Treasurer of said County, and shall be kept separate by him subject to the order of a majority of said Building Committee, to whose order it shall be paid.
SEC. 5. That for the purpose of paying the said bonds and the interest thereon, so much as three mills on the dollar of the special tax which is now being levied upon the taxable property of the taxpayers of the said district is to be placed upon the duplicate of this County by the County Auditor and collected by the County Treasurer, as other taxes are collected, and at the same time, and shall be kept separate by him and paid out upon the order of the School Trustees of the said district, who are hereby required to issue their warrants in favor of the holders of the said bonds and coupons when due, approved by the County Auditor and School Superintendent, and the payment of said tax shall be enforced in the same manner as the payment of other taxes. Which annual tax shall continue until the General Assembly shall otherwise provide. And in case of the neglect or failure of the County Auditor or any other official to perform the duties herein required to be performed by him, or either of them, the holder or holders of any of said bonds may enforce them by mandamus, either in the Circuit or Supreme Court of the State.
SEC. 6. That the said Building Committee, in addition to the mittee. power to them delegated in foregoing Sections, shall have full power and authority in selecting plans, employing and paying an architect, taking builder's bond, and in passing upon and receiving the building when properly constructed, and in all other matters that may be necessary to the full carrying out of this Act. That should a vacancy occur upon said Committee, that same shall be immediately filled by an appointment of a member of the Board of Education of Newberry County.
SEC. 7. That any balance of the proceeds of said bonds which may remain after the erection and equipment of said building, Surplus. and of said tax remaining after the payment of the annual interest and coupons of the said bonds, shall be placed to the credit of the said district, and may be used by the Trustees thereof for the payment of the teachers of the said School District or for ordinary school purposes therein.
SEC. 8. This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
