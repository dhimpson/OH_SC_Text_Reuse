AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT IN EDGEFIELD COUNTY (NOW SALUDA COUNTY), AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A SPECIAL SCHOOL TAX THEREIN,"" APPROVED DECEMBER 24TH, A. D. 1888, BY STRIKING OUT SECTIONS 2, 3 AND 4 OF SAID ACT, AND INSERT-ING IN LIEU THEREOF OTHER SECTIONS, TO BE KNOWN AS SECTIONS 2, 3, 4, 5 AND 6; AND TO REPEAL ALL ACTS AND PARTS OF ACTS AMENDA-TORY TO THE AFORESAID ACT, SO AS TO INCREASE THE NUMBER OF TRUSTEES FOR SAID SCHOOL DISTRICT, PROVIDE FOR THEIR ELECTION, AND TO DEFINE THEIR QUALIFICATIONS FOR OFFICE, THEIR POWERS AND DUTIES.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to provide for the establishment of a new school district in Edgefield district in County (now Saluda County) and to authorize the levy and County. collection of a special school tax therein, " approved December 24th, A. D. 1888, be amended by striking out all of Sections 2, 3 and 4, and inserting in lieu thereof the following Sections, to be known as Sections 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, respectively:
Section 2. That the number of the present Board of Public Board of Trustees, consisting of J. D. Jones, J. O. Jones and W. H. Stuckey, is hereby increased to seven, and that J. W. Thrailkill, F. G. Asbill, J. S. Elkins and Benjamin Boatright, Jr., are hereby made members of said Board of Trustees. That one of whom shall serve until the first Monday in August, 1910, and three shall serve until the first Monday in August, 1911. That the tenure of office of each shall be determined by lot. That the terms of office of J. O. Jones, J. B. Jones and W. H. Stuckey are hereby extended from July 7, 1909, until the first Monday in August, 1909. That all vacancies caused by the expiration of terms of office shall be filled by a vote of to be filled the qualified voters of said school district. That at least three by vote. weeks prior to the first Monday of August, in 1909, and annually thereafter, the said Board of Trustees shall post, for two consecutive weeks in three different public places, in said school district, notices of the election of Trustees to fill such vacancies as may exist at the time of said election. Said election shall be held by three freeholders of said district, who shall be appointed by the Board of Trustees, and they shall declare the result of said election. That only those qualified in Section 1208, of Volume I, Code of Laws of South Carolina (1902), shall be permitted to vote in said election. That whenever a vacancy shall occur by reason of death, resignation, removal from the school district, or for any other cause, such vacancies shall be filled by the Board of Trustees. That only bona fide citizens and electors shall be eligible to membership in said Board. That members duly elected under the provisons of this Section shall serve three years, or until their successors are elected and qualified.
Sec. 3. That the said Board of Trustees shall have the power: (1) To sue and be sued; (2) to contract and be contracted with; (3) to buy, sell, exchange, lease or repair any real or personal property necessary for school purposes; (4) to pay all debts which the district now owes; (5) to appoint a finance committee of three, who shall have the power to countersign all warrants for the usual school expenses; (6) to fix the rate of tuition of non - resident pupils; (7) to fix and collect any matriculation charges that may be necessary; (8) to exercise any authority, not mentioned above or in the general law governing public school trustees, that may be conducive to the better maintenance of the schools of said district.
Sec. 4. That said Board of Trustees shall determine, on or mine rate of before the 30th day of June each year, or as soon thereafter as practicable, what rate of taxation will be necessary, in addition to the constitutional three mill tax, the poll tax, the dog tax, and any other funds or taxes to which the district is or may be entitled, to operate the schools for the school year following the said 30th day of June, and to pay whatever debts the district may owe: Provided, Said rate does not exceed five (5) mills. That the Board of Trustees shall notify the County Auditor of this rate of levy, whose duty it shall be to enter said amount of levy in his tax duplicate. That it shall be the duty of the County Treasurer to collect, at the following tax gathering time, said tax in the same manner as County, State and otherspecial levies are now collected: Provided, That said power of A. D. 1908. taxation may be repealed in the same manner as is now provided for by law for the repeal of special school levies. That said tax shall be a lien on all real and personl property of the taxpayers of said school district. That the taxes so collected shall be held by County Treasurer, whose bond shall be liable for any default; and said taxes shall be paid out by said County Treasurer upon the warrant of the Finance Committee for said school district and countersigned by the County Superintendent of Education.
Sec. 5. That upon the written petition of five or more taxpayers on real or personal property, the present Board of Pubratification or rejection lic Trustees, consisting of J. B. Jones, J. O. Jones and W. H. of this Act. Stuckey, within six months after the approval of this Act, shall call an election for the purpose of ratifying or rejecting the provisions of the foregoing and following Sections of this Act. That said Trustees shall serve upon the qualified voters of said school district for two consecutive weeks prior to the election, a notice of said election, both through the newspaper at the County seat and by posting notices in three public places in said school district. That those qualified in Section 1208, of Volume I, Code of Laws of South Carolina (1902), to vote in special elections of tax levies shall vote in this election. That said Board of Trustees shall appoint, from the freeholders in said district, managers for this election. That said managers shall prepare ballots with " Yes " on them and other ballots with " No " on them. If a majority of the ballots cast in said election contain " Yes " on them, all of the provisions of this Act shall, at once, be in full force and binding upon the taxpayers of said school district. That should the election be unfavorable to this Act, the said Board of Trustees shall have the power of calling other elections by conforming to the requirement of this Section for calling an election for the purpose.
Sec. 6. That the said Board of Trustees of seven members is hereby authorized to receive and use gifts, transfers, beBoard. quests or devises of property for corporate and school purposes, whether they be otherwise conditional or whether absolute in their terms; also to issue and validate coupon bonds within the constitutional limit and to dispose of the same to raise money for the purpose of purchasing or leasing sites and the erection of buildings thereon, or for the purpose of purchasing, leasing, or repairing property suitable for school or dormitory purposes: Provided, The question of amount of issue and the rate of interest, and the time or times of payment of the principal shall first be submitted to the qualified voters within the said school district who return real or personal property for taxation, at an election to be held in the same manner as elections for special levies for school district purposes are now required to be submitted under Section 1208, of Volume I, Code of Laws of South Carolina (1902): Provided, That a written petition of one - third of the freeholders of said school district be submitted to the said Public School Trustees; And Provided, further, That an annual interest of said issue shall not exceed six per cent. per annum, and that the sale of said bonds shall not be for less than par and accrued interest. SEC. 2. That all Acts and parts of Acts amendatory of the said Act entitled " An Act to provide for the establishment of a new school district in Edgefield County (now Saluda County), and to authorize the levy and collection of a special school tax therein, " be, and they are hereby, repealed.
SEC. 3. This Act shall take effect upon its approval by the Governor.
