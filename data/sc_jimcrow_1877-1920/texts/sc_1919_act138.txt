AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF SCHOOL TRUSTEES IN YORK COUNTY.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State, That hereafter all School Trustees for school districts lying wholly without any incorporated town or city in the county of York shall be appointed by the County Board of Education. § 2. Act Effective on Approval—Repealing Clause.—That this Act shall take effect upon its approval. All Acts or parts of Acts in conflict with the provisions hereof are hereby repealed.
