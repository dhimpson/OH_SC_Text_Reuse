AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION 2 OF AN ACT ENTITLED “AN ACT TO CREATE A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT WITHIN THE COUNTY OF BARNWELL TO BE KNOWN AS THE ELKO GRADED SCHOOL DISTRICT AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A LOCAL TAX THEREIN," APPROVED DECEMBER 23,
1891.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Section 2 of an Act entitled “An Act to create a new school district within the county of Barnwell to be known as the Elko graded school district and to ” authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, approved December 23, 1891, be, and the same is hereby amended by striking out the whole of the said section and substituting therefor the following:
Sec. 2. That three (3) trustees for said school district shall be elected by the qualified electors residing within the said district, whose term of office shall be two years and until their successors are elected and qualified, said election to be held on the second Tuesday in May, 1909, and on the same day every second year thereafter.
