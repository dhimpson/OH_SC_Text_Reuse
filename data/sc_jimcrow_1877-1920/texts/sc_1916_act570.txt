AN ACT AUTHORIZING AND EMPOWERING THE RECENT DISPENSARY BOARD OF BAMBERG COUNTY TO WIND UP THE AFFAIRS OF THE BAMBERG COUNTY DISPENSARY.
Section 1. Winding Up Commission for Bamberg County— Powers.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That J. M. Grimes, J. B. Kearse and W. H. Faust, composing the recent Dispensary Board for Bamberg county, and J. S. Walker, former clerk of said board, be, and they are hereby, created a commission to wind up the affairs of Bamberg county dispensary; and they are hereby authorized and empowered to receive all funds now belonging to said dispensary, and are hereby directed to pay any indebtedness due and owing by said dispensary and to pay out the balance of all funds coming into their hands as profits realized from the operation of said dispensary in accordance with the law of force on the last day of December, A. D. 1915. § 2. Disposition of Books and Records.—Said Commission shall, immediately after winding up the affairs of said dispensary, deliver to the Clerk of Court for Bamberg county all books and records relating to the affairs of said dispensary and the same shall be safely held by said Clerk of Court subject to inspection by the Grand Jury or any member thereof or any authorized officer of the law. § 3. Immediately Effective.—This Act shall take effect on approval by the Governor.
