AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE CITY OF ABBEVILLE.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That from and after the passage of this Act all persons who shall have been domiciled in this State one year, and who may have resided within the corporate limits of the town of Abbeville sixty days, and their successors, are hereby deCreation, name clared to be a body politic and corporate, under the name of the city of Abbeville, and the corporate limits of the said city shall extend one mile and a quarter in every direction from the court house in said town as a centre.
Sec. 2. That the said city is hereby divided into four wards, as follows: The territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Southwest and a line drawn along the centre of Washington and Cambridge streets on the Southeast shall be known as Ward One; the territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Washington and Cambridge streets on the Northwest and a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Southwest shall be known as Ward Two; the territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Northeast and a line drawn along the centre of Pinckney and Ferry streets on the Northwest shall be known as Ward Three; the territory lying between a line drawn along the centre of Pinckney and Ferry streets on the Southeast and a line drawn along the centre of Main street on the Northeast shall be known as Ward Four.
Sec. 3. That the said city shall be governed by a Mayor Mayor and and eight Aldermen, who shall be citizens of the United Aldermen. States, qualified electors of said city, who shall have been
domiciled in the State one year and in the city sixty days next before their election, and shall continue in office for two years from the day of their election, and until their successors are elected and qualified; that the Mayor and one Election and Alderman for each ward shall be elected during one year, and one Alderman for each ward the next year, and so on in each alternate year; that the present Intendant and four Wardens of the town of Abbeville shall be the Mayor and four of the Aldermen of the city of Abbeville until the regular city election, which shall be held on the second Tuesday in April, 1896, and that one Alderman from each ward shall be elected on the second Tuesday in April, 1895, as hereinafter provided, and that thereafter the regular city election shall be held on the second Tuesday in April of each year; that the Mayor and Aldermen shall take the oath of office prescribed by the Constitution of this State before entering on the discharge of their official duties, and a majority of Quorum. the Council shall constitute a quorum to transact business; that the Mayor shall be paid for his services such compensaMayor's salary. tion as the City Council by ordinance fix, which shall not be diminished during his term of office; that in case the Mayor be temporarily absent or unable to attend to the duties of his office, from sickness or other cause, the Aldermen shall have power to elect any one of their number to serve as Mayor pro tempore; that the City Council shall hold stated Mayor pro tem. meetings at least once in each month, and such other meetings as it may desire; that the Mayor shall nominate at the commencement of his term, subject to the approval of the City Council by vote, such committees to transact the public business as the Council may by ordinance determine. That the City Council shall elect a Clerk and Treasurer, a Superintendent of Streets, such policemen as may be necessary, and such other officers as may be necessary to transact the business of the city; and it may by ordinance prescribe the duties of said officers and may fix the salaries of the same and require bonds of them for the faithful performance of their duty; that the policemen shall take the oath prescribed by law for Constables, and shall have all the powers and be charged with all the liabilities now or hereafter incident to the office of Constable; and they may arrest and commit to the guard house of the city for speedy trial any person who, within the corporate limits, may be engaged in a breach of the peace, riotous or disorderly conduct, open obscenity, public drunkenness, or any conduct grossly indecent or dangerous to the citizens of said city. All of said officers shall be liable to removal at the pleasure of the Council. That the Clerk of Council shall annually, on the first day of April, make out and publish on the court house door in said city, and file in the office of Council, an itemized statement of the receipts and disbursements of the city during the preceding year; and when a new Council has been elected and sworn in, then the outgoing Council shall forthwith turn over to it all the records, property and funds belonging to the said city, and a failure to do so without reasonable excuse for twenty - four hours after a demand, in writing, so to do shall subject the members of the outgoing Council to a fine of five hundred dollars, to be recovered by the City Council for the use of said city in any Court of competent jurisdiction.
Sec. 4. That the election for Mayor and Aldermen hereinbefore provided for shall be held, at such place as may be designated by the City Council, from nine o'clock A. M. until four o'clock P. M., and that at least ten days ' notice thereof be given by publication in a newspaper at Abbeville; that such election shall be conducted by three Managers to be appointed by the City Council, one of whom shall be Chairman and another clerk, and all of whom shall, before entering on their duties, take an oath to fairly and impartially conduct the election; that the said Managers, or any of them, shall administer to every person offering to vote at Oath for voter. the election an oath that he is duly qualified to vote at such election according to the Constitution and laws of this State, and has not voted before at this election; that said Managers shall keep a correct list of the names of the persons voting and of those offering to vote at such election, shall count the ballots, cast up the result and proclaim the election and file the lists and results with the Clerk of Council, who shall forthwith notify in writing the persons who have received a plurality of the ballots cast; that in the event no candidate should receive a plurality of the votes cast, or in the event of a vacancy in office caused by death, resignation or otherwise, another election shall be ordered in like manner as the first, but one registration in any one year shall be sufficient; that in said elections all male citizens over the age of twenty - one years who have been domiciled in the State one year and in the city sixty days next before the election, and who have been registered as hereinafter provided, and who are not disqualified by law, shall be entitled to vote.
Sec. 5. That the Clerk of Council shall annually, on the second Tuesday in March and the Wednesday and Thursday succeeding, between the hours of nine o'clock A. M. and three o'clock P. M., at the office of said Clerk, open a list for Registration. the registration of qualified voters of said city, on which list shall be shown in writing the name, age, occupation and ward of residence of the voter, and any person who shall attain the age of twenty - one years after the said time of registration and before the next succeeding day of election, and is not otherwise disqualified, shall be allowed to register at such registration. Notice of said registration shall be given for two weeks previous thereto by publication in one or more newspapers published in said city of Abbeville and by posting on the court house door. The certificate of registration shall be signed by said Clerk and issued to each person registering in the following form:
This is to certify that... of Ward...... of the city of Abbeville... years of age, and entitled to vote in the municipal election of said city. Clerk of Council.
After each municipal election only such persons shall be required to register as shall become entitled to vote at each Subsequent approaching election. At each subsequent registration the said Clerk shall revise the registration lists and erase therefrom the names of all registered voters who have died or become disqualified to vote since the previous election. The said registration lists shall at all times be open to the inspection of the qualified voters of the city, shall be carefully preserved by the Clerk of Council, and on annual or any special elections shall be placed in the possession of the Managers appointed to conduct the election, and at the close of such election shall be returned by said Managers to said Clerk.
Sec. 6. That the Mayor and Aldermen collectively and severally are hereby vested with the jurisdiction and powers within the city limits which Trial Justices now have or may hereafter have, except in civil actions; that they shall be designated as the City Council of Abbeville; may have a common seal; may sue and be sued; may purchase or receive by gift or devise, and hold, personal and real property, not to exceed in value in the aggregate at any one time $ 100,000.00, and may sell, alien or lease the same; may borrow money and execute evidences of debt, but such debts shall not exceed $ 10,000 at any one time; may make such ordinances respecting sidewalks, streets, drainage, natural water courses, powder house or houses for storing explosives, the interment of human bodies, the running of railroad cars or engines, butchers, livery stables, public drays or vehicles, live stock, places of amusement, public houses, saloons, auctioneers, itinerant tradesmen or peddlers, shows, circuses or exhibitions, and such other matters pertaining to the concerns of said corporation as shall be proper, necessary or advisable for the preservation or safety of the peace, order, comfort, health, convenience and welfare of the citiFines and zens of said city; may fix and impose fines and penalties for the violation of law or its ordinances, but no fine or penalty shall exceed one hundred dollars or imprisonment in the guard house for a longer period than thirty days, or by sentence to work on the streets for thirty days, and the judgment may be in the alternative; and may abate and remove all nuisances in the said city..
Sec. 7. That the said City Council shall annually, during the month of August, appoint three citizens of the said city to assess in that month the value of all the taxable property therein, and the said appraisers shall ascertain from Assessments. the taxpayers in person the true value of all their real and personal and mixed estates situated in said city, and enter the same in a book in separate columns, and it shall be signed by the appraisers and returned to the Clerk of Council forthwith; but the taxpayers shall have thirty days from the return of said assessment in which to complain thereof to the City Council, upon the entering of which the Council may lessen any assessment upon good cause shown. That upon the values so ascertained the City Council shall, during the month of October, levy a tax for corporate purposes not to exceed in any year five mills on the dollar, payable in advance to the Clerk and Treasurer of the Council during the months of November and December in each year: Provided, That the provisions of this Section shall not interfere with the levy and collection of the taxes for the town of Abbeville for the present year during the months of February and March next, but shall go into effect immediately thereafter; and all property upon which such taxes shall be levied shall be subject to a first lien for the payment thereof, except for debts due to the State; and upon failure of any person to pay such tax, the party in fault shall be
subject to the penalties and remedies provided, or which may hereafter be provided, by law for failure to pay State and County taxes, except that the execution for the collection thereof shall be signed and sealed by the Clerk and Treasurer of the Council and directed to a police officer of the said city.
Sec. 8. That the said City Council may grant or refuse licenses to keepers of livery stables, drivers of public drays, hacks and vehicles kept for hire, butchers, auctioneers, peddlers, itinerant tradesmen, keepers of billiard tables, tenpin alleys, or any other kind of game, exhibitions of skill or chance within said corporation, and the said City Council may impose a license fee on any of the said occupants, and also on any person who keeps a store, shop, office or other place of business and carries on his trade, profession or business in said city but resides and holds his property beyond the limits of said city, thus escaping his or her just share of the burdens of the city government; and said City Council may enforce the collection of said license fees, which shall be turned into the city treasury
Sec. 9. That persons charged with the violation of law or the ordinances of said city shall undergo trial before the Trials. Mayor or before the Council, as the defendant may elect, and with or without the intervention of a jury; that the Mayor or Council may require the attendance of witnesses and require them to give testimony upon the trial of any cause then pending, and shall be governed by the same rules of procedure and evidence as are now or may hereafter be prescribed for the government of the Courts of Trial Justices; and the party charged with an offense shall be cited to trial by the service upon him of a summons under the Summons. hand of the Mayor, unless the offense is committed in full view of an officer, wherein shall be specified the offense charged and the time and place of trial and wherein the City Council shall be plaintiff; that the said Council shall keep a book wherein shall be entered the names of parties Docket. charged with the violation of law or ordinances, the date of trial and the judgment in such case; that fines so imposed may be collected by an execution issued under the hand and seal of the Clerk and Treasurer, tested by the corporate seal and directed to a policeman of the corpora-
tion, who shall seize such property of the defendant as shall be subject to levy and sale and sell in a public place the same to satisfy the judgment, after two weeks ' advertisement on the court house door.
Sec. 10. That the said City Council is hereby invested Jurisdiction with ample jurisdiction over the streets, alleys and sidewalks within the corporate limits; to extend or close, widen or narrow, lay out, grade, curb, pave and otherwise improve streets, alleys and sidewalks; to build bridges in and culverts under said streets; to prevent or remove any structure or obstruction or encroachment over or in or under said streets, alleys or sidewalks, and may permit the planting of shade trees along said streets; to prevent any company or persons from occupying with its or their works, or any appurtenances thereof, the streets, alleys or sidewalks of the city without the consent of Council; to prevent the cumbering of streets, sidewalks, alleys, lanes or bridges in the city in any manner whatever; and for the purpose of opening new streets or of widening or changing existing streets, the Condemnation. City Council may condemn lands or buildings, or parts thereof, but just compensation therefor shall first be made to the owner, and if the City Council and such owner fail to agree upon the amount of such compensation the same may be fixed as follows, to wit: The City Council shall nominate two citizens of the corporation, the owner of the property shall nominate two other citizens of the corporation, and these four shall nominate a fifth other citizen of the corporation, and these five citizens shall constitute a commission to examine the locus in quo, to take testimony and assess the compensation, after having first been sworn to decide justly and impartially in the premises; and they shall file their decision with the Clerk of the Circuit Court for the County of Abbeville within ten days from the time of their appointment, and it shall be final unless either party appeals therefrom to the Court of Common Pleas for said County by serving on the other party within five days after the filing of such decision a notice of appeal; and in that case the issue of compensation shall be submitted to a jury at the term of the Circuit Court next thereafter ensuing. In the event the property owner refuses to nominate two commissioners or fails to do so within five days after he is notified to do so, then they shall be nominated by the Clerk of the Circuit Court for the County of Abbeville, and the fifth commissioner shall be chosen as before. The said commissioners shall be entitled to receive one dollar each for their services, to be paid by the City Council, but the costs of appeal shall be paid by the losing party. That for the purpose of keeping the streets, alleys, sidewalks, bridges, drains and culverts in repair, or for laying out and constructing new streets, all the male citizens of said corporation between the ages of sixteen and fifty - five years shall render to the City Council personal service therein for at least five days in each year, at such time as they may be called on by the corporate authorities; but such services may be commuted by the payment of two and 50-100 dollars to compound tax. the Treasurer of the city at the time of the annual payment of taxes, herein before provided for, to be expended for like purposes; and in the event any person liable to perform such service shall refuse or fail to render the same, after notice of the time and place to serve, the said City Council or the Mayor shall have the power to try such person for such offense, and if found guilty to sentence him to as much as thirty days ' imprisonment or thirty days ' labor on the streets or to pay a fine of as much as thirty dollars, to be collected as other fines are collected. Members of the City Council shall be exempt from such duty, and all citizens of Privileges of said corporation shall be exempt from road duty without the corporation. That whenever any new street is to be laid out or opened, a street graded or paved, a culvert built or any other public improvement constructed, the City Council shall determine what proportion, if any, of the cost Cost of streets. thereof shall be paid out of the city treasury and what proportion by the owners of real estate to be benefited thereby; but no such public improvement shall be made, to be defrayed in whole or in part by local assessment, until it has been requested to be undertaken by at least a majority of the owners of the property to be assessed for such improvement; and such assessments shall be collected in the same manner as is the cost of the construction of sidewalks. That the said City Council may require all persons owning and occupying a lot or lots on any street or alley in the said city to construct and keep in good repair sidewalks abutting such lot or lots, and the Council may designate the width of such sidewalks and the manner of their construction; and in the event the owner shall refuse or neglect to comply with such requirement or regulation, the City Council may repair or construct the said sidewalks and recover from the owner the cost thereof in a Court of competent jurisdiction; but the contract for such repairs or construction must first have been let out to the lowest bidder after notice by one publication in a newspaper in said city.
Sec. 11. That the said City Council may establish and Fire departmaintain a fire department for the extinguishment of fires, and to that end may purchase engines and pumps and hose, and construct tanks and reservoirs, and dig wells and lay pipes; that it may secure the services of firemen, either by exemption from public municipal duties, or by payment of money, and to this end it may pass all proper ordinances and make appropriations from the city treasury for the establishment and support of such department. And the said City Council shall have full power to prevent the erecWooden buildtion of any wooden or frame building, or any wooden roof, in any section of said city which they may by ordinance define; and they are empowered to pass ordinance to carry into effect the provisions of this Section, and may abate such buildings as a nuisance.
Sec. 12. That the said City Council may construct, have Water works. constructed or purchase public works for the supply of pure water to the citizens of said corporation, may operate the same, and may sell the water to consumers; may construct, have constructed or purchase public works for the generaGas or electric tion of gas or electricity for purposes of light, may operate the same, and sell light to consumers; and to these ends it may pass all ordinances necessary or proper for the regulaNo bonds, untion and control of such works: Provided, That no bonded obligation shall be incurred by said city until that question has been submitted to a popular election therein, and shall have been approved by a majority of the voters then voting. That the said City Council shall have the power to grant a franchise or franchises for the erection or construction of such light and water works to private parties or corporation upon such terms and restrictions, to be incorporated in such grant, as may be necessary and proper to secure and protect the interests of said city and its citizens. But no such grant shall be made for a longer period than thirty years.
Sec. 13. That this Act shall take effect from and after its passage; that all other Acts conferring powers on the town Consistent Acts of Abbeville, and not herein referred to nor inconsistent with this Act, are continued of full force; that the ordiAlso ordinances. nances of the town of Abbeville are hereby continued, to be of full force and effect for the government of the city of Abbeville until repealed or modified by other ordinances by the City Council; all the obligations and contracts of the town of Abbeville and to the town of Abbeville are hereby continued as the valid and subsisting obligations and contracts of and to the city of Abbeville; and all the property of the town of Abbeville is hereby vested in the city of Abbeville.
Sec. 14. That this Act is a public Act, and shall continue of force for thirty years next hereafter; and all Acts or pealing clause. parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
