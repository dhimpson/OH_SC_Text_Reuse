AN ACT TO PROVIDE THAT SPARTANBURG COUNTY SHALL ISSUE FOUR HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS IN BONDS FOR PERMANENT HIGHWAY IMPROVEMENT UPON THE SAME BEING APPROVED BY THE QUAL-
IFIED ELECTORS OF SAID COUNTY, AND TO CREATE A HIGHWAY COM-MISSION FOR SAID COUNTY.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the burg county State of South Carolina, That Spartanburg county shall issue to issue four hundred thousand dollars in registered or coupon bonds for permanent highway improvement. Said bonds shall be of the denomination of one thousand dollars each and shall draw interest at the rate of not more than four and one - half per cent. per annum, payable on the first day of January and July of each year.
SEC. 2. The said bonds shall be signed by the county superBonds; how visor and attested by the clerk of the board of county commissioners and delivered to the public highway commission hereinafter provided for. The lithographing of the names of the said officers upon the coupons, if coupon bonds are issued, shall be deemed the signing thereof, but the bonds themselves shall bear the manual signature of the said county supervisor and clerk of the board of commissioners. When sold the official seal of the county board of commissioners shall be affixed, and no bond shall be valid until said seal is affixed. Said bonds shall all bear the same date and shall be numbered consecutively as issued, from No. 1 upwards; and a record of same by number shall be kept in the office of the county supervisor and the disposition of same recorded therein. When any such bond shall have been redeemed or paid, it shall be mutilated and filed in the supervisor's office and the fact recorded in the book above provided.

The said public highway commission shall also keep a record of said bonds, by number consecutively, and the disposition of same, in a book kept for that purpose.

For the purpose of paying funds thereinterest on said bonds and of creating a sinking fund with from. which to redeem same at their maturity, there shall be levied each year a sufficient tax. It shall be the duty of the county treasurer to collect said tax, keeping a separate and accurate account of same, and, upon application by the treasurer of said commission to turn the same over to him as collected, whose receipt shall be a sufficient discharge of the county treasurer for the amount, or amounts, so paid. It shall be the duty of the public highway commission, by its warrant upon the treasurer, to pay the interest on said bonds as it becomes due and also to redeem the bonds themselves as they fall due, out of the moneys so turned over by the county treasurer. If, at any time, said commission shall have any moneys undisposed of or on hand for any purpose, they shall invest the same safely and securely or deposit the same in some bank or banks at as high a rate of interest as it can obtain. It shall in no case be the duty of the purchaser or purchasers of any of said bonds to see that the funds are properly expended. Said bonds shall be exempt from State, county, school or municipal taxation.
SEC. 5. The public highway commission shall be composed of three citizens of the county of Spartanburg, to be appointed highway and commissioned by the Governor on the recommendation of commission. the Senator and members of the House of Representatives of said county, and no member thereof shall hold any public office except that of notary public. One of said commissioners shall reside in the city of Spartanburg, one outside of the city of Spartanburg and north of the main line of the Southern Railway, and one outside of the city of Spartanburg and south of the main line of the Southern Railway.
SEC. 6. Said commission shall as soon as convenient, after appointment and commission, elect one of their number to act as chairman and treasurer, and another to act as secretary. The commissioners herein provided for shall be appointed as soon as convenient after the passage of this Act.
SEC. 7. One member of said public highway commission shall of be appointed for two years, one for three and one for four. At the expiration of said terms their successors shall be appointed for four years, respectively, and until their successors have been recommended, commissioned and qualified.

SEC. 12. The above bonds shall not be issued and nothing shall be done towards their issue until the same have been apbond issue. proved by a majority of the qualified electors of said county at a special election to be held for the purpose on the 24th day of August, 1909. The commissioners of election for said county shall appoint the managers and receive and tabulate the votes as in other elections. They shall make their report in writing in duplicate, one copy to be filed with the clerk of court of Spartanburg county, and the other with the Secretary of State, and said duplicate copies shall be kept on perpetual record in both of said offices. The managers of election shall serve without any compensation; and the managers appointed shall have the power and authority to appoint substitute managers to act in their stead. The county board of commissioners of said county is authorized and required to furnish to the election commissioners of said county, in ample time before said election, a sufficient number of ballots for and against said coupon bond issue. On one set of the bonds shall be printed the words, " For public highway improvement bonds, " and on the other set shall be printed the words, " Against public highway improvement bonds. " And the said commissioners are authorized to pay the expense of said printing out of the general county funds. In all other respects, said election shall be held as other elections, under the general laws of this State, and the same regulations apply thereto, in so far as are consistent with this Act. If said election shall be declared for the issue of the bonds, then the provisions made in former sections of this Act be carried out. Said commission shall not be allowed any salary until the results of the election is disclosed. If said election is declared against the issue of the bonds the term of office of said commissioner shall cease.
SEC. 13. The supervisors of registration for Spartanburg county shall not be required, for the purpose of this election, to visit different portions of the county as they are now required to do in general election year or years in which special elections are held, but shall keep said books open as they are required to do for the year 1909 under the law as it now stands.
