AN ACT TO AMEND CODE OF LAWS OF SOUTH CAROLINA, 1912, VOLUME I,
SECTION 178, RELATING TO THE COMMISSION FOR THE CONFEDERATE
INFIRMARY.
AN ACT to Amend Code of Laws of South Carolina, 1912, Volume I, Section 178, Relating to the Commission for the Confederate Infirmary. Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Code of Laws of South Carolina, 1912, Volume I, Section 178, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the same and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “Section 178. Confederate Home Established—Commission— How Composed — Appointment—Term—Removal—Rules.—That there is hereby established the Confederate Home as an infirmary for the infirm and destitute Confederate soldiers and sailors of the State, located on what is known as the Bellevue place on The said Confederate Wallace land now owned by the State. Home shall be under the control of a Commission of five members, to consist of the Chairmen of the Committees on Military Affairs of the House and the Senate, and three other members to be appointed by the Governor, one to serve for one year, one to serve for two years, and one to serve for three years, and every year hereafter one of the three appointive positions shall be filled by the Governor for the term of three years, and until the said Commissioner’s successor is appointed and qualifies:
OF SOUTH CAROLINA. 157 Provided, That the Governor shall have authority to remove any such member for cause. The said Commission shall prescribe tules regulating admission to said infirmary: Provided, That two veterans shall be admitted from each county, on the recommendation of the County Pension Board: Provided, further, That in case any County Board fails to make such recommendation, the said Commission may fill the vacancy from the same or any other county.” Approved
