AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR A MONUMENT TO THE HEROISM, FIDELITY AND FORTITUDE OF THE WOMEN OF SOUTH CAROLINA DURING THE WAR BETWEEN THE CONFEDERATE STATES AND THE UNITED STATES.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the sum of seven thousand five Appropriahundred dollars be appropriated, to be used in connection with tion an equal sum to be raised by voluntary contributions from the the heroism male inhabitants of South Carolina, and to become available of South only when such equal sum has been so raised and deposited with Carolina in the commission hereinafter provided for, for the erection on the State House grounds of a monument with at least a base of South Carolina granite or design similar to that of the monument erected by the women of South Carolina to the Confederate soldiers of South Carolina, to the heroism, fidelity and fortitude of the women of South Carolina during the war between the Confederate States and the United States.
SEC. 2. That a commission of five male citizens of South Carolina be appointed by the Governor to take charge of the Commisfunds hereinbefore appropriated, when available, and also of all voluntary contributions which may be made to them for the purpose of this Act, and shall select the location and inscription and contract for and superintend the erection of the monument.
