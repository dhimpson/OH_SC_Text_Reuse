AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE TRUSTEES OF HUNTER SCHOOL DISTRICT OF ANDERSON COUNTY TO ORDER AN ELECTION TO INCREASE THE LEVY OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT AND COLLECT A SPECIAL TAX FOR A SCHOOL BUILDING IN THE EVENT THE ELECTION IS IN FAVOR OF SAID LEVY.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Hunter State of South Carolina, That in addition to the power the trict in AnTrustees of Hunter School District of Anderson County now have as to local taxes therein, they are hereby authorized to special levy annually, if more than one levy be necessary, on all the real and personal property returned in said district a special local tax not exceeding two mills on the dollar of said district for the years 1908 and 1909 to complete the schoolhouse in the town of Pendleton, subject to the following provisions:
That the said Board of Trustees shall, as soon as possible Question to thereafter, submit the question of " Special Tax " or " No Speto electors. cial Tax, " to the owners of both real and personal property, who are qualified electors residing within said district, and they shall have power to order an election specifying the time, place A. D. 1908. and purpose of the same, and to make all necessary rules and regulations for governing the same. The said Board of Trustees shall appoint three managers at such election, who shall, without compensation, hold and conduct the same, at which said election the ballots shall have written or printed thereon either the words " Special Tax " or " No Special Tax, " and of which election notice shall be given by the Chairman of the Board of Trustees, as now constituted, not less than ten days before the same is held, in one or more of the newspapers of the city of Anderson.
SEC. 2. It shall be the duty of the managers, within one week after said election, to count the votes and declare the recounted and sult as in other elections, which results must be certified in returned. writing by the Chairman of the Board of Managers to the said Board of Trustees, and if a majority of the votes cast shall be in favor of the special tax, the Chairman of the Board of Trustees shall certify to the Auditor of the County the tax thus levied, and the Auditor shall at once and thereafter for said years 1908 and 1909, to accomplish the purpose mentioned in Section 3 hereof, assess such tax on all real and personal property returned in said School District, and the County Treasurer shall collect the same with the State and County taxes, and such tax shall be a lien on all property, and all defaulting taxpayers shall be liable to like process as for State and County taxes.
SEC. 3. That in case said tax shall be voted for at said elecFund to be tion, the fund raised thereby shall constitute a special fund in used to comthe hands of said Trustees, and shall be used by them for the building. purpose of completing said school building of said School District, and the said Trustees are hereby authorized and empowered to borrow for the purpose a sum of money not to exceed the amount to be raised by the levy, and to appropriate. for securing and repaying this loan, with interest, the said special tax levy herein provided for. The said Trustees are hereby authorized to give a sufficient pledge or transfer of said special levy for the purpose of borrowing this sum of money, and securing the repayment thereof, with interest.
SEC. 4. That any of said tax remaining after the completion of said schoolhouse may be expended by said Trustees to maintain said school.
