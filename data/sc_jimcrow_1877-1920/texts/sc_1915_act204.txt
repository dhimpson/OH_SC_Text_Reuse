AN ACT Authorizing and Empowering the Board of Trustees of Barnwell Graded School District No, 45, of Barnwell, County, South Carolina, to Pledge or Mortgage the Old. School Grounds and Buildings for Certain Purposes, and Authorizing the County Treasurer of Barnwell County to Use the Sinking Fund of Said School District, and Provide for the Return of the Same. Section 1. Borrow Money—Mortgage Property.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the Board of Trustees of the Barnwell Graded School District No. 45, in said county, be, and they are hereby, authorized and empowered
STA AR to pledge or mortgage all that property belonging to the said school district known as “the old school building and grounds of the white school,” containing two acres, more or less, and bounded as follows: On the north by Academy street, on the east by Marlboro street, on the south by alleyway, which separates it from the lot of R. A. Deason, and on the west by alleyway, which separates it from the lot now owned or formerly owned by John K. Snelling, to secure whatever sum it may be found necessary for the said Trustees or the County Treasurer of said county to borrow, if necessary, for the purpose of completing and equipping the new school building for the white school in said district, pay back indebtedness, or to be used in the general account of the said school district; and that whatever sum, which may be borrowed in accordance with the provisions of this Act, shall be borrowed for such length of time as the above Board of Trustees, in their discretion, may be able to sell and dispose of the said property advantageously. § 2. Disbursement of Borrowed Money.—That the money, which may be borrowed by the Trustees, or County Treasurer, as authorized by this Act, shall be held by the County Treasurer of Barnwell county, subject to the warrant of the Board of Trustees. § 3. Sinking Fund—That the County Treasurer of Barnwell county be, and he is hereby, authorized and empowered to transfer the sinking fund of the said school district to the general account of the same, to be drawn by the warrant of the said Board of Trustees; and that he, the said Treasurer, shall return the amount sc transferred from the sinking fund, in installments, from the taxes collected for the said school district as follows: That is, $835.00 from the first collection of taxes for the year 1915, and the same amount from the first collections of taxes for each of the years 1916, 1917, 1918, and a sufficient amount out of the first collection of taxes of the year 1919, to make up the full amount, which has been transferred from the sinking fund; and that as each of the said installments of $835.00 are collected, the Treasurer shall place them to the credit of the sinking fund of said school district, and deposit them in the savings department of the bank in which the said sinking fund is now held, at as high rate of interest as can be obtained. § 4. Dispensary Profits—That all moneys hereafter apportioned to said school district from the profits of the County Dispensary, or from the license or income of any kind for the sale of intoxicating liquors, shall be placed to the general fund of the said
