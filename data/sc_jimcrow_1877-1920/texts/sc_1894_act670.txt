AN ACT TO REVISE AND RE-ENACT THE CHARTER OF THE
TOWN OF GAFFNEY CITY, IN SPARTANBURG COUNTY.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That from and after the passage of this Act all citizens of this State having resided twelve months within the State and six months within the town of Gaffney City shall be deemed, and are hereby declared to be, a body politic and corporate, and the said town shall be known and called by the name of Gaffney City, and its corporate limits shall extend one (1) mile in every direction from the centre of said town, which centre shall be at the intersection of Race and Johnson streets.
Sec. 2. That the municipal powers of the town of Gaffney City shall be, and they are hereby, vested in an Intendant and four Wardens of said town, which shall be and be known as the Town Council of said town, and who shall be persons constitutionally qualified to vote for members of the General Assembly of this State, and who shall have resided within the corporate limits of the said town for twelve months next preceding election to said office.
Sec. 3. That the election for the Intendant and Wardens of said town shall be holden on the first Tuesday in March, 1896, and on the same day every two (2) years thereafter, ten days’ public notice thereof being previously given; and the said officers elected shall continue in office for two years and until the election and qualification of their successors; and all male inhabitants within said town who shall have attained the age of twenty-one (21) years, and resided in the said State and town of Gaffney City for twelve (12) months immediately preceding the election, and who are qualified to vote in the State elections, shall be entitled to vote for said Intendant and Wardens. It shall be the duty of the Intendant and Wardens of said town to require a registration of all voters of said town as a prerequisite to voting at any election for Intendant and Wardens thereof, and for this purpose full power is hereby vested in the Town Council of said town to prescribe the time, place, manner and terms of said registration of voters by an ordinance for that purpose. That the said election shall be held in some convenient place in said town, from nine (9) o’clock in the morning until three (3) o’clock in the afternoon, when the polls shall be closed and the Managers shall forthwith count the votes and declare the election, and give notice in writing to the persons elected. The Intendant and Wardens for the time being shall appoint three Managers to conduct the election, who, before they open the polls for the said election, shall take an oath fairly and impartially to conduct the same. The Intendant and Wardens, before entering upon the duties of their office, shall respectively take the oath prescribed by the Constitution of the State, and also the following oath, to wit: “As Intendant (or Warden) of the town of Gaffney City, I will equally and impartially, to the best of my ability, execute the trust imposed in me, and will use my best endeavors to preserve.the peace and carry into effect, according to law, the purposes for which I have been elected, so help me God.” And if any person upon being elected Intendant or Warden shall refuse to act as such, he shall forfeit and pay the said Town Council the sum of twenty-five dollars for the use of said town: Provided, That no person who has attained the age of sixty (60) years shall be compelled to serve in either of said offices, nor shall any other person be compelled to serve, either as Intendant or Warden, more than one term in succession: Provided, That the present Intendant and Wardens of said town shall hold their offices for the term to which they were elected, that is to say, until the first Tuesday in March, A. D. 1896, and until their successors shall be elected and qualified as herein provided for.
Sec. 4. That if for any reason the election hereinbefore provided for shall fail to be held at the time appointed therefor, it shall be the duty of the Intendant and Wardens of the said town to order an election to be held as soon as practicable thereafter, ten days’ public notice thereof being previously given.
Sec. 5. That said Intendant and Wardens are empowered to pass all ordinances, rules and regulations for the fair and just management of said elections, and to prevent any fraudulent voting thereat, and to punish all offenses against the same by fine not exceeding fifty dollars or imprisonment not exceeding thirty days.
Sec. 6. That the Intendant and two Wardens, or, in the absence of the Intendant, three Wardens, shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business; and in case a vacancy shall occur in the office of Intendant or Warden by death, resignation, removal, or for any other cause, an election to fill such vacancy shall be held by order of the Intendant, or Intendant pro tem., ten days’ public notice being previously given, which election shall be conducted in all respects and be subject to all the regulations hereinafter provided for the regular elections; and in case of sickness, temporary absence or disability of the Intendant, the Wardens shall be empowered to elect one of their number to act as Intendant pro tem.
Sec. 7 That the Intendant and Wardens shall be known as the “Town Council of Gaffney City, S. C.,” and they and their successors in office, hereafter to be elected, may have a common seal, which shall be affixed to all their ordinances; may sue and be sued, plead and be impleaded, in any Court of law or equity in this State, and purchase, hold, possess and enjoy, to them and their successors, in perpetuity or for any term of years, any estate, real, personal or mixed, and sell, alien, convey and Jease the same: Provided, The same shall not exceed at any one time the sum of one hundred thousand dollars. And the said Town Council shall have full power and authority to establish or authorize the establishment of a market house in the said town, and to prescribe suitable rules and regulations for the same; and to establish or authorize the establishment of a guard house and to prescribe suitable rules and regulations for keeping and governing the same; and the said Town Council shall have full power and authority, under their corporate seal, to make all such rules, by-laws and ordinances respecting the streets, roads, parks, drainage, market houses and the business thereof, buildings, trade and police system of said town, as shal] appear to them necessary and proper for the security, welfare and convenience of the said town, and for preserving health, peace, order and good government and morals within the same, and for the protection of life and property within said town; and said Town Council shall have full power and authority to abate and remove all nuisances in the said town.
Sec. 8. That said Intendant, Intendant pro tem., or Town Council, may fine and impose fines and penalties and imprisonment in the guard house of the town for violation of the ordinances and by-laws of said town; said fines, penalties and imprisonment to be imposed singly or in the alternative; but no fine or penalty to exceed the sum of one hundred dollars, and no term of imprisonment to exceed thirty (30) days; and they shall appropriate all fines to the public use of the said corporation.
Sec. 9. That the said Intendant or Town Council, or both of them, are hereby vested with all the powers and jurisdiction of Trial Justices in this State within the limits of said town, so far as the violation of municipal ordinances are concerned, and except for the trial of civil cases. The said Intendant, or Intendant pro tem., shall have power to try all offenders against the ordinances and by-laws of the said town immediately upon their apprehension, arrest, or as soon thereafter as practicable, unless such persons enter into a good and sufficient bond, to be approved by the Intendant, or, in his absence, by the Clerk of the Town Council, or make satisfactory money deposit, to appear for trial at a time fixed not later than five days after the arrest of said person or persons, in which event the trial shall be deferred until that time; and the said Intendant or Town Council, or both of them, shall have the same power which Trial Justices now have, or shall hereafter be vested with, to compel the attendance of witnesses, wherever they may reside, and within the jurisdiction of the Trial Justices of the County, the same as in prosecutions for criminal offenses in said Trial Justice Court, and require them to give evidence upon the trial before him or them of any person or persons for a violation of any of the ordinances or by-laws of the said Town Council; and in the trial of all offenders against the ordinances or by-laws of said town, the Intendant or Town Council shall be governed by the same rules shall of procedure and evidence as are now or hereafter be prescribed for the government of Courts of Trial Justices in criminal cases: Provided, That the same right of appeal shall be allowed as in cases of appeal from Courts of Trial Justices in this State. Whenever the Intendant shall find any person before him guilty of violating an ordinance or by-law of said town, he shall have power to impose, in his discretion, fine or imprisonment within the limits herein prescribed for such violations: Provided, That whenever the Intendant, Intendant pro tem., or Town Council, shall impose a fine upon a person convicted of an offense who shall fail, refuse or be unable to pay the same, said Intendant or Town Council shall have the power and authority to commute the sentence of said person to that of labor on the public streets or public improvements of said town for a term not exceeding thirty (80) days. From all trials, sentences and decisions of the said Intendant, any person feeling aggrieved shall have the right to appeal to the Town Council: Provided, Written notice of such appeal be immediately given to the Intendant upon such sentence being pronounced, and said person entering into a bond to appear and defend before the Town Council at a time to be specified in such undertaking, not more than ten (10) days after the date of such bond.
Sec. 10. In any case tried under the foregoing Section, upon appeal to the full Council, the trial shall proceed de novo, and the Intendant and three Wardens, or, in the absence or inability of the Intendant to serve, the four Wardens, shall be sufficient to proceed with the trial of such cause or appeal, and a decision of a majority of those present shall determine all questions before the said Council, except the questions of fact involved, in which the Intendant who tried the case shall have no vote, but shall be determined by a majority of the Wardens trying the case: Provided, That in case of a tie vote of the Wardens on the facts involved, the Intendant shall have the casting vote, and the decision of the majority as thus ascertained shall determine the case and be final: Provided, further, That the Intendant, Intendant pro tem., or Town Council, shall have power to enforce the observance of order and decorum in their Municipal Court while any trial is being held, and for said purpose said Intendant or Town Council is hereby vested with full power to punish as for a contempt any person who shall, in the presence of said Court, offer an insult to said Intendant or to any one of the Wardens, or who shall be considered by the Court guilty of a disturbance of the proceedings in said Court, by fine not exceeding twenty dollars, or imprisonment in the guard house not exceeding ten days, for each and every offense.
Sec. 11. The Intendant shall have authority to summon the Council to meet for the transaction of business pertaining to the corporation whenever in his judgment it may be necessary. Sec. 12. The Town Council of said town shall have full power and authority to purchase and operate, rent or lease, under such rules, regulations and orders as they may by ordinance adopt, water and electric works, in or near said town, for the use and benefit of said town and its citizens; also, to purchase, own and operate, rent or lease, apparatus for generating either electricity or gas for the use,and benefit of said town and its citizens, or to contract for the erection of plants, either for water works or lighting purposes, one or both, for the uses of said town and to supply the citizens thereof, to grant a franchise or franchises for said purposes, as well as for the construction and operation of a street railway within said town, to any private corporation or corporations for a term of years as may be deemed to be to the best interest of said town, with power to make such by-laws, rules and ordinances in reference thereto as shall be deemed necessary for the regulation and proper conduct of the same, either for public or private use by the citizens of said town: Provided, That no such franchise shall be granted until the question of granting thereof has first been submitted to the qualified voters of said town and approved by the majority of such persons voting at such election.
Sec. 13. The Intendant and Wardens shall have full and exclusive control over all streets, roads, ways and bridges in the said town, and it shall be their duty to keep them open and in good repair; and for that purpose they are invested with all the powers of County Board of Commissioners for and‘ within the coporate limits of said town, and they shall have power to classify and arrange the inhabitants or citizens of said town liable to street, road or public duty therein, and to enforce the performance of such duty under such penalties, rules and regulations as shall be provided for by the ordinances of said town; and they shall have power to compound with all persons liable to work the streets, ways and roads in said town, upon such terms as their ordinances or by-laws may establish or their rules and regulations require, the money so received to be applied to the public use of said corporation; and all persons refusing to labor or failing to pay such commutation shall be liable to such fine, not exceeding ten dollars, or imprisonment, not exceeding twenty days, as the said Town Council may impose; and they shall have power to enforce the payment of such. fine or to impose such imprisonment as in all other cases for violation of ordinances.
Sec. 14. The Town Council of Gaffney City shall have full power and authority to open new streets in said town, and to close up, widen or otherwise alter those now in use, or which may hereafter be established, wheresoever, in their judgment, the same may be necessary for the improvement and convenience of said town, and to name and change present names of streets. Should the land owner or owners through whose premises such street or streets may run re fuse his, her or their consent to such action of the Town Council in opening, closing up, widening or altering such street or streets, the said Town Council shall have the right to condemn such land for the purpose aforesaid, according to the provisions of the law now of force for condemning land for public use.
Sec. 15. The cemeteries and public graveyards are also placed under the jurisdiction of the said Town Council.
Sec. 16. That said Town Council shall have the right, either independently of or in connection with any association of the citizens of said town, to establish and maintain, or to assist in establishing and maintaining, a public library for the use and benefit of the citizens of said town, under such rules and regulations as they may by ordinances adopt.
Sec. 17. That the said Town Council shall have full power and authority to prohibit, by suitable penalties, the erection of any wooden building or buildings within such limits in the said town as the Town Council shall designate by an ordinance or ordinances fur such purpose, duly passed and ratified by the said Town Council, not inconsistent with the Constitution and laws of this State, and published one time in a newspaper published in said town, or by posting the same for one week in a conspicuous place in said town.
Sec. 18. The said Town Council shall have full power to regulate sales at auction within the corporate limits of said town, and to grant licenses to auctioneers, itinerants, traders and keepers of hotels and livery, sale or exchange stables, and to persons who sell fresh or uncured pork, fish or other fresh meats within the corporate limits of said town, upon such terms and condtitions, and subject to such regulations, as said Council may by ordinance establish: Provided, That nothing herein contained shall extend to sales by Sheriffs, executors or administrators, or by any other person, under the order of any Court; and the said Town Council shall have the power to impose a tax on all drays, carts, wagons, carriages, omnibuses, buggies, horses, mares or mules kept for hire or used for public purposes in said town, and upon every tenpin alley, pool, billiard, bagatelle or other table of any kind or name upon which games are played for gain or reward, within the corporate limits of said town; and to require all persons owning or keeping a dog or dogs within said town to pay an annual license tax not exceeding two dollars for each dog so owned or kept, and to enforce and regulate the payment of said license tax under such rules and regulations as said Town Council may deem proper, to charge and collect in advance a license tax upon every show or exhibition for gain or reward within the corporate limits of said town; and said Town Council shall have full power to grant or refuse licenses for retailing intoxicating liquors, wines or malt liquors, subject, however, to the laws of the State, and when not in conflict therewith.
Sec. 19. The said Town Council shall be authorized and empowered to require all persons, companies and corporations now engaged, or who may hereafter become engaged, in any business vocation, trade or profession of any kind whatsoever within the limits of said town, and all transient persons, companies or corporations engaged in or about to engage in any business in said town, to take out a license from the Town Council, who shall be authorized to impose such charges or license tax for the conduct of the same as by ordinance of the said town may be provided; and the revenue derived from such license or tax shall be for the public use of said town, and the Town Council may enforce the payment of such license or tax in such manner as they may by ordinance provide.
Sec. 20. The said Town Council shall have the power to impose an annual tax not exceeding eighty (80) cents on every hundred dollars of the value of all real estate and personal property lying within the corporate limits of said town, real and personal property of churches, cemeteries, schools, colleges, associations and public libraries excepted. An ordinance declaring the rate of the annual taxation for the year shall be published or posted at least two (2) weeks during the month of January in each year. All persons liable to taxation in said town shall, during the month of January, make return, under oath, to the Clerk of said Town Council, or to some other person appointed for said purpose, of their taxable property within said town, in the same manner as is required by law for the return of property for taxation for State and County taxes; and the said Town Council shall appoint three discreet freeholders, residents of said town, who, upon taking the oath prescribed for officers in the Constitution, shall meet during the last week in January and the first week in February and elect a Chairman, and when so organized shall form a Board of Assessors for said town, with full power to review the said returns of property made by taxpayers, with power to alter the same with the view of assessing all property, both real and personal, at its true value, and of equalizing the assessment of said property, and to assess all property for taxation not so returned, and their assessment so made shall be reported to the Town Council and shall be taken as the true value thereof for the purposes of taxation in said town: Provided, That the taxpayers shall have ten days thereafter in which to make complaint of such assessment to the Town Council; and upon good cause shown, the Town Council may alter any assessment. Payment of said taxes shall be made to the Clerk of said Town Council, or to such other person as may be authorized to colléct and receive the same, during the month of February after the publication, and until the first Tuesday in March thereafter; and upon failure to make such return and payment, or either, as herein required, the person so in default shall be subject to the penalties provided by law for failure to make return and pay the general State and County tax, to be enforced by the order of the Intendant and Wardens, or a majority of them, for the use of the said town, except that in such cases executions to enforce the payment of such taxes shall be issued under the seal of the corporation, and may be directed to the Chief of Police or other person appointed by the Town Council to levy, collect and receive the same, with costs, as in such eases made and provided by law; and all property upon which such tax shall be levied and assessed is hereby declared and made liable for payment thereof in preference to all other debts, except debts due to the State and County, which shall be first paid; and all other taxes imposed by the Intendant and Wardens, or a majority of them, shall be enforced and collected as in manner and form just before stated: Provided, That said Town Council shall have full power to levy and collect a tax for the year 1895 under the same rules, regulations and provisions as set forth in this Section.
Sec. 21. That said Town Council shall have full power and authority to abate all nuisances within, or partly within, the corporate limits of said town, and also to appoint a Board of Health for said town, and to pass such ordinances as may be necessary to define the duties and powers of said Board of Health, and to impose a fine of not more than twenty-five dollars upon any person who shall refuse to serve upon said Board of Health; and it is expressly provided that upon the failure of any person in possession of land, lot or house, within the corporate limits of said town, to cleanse the privies or water closets, or remove any other nuisance from the premises in his or her possession, or under his or her control, when so directed to do by either the Town Council or the Board of Health, it shall be the duty of the said Town Council to cause their Marshal or other employees to enter the premises in question and to abate said nuisance at the expense of the person in possession of said premises; and said Town Council shall have power to provide by ordinance for the punishment of any and all persons who shall interfere with the Marshals in the discharge of the duties imposed on them in said ordinance, or who shall fail or refuse to pay the expenses of abating the nuisance upon the demand of the same by the Town Council.
See. 22. That the said Town Council shall have power, and are hereby authorized, to elect one Chief of Police and as many assistants as may be necessary, to fix their salaries and prescribe their duties. They shall be sworn in and vested with all the powers now conferred by law upon Constables, and subject to all the duties and liabilities that are now or may hereafter be conferred upon Constables, in addition to the special duties devolved upon them by the Town Council: Provided, That their jurisdiction shall not extend beyond the limits of the said corporation; that the Intendant shall have power to suspend, dismiss or discharge from office any town Marshal or policeman for neglect of duty or improper conduct in the discharge of his official duties, subject, however, to the approval of the Wardens or a majority thereof; and said Council shall have the power to elect a Town Clerk and Treasurer, whose duties shall be prescribed by them, and they shall also fix said Town Clerk’s salary, which shall not be less than two bundred dollars per annum, and require him and the Treasurer to enter into a bond, to be approved by the Intendant, conditioned upon the faithful performance of the duties of their oftice. Said bond not to be for a less sum than two thousand ($2,000) dollars each.
See. 23. The said Town Council shall have the power by ordinance, or the Intendant and Wardens in person, any one or more of them, to authorize and direct any policeman of said town to arrest and commit to the guard house of the town for a term not exceeding twenty-four (24) hours before trial, unless such arrest be made on Saturday, in which case it shall not exceed forty-eight (48) hours before trial, any person or persons who, within the said corporate limits, may be engaged in any breach of the peace, riotous or disorderly conduct, open obscenity, public drunkenness, or in any conduct grossly indecent or dangerous to the citizens of the said town or any of them; and it shall be the duty of any Marshal or policeman of said town to arrest and commit all such offenders, when required by said ordinances, or by any member of the Town Council, so to do; and they shall have power to call to their assistance the posse comitatus, if need be, in making such arrest; and upon failure of such Marshal or policeman to perform their duty as required, they shall severally be liable to such fines and penalties as said Town Council may establish; and all persons so imprisoned shall pay the costs and expenses incident to such imprisonment, which said costs and expenses shall be collected in the same manner as is provided for the collection of fines imposed for the violation of the town ordinances: Provided, That the imprisonment provided for in this Section shall not exempt the person so imprisoned from trial and sentence, as hereinbefore provided.
Sec. 24. That the said Town Council shall have full power to erect a powder magazine, and to compel every person holding more than one hundred pounds of powder at any one time to store the same therein, and to make regulations for rates of storage therein. They shall also have power to prohibit absolutely the keeping of dynamite in any quantity whatsoever within the corporate limits of said town, except as permitted by the Town Council for special purpose in said town by ordinance. That the said Town Council is hereby authorized and empowered to establish and keep up one or more public scales for weighing cotton and other articles sold by weight in said town, and to appoint one or more public weighers, who shall give good bond, to be approved by the Intendant, and who shall be sworn by said Intendant faithfully to perform the duties of said office, and who shall be removable for misconduct or incompetency by the said Town Council; and the said Town Council are hereby authorized to assess a sum not exceeding ten (10) cents on each bale of cotton, and a proportionate sum on other articles weighed, to be paid by the seller or buyer, as may be agreed upon between them, for the use of said town. That the public scales established in pursuance of this Act shall be the standard to which all others in said town shall conform; and the Town Council may establish by ordinance such rules and regulations as may be deemed necessary to effectually carry out the provisions of this Section.
Sec. 25. That the Intendant of said town shall receive as a salary for his services a sum to be fixed by the Town Council, not less than three hundred dollars per annum. He and the Wardens and their Marshals shall each be exempt from street and jury duty within said town during their term of office, and each Town Council shall, within thirty days after the expiration of their term of office, make out and return to their successors a full account of their receipts and expenditures during their term of office. And at the expiration of the term of office of each Town Council it shall be their duty to pay over to their successors any moneys in their hands, at the time of making such returns, belonging to said corporation, and likewise to deliver up promptly, at the end of each Town Council term, all books, records, property and papers incident to their said oflice to their successors; and on failure so to do they shall be liable to be fined in a sum of five hundred dollars, to be collected by said Town Council by suit in any Court of competent jurisdiction.
Sec. 26. The said Town Council shall have power and authority to close in all the lots in said town, and to make and keep in good repair, by paving or otherwise, all sidewalks in front of any lot or lots whereon the same shall front or adjoin any public street of the said town, if, in the judgment of the Council, such sidewalks shall be necessary, the width thereof and the manner of construction to be designated and regulated by the Town Council, and to require the owner or owners to pay the price of making or repairing; and the said town shall have a lien in preference to all other liens for said work performed and material used on the property benefited by said improvements; and the said Town Council is hereby empowered to sue for and recover the same by action of debt in any Court of competent jurisdiction: Provided, That such owner or owners shall not be required to pay more than the actual cost of such improvements. That said Town Council shall have the power to elect or appoint a civil engineer for said town, whose duties shall be prescribed by an ordinance duly passed for that purpose.
Sec. 27. For any willful violation or neglect of duty, malpractice, abuse or oppression, the Intendant or any Warden so offending shall be liable to punishment by fine not exceeding one hundred dollars, or imprisonment not exceeding thirty days, besides being liable for damages to any person injured by such neglect, malpractice, abuse or oppression.
Sec. 28. Any person who shall receive bodily injury or damages in his person or property through a defect in any street, causeway, bridge, or public way, or by reason of defect or mismanagement of anything under the control of the corporation within the limits of said town may recover in an action against the same the amount of actual damages sustained by him or her by reason thereof. If any such defect in a street, causeway or bridge existed before such injury or damage occurred, such damage shall not be recovered by the person so injured if his load exceeded the ordinary weight: Provided, That said corporation shall not be liable unless said defect was occasioned by its neglect or mismanagement: Provided, further, Such person has not, in any way, brought about such injury or damage by his or her own negligent act or negligently contributed thereto.
Sec. 29. That this Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval, and all ordinances, by-laws, rules and regulations which are now in force in said town of Gaffney City, and all contracts and all acts done by the Town Council of Gaffney City, shall continue and be of force to the same extent, tenor and effect as now exists, and nothing herein shall be deemed or taken in any way to impair any such rules, regulations, ordinances and contracts of the said town.
Sec. 30. That all Acts or parts of Acts heretofore passed relating to or bearing upon the incorporation of the town of Gaffney City and inconsistent with and repugnant to this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
Sec. 31. That this Act shall be deemed and taken to be a public Act, and shall continue in force until repealed.
