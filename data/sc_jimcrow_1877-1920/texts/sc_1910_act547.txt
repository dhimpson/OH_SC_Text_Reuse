AN ACT OT REQUIRE THE SOUTHERN RAILWAY COMPANY, LESSOR OR LESSEES, TO ERECT A DEPOT AT CAMDEN, IN KERSHAW COUNTY.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Southern State of South Carolina, That within eight months from the Railway approval of this Act, Southern Railway Company, its lessors pot at Camor lessees, shall enlarge and arrange its station at Camden, den. in Kershaw County, so that the same may be suitable to the needs and demands of the patrons of said railroad or railroads at said city. That said depot when enlarged and repaired shall consist of not less than a suitable waiting room for white passengers and a suitable waiting room for colored passengers and a suitable room for receiving and delivereing of freight and baggage.
SEC. 2. That said depot shall be so reconstructed upon Plans thereplans and specifications to be approved by the city council of for. the city of Camden and the Railroad Commission.
SEC. 3. That upon the failure of said railroad or railroads to so reconstruct and enlarge said depot in accordance with the terms of this Act, said railroad or railroads shall be subject to and become liable to a penalty of five hundred ($ 500) dollars per day for each day after said date the said railroad or railroads shall so fail to comply with the terms of this Act, to be recovered in an action by the city council of Camden in any court of competent jurisdiction.
