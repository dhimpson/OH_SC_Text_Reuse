Section 1. But prosecutions and civil actions may be removed from one magistrate to another for the same cause and in the same manner as is now provided by law, and the Courts of Common Pleas shall have concurrent jurisdiction in all civil actions triable by magistrates. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
