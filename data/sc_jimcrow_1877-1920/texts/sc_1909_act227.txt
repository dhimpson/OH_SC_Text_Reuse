AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE GREENVILLE FEMALE COLLEGE.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Greenville State of South Carolina, That B. M. Shuman, J. A. Brown, J. lege charterR. Aiken, C. P. Wray, J. H. Wharton, J. D. Gilreath, J. R. Ware, J. W. Quattlebaum, W. T. Tate, W. B. Oliver. O. C. Scarborough, J. J. Gentry, C. M. McGhee, C. P. Miles, and E. G. Quattlebaum and their associates and successors, be, and they are hereby, created a body corporate and politic under the name and style of Greenville Female College.
SEC. 2. That the objects and purposes of said corporation Purposes shall be to keep up, maintain and conduct a college in the city of Greenville, in the State of South Carolina, for the education of girls and women, in which shall be taught all those branches of learning which are usually taught in colleges for girls and women in this State and the United States, and to confer such degree of graduation, honor and distinction as it may think proper upon its students.
SEC. 3. That said corporation have and is hereby given full power to keep up, conduct and maintain such college and to teach and to cause to be taught therein those branches of learning A. D. 1909. which are usually taught in colleges for girls and women in this State and the United States and to confer such degree of graduation, honors, and distinctions as it may think proper upon its students, and such honorary degrees on other persons as may think proper.
SEC. 4. That in addition to said powers said corporation shall have and is hereby given the right and power to keep up and Same. maintain in said city of Greenville a school for females, where all the branches of education usually pursued in a female academy shall be taught, which school shall be open to all the females of the community of the white race as the Greenville academies were.
SEC. 5. That in addition to the foregoing powers said corporation shall have the following powers: (a) To make contracts, to loan money, to acquire, receive, hold and enjoy property, both real and personal, and transfer and alien the same, possessing the same powers in these respects as individuals now enjoy. (b) To sue and be sued by its corporate name. (c) To have a common seal and alter the same at pleasure. (d) To make by - laws and rules and regulations deemed expedient for its management not inconsistent with the laws of this State or of the United States. (e) To borrow money for the purpose of carrying out its object and purposes; to make notes, bonds and other evidences of debt, and to secure the payment of its obligations by mortgage or deed of trust on all or any of its property or franchises, both real and personal.
SEC. 6. That said corporation shall be managed, governed and controlled by a board of trustees consisting of not less than fifteen persons, or such larger number not exceeding twentyfive, as shall be determined by the convention of the Baptist denomination in South Carolina, a majority of whom shall constitute a quorum to do business, and that such trustees shall be elected by the convention of the Baptist denomination in South Carolina at its next session, one - fifth of whom shall be elected to hold office for one year; one - fifth for two years; one - fifth for three years; one - fifth for four years, and one - fifth for five years, the successors to those whose term of office has expired by limitation to be elected by said convention every year at the regular session thereof, and when so elected shall hold office for five years, unless sooner removed by death, resignation or otherwise. Said convention shall by election fill all vacancies caused by death, resignation or otherwise, but the term of any trustee or trustees elected to fill such vacancy or vacancies shall expire when the term of the trustee or trustees to fill whose places he or they may have been elected, would have expired.
SEC. 7. That said B. M. Shuman, J. A. Brown, J. R. Aiken, C. P. Wray, J. H. Wharton, J. D. Gilreath, J. R. Ware, J. W. Quattlebaum, W. T. Tate, W. B. Oliver, O. C. Scarborough, J. J. Gentry, C. M. McGee, C. P. Miles and E. C. Quattlebaum are hereby empowered to act as such trustees of said corporation and manage, govern and control the same until the State convention of the Baptist denomination in South Carolina shall elect trustees for it as hereinbefore provided.
