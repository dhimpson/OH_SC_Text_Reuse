AN ACT TO PRESCRIBE AND PROVIDE FOR THE SALARIES OF CERTAIN COUNTY OFFICERS IN DORCHESTER COUNTY.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That on and after the approval of this Act the salary of the Sheriff of Dorchester County, in lieu of all costs and fees in criminal cases, shall be fourteen hundred dollars, and no more, and said salary shall include the compensation of any jailer which may be employed by said Sheriff.
Section 2. That the compensation of the County Treaurer of Dorchester County shall be the same as now provided by law for Treasurers of other counties so far as the same may be based upon commissions and percentage: Provided, Said compensation shall be at the rate of not less than eight hundred dollars nor more than nine hundred dollars per annum.
Section 3. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
