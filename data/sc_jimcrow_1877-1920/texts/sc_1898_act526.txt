INTEREST ON PUBLIC DEBT.
SECTION 8. That the sum of two hundred and eighty - three thousand seven hundred and forty - four 03-100 dollars, if so much be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated to pay the interest on the valid debt of the State which shall mature first day of July, 1898, and on the first day of January, 1899, and for unpaid interest payable in prior years: Provided, That payment by the State Treasurer of the semi - annual interest on the valid debt of the State which matured on the first day of January, 1898, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed and validated.
SECTION 9. That the amount specified in the preceding sections of this Act for salaries and clerical services shall be shall be paid. paid in monthly installments, unless otherwise provided for, and shall be paid upon the warrants of the Comptroller General on the application of the various officers entitled to the same: Provided, That the accounts and vouchers upon which such applications are made shall be filed with the Comptroller General before issuing his warrants on the State Treasurer for the same, and that for the purchase of stamps the Comptroller General is hereby authorized to issue his warrant to such officers.
SECTION 10. That the moneys herein before set apart to be used as contingent funds, and for other purposes, for the various officers for the State government shall be duly accounted for, and such officers shall make a detailed statement of the disposition made thereof to the General Assembly, at its next regular session: Provided, That no officer authorized to make contracts or draw funds from said appropriation shall expend, or make contracts for expending, more than has been specified for any purpose by this Act.
SECTION 11. That this Act shall take effect from and immediately after its approval.
SECTION 12. That all Acts or parts of Acts and Joint Resolutions, or parts of Acts inconsistent or conflicting with the provisions of this Act, be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
