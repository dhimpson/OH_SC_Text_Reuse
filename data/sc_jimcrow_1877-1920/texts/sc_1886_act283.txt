AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE ATLANTIC SELF-ENDOWMENT AND BENEVOLENT ASSOCIATION OF AMERICA.
SEC. 1. Incorporation. Name.
SEC. 2. Powers and rights. Property.
SEC. 3. Purposes. Location.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That John W. Austin, Abner B. Wright, John B. Hyde, William S. Agnew and William R. White, and their associates, who have formed an association under the name of the Atlantic SelfEndowment and Benevolent Association of America, and their successors, shall be, and they are hereby declared to be, a body Incorporation. politic and corporate, under the name and style of the Atlantic Name. Self Endowment and Benevolent Association of America.
SEC. 2. That the said association shall have power to make a constitution and by laws, and alter the same, not repugnant to the Constitution and laws of the land; shall have succession of officers and members according to its constitution and by laws; may have, use and keep a common seal, and alter the same at will; to sue and be sued, plead and be impleaded, in any Court in this State; to have and enjoy every right incident to incorporations; and is empowered to purchase, possess, retain and Property use all such property, real and personal, as it may now possess, or be entitled to, or which shall hereafter be given, bequeathed to, purchased, entrusted or in any manner be acquired by it,and to sell, alien, loan or in any way transfer and contract concerning the same or any part thereof, not inconsistent with the law of the land: Provided, That the amount of property so held shall not exceed two hundred and fifty thousand dollars at any one time over and above its debts and liabilities.
SEC. 3. That the said association shall have power to provide for and regulate, by its constitution and by laws, a system of self endowment and aid to its members during life, and, after death, relief to their beneficiaries; the endowment and relief to be conferred to be created by assessments on its members. The principal office of said association shall be located at Greenville, S. C., with power to locate divisions and local offices at such time and place as its interest may demand.
