AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT TO ESTABLISH THE ANDERSON SCHOOL DISTRICT, TO AUTHORIZE THE ESTABLISHMENT OF FREE GRADED SCHOOLS THEREIN, AND TO PROVIDE THE MEANS FOR THE EQUIPMENT AND EFFICIENT MANAGEMENT OF THE SAME, APPROVED JANUARY 5, A. D. 1895, SO AS TO ENLARGE THE SAID DISTRICT AND AUTHORIZE THE TRUSTEES TO ISSUE BONDS AND PROVIDE THE MEANS FOR THE EQUIPMENT AND THE EFFICIENT MANAGEMENT OF THE NEW DISTRICT AS AMENDED.

§ 2. Board of Trustees - Term of Office. That a Board of Trustees, now acting and elected to such office of the said district, shall hold their respective terms of office as follows: J. L. Sherard, W. P. Wright and W. H. Shearer, from the approval of this Act and for six years from the second Tuesday in January, 1914; J. A. Brock, R. E. Ligon and J. P. Duckett, from the approval of this Act and for four years hence from second Tuesday in January, 1914; G. N. C. Boleman, J. D. Rast and J. M. Paget, from the approval of this Act and for two years from the second Tuesday in January, 1914; and on the second Tuesday of January every two years thereafter, three trustees shall be elected to serve for a term of six years. The election of all trustees for said school district shall be by ballot,

and shall be conducted under the supervision of three qualified electors residing within the district who shall be appointed by the Board of Trustees at least three weeks prior to the holding of the election, and that due advertisement of such election shall be made in a newspaper published in the county of Anderson at least three weeks before said election, at which election only qualified electors of such school district shall be entitled to vote, and such election shall be held in the courthouse of the county of Anderson. The managers shall report the result of the election to the Board of Trustees, who in turn shall report the same to the County Board of Education within ten days thereafter, which board shall commission the trustees so elected. The said Board of Trustees shall elect from their number a chairman, who shall preside at their meetings; a vice chairman, who shall preside in the absence of the chairman; a secretary or secretary and treasurer, who shall record the proceedings of the board, and who shall keep a full and accurate account of all moneys received and expended, showing the source and disposition of each item, and who shall make a complete itemized report of the receipts and disbursements of scholastic year to the County Superintendent of Education on or before the 15th day of July of each year. The books and vouchers of the secretary and treasurer shall be open at all times to inspection by the public. In case of a vacancy in the said Board of Trustees by death, resignation or otherwise, the said board shall have the power to fill said vacancy, and the one so appointed shall be commissioned by the County Board of Education, and the board shall have the power to remove from office any member for sufficient cause: Provided, Six members vote in favor of his removal.
§ 3. Duties of Trustees. - That the trustees herein provided for shall have exclusive control of the free and graded schools in said. district and shall be vested with all powers of the School Trustees under the general school law. They may organize schools, purchase or lease school property, erect school buildings, elect superintendents and teachers, and fix their salaries, prescribe the textbooks to be used and the rate of tuition to be paid by pupils not entitled to the benefit of the free school funds of said district; and make rules and regulations for the control of the schools and the school property not repugnant to law, and shall have the power to assess upon each scholar such sum or sums as may be necessary to meet the incidental expenses of the said school, not to exceed the sum of $ 2 each for one school year: Provided, That should the parent, guardian or other

§ 4. May Levy Tax. - That in addition to the rights and privileges hereinbefore granted the said school district shall have the power to levy and collect the tax on the assessed value of all real and personal property returned in said school district, not exceeding six mills on the dollar, subject to the following provisions: That the said Board of Trustees shall at any time previous to the first day of July, submit to the qualified voters in said school district an election to be held for that purpose after at least three weeks ' notice, which notice shall be duly advertised as provided in the case of trustees, the question of " special tax " or " no special tax. " It shall be the duty of the managers within one week after said election to count the votes and declare the result as in other elections, which result must be certified in writing by the Chairman of the Board of Managers to the Board of Trustees, and if a majority of the votes shall be in favor of the special tax the Chairman of the Board of Trustees shall certify to the Auditor of the county the tax thus levied, and the Auditor shall at once and thereafter from year to year until some other election shall have been held whereby the said special tax shall be repealed or changed, assess such tax on all real and personal property returned in said school district, and the County Treasurer shall collect said taxes the same as for State and county taxes, and such taxes shall be a lien on all property and all defaulting taxpayers, to be subjected to like process as that of State and county taxes.
§ 5. Disbursement of Funds. - That all moneys assessed and collected herein for school purposes shall be paid by the Treasurer of the county of Anderson for salaries and other school purposes in said district upon the warrant of the Chairman and the Secretary of the Board of Trustees, and with the approval of County Superintendent of Education.
§ 6. Duty of Trustees to Make Report. - It shall be the duty of the Board of Trustees of the graded schools that at least once a year to make out a report showing the total enrollment, the average attendance, and average attendance of all entitled to benefit of free tuition in said district under the general school law of the State, receipts and disbursements during the year, which shall be filed with the County Superintendent of Education of the county of Anderson.

§ 7. Special Tax Elections. - That the said Board of Trustees of the said school district shall, upon the second Tuesday of January of any year upon a petition of one - third of the registered voters of said district, order an election upon the question of " special tax " or " no special tax, " at which election the same provision for notice of election certificate of election by Board of Managers and Trustees shall be made as hereinbefore provided in the case of election for " special tax " in Section 4, and result certified to the Auditor, who shall make his levy according to said election.
§ 8. Election of Bond Issue. That the Board of Trustees of the school district of the city of Anderson is hereby empowered to submit to the qualified voters residing in said school district, on an election to be held for that purpose after at least three weeks ' notice published in some newspaper published in the county of Anderson, the question of authorizing an issue of one hundred thousand dollars not more than five per cent. bonds, interest payable semiannually, July 1st and January 1st of each year, payable in forty years from the first day of July, 1913, to be issued in $ 1,000 each and numbered from one to 100, inclusive. Said Board of Trustees shall appoint managers to receive the returns and declare the result of said election and shall issue and sell said bonds as the necessity may arise for the proceeds thereof in the event said election shall result in favor thereof.
§ 9. Issue of Bonds. - If the election is in favor of issuing said. bonds the said trustees shall set aside $ 35,000 of said issue and shall exchange the same for the $ 35,000 of the school bonds issued by the city of Anderson for school purposes in said district, in which case the school bonds issued by the said city of Anderson, and all coupons. thereon remaining unpaid, shall be cancelled and destroyed by the Board of Trustees and the said Council of the city of Anderson. In case the owner or owners of the said city bonds heretofore issued for school purposes are unwilling or refuse to make the change, then said School Trustees shall hold the $ 35,000 worth of bonds, or as many as may be necessary, and deposit them in some safety deposit strong box for the purpose of taking up the city bonds when they

That it shall be the duty of said trustees to issue and sell the balance of said bonds at such times as the necessity may arise to the best advantage possible, and the funds derived from the sale of said bonds shall be used for the purpose of improving the present school property, acquiring additional property and erecting a building or buildings for school purposes, and said funds are not to be used for any other purpose.
§ 10. Annual Tax Levy to Pay Interest. - For the purpose of paying the interest on said bonds and the principal when due it shall be the duty of the trustees of the district to compute annually the amount necessary to be raised, not exceeding two mills on the dollar, and to report same to the County Auditor, who shall levy the same upon real estate and personal property of the said school district, and the amount so levied shall be collected by the County Treasurer of the county of Anderson, and held by him for the purpose of paying said interest or the principal, if any be due, and for the purpose of providing a sinking fund for the payment of said bonds, and shall be for no other purpose whatsoever.
§ 11. County Treasurer to Make Annual Statement of Taxes Collected, etc. - That on or by the first day of May of the year 1914, and annually thereafter, it shall be the duty of the County Treasurer to make a statement to the Board of Trustees of the said school district of the city of Anderson, showing the amount of money he has collected on said taxes, and shall turn over to the said Board of Trustees any sum remaining in his hands after the payment of the interest due on said bonds; and it shall be the duty of the Board of Trustees as soon as practicable thereafter to invest said money in some safe security or securities to be approved by them for the benefit of the sinking fund provided for within the terms of this Act, and to keep the same invested as far as possible, until the maturity of said bonds or until they shall be redeemed in accordance with law, and said trustees shall have the right, in case any sums so held by them should be too small for the purpose of investments in approved securities, to deposit the same in some banking institution in the saving department at the best rates of interest obtainable until such time as the amount so obtained shall be practicable for permanent investments at a better rate of interest.
