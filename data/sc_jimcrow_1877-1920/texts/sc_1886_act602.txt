AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE YORKVILLE BUILDING AND LOAN ASSOCIATION.
SEC. 1. Incorporation, name and capital stock.
SEC. 2. Real estate.
SEC. 3. Powers and limitations.
SEC. 4. Public Act. When to take effect.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That

Lindsay and others, who are or may hereafter be associated with them, be, and they are hereby, declared a body politic and corporate for the purpose of making loans of money Purposes. secured by mortgage of real estate or personal property, or by conveyance of the same to their members and stockholders, under the name and style of the Yorkville Building and Loan Association, located at Yorkville, in the State aforesaid. The capital stock of said Association shall consist of one thousand Capital stock but as soon as two hundred and fifty shares or more are subscribed there to the said Association shall organize and commence operations, the said shares to be paid by successive monthly installments of one dollar on each share as long as the corpora-tion shall continue; the said shares to be held, transferred, Shares of stock. assigned and pledged, and the holders thereof to be subject to such fines and forfeitures for default in their payments, as may be provided by the by laws and other regulations of said corporation.
SEC. 2. That the said corporation shall have power to take, purchase and hold real estate, and to sell and transfer the same from time to time on such terms and under such conditions and subject to such regulations as may be provided by the rules and by laws of said corporation: Provided, That the real estate held by said corporation shall not at any time exceed the value of two hundred thousand dollars.
SEC. 3. That the said corporation shall have all the rights, powers and privileges set forth and granted by an Act entitled " An Act to provide for and regulate the incorporation of building and loan associations in this State, " approved December 22d, 1885, and shall be subject to all the provisions and limitations therein contained, except that the corporation shall have Loans to others the power to loan whatever amount may, at any time, be on hand to others than stockholders whenever the same shall remain unproductive and uncalled for the space of one week.
SEC. 4. This Act shall be deemed a public Act, and the same When to take may be given in evidence without specially pleading the same, and shall take effect immediately on its passage.
