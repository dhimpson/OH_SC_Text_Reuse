" AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION 2 OF AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN
ACT TO REGULATE THE DIETING OF ALL PRISONERS BEFORE
AND AFTER CONVICTION WHEN IN THE CUSTODY OF THE
SUPERVISORS AND SHERIFFS OF THE STATE,"" APPROVED
THE NINTH DAY OF MARCH, A. D. 1896."
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Section 2 of an Act entitled “An’ Act to regulate the dieting of all prisoners before and after conviction when in the custody of the Supervisors and Sheriffs of this State,” approved the ninth day of March, A. D. 1896, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the word “Spartanburg” on the eighth line and “Beaufort” on the twelfth line of said Section 2, and by inserting “Chesterfield” after “Charleston;” by striking out “Kershaw,” and by inserting “Laurens” after “Lexington;” and by striking out “Clarendon.” So that said Section when so amended shall read as follows:
Section 2. That the said County Supervisors and Boards of Commissioners shall furnish suitable covering and clothing, when necessary, to all prisoners sentenced to chaingangs in the various Counties of this State, at actual cost, to be paid by the various Counties, respectively: Provided, The provisions of this Bill shall not apply to the Counties of Marion, Gharleston, Chesterfield, Colleton, Barnwell, Richland, Berkeley, Sumter, Anderson, Pickens, Williamsburg, Newberry, Union, Georgetown, Aiken, Lexington, Laurens, Oconee, York, Abbeville and Darlington.
Srcrion 2. All Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
