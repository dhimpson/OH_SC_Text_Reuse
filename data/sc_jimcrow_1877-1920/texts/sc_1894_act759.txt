"AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AMEND THE CHARTER OF THE TOWN OF SUMMERVILLE,""APPROVED DECEMBER 22ND, A. D. 1885."
Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That Section 2 of an Act entitled " An Act to amend the charter of the town of Summerville " be, and the same is hereby, amended so that said Section when so amended shall read as follows:
Section 2. The said town shall be governed by an Intendant and six (6) Wardens, who shall be citizens of the State, twenty - one (21) years of age, and shall have resided at least twelve (12) months within the corporate limits of the town immediately preceding the day of their election. The said Intendant and Wardens shall be known and called the " Town Council of Summerville, " and shall be elected at the same time on the second (2d) Wednesday in July of every second year, and shall hold office for the term of two years, and until their successors shall have been duly elected and qualified. The next election shall take place on the second Next election. Wednesday in July, A. D. 1895, and the present Intendant and Wardens and other officers shall hold office until their successors are elected and qualified. The Intendant and three (3) of the Wardens, or any four (4) of the Wardens, shall constitute a quorum for the purpose of transacting business or exercising the powers herein granted to the " Town Council of Summerville, " or the said Intendant and Wardens.
Sec. 3. That Section 4 of said Act be, and the same hereby is, amended so that said Section when so amended shall read as follows:
Section 4. For the purpose of conducting the elections for members of the Town Council, it shall be the duty of the Governor, at least sixty (60) days before the next election, and at least sixty (60) days before each succeeding appoint Comelection, to appoint three (3) electors, who shall constitute a Board of Commissioners of Election for the town of Summerville, who shall hold their office, unless removed by the Governor, until sixty (60) days before the next election for members of the Town Council of said town, and until their successors are duly appointed and qualified. The Commissioners of Election shall appoint three (3) Managers of Election, who shall on the first Wednesday in May immediately preceding the second Wednesday in July appointed for holding such election, for two consecutive days, at or near the depot of the South Carolina and Georgia Railroad Company, open lists for the registration of the qualified voters of the town who are not entitled to vote upon a property qualification, between the hours of seven A. M. and twelve M., and again between the hours of three P. M. and six P. M. The after taking the oath of office prescribed by the Constitution of the State, Section 30, Article II., before any person qualified to administer an oath, shall administer the same to the two other Managers. The Managers shall administer to every person offering to register the following oath: " You do solemnly swear that you are duly qualified to vote Oath for regisfor members of the Legislature of the State of South Carolina, and that you have resided within the corporate limits of this town for the four months last past, and that it is your intention to continue to reside within the corporate limits until the day for holding the ensuing election, and that you have truly stated your name and place of residence; so help you God. " Upon the Managers being satisfied of such person's right to register, they shall duly register his name and place of residence, and issue to him a certificate of his registration, which certificate shall state the name of the voter, the date of registration, his place of residence, and shall be signed by the Chairman of the Board of Managers and by their Clerk. The registration list, when completed, shall List. be duly sealed up and delivered by the said Managers to the Commissioners of Election, together with all books and papers that may pertain to such registration, and the same shall be kept by the said Commissioners, unopened, and shall be so returned to the said Managers on the day of the said election.
Sec. 3. That Section 5 of said Act be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out " May " where the same occurs on the first line of said Section and inserting in lieu thereof " July. " So that said Section when so amended shall read as follows:
Section 5. On the second (2d) Wednesday in July, appointed for holding the election for members of the Town Council, the Managers of Election shall open the polls at or near the depot of the South Carolina and Georgia Railroad Time and Company at 6 A. M., and keep the same open until 6 P. M. Every person entitled to vote upon a property qualification as set forth in Section 3 of this Act shall be allowed to vote upon taking the following oath: " I do solemnly swear Oath for voter. that I am duly qualified to vote at this election for the members (or member, as the case may be,) of the Town Council of Summerville; so help me God. " If any person's right to vote on a property qualification is denied, the Managers shall require some evidence of his title to the property giv ing the qualification. No person, except those who vote on a property qualification, shall be allowed to vote who does not deliver to the Managers his certificate of registration, to be by them destroyed. If the right of any person to vote be challenged, the Managers shall decide the matter summarily, and are hereby authorized to examine such person under oath as to his right to vote. Every person proposing to vote shall be required by the Managers to vote his ballot Manner of enclosed in an envelope to be furnished by the Managers. Upon the closing of the polls, the Managers shall proceed to count the votes publicly, and every envelope which shall contain more than one ballot shall, with whatever it contained, be thrown out and not counted. They shall make return of the number of votes cast for each individual, and deliver the same, together with the poll and register lists, ballot box and ballots, to the Commissioners of Election.
Sec. 4. That Section 8 of said Act be, and the same is hereby, amended so that said Section when so amended shall read as follows:
Section 8. In case a vacancy occurs in the office of Intendant, by death, resignation or otherwise, an election to fill such vacancy shall be held by the appointment of the Wardens, or Warden if there be only one, ten days ' notice being given of the time when and the place where a registration of the votes will be taken, and that such election will be held twenty days thereafter. Said election to be conducted under the regulations hereinbefore provided for the regular election of members of the Town Council.
In case a vacancy occurs in the office of any one or more of the Wardens, by death, resignation or otherwise, the vacancy or vacancies shall be filled by the remaining members of the Town Council.
Sec. 6. That the Town Council of the town of Summerville, in this State, be, and the same is hereby, authorized, Encroachments in cases where any person or persons have encroached by remedied. means of buildings, fences or other structures upon the streets or roads or premises claimed to be part and parcel of the streets or roads of the said town, in its discretion, to treat and arrange with said party or parties in regard to the same, with power to agree that the portion encroached upon or any part thereof shall be and become the property of the encroaching party or parties, upon such terms and for such considerations as may be mutually agreed upon; and also in its discretion to arrange with any such party or parties for the proper location of the line and lines between the streets aforesaid and private property, and on such arrangements being made, and said lines duly established, the same shall be the true lines, notwithstanding that what was or was claimed to be a part of any street may thus become a part of private premises: Provided, however, That in any Proviso. and all cases where no such arrangement shall be proposed or completed, and until any such arrangement shall be agreed upon and completed, nothing herein contained shall be construed to prejudice the State, or any person or persons, or the said Town Council, in respect to any and all rights and remedies and the exercise thereof regarding obstructions of or encroachments upon the streets or roads of the said town.
