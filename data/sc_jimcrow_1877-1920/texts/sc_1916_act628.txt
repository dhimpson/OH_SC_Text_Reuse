AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAW AS TO THE ELECTION, POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE TRUSTEES OF THE GRADED SCHOOL DISTRICT OF THE TOWN OF BISHOPVILLE,"" APPROVED THE 22D DAY OF FEBRUARY, A. D. 1904, SO AS TO FURTHER REGULATE THE ELECTION AND TERM OF OFFICE OF THE TRUSTEES FOR SAID SCHOOL DISTRICT.
Section 1. Election of Trustees Number Term. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina:
That Section 2 of an Act entitled " An Act to amend the law as to the election, powers and duties of the Trustees of the Graded School District of the town of Bishopville, " approved the 22d day of February, 1904, be amended by striking out the words, " Whose term of office shall be for two years and until their successors are elected, said election to be held on the second Tuesday in April, 1905 " and by inserting in lieu thereof the following: " Consisting of five members said election to be held on the second Tuesday in April, 1917, " and by inserting after the words, " Every second year thereafter, " the following: " The trustees elected at the election held under this Act shall determine their term of office by lot, so that one of said trustees shall hold office for two years, two of said trustees for four years and two of said trustees for six years; and that thereafter the term of office of each trustee shall be for a period of six years, " so that said Section 2 of said Act shall read as follows:
Section 2. That the School Trustees of said School District shall have power and they are hereby required to call a meeting of the qualified electors of said school district for the purpose of electing a Board of Trustees for said school district consisting of five members, said election to be held on the second Tuesday in April, 1917, and on the same day of every second year thereafter, the Trustees elected at the election held under this Act shall determine their term of office by lot, so that one of said trustees shall hold his office for two years, two of said trustees for four years, two of said trustees for six years; and that thereafter the term of office of each trustee shall be for a period of six years. The trustees shall appoint the managers and publish a notice of said election for at least two weeks previous thereto in one or more newspapers published in the county, and post the same in one or more conspicuous places in said school district. Said election shall be conducted according to the rules and regulations prescribed for conducting town elections in the town of Bishopville: Provided, If from any cause there be a failure to hold such election on the said day in any year, the acting trustees shall have the authority and it shall be their duty to name the managers and give notice of the election to be held as soon thereafter as practicable for the election of trustees of said district, to hold office until the expiration of the regular term under this section. In addition to the duties and responsibilities now provided by law for the trustees of school districts, the said Trustees shall have the following powers and duties, to wit: To purchase and erect suitable buildings for the use of the public schools of the said School District, to elect and dismiss superintendents and teachers of the schools of the district, prescribe their duties, terms of office, and to fix their salaries, and to cause an examination of said teachers to be made whenever in their judgment it is necessary; to determine the class of books and studies to be used in said schools, and to make rules and regulations for the government of said schools; to determine the manner in which the tax heretofore authorized and the three mills constitutional and poll tax provided by law shall be expended in maintaining said public schools, and to fill all vacancies occurring in said Board of Trustees by death, resignation, removal, refusal to qualify, or otherwise, during their term of office or service; to grant high school diplomas to pupils finishing the high school course in the white school, and to make an annual report to the State Superintendent of Education as to the schools as is required of Trustees of the county schools; and to take in charge and keep in order all buildings and property used for school purposes in said district; to determine the manner in which the tax provided for shall be expended in maintaining the schools of the district; to assess and collect from each pupil in the white school an incidental fee which shall not exceed two dollars ($ 2.00) per session: Provided, That no pupil shall be deprived of the privileges of school on account of inability to pay the contingent fee.
§ 2. Immediately Effective. This Act shall go into effect. immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
