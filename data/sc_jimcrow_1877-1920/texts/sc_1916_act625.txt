AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE SCHOOL TRUSTEES OF LANCASTER SCHOOL DISTRICT TO HOLD AN ELECTION ON THE QUESTION OF MAINTAINING A PUBLIC LIBRARY, BY AN ANNUAL TAX OF ONE-
HALF OF ONE MILL.
Whereas, Mr. Andrew Carnegie proposes to devote not less than six thousand dollars to the erection of a public library within Lancaster School District, No. 14, of Lancaster county, upon conditions that the town of Lancaster, or citizens thereof, furaish a lot therefor, free of cost, and the School District supply the funds for maintenance of the same; now, therefore, Section 1. Election for Public Library Authorized.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State: That said School Trustees, upon a petition of a majority of the freeholders of said school district, in that behalf, be and are hereby authorized and empowered and required to hold an election on the question of an annual levy of one-half of one mill, on all the property returned in said district for taxation, for the maintenance of a public library therein; said election shall be held on a day to be fixed by said Trustees, after three weeks’ au advertisement in a newspaper published at Lancaster, at which election all qualified electors of said district, shall be entitled - “~~ to vote.
§ 2. Form of Ballot.—The said Trustees shall furnish a sufficient and equal number of ballots for said election as follows: One set of ballots shall have plainly printed or written thereon the following words: “In favor of the annual levy of one-half of one mill for public library, “Yes;” the other set of ballots shall have plainly written or printed thereon the following words: “In favor of the annual levy of one-half of one mill for public library, No.” § 3. Oath for Trustees.—Before holding said election the said Trustees shall subscribe to the oath usually provided for in case of managers of general elections, which oath shall be taken before the Clerk of the Court and filed in his office for record.
§ 4. Tax Levy—Disbursement.—If a majority of the ballots cast at such election be in favor of said annual levy, said Trustees shall make return thereof to the County Auditor of Lancaster County, under oath, stating the true number of ballots cast in favor and against the same, which return shall become a permanent record of his office, and said Auditor and his successors in office be, and are hereby authorized and required, annually thereafter, to levy upon all the property returned for taxation within said school district, one-half of one mill on the dollar, and the County Treasurer collect the same, as a special fund for the maintenance of a public library therein, to be paid to the Trustees, or other authorized officers of such public library, upon their warrant.
