AN ACT TO PROVIDE THAT HORRY COUNTY SHALL ISSUE ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS IN BONDS FOR PERMANENT HIGHWAY IMPROVEMENT UPON THE SAME BEING APPROVED BY THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF SAID COUNTY, AND TO CREATE A HIGHWAY COMMISSION FOR SAID COUNTY.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the County State of South Carolina, That Horry County shall issue one hundred thousand dollars in registered or coupon bonds for permanent highway improvement. Said bonds shall be of the denomination of one thousand dollars each and shall draw interest at the rate of not more than five per cent. per annum, payable on the first day of January and July of each year.
SEC. 2. That said bonds shall be signed by the County Supervisor and attested by the Clerk of the Board of County Commissioners and delivered to the public highway commission hereinafter provided for. The lithographing of the names of the said officers upon the coupons, if coupon bonds are issued, shall be deemed the signing thereof, but the bonds themselves shall be signed by the said officers personally. When sold the official seal of the County Board of Commissioners shall be affixed, and no bond shall be valid until said seal is affixed. Said bonds shall all bear the same date and shall be numbered consecutively as issued, from No. 1 upwards; and a record of same by number shall be kept in the office of the County Supervisor and the disposition of same recorded therein. When any such bond shall have been redeemed or paid, it shall be mutilated and filed in the Supervisor's office, and the fact recorded in the book above provided.
SEC. 3. After said bonds have been signed and recorded in the Supervisor's office as above provided, the same shall be delivered by the Supervisor to the public highway commission hereinafter provided for. The said public highway commission shall also keep a record of said bonds by number consecutively, and the disposition of same, in a book kept for that purpose.
SEC. 4. One - half of said bonds shall become due and payable not more than twenty years from the date of their issue, and the other half shall become due and payable not more than thirty years from date of issue. For the purpose of paying interest on said bonds and of creating a sinking fund with which to redeem same at their maturity, there shall be levied each year a two mill tax. It shall be the duty of the County Treasurer to collect said tax, keeping a separate and accurate account of same, and upon application by the Treasurer of said commission to turn the same over to him as collected, whose receipt shall be a sufficient discharge of the County Treasurer for the amount or amounts so paid. It shall be the duty of A. D. 1910. the public highway commission, by its warrant upon the Treasurer of said commission, to pay the interest on said bonds as it becomes due and also to redeem the bonds themselves as they fall due out of the moneys so turned over by the County Treasurer. If, at any time, said commission shall have any moneys undisposed of or on hand for any purpose, they shall invest the same safely and securely or deposit the same in some reliable and safe bank or banks at as high a rate of interest as it can obtain. It shall in no case be the duty of the purchaser or purchasers of any of said bonds to see that the funds are properly expended. Said bonds shall be exempt from State, county, school or municipal taxation.
SEC. 5. The public highway commission shall be composed of Public three citizens of the County of Horry, to be appointed and Commission. Highway commissioned by the Governor on the recommendation of the Senator and members of the House of Representatives of said county, and no members thereof shall hold any public office except that of notary public. One of said commissioners shall reside in the town of Conway, one on the east side of Waccamaw River, and one outside of the town of Conway and west or north of the Waccamaw River.
SEC. 6. Said commission shall, as soon as convenient after appointment and commission, elect one of their number to act thereof. as chairman and treasurer, and another to act as secretary. The one elected to act as chairman and treasurer shall receive the sum of five dollars per day, and the other two the sum of three dollars per day, for not exceeding fifty days in any one year. The chairman and treasurer shall give bond in some approved surety company to the county in the sum of ten thousand dollars, conditioned for the faithful performance of his duty, the cost of which shall be paid by the commission out of the funds herein provided. The commissioners herein provided for shall be appointed as soon as convenient after the election hereinafter provided for.
SEC. 7. One member of said public highway commission shall be appointed for two years, one for three years and one office. for four years. At the expiration of said terms their successors shall be appointed in the manner heretofore provided for four years, respectively, and shall serve until their successors have been recommended, commissioned and qualified.
SEC. 8. The said commissioners shall have power and authority, and it shall be its duty to lay out, survey, change, alter, open and make public highways in Horry County, and for any of said purposes it is hereby given full power and authority: Provided, That the County Supervisor shall be ex officio a member of said board, and have a vote thereon upon all questions of improving the highways in the county under consideration. Should there be a tie vote upon any such questions, then it shall be the duty of the Clerk of the Court of said county to cast the deciding vote, which shall be final.
SEC. 9. Said commission shall not at any time or place of expend any money upon any highway unless the parties whose properties touch or abut upon said highway shall, without expense or cost to the county, convey in writing all easements and rights of way necessary over and along same: Provided, This restriction shall not interfere with the right of the commission to improve any portions of said highways that do not touch or abut upon the property owner refusing to give the right of way. The commission shall have full power to employ competent engineer or engineers, contractors or overseers, who shall be paid such compensation, as the commission may fix, out of the funds herein provided. Said commission shall file with the County Supervisor four times each year quarterly a full statement of their receipts and disbursements.
SEC. 10. Said commission shall expend the funds arising How funds from the sale of the bonds dividing the same among the various townships of the county in proportion to the assessed values in said township: Provided, That due consideration shall be given to the mileage and condition of the various roads of the respective townships in the discretion of said commissioners. SEC. 11. The drawings of any fictitious or fraudulent orders drawing ficor the expenditure of any of said funds for any purposes except as herein provided shall be a breach of trust, and upon conviction the party so offending shall be fined or imprisoned, or both, at the discretion of the court.
SEC. 12. The above bonds shall not be issued and nothing shall be done towards their issue until the same have been
approved by a majority of the qualified electors of said county A. D. 1910. voting at a special election to be held for the purpose, on the second Tuesday in November, 1910. The Commissioners of Election for said county shall appoint the managers and receive and tabulate the votes as in other elections. They shall make their report in writing in duplicate, one copy to be filed with the Clerk of Court of Horry County, and the other with the Secretary of State, and said duplicate copy shall be kept on perpetual record in both of said offices. The managers of election shall serve without any compensation; and the managers appointed shall have the power and authority to appoint substitute managers to act in their stead. The County Board of ComBallots. missioners of said county is authorized and required to furnish to the Election Commissioners of said county, in ample time before said election, a sufficient number of ballots for and against said coupon bond issue. On one set of the ballots shall be printed the words, " For Public Highway Improvement Bonds, " and on the other set shall be printed the words, " Against Public Highway Improvement Bonds; " and the said commissioners are authorized to pay the expenses of said printing out of the general county funds. In all other respects, said election shall be held as other elections, under the general laws of this State; and the same regulations apply thereto, in so far as are consistent with this Act. If said election shall be declared for the issue of the bonds, then the provisions made in former sections of this Act be carried out.
