AN ACT TO GRANT TO THE TOWN COUNCIL OF MOULTRIEVILLE A CERTAIN PIECE OR PARCEL OF LAND, WITHIN THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF SAID TOWN, FOR THE PURPOSE OF
ERECTING AND MAINTAINING A TOWN HALL, AND SUCH OTHER PUBLIC BUILDINGS AS MAY BE DEEMED PROPER OR EXPEDIENT.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That all that piece or parcel of land, situate on Sullivan’s Island, and within the corporate limits of the town of Moultrieville, situate on Middle street, west of Fort Moultrie, bounded north on Middle street, east on the United States reservation in which stands Fort Moultrie, south on public land, and west on West Fort street, measuring on north line eighty-one feet and three inches, on east line one hundred and twenty-six feet, on south line eighty-two feet and four inches, and on west line one hundred and fourteen feet, as will more particularly appear by reference to a plat thereof made by S. Lewis Simons, surveyor, dated July, 1898, be, and the same is hereby, granted and ceded to the Town Council of Moultrieville, their successors in office and assigns forever, for the purpose of erecting and maintaining a town hall and such other public buildings as to said Town Council may seem proper and expedient: Provided, That in case the exigencies of war shall at any time require the use of said lot for military purposes, then the State of South Carolina shall have the same right and power of control of said lot as it now has over other lands on the said island, without compensation or other liabilities.
