AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE AND EMPOWER ELLOREE HIGH AND GRADED SCHOOL DISTRICT IN THE COUNTY OF ORANGEBURG, TO CHARGE A MATRICULATION FEE.
Section 1. School May Charge Matriculation Fee - Certain Pupils Excepted. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: That the Board of Trustees of the Elloree High and Graded School, being designated as School District No. 70, in Orangeburg County, are hereby authorized and empowered to charge and collect an incidental or matriculation fee from each pupil, not exceeding two dollars per annum the same to be payable at such times and in such installments as the said trustees may require. The said fees may be used for the purposes of heating, repairing and lighting the school building, for janitors ' service, for fire insurance, and other incidental expenses: Provided, That no pupil shall be prohibited from attending the school for failure to pay the said incidental or matriculation fee, upon satisfactory showing to the said trustees of the inability of such pupil or his or her parent or guardian to pay the same.
