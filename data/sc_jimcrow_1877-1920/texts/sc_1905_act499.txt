AN ACT TO RAISE SUPPLIES AND MAKE APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING 1905.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That a tax of four and one-half mills (4 1-2), exclusive of the public school tax hereinafter provided for, upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property of this State be, and the same is hereby, levied for the purpose of meeting appropriations to defray the current expenses of the government for the fiscal year beginning January the first, 1905, and to meet such other indebtedness as has been or shall be provided for in the several Acts and Joint Resolutions passed by this General Assembly at the session of 1905, providing for the
Same. That in addition to the tax levies above provided for, a tax of one (1) mill is hereby levied upon every dollar of the value of all taxable property of this State, to be used for the payment of pensions; and in case any surplus shall remain after the payment of all pensions as provided by law, the said surplus shall be turned into the general funds of the State Treasury.
