AN ACT to EXPEDITE AND FACILITATE THE CLOSING OF THE AFFAIRS OF THE DISPENSARIES IN BAMBERG, BARNWELL, BERKELEY, CALHOUN, COLLETON, Hampton, KERSHAW, LEE, LEXINGTON, SUMTER AND WILLIAMSBURG, FAIRFIELD COUNTIES.
Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That within sixty (60) days after the approval of this Act the County Dispensary Boards of the following named counties, to wit: Bamberg, Barnwell, Berkeley, Calhoun, Colleton, Hampton, Kershaw, Lee, Lexington, Sumter and Williamsburg, Fairfield, shall convert all of the available assets of the county dispensaries of the within named counties into cash, and after having paid over to the County Treasurer of the respective counties a sum of money sufficient to meet all claims that have been approved by the Dispensary Auditor, and paying all current expenses, shall disburse any funds remaining in their hands as now provided by law.
Sec. 2. That any remaining assets shall be turned over to the County Supervisor, who shall receipt for and dispose of same on such terms as he deems wise
Sec. 3. That the compensation for each member of the respective County Dispensary Boards shall be twenty-five dollars, and no more for services rendered after the passage of this Act
Sec. 4. That the respective County, Treasurers of the within named counties shall pay, upon the advice of the Dispensary Auditor, such just claims as have been filed against the counties
Sec. 5. That the Dispensary Auditor be, and is hereby, authorized to appoint one assistant to aid him in investigating the accounts and affairs of the fifteen counties voting out the dispensary. in August, 1909, at a salary of $5.00 per day, with necessary traveling expenses; same to be paid by each County Dispensary Board as the services are rendered.
Sec. 6. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
