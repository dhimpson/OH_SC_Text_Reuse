State of South Carolina, That the question of the assumption by the County of Greenville of the bonded debt of Dunklin and Oaklawn Townships, in said county, issued in pursuance of the Act of 1906, XXIV Statutes at Large, 309, be submitted to the qualified voters of Greenville County at the general election to be held in 1910.
SEC. 2. That every voter who favors said assumption shall deposit in a ballot box prepared for the purpose, a ballot, upon which shall be printed or written, " Assumption of Dunklin and Oaklawn Bonds - Yes, " and every voter who is opposed thereto, a ballot upon which shall be printed or written the words, " Assumption of Dunklin and Oaklawn Bonds - No. "
SEC. 3. That said election shall be conducted by the same tion conduct officers, in the same manner and subject to all rules and regulations applicable to the general election.
