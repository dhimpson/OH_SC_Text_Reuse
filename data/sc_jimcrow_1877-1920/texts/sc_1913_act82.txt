AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR AN ELECTION ON THE QUESTION OF EXEMPTION OF A CERTAIN PORTION OF BERKELEY COUNTY FROM THE OPERATION OF THE GENERAL STOCK LAW.
Section 1. Election to Exempt Portion of Berkeley from Stock Law.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That the question of exemption, or no exemption from the operation of the general stock law of all that portion of Berkeley county, beginning at Wallace Smith’s place on the new road and up the Sand Ridge road to a point one-fourth mile below Singletary’s, on State road; thence across woods to a point one-fourth mile below Singletary’s on the old State road and on down the east side of the old State road to the present exemption line, near Budd’s place, be submitted to the qualified electors who are freeholders, residents within said territory, at an election to be held on the second Tuesday in May, 1913.
§ 2. Managers to Be Appointed.—That Parish Commissioners of the upper Goose Creek parish are hereby ordered to appoint managers who shall hold and conduct said election, without compensation, and the Parish Commissioners shall receive the returns and declare the result.
§ 3. Ballots—Those favoring exemption shall cast a ballot on which shall be printed or written the words, “For exemption from general stock law;” those opposing shall cast a ballot on which shall be printed or written the words, “Against exemption from general stock law.” If a majority of votes cast shall be in favor of exemption then said territory shall be exempt, but if a majority of votes cast shall be against exemption then said territory shall remain under the general stock law, as at present.
§ 4. Tax Levy to Erect Fence.—There is hereby levied a tax of ten cents on each head of cattle and five cents on all hogs, sheep and goats. which shall be collected by the County Treasurer at the same time as other taxes are collected and shall be used for the purpose of erecting and maintaining a line fence on the dividing line above described.
§ 5. Effective December 1, 1913.—This Act shall go into effect on the first day of December, 1913.
