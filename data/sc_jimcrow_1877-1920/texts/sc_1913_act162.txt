"AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR AN ELECTION ON THE ISSUE OF FIFTY THOUSAND ($50,000) DOLLARS IN COUPON BONDS BY RICHLAND COUNTY FOR THE PURPOSE OF ERECTING A JAIL FOR SAID COUNTY, AND FOR THE ISSUANCE OF SAID BONDS IN CASE THE SAID ELECTION RESULTS
IN FAVOR THEREOF.
Section 1. Bond Election in Richland County for Jail. — Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an election shall be held in the county of Richland on the first Tuesday in April, 1913, at which election the question of an issue of fifty thousand ($ 50,000) dollars of twenty - year coupon bonds, bearing interest at five per cent., payable semiannually, at such times as shall be designated by the Supervisors and Commissioners hereinafter provided for, shall be submitted to the qualified electors of said county.
§ 2. Election. The said election shall be held under the rules. and regulations governing general elections, and the Election Commissioners shall provide two sets of tickets for the use of the voters at said election, on one set of which shall be printed, " For the Issuing of Bonds, " and on the other set shall be printed, " Against Issuing Bonds. "
§ 3. Bonds to Be Issued. If a majority of the votes cast at said election be in favor of the issuing of said bonds, the Supervisor and County Commissioners of Richland county, elected at the last general election, shall issue said coupon bonds for the amount hereinbefore specified.
§ 4. Signature of Bonds. - That said bonds shall be signed by the Supervisor and members of the Board of County Commissioners, who shall affix their official seal thereto, and shall be in the sums of five hundred ($ 500) dollars each. The said Supervisor and Board of County Commissioners shall also sign the coupons of the said bonds in the same manner as the said bonds are signed: Provided, That their names may be lithographed on the said coupons.
§ 5. Sale and Proceeds. - That said bonds shall be sold by the said Supervisor and County Board of Commissioners and the proceeds derived from the said sale shall be used by them in the erection of a jail for Richland county, in the city of Columbia.
§ 6. Annual Tax Levy for Interest and Sinking Fund. - Upon the issuance of said bonds, it shall be the duty of the officers charged with the assessment, levying and collection of taxes, to levy and collect annually from all property, real and personal, within the limits of said county, a sum sufficient to pay the interest on said bonds, and also one - twentieth of the principal, to constitute a sinking fund with which to retire said bonds at maturity; the same to be paid. over to the County Board of Commissioners to be invested by them for that purpose.
§ 7. Balance of Fund. - If, after the erection and completion of said jail, there remains a balance of said sum unexpended, said balance shall be added to the fund herein provided for the retirement of said bonds at maturity.
§ 8. Election to Be Advertised. That said election shall be advertised for a period of fifteen days in two newspapers printed in the said county prior to the time of the holding of same.
§ 9. Plans and Specifications. - That the said Supervisor and County Board of Commissioners shall employ an architect to draw plans and specifications for said jail and shall advertise for bids for the erection of said jail, in accordance with said plans and specifications. Said bids shall be filed with the Clerk of said Supervisor and Board of Commissioners and shall be opened publicly on the day specified in said advertisement. The said Supervisor and Board of Commissioners shall accept the lowest responsible bid for said work: Provided, That any and all bids may be rejected by the said Supervisor and Board of Commissioners.
§ 10. Bond of Contractor. - Before entering into a contract for the erection of the said jail, the person or persons who shall be awarded the said contract shall enter into a good and sufficient bond in the sum of ten thousand ($ 10,000) dollars, to be approved by the said Supervisor and Board of Commissioners, conditioned for the performance of said contract, according to the plans and specifications submitted by said architect.
§ 11. Inconsistent Acts Repealed. - All Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.
§ 12. Immediately Effective. - This Act shall go into effect. immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
