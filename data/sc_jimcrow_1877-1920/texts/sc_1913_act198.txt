AN ACT TO REPEAL AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO CREATE A NEW SCHOOL DISTRICT WITHIN THE TOWNSHIP OF BARNWELL, IN BARNWELL COUNTY, TO BE KNOWN AS ""THE BARNWELL GRADED SCHOOL DISTRICT,' AND TO AUTHORIZE THE LEVY AND COLLECTION OF A LOCAL TAX THEREIN,"" AND TO PUT SAID SCHOOL DISTRICT UNDER THE CONTROL OF THE GENERAL SCHOOL LAW.
Section 1. Barnwell Graded School District. - Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to create a new school district within the township of Barnwell, in Barnwell county, to be known as " The Barnwell Graded School District, ' and to authorize the levy and collection of a local tax therein, " approved December 22, A. D. 1886, and all Acts amendatory thereof be, and the same are hereby, repealed.
§ 2. General School Law. That the said school district shall, after the approval of this Act, be governed by the general school law of South Carolina.
§ 3. That no teacher over sixty - five years old shall be employed as a teacher in the said graded and high school.
Approved the 19th day of February, A. D. 1913.
