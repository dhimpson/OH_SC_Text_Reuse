AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE SCHOOL TRUSTEES IN CERTAIN DISTRICTS TO PURCHASE AND MAINTAIN LIBRARIES.
Section 1. Trustees in Certain School Districts Authorized to Maintain Libraries—Proviso.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, In school districts in which are situated towns or cities of more than three thousand inhabitants the trustees are hereby authorized to appropriate out of the annual levy for the support of such schools such sum as may seem to them wise, not exceeding three per cent. of the amount annually raised, for the purchase and maintenance of libraries: Provided, That the provisions of this Act shall apply only to Spartanburg county.
