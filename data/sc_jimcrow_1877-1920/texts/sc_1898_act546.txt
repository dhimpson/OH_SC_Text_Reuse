AN ACT TO RENEW THE CHARTER OF THE UNITARIAN CHURCH IN CHARLESTON.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Church in CharState of South Carolina: (A Concurrent Resolution allowing this Bill to be introduced having been passed by a twothirds vote of each house, as required by the Constitution.) That the charter of the Unitarian Church in Charleston, which was renewed for twenty - one years by an Act of the Legislature of this State, approved March 1st, 1878, be, and the same is further renewed, from and after the time limited for its duration by said Act, and to continue until altered, amended or repealed by the General Assembly.
