"AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AMEND
SECTION 27 OF AN ACT ENTITLED 'AN ACT TO PROVIDE A
SYSTEM OF COUNTY GOVERNMENT FOR THE SEVERAL COUN-
TIES OF THIS STATE,' SO FAR AS IT RELATES TO THE WORK-
ING AND MAINTAINING THE ROADS AND HIGHWAYS IN
THIS STATE,"" APPROVED THE 2D OF MARCH, 1897.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Section 27 of an Act entitled " An Act to amend Section 28 of an Act entitled ' An Act to amend an Act entitled ' An Act to provide a system of county government for the several counties of this State, ' so far as it relates to the working and maintaining the roads and highways in this State, ' " approved March 2, 1897, be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to read as follows:
Section 27. That the County Treasurers of the counties of this State are hereby authorized and empowered to receive from any and all persons liable to road duties: In the County of Abbeville, dollars, to be fixed by the County Board of Commissioners; Aiken, one dollar; Anderson, one dollar; Barnwell, two dollars; Beaufort, two dollars; Berkeley, one dollar; Bamberg, one dollar; Chester, two dollars; Chesterfield, one dollar; Charleston, two dollars; Cherokee, one dollar; Colleton, two dollars; Clarendon, one dollar; Darlington, one dollar; Dorchester, two dollars; Edgefield, two dollars; Fairfield, one dollar; Fiorence, one and one - half dollars; Georgetown, two dollars; Greenwood,
dollars, to be fixed by the County Board; Greenville, one dollar; Hampton, one dollar; Horry, two dollars; Kershaw, one dollar; Lancaster, two dollars; Laurens, two dollars; Lexington, two dollars; Newberry, one dollar; Marlboro, one dollar; Marion, two dollars; Pickens, two dollars; Richland, one dollar; Spartanburg, one dollar; Saluda, two dollars; Sumter, one dollar and fifty cents; Union, one dollar; Oconee, one dollar; Orangeburg, one dollar; Williamsburg, two dollars; York, two dollars, as commutation tax; and all moneys so paid shall be set apart and known as the County Road Fund: Provided, That such commutation tax be paid for the fiscal year 1896 between the first day of March and the first day of April, and hereafter said commutation tax shall be paid for the succeeding year when State and county taxes are paid, and that the County Treasurer shall furnish a receipt to the person so paying the same.
