SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Tax levy State of South Carolina, That a tax is hereby levied upon all for county taxable property in each of the counties of the State, for county purposes, for the fiscal year commencing January 1, 1909, for the amounts hereinafter stated, respectively, that is to say:
Three - mill
SEC. 2. That the county board of commissioners in each of the several counties of the State shall levy a tax of three mills school tax. on the dollar upon all taxable property of their respective counties, for the support of public schools in their respective counties, which shall be collected at the same time and by the same officers as the other taxes for the current year, and it shall be held in the county treasuries of the respective counties and paid out exclusively for the support of the public schools, as provided by law.
