Section 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: That a tax of five (5 mills, exclusive of the public school tax hereinafter provided for, upon every dollar of the value of all the taxable property of this State, be and the same is hereby, levied for the purpose of meeting apPropriations to defray the current expenses of the government for the fiscal year beginning January Ist, 1901, and to meet such other indebtedness as has been, or shall be, provided for in the several Acts and Joint Resolutions passed by this General Assembly, at the session of 1901, providing for the same.
