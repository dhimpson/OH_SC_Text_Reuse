AN ACT TO AMEND ARTICLE VI OF CHAPTER XLIX OF THE CODE OF LAWS OF SOUTH CAROLINA, VOLUME I, RELATING TO MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS, BY ADDING THERETO A NEW SECTION, TO BE KNOWN AS SECTION 2022A.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Civil Code amended by State of South Carolina, That Article VI of Chapter XLIX of adding the Code of Laws of South Carolina, Volume I, relating to be known as municipal corporations, be, and the same is hereby, amended $ 2022a. by inserting after Section 2022, of said Article, another Section, to be known as Section 2022a, which shall read as follows:
Section 2022a. That the city authorities of any incorporated Municipal city or town of this State shall be, and are hereby, authorized corporations may issue and empowered to exchange certificates of stock for any coupon certificates of bonds which have heretofore been issued by said cities or towns, or which they may hereafter issue, by virtue of Sections 2008, 2015, 2021 and 2022, for the like amount on cancellation of said bonds with like amount, rate of interest, time of maturity with legal validity and liability when issued by said cities and towns, as said coupon bonds, and said cities and towns be authorized and empowered to issue certificates of stock when desired by the purchasers of coupon bonds, in lieu of said coupon bonds, and register all such certificates of stock in the names of the holders in a book or books kept for the purpose, and on such terms as said corporate authorities may require, not inconsistent with law.
