AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SOUTH CAROLINA IMMIGRATION ASSOCIATION.
Whereas, a Concurrent Resolution was passed by a two-thirds vote of the General Assembly, allowing the introduction of this Bill; therefore,
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the ciation, char State of South Carolina, That A. J. Mathison, E. J. Watson, J. C. Hemphill, T. H. Rainsford, W. O. Hammond, C. C. Langston, Knox Livingston, E. W. Dobbs, W. B. Moore, E. S. Addison, John Scott, D. A. Spivey, F. A. Burroughs, J. H. David, Wm. Goldsmith, and others, the members of the Executive Committee of the South Carolina Immigration Association, and their successors, be, and they are hereby, made and created a body politic and corporate, under the name and style of the South Carolina Immigration Association, for the purpose of encouraging immigration and securing and bringing to the several Counties of this State white settlers and laborers, and generally to conduct, do and perform whatsoever may be proper and necessary to accomplish the purposes of the Association, not inconsistent with the Constitutions and laws of the United States and of this State.
SEC. 2. That the Association shall have all the powers usual and appropriate to similar corporations and organizations, and especially by the title aforesaid to have perpetual succession, and be capable in law of purchasing, leasing, using, holding, letting, improving and disposing of such real and personal property as may be necessary, proper and in furtherance of the objects of this incorporation; may sue and be sued, plead and be impleaded, answer and be answered unto, defend and be defended, in all Courts of law and equity; may receive and make all deeds, transfers, covenants, conveyances, grants, contracts, bargains and agreements whatsoever necessary and proper for said purposes; and may have and use a common seal, which they may have power to change at pleasure, to make such by - laws for the government of said corporation as are consistent with this Act and not inconsistent with the Constitutions and laws of the United States and of this State; and generally may do every act or thing necessary or proper to carry into effect the provisions of this Act, and promote the objects and designs of said Association and as authorized by this Act.
SEC. 3. That in addition to the usual officers of a corporation, said Association may immediately upon the approval of this Act elect one or more of its members to be known and
designated as Agent or Agents of the South Carolina Immigration Association, who shall hold his office for the term of four years and until his successor is elected and qualified, who shall perform such duties as usually devolves upon such officers in procuring and obtaining immigrants and settlers, and who shall to the extent of the means at his command, obtain and disseminate information relative to the advantages of soil and climate, and the natural resources and industrial opportunities offered in this State to capitalist and immigrants, and persons seeking homes; to act as a medium between land owner and proposed immigrants and settler, and otherwise to do and perform such other labors and duties as may be imposed upon him by the bylaws of the Association.
SEC. 4. That said Association be, and it is hereby, empowered, Powers as to to divide, subdivide, improve and exchange any and all lands etc. which it may purchase; to act as agent or trustee of any land owner or citizen of this State in the sale or leasing of his lands, and to procure immigrants, settlers, tenants or laborers for him; to issue bonds, execute mortgages and enter into contracts as fully to all intents and purposes as a private person, not inconsistent with the Constitutions and laws aforesaid.
SEC. 5. That this Act shall be deemed a public Act and take effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor. Approved the 25th day of February, A. D. 1904.
