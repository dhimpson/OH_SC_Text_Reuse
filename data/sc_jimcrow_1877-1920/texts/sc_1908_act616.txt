AN ACT TO REPEAL THE ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO ENLARGE THE AREA OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF YORKVILLE TO TWO AND ONE-HALF MILES, APPROVED FEBRUARY 15, 1907, AND TO RESTORE THE AREA OF SAID SCHOOL DISTRICT TO ITS ORIGINAL LIMITS OF ONE MILE FROM THE INTER-SECTION OF CONGRESS AND LIBERTY STREETS IN THE TOWN OF YORKVILLE, IN EVERY DIRECTION, SO THAT THE TERRITORY EMBRACED WITHIN SAID SCHOOL DISTRICT SHALL BE THE SAME THAT IS EMBRACED WITHIN THE LIMITS OF THE TOWN OF YORKVILLE, AS WAS PROVIDED IN AN ACT ENTITLED 'AN ACT TO CREATE THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF YORKVILLE, IN YORK COUNTY, AND ENABLE IT TO ORGANIZE A SYSTEM OF FREE SCHOOLS, AND TO LEVY A TAX IN SUPPORT OF THE SAME, AND TO PURCHASE AND
HOLD PROPERTY,' APPROVED DECEMBER 22, 1888, AND ACTS AMENDA-TORY THEREOF.""
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Act Feb. 15 State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to 1907, 25 Stats., 731, enlarge the area of the School District of Yorkville to two and one - half miles, " approved February 15, A. D. 1907, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.
SEC. 2. That the limits of the area of the School District of School DisYorkville be, and they are hereby, restored to its original limits trict of of one mile from the intersection of Congress and Liberty
streets in the town of Yorkville, in every direction, so that the territory embraced within the said School District shall be the same that is embraced within the limits of the town of Yorkville, as was provided in an Act entitled " An Act to create the School District of Yorkville, in York County, and to enable it to organize a system of free schools, and to levy a tax in support of the same, and to purchase and hold property, ” approved December 22, 1888, and the Acts amendatory thereof, except the Act approved February 15, A. D. 1907, which is hereby repealed.
SEC. 3. That this Act shall take effect immediately on its approval.
