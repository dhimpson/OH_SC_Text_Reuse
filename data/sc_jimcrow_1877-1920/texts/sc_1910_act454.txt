AN ACT SUBMITTING THE QUESTION OF THE REPEAL OF A CERTAIN ACT APPROVED DECEMBER 24, 1891, AND KNOWN AS ACT NO. 988, AND FOUND IN VOLUME XX, AT PAGE 1488, WHEREBY CERTAIN TERRITORY IN WILLIAMSBURG COUNTY IS EXEMPT FROM THE OPERATION OF THE GENERAL STOCK LAW DURING A CERTAIN PERIOD OF EACH YEAR, TO A SPECIAL ELECTION TO BE HELD IN SAID TERRITORY.
Whereas, under and by a certain Act Approved December 24, 1891, and known as Act No. 988, and found in Volume XX, at page 1488, certain territory in Williamsburg County is exempt from the operation of the general stock law of the State from the 15th day of December of each year, until the 15th day of March following in each year.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the Election in State of South Carolina, That, within forty days from the Williamsburg County on approval of this Act, the auditing board of Williamsburg General Stock Law. County shall order an election, to be held within thirty days. from the date of said order, upon the question of the repeal of the two Acts hereinabove mentioned; said order shall be published for two weeks in some newspaper published in said county, and shall designate the date and the purpose of such election, the manner of voting, the persons qualified to vote, four voting places in the territory exempted as above set forth, and shall appoint three managers of election for each voting place. Said managers shall take the same oath as required of managers of general elections, shall serve without compensation, and at least one of each set of managers shall be in favor of said repeal and one shall be opposed to said appeal. All persons who are qualified electors, and are freeholders of the territory exempted from the operation of the general stock law by the Acts above mentioned, shall be qualified to vote at said election. The said election shall be conducted during the same hours and in the same manner as provided for general elections. No particular form or size of ballot shall be required, but each ballot shall have plainly written or printed thereon the words " For Repeal " or " Against Repeal. " The managers shall make return of said election to said auditing board, who
shall canvass the vote cast and declare the result of said elecA. D. 1910. tion. If a majority of the votes cast in said election be found and declared to be " For Repeal, " that then, and in that case, immediately upon such declaration the Act hereinabove mentioned shall stand repealed; and if a majority of the votes cast in said election be found and declared to be " Against Repeal, " that then, and in that case, upon such declaration said Act shall stand and remain in full force and operation.
SEC. 2. That this Act shall go into effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
