AN ACT TO MAKE THE TERM OF OFFICE OF THE AUDITOR, TREASURER AND SUPERVISOR OF BEAUFORT COUNTY FOUR YEARS.
Section 1. Terms of Office of Auditor, Treasurer and Supervisor Beaufort County.—Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That hereafter the terms of office of the County Auditor, Treasurer and Supervisor of Beaufort county shall be four years and until his successor in office shall have been elected and qualified.
§ 2. To Be Elected.—That hereafter the Auditor, Treasurer and Supervisor of Beaufort county shall be elected by the qualified electors of said county in the general election of 1914, and in the general election every four years thereafter. § 3. Inconsistent Acts Repealed.—That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and the same are hereby, repealed. -
