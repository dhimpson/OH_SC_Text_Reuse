AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE ELECTION OF SCHOOL DISTRICT TRUSTEES IN LANCASTER AND SALUDA AND KERSHAW COUNTIES.
Section 1. Election for Trustees - Terms - Ballot - Proviso.- Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: The County Boards of Education of the counties of Lancaster and Saluda and Kershaw are hereby authorized and directed to order in each school district of the county an election for public school trustees to be held on the first Saturday in May, 1916. At said election there shall be chosen from the qualified electors residing in the district three trustees, who shall designate by lot, one member to serve for one year, a second member to serve for two years, and a third member to serve for three years. Annually thereafter the said County Board of Education shall order an election in each school district, at which one trustee shall be chosen for a term of three years, and until his successor shall have been elected and qualified. At any election herein authorized, the Board of School District Trustees shall act as managers, shall count the ballots, declare the result and report same in writing to the County Board of Education, and any and all elections for said school District Trustees shall be held under the rules regulating general elections for State and County officers. At any election for School District Trustees, every qualified elector residing in said school district shall be entitled to vote: Provided, That the County Board of Education for Kershaw County shall order such election only in such school districts as lie partly in Kershaw and partly in Lancaster Counties.
§ 2. Oath - Qualification. - Upon notice of election, any trustee shall take and subscribe the oath of office prescribed by Section 26, Article III of the Constitution of this State. Said oath shall be administered free of charge by the County Superintendent of Education, and shall be kept on file in the County Superintendent's office. Any trustee failing to qualify within thirty days after the date of his election shall be disqualified for trusteeship and the vacancy thus created shall be filled as hereinafter provided. All Commissions of trustees shall be issued by the County Board of Education upon the filing of the oath of qualification.
§ 3. Vacancies - Removals. Any and all vacancies from death, resignation, removal or otherwise shall be filled by the County Board of Education for the unexpired term. The County Board is further authorized to suspend or remove from office any District Trustee upon the assignment of written cause after a public hearing, and to fill the vacancy thus created by the appointment of a successor to fill out the unexpired term.
§ 4. Powers - Duties. - All the powers and duties now devolved upon School District Trustees shall continue to be exercised by the Trustees herein authorized to be elected. Nothing in this Act contained shall be construed to effect any existing school district organized and operated under a special Act of the General Assembly, and it is expressly declared that this Act shall apply only to public school districts organized and operated under the general school law of the State.
§ 5. Inconsistent Acts Repealed. - All Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act, be, and the same are hereby, repealed. Approved Feb. 19, 1916.
