AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED 'AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE BOARD OF VISITORS OF THE THORNWELL ORPHANAGE, AND TO AUTHORIZE THE CLINTON PRES-BYTERIAN CHURCH TO TRANSFER CERTAIN PROPERTY TO THE SAME,' APPROVED JANUARY 8, 1895, SO AS TO CHANGE THE NAME OF SAID CORPORATION AND TO CONFER ADDITIONAL POWERS.
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That an Act entitled " An Act to amend an Act to incorporate the Board of Visitors of the Orphanage Thornwell Orphanage, and to authorize the Clinton Presbyterian Church to transfer certain property to the same, " approved January 8, 1895, be amended by striking out all of the said Act and inserting in lieu thereof the following: That the said board of trustees of the Thornwell Orphanage shall be created a body politic and corporate, and shall be known hereafter as the board of trustees of the Thornwell Orphanage, and shall be constituted as follows, viz.: of seven members duly appointed by, or from within the bounds of the Synod of South Carolina, five from the Synod of Georgia and three from the Synod of Florida, connected with the General Assembly of the Presbyterian Church in the United States. This body, duly met, shall add to their number, by election, twelve ministers, elders or deacons, resident in the town of Clinton, connected with the said synods. To these local members they may commit under the name of local board the administration of the affairs between the annual sessions of the body, under such instructions and regulations as they may adopt. The board so constituted shall be a perpetual body, having due succession of officers and members; all vacancies in their body made by death, removal or resignation, being filled as above provided. In case said synods should fail or refuse to fill such vacancies, the same shall be filled by the remaining members of the board.
This board so constituted may select and appoint advisory members, consisting of one person from each one of the othe synods of the Presbyterian Church in the United States, to serve under such regulations and with such right and privileges as may be agreed upon.
SEC. 2. The said Thornwell Orphanage shall be maintained May mainas a home and school for the support and education of white school, children of respectable parentage and deprived of the father by death. Exceptions to this rule may be made only on specific order of the board of trustees. The board of trustees of the Thornwell Orphanage, under said name, shall have a common seal, with power to change, alter and amend the same as often as said board may deem expedient.
SEC. 3. That said board shall have power to purchase, hold, Powers of secure, enjoy, possess and retain to itself, in perpetuity or for any term of years, any tenements or hereditaments or any other property of any nature whatsoever, or to sell or alien the same as the board may see fit, and by its said name to sue and to be sued, plead and be impleaded in a court of competent jurisdiction in this State, and to make such rules and by - laws for its own government as are not repugnant to the laws of the land and for the order and good government and management thereof as may be thought necessary and expedient.
SEC. 4. The said board is authorized hereby to maintain such May mainschool or schools of a collegiate, normal and technical character tain schools. as may be found necessary, and may under the name of the Thornwell College for Orphans and the Thornwell Technical Institute and the Thornwell Home and School, confer such diplomas and degrees as are usual in colleges. It is hereby permitted to the president of the board of trustees of the Thornwell College for Orphans to submit their course of study to the approval of the State Board of Education, and to conform to the course of study required by the said board to secure the degree of Licentiate of Instruction, and when conformed to by students, the said degree shall be conferred upon them as a license to teach in free schools of the State, subject to the approval of the State Superintendent of Education.
SEC. 5. That the said board is prohibited from accepting the guardianship. legal guardianship of any child in the institution either of its
person or property, except that it may refuse to surrender orphans to any but their legally appointed guardians or their surviving parent when their protection is sought by any of their orphans.
SEC. 6. The building and endowments given by the charitable public and committed to the care of the board of trustees of place the Thornwell Orphanage, being of the nature of a trust, the property. said board shall not be allowed to place any lien or mortgage or other indebtedness upon them, nor to hypothecate the same, nor shall the real estate or scholarship endowment of the said orphanage be liable for any debt incurred by said board or by any officer appointed by the said board, all authority given in Section 3 to the contrary notwithstanding. All legacies received shall be placed to the endowment fund, the interest thereon used for the general work of the institution, except where express stipulation otherwise has been made by the last will and testament of the devisor, and it is made the duty of the trustees to see that all funds are administered according to the will of the donor.
SEC. 7. The duty of the controlling synods to and their responsibility for the institution shall consist in the appointment synods. of trustees, as in this charter provided. Whenever the said trustees shall, in the judgment of the synods, fail in the discharge of their duties, it shall be the duty of the synods to remove these trustees and to appoint others. The responsibility for the general government of the home shall rest upon the trustees, so appointed, who shall annually report their conduct of the affairs of the home and schools to the controlling synods.
SEC. 8. No alteration may be made in this charter until the Amendsame has been approved by three - fourths of the board of trustees and adopted by vote of each of the three synods of South Carolina, Georgia and Florida, nor may the name or location of the institution be changed except in the same manner change in the charter shall be valid until made in accordance with the laws of the State of South Carolina, therefor provided.
SEC. 9. That this Act shall be deemed a public Act and continue in force till repealed.
