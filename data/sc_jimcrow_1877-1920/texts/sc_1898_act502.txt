"AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED ""AN ACT TO AMEND AN ACT ENTITLED 'AN ACT TO REDUCE THE REQUIRED HEIGHT OF A LAWFUL FENCE AND TO PUNISH PERSONS FAILING TO MAINTAIN LAWFUL FENCES WHEN ANY STOCK SHALL CROSS THE SAME,'"" APPROVED MARCH 2D, 1896,
SO FAR AS THE SAME AFFECTS BERKELEY COUNTY."
SECTION 1. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina, That Section 4 of an Act entitled " An Act to amend an Act entitled ' An Act to reduce the required height of a lawful fence and to punish persons failing to maintain lawful fences when any stock shall cross the same, " approved March 2d, 1896, so far as the same affects Berkeley County, be amended by inserting therein, between the words " of " and " Berkeley, " the word " Dorchester, " so as to read when so amended:
Section 4. That all Acts and parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed: Provided, The provisions of this Act shall only apply to the Counties of Dorchester, Berkeley and Horry: Provided, This Act shall not take effect until January the first, 1897.
SECTION 2. That so much of this Act as hereby amended as applies to the County of Dorchester shall take effect immediately upon its approval by the Governor.
