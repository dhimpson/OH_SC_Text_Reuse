Should the Senate approve, the Chief Game Warden shall assume the duties of his office by taking oath to adminster the laws before any Notary, Magistrate or Judge or Clerk of Court, and shall furnish bond in the sum of $ 2,000.00, to be approved by the Secretary of State. Should the Governor or the Senate not approve any nomination sent them, the name shall be returned to the said Audubon Society of South Carolina until a person suitable to both the Governor and the Senate shall have been named.
SEC. 3. The Chief Game Warden shall have charge of the Warden force, and exercise supervision over the enforcement and duties. of the laws. He shall be empowered to make contracts with Wardens: Provided, That no Warden shall be paid in excess of the twelve hundred dollars per annum, nor be allowed more than six hundred dollars for traveling expenses. And he shall have the power to dismiss the Wardens at his discretion.
SEC. 4. The State Treasurer shall pay out money from the Game Protection Fund on the order of the State Fish and funds. Game Commissioner, and the said Chief Game Warden shall make an annual report to the Treasurer covering all moneys expended. He shall at the same time make report to the Governor and the General Assembly of the State, covering all matters relating to his department for each year he is in office.

SEC. 6. That this Act shall take effect immediately upon its passage and approval by the Governor.
SEC. 7. That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with this Act be, and are hereby, repealed.
