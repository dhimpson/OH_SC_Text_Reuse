No negro or mulatto shall ever be eligible to any office, civil or military, or give their oath in any court of justice against a white person be subject to do military duty, or pay a poll tax in this state provided always, and it is fully understood and declared, that all negroes and mulattoes, now in or who may hereafter reside in this state, shall be entitled to all the privileges of citizens of this state, not excepted by this constitution.
