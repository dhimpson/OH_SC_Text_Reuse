Sec. 4. That when any three or more of the black or colored taxpayers in a school district shall be desirous of dividing said district, as hereinbefore provided, they shall first cause ten days’ notice to be given, by posting up written or printed notices, in at least three public places within said district, of the time and place that the directors will meet for that purpose, which notice shall, also, contain a description of the boundaries of the proposed new division and it shall be the duty of said directors to meet at the time and place named in said notice, and if a majority of the black or colored taxpayers in said district are in favor of dividing said district, as described in said notice, it shall be the duty of said directors to make said division.
