Sec. 2. Whenever any district shall be established as aforesaid, the trustees or other authorities establishing the same, shall give notice, by public advertisement, to the adult male colored taxpayers residing in such district, to meet at a time and place specified in the notice, and choose the school directors, and such meeting, and all subsequent meetings, for the election of directors, and for other purposes, shall be held and conducted as is directed in respect to meetings for like objects by the said act of March 7, 1838, and the acts amending the same: and the powers, rights, and duties of the directors so chosen, and of their successors, shall be the same in respect to the school officers of their several districts, as are conferred upon, or required of school districts by said acts.
