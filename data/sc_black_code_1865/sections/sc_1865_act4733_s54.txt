LIV. The master shall not be liable for the voluntary trespasses, torts or misdemeanors of his servants. He shall not be liable for servants. any Contract of his servant, unless made by his authority ; the acts of the servant, unless they shall be done within the scope of the authority entrusted to him by the master, or in the course of his employment for the master; in which excepted cases the master shall be answerable for the fraud, negligence or want of skill of his servant. The master's right of self-defense shall embrace his servant.
