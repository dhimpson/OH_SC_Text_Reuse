No person of color shall pursue or practice the art, CoioreJ pertrade or business of an artisan, mechanic or shopkeeper, or aoy other trade, employment or business (besides that of husbandry, or that of a servant under a contract for service or labor,) on his own account and for his own benefit, or in partnership with a white person, or as agent or servant of any person, until he shall have obtained a license therefor from the Judge of the District Court; which license shall be good for one year only. This license the Judge may grant upon petition of the applicant, and upon being satisfied of his skill and fitness, and of his good moral character, and upon payment, by the applicant, to the Clerk of the District Court, of one hundred dollars, if a shopkeeper or pedlar, to be paid annually, and ten dollars, if a mechanic, artisan, or to engage in any other trade, also to be paid annually : Provided, however. That upon Proviso. complaint being niade and proved to the District Judge of an abuse of such license, he shall revoke the same: And provided, also.
