The District Court shall have Constables and power in all cases of tenants holdincr over, cases of forcible entry and detainer, cases under the Insolvent Debtors Acts, where the arrest and detention are under the process of a District Court, all cases under the Prison Bounds Acts, and all matters of District police; the practice being always conformed, in general, to that of the Superior Court, subject to the rules which may be made by the Court of Appeals.
