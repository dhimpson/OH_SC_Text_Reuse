XI. It shall be a misdemeanor for any person not authorized to write or give to a person of color a writing which professes to show evidence of the right of that person of color to sell any product of a farm, which, by the Section last preceding, he is forbidden to sell without written evidence ; and any person convicted of this misdemeanor shall be liable to the same extent as the purchaser, in the Section last preceding, is made liable ; and it shall be a misdemeanor for a person of color to exhibit, as evidence of his right to sell any product, a writing which he knows to be false or counterfeited, or to have been written or given by any person not authorized ; and, on conviction of this misdemeanor before the District Court or a Magistrate, such person of color shall be liable, as, in Section last preceding, the seller is made liable.
