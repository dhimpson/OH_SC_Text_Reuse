LXXVIl. It shall be the duty of the Judge of the District Court, or of any Magistrate, on complaint made to him that persons of color have intruded into any house or upon any premises, as trespassers or otherwise, or that they unlawfully remain therein Avithout permission of the owner, on ascertaining the complaint to be well founded, to cause such persons to be immediately removed therefrom ; and in case of the return of such persons, without lawful permission, the party so offending may be subjected to such fine and corporal punisnment as the Magistrate or District Judge may see proper to impose.
