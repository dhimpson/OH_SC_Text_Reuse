XVIII. If any person shall himself as a servant, asserting or pretending that he hath served in any service in which he shall not actually have served, or with a false, forged or counterfeit eertificate of his character, or shall, in anywise, add to, or alter, efface, or erase any date, matter or thing contained in, or referred to, in any certificate given to him by his last or former master or employer, or by any other person duly authorized by such master or employer to give the same, then, in either of these cases, such person so offending shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.
