IV. The Judge, Clerk and Sheriff of a District Court shall each, before entering on the duties of his office, take the official oath prescribed in the Constitution of the State, and also the following oath, viz: solemnly swear, ' (or affirm) that as (Judge, Clerk or Oaths of office. case may be,) of the District Court for the District of , I will earnestly endeavor to do what is required of me by law, and without prejudice for or against race or color, to conduct myself as becomes an upright and faithful officer." These oaths shall be endorsed on the commission or certificate of appointment of the officer, and with it be entered on the journal of the District Court.
