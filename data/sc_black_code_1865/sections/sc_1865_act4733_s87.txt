LXXXVir. For collection of a tax imposed by the Board aforeby Board. said, every Magistrate of a precinct shall give ten days' public notice, within his precinct, of the classes of persons liable to the tax, the sum which is required from every person of each class, and of the day when payment shall be made. Every person liable to pay, who fails to pay on the day appointed, shall become liable to pay a double tax, if he shall not make to the Magistrate a satisfactory excuse; and against him the Magistrate may issue process in the nature of fieri facias, special attachment, and other process most likely to exact pavment ; any or all of which shall be executed by a Constable or the Sheriff.
