XVI. Four times a year there shall be a quarterly sitting of the be continued so long as business may require, and if necessary, to be adjourned from week to week ; at which, besides any business that may be done at a monthly sitting, may be tried cases of summary process pending in the Court; civil suits, above the summary jurisdiction, wherein one or both of the parties are persons of color, cases of crime or felony punishable by benefit of clergy, of which persons of color may be accused, and any other matters pending in the Court. The proceedings in the cases severally shall be, in general, similar to those in like cases in the Superior Court of law, subject to the diversities in form which may be produced by the rules adopted by the Court of Appeals, as hereinafter provided.
