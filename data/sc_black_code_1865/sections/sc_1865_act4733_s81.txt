LXXXI. When a person of color shall be unable to earn his support, and is likely to become a charge to the public, the father for support o.f and grand-fathers, mother and grand-mothers, child and grandchild, brother and sister of such person, shall, each according to his ability, contribute monthly, for the support of such poor relative, such sum as the District Judge, or one of the Magistrates, upon complaint to him, shall deem necessary and proper ; and on failure to pay such sum, the same shall be collected by summary order or process.
