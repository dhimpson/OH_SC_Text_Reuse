I. Be it enacted, by the honorable the Senate and House of Representatives, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and it is hereby enacted by the authority of the same. That an Act passed on the nineteenth day of December, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-six, entitled "An Act to prohibit the importation of negroes until the jfirst day of January, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-nine," be, and the same is hereby, extended to the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and one.
