Be it ordained, by the honorable the Senate and House of Representatives, met in General Assembly, and by the authoritj- of the same, That any person importing or bringing into this State a negro slave, contrary to the Act to regulate the of debts and prohibiting the importation of negroes, shall, besides the forfeiture of such negro or slave, be liable to a penalty of one hundred pounds, to the use of the State, for every such negro or slave so imported and brought in, in addition to the forfeiture in and by the said Act prescribed.
