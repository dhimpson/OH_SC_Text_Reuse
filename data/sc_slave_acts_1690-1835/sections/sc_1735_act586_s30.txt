XXX. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That all and every person and persons, for every matter or thing whatever, which, before the ratification of this Act, has been done by them in performance, execution or by virtue of a former Act, entitled "An Act for the better ordering and governing of negroes and other slaves, ratified the twentythird day of February, one thousand seven hundred and twenty-two, shall have hberty to give the said Act and any other matter in evidence, as fully as if the said Act was still in force, and shall also have the benefit of the said Act as to the allowance of costs, as if the said Act was still subsisting.
