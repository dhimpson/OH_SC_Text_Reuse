XIV. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That every master, mistress, manager or overseer of any plantation, or owner of any slave or slaves in this part of the Province, shall not, after the ratification of this Act, give or allow any Saturday in the afternoon, to any negro or slave, as hath been accustomed formerly, upon the penalty of seven shillings for every such default made contrary to the true intent and meaning of this Act.
