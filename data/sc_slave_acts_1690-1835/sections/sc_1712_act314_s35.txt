XXXV. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That one Act of Assembly of this Province, entitled "An Act for the better ordering of slaves," ratified in open Assembly, the twenty-eighth day of August, 1701, and every clause, article, sentence, word, matter or thing contained in the same Act, be from henceforth repealed, annulled, revoked, and forever made void , to all intents and purposes whatsoever. Read three times, and Ratified in open Assembly, the seventh day of June, Anno Domini 1712. THOS. BROUGHTON. RICH D. BERESFORD. SAM. EVELEIGH.
