XIII. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That if any person shall send his negro or other slave off this Province, that hath killed another negro or slave, such person shall pay unto the master or owner of such negro, the full value of such negro so killed, as aforesaid. And in case any person shall send, or cause to be sent, his negro out of this Province, that hath killed any white person, knowing the negro to be guilty of such crime, he shall forfeit the sum of five hundred pounds.
