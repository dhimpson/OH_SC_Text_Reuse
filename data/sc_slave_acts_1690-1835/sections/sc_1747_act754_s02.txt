II. And whereas, the said negro man Arrah did return to this Province before the passing any law giving freedom to slaves returning under such circumstances, and as the giving freedom to Arrah in particular may be of great service to the public, and as it is not reasonable the expense of such service should be wholly borne by the said Hugh Cartwright, Be it jurther enacted by the authority aforesaid. That there shall be paid out of the public treasury of this Province, to the said Hugh Cartwright, the sum of two hundred and pounds, current money, in lieu and full satisfaction for the said negro man Arrah.
