XXII. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That no person whatsoever, except the marshal or goaler, shall keep any runaway slave above the aforesaid time of ten ; nor shall the marshal or goaler employ, or sutFer to be employed, any slave or slaves in his custody, nor sutler him or them to want suthcient food and water, on the penalty of five pounds for every such offence ; and if any marshal or goaler shall saft'er any slave in his custody to die for want of food or water, or dry and convenient lodging, the marshal, goaler, or any other person in whose custody the slave was, shall pay the master or owner the full value such slave shall be appraised at by any three freeholders, who shall be required to appraise the same, by warrant or order from the Governor, or any two justices of the peace ; the said value, according to such appraisement, to be recovered by action of debt, in the court of common pleas in this Province, brought at any time within six months after the death of the negro or slave.
