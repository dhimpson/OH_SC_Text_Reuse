I. Be it enacted, by the honorable the Senate and House of Representatives, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That an Act passed the nineteenth day of December, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-six, entitled "An Act to prohibit the importation of negroes until the tirst day of January, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-nine, be, and the same is hereby, further extended to the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and three.
