And it is further enacted, That all slaves shall have convenient clothes, once every year ; and that no slave shall be free by becoming a christian, but as to payment of debts, shall be deemed and taken as all other goods and chattels, and when other goods and chattels are not sufficiet to Jicts relating to Slaves. satisfy the said debts, then so many slaves only as are necessary, as well proportionately out of the slaves assigned for dowry, as those that belong to the heirs and executors, shall be sold for payment of debt; and all negroes and slaves shall be accounted as freehold in all other cases whatsoever, and descend accordingly.
