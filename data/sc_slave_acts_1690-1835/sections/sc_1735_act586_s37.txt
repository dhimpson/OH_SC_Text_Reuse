XXXVII. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That if any owner or master of any negro or other slave shall, after the passing of this Act, any negro or other slave to keep, either in their own name, or in the name or under the protection of the said owners or masters, any house or houses of entertainment or trade, shall forfeit the sum of fifty pounds, current money, to be recovered in like manner as any forfeiture by this Act above twenty pounds is directed.
