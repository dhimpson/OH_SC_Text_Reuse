And beit enacted hy the authority aforesaid, That so soon as thejustices and freeholders shall be assambled, as aforesaid, in pursuance of thisAct, the said justices shall administer each to the other the following oath : "I, trates and freeA B, do swear, in the presence of Almighty God, that I will well and truly holders. and impartially adjudge and determine the matter now submitted to me, according to the best of my skill and knowledge. So help me God." And then one of the said justices shall administer the same oath to each of the freeholders.
