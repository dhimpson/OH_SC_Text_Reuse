XXV. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That in case any person or persons whatsoever, shall happen to be maimed, wounded or disabled, either in pursuit, attacking or taking any runaway negro or slave, that then it shall and may be lawful, after the said person's making it appear, by his own oath, or otherwise, to two justices of the peace and three freeholders, for them to adjudge and determine what shall be allowed to the person or persons so disabled, and by virtue of a warrant, under their hands and seals, directed to the public receiver, such sum or sums of money as they shall direct for the said person or persons relief, and the pubhc receiver is hereby required to pay the same accordingly.
