III. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That if the said officers, or any of them, shall neglect or refuse to execute and perform all and whatsoever is directed and enjoined them by this Act, that then the officer or officers so neglecting or refusing as aforesaid, shall, for each neglect or refusal, forfeit the sum of live pounds each, to be recovered as in this Act is hereafter prescribed in the case of the master or mistresses their neglect or refusal to perform their part, prescribed by this Act.
