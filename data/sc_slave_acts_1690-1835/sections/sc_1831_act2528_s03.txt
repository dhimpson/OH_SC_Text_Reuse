III. No owner of a slave, nor any person having charge of a slave, shall suffer such slave to be employed or concerned in vending of .spirituous liquors of any kind or description, in any quantity whatever ; and any person oflfending against the true intent and meaning hereof, shall, on conviction thereof, be held guilty of a misdemeanor, and shall be fined or imprisoned at the discretion of the court, not exceeding one hundred dollars fine, nor one month's imprisonment.
