II. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That in every Recognizance, action or suit to be brought by any such guardian as aforesaid, pursuant to the direction of this Act, the defendant shall enter into a recognizance, with one or more sufficient sureties, to the plaintiff", in such sum as the said court of common pleas shall direct, with condition that he shall produce the ward of the plaintiff at all times when required by the said court, and that whilst such action or suit shall be depending and undetermined, the ward of the plaintiff shall not be eloined, abused or misused.
