XXV. And he it further enacted hy ihe authority aforesaid. That thfe marshal or gaoler of Charles City and Port, and of each precinct, shall give an account, in writing, at every sessions that shall be held within this Province, whereto such marshal belongs, of what slaves he hath in prison, with their marks and names and sex, and the time they have been in his custody, and, as near as he can learn, how long each slave hath been from his respective owner, on the penalty of seven pounds and ten shillings, proclamation money, for every neglect.
