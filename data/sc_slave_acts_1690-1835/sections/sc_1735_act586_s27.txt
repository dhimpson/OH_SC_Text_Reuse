XXVII. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That no person whatsoever, from and after the ratification of this Act, shall settle or manage any plantation, cowpen or stock, wherein ten taxable negroes or slaves shall be employed, without one white man living and residing on the said plantation, upon the penalty or forfeiture of ten pounds current money, for every month any person shall so offend,; half which to go to the informer, and the other half to the poor of the parish where such plantation lies, to be recovered as provided by the law for the trial of small and mean causes.
