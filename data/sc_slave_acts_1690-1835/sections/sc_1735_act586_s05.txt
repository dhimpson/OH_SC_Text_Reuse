V. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That if any slave so intrusted or licensed as aforesaid to keep or carry a gun, pistol or cutlass, shall shoot or kill any cattle, sheep, hogs or poultry, other than his owner's, or shall suffer it out of his custody to any other slave, who shall do such like injuries, the owner or manager of such slave so entrusted, shall in such case forfeit to the party injured, double the value of the thing so killed, to be recovered by warrant of any justice residing in the parish where such injury is done, upon proof thereof by such offending slave's confession, or by the oath of any one white man, or evidence of two slaves, Avhom the said justice shall have reason to believe on the circumstances before him ; and the slave so offending shall be ordered to be whipt not exceeding twenty laslies.
