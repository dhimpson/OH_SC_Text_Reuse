II. And for the encouragement of such as shall go in pursuit of fugitive or runaway slaves as aforesaid, Be it further enacted by the authority eward. aforesaid. That there shall be paid the sum of five pounds, current money, for every slave apprehended or taken by any party of the patrol or militia as aforesaid, by the owner or owners of such fugitive or runaway slaves, which may be recovered by warrant from any justice of the peace for the county.
