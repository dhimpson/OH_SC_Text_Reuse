WHEREAS, many colored persons from the northern states and elsewhere, have arrived in the ports and harbours of this State, under pretence of their being descended from free Moors, Indians and Lascars on both father's and mother's side, bringing with them certificates and papers purporting that they are of such descent, when on inspection they appear to be mulattoes or mestizoes, by means of which false papers many persons of color are introduced into this State, contrary to the intention of the Act in such case made and provided.
