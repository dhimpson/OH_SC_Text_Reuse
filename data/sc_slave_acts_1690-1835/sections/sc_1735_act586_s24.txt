XXIV. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That in case any person or persons whatsoever, whether a free man or a slave, shall happen to be wounded, maimed or disabled, either in pursuing, attacking or taking any such runaway slave or slaves, then it shall and may be lawful for any two justices of the peace and three freeholders, on the said person's oath or other evidence, to adjudge and determine what shall be allowed to the person so wounded, maimed or disabled, and, by virtue of an order under their hands and seals, shall direct the pubhc receiver to pay to the said person or persons such sum or sums of money as they shall think fit for his or their relief, which the said receiver is hereby required to pay accordingly.
