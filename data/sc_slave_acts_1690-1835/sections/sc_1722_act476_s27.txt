XXVII. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That in case any person or persons whatsoever, shall happen to be maimed, wounded or disabled, either in pursuing, attacking or taking any runaway negro or slave, then it shall and may be lawful, after the said person shall make it appear, by his own oath, or otherwise, to two justices of the peace and three freeholders, for them to adjudge and determine what shall be allowed to the person or persons so disabled, and by virtue of an order, under their hands and seals, shall direct the public receiver to pay such sum or sums of money as they shall draw for the said person or persons relief; and the public receiver is hereby required to pay the same accordingly.
