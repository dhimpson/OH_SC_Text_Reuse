XXIX. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That no person whatsoever, after the ratification of this Act, shall settle or manage any plantation, cow-pen or stock, that shall be six miles distant from his usual place of abode, and wherein six negroes or slaves shall be imployed, without one or more white persons living and residing upon the same plantation, upon the penalty or forfeiture of forty shillings for each month so offending.
