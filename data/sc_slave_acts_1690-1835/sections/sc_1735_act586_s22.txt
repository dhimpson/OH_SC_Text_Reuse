XXII. And he it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That if the marshal or gaoler shall work, or employ, or suffer any slave in his custody to be worked or employed, which was delivered into the custody of the said marshal as a fugitive slave, or shall suffer such slave to want sufficient food and water, the said marshal or gaoler shall, for every day's otience, forfeit to the owner or manager of the said slave, the sum of ten shillings, from the first day he was received into the prison ; and if the said marshal, gaoler, or other person having custody as aforesaid of any slave or slaves delivered to him or them as fugitive slaves, shall let any such slave or slaves starve or perish for want of sufficient food and water, or dry lodging, the said marshal, gaoler, or othei person having such slave or slaves in custody as aforesaid, shall pay to the owner or manager of such slave or slaves, the full value of the same, to be appraised by any three freeholders, by order of any two justices of the peace ; and the said appraised value is to be recovered witliin six months after the death of the said slave or slaves, by action of debt in the court of common pleas of this Province.
