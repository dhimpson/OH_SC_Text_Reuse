XXXIV. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid. That all and every person and persons whatever, for any matter or thing whatsoever, which has been or shall be done by them, in performance, execution, or according to the directions, true intent and meaning of an Act entitled " An Act for the better ordering and governing of slaves," passed the seventh June, 1712, before this Act takes place, shall have the like liberty to plead the general issue, and give this Act, the said Act of the seventh of June, 1712, and any special matter, in evidence, notwithstanding the repeal of the said Act of the seventh of June, one thousand seven hundred and twelve; and if the plaintiif become non-suit, suffer a discontinuance, or a verdict pass against him, the defendant shall recover treble costs of suit.
