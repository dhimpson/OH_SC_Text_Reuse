XXIII. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid , That the marshal or goaler shall give an account, in writing, at every general sessions that shall be held within this Province, of what slaves lie hath in prison, with their marks and names and sex, and the time they have been in his custody, and, as near as he can learn, how long each slave hath been from his respective owner, on the penalty of thirty pounds for every neglect.
