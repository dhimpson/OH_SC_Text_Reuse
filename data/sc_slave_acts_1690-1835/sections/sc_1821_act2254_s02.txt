II. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That if any free negro, mulatto or mestizo shall harbour, conceal or entertain any fugitive or runaway slave, and be convicted thereof before two justices and five freeholders, he shall suffer such corporal punishment, not extending to life Act-o relating to Slaves. or limb, as the said justices and freeholders who try such offender, shall, in their discretion, think fit.
