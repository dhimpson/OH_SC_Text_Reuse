II. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That nothing in this Act shall be construed so as to prevent free persons of color from being taken into custody by the sheriff, or masters of vessels, and other persons, from being prosecuted under the former Act, when there is cause to believe that such persons of color are not of such descent as to exempt them from the operation of that Act ; but all such arrests and prosecutions shall be maintained in the same manner as if this Act had not been passed.
