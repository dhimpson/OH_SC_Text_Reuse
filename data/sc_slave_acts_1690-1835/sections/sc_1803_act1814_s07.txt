VII. And Thomas Hunt is desirous of bringing into this State, certain negroes which have been given him by y, his father : Be it therefore enacted by the authority aforesaid, That the permitted to said Thomas Hunt shall be, and he is hereby, permitted, from and after the certain passing of this Act, to bring into this State, from the Bahama islands, any this State" number of the said slaves, not exceeding fifty ; the said Thomas Hunt, immediately on the arrival of the said negroes in this State, going before one of the associate judges of this State, and making affidavit that the said negroes so brought in, were bona fide the property of the said Thomas Hunt, at the time of the passing of this Act, are negroes of good character, have never been concerned in privateering, and that they are brought in for settlement, and not for sale.
