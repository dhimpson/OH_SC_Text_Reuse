XXXIX. And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That the justices and freeholders assembled in the manner hereinbefore directed, one of which justices and two freeholders, or two justices and one freeholder, agreeing in opinion, shall be deemed a quorum, and are hereby authorized and impowered to give judgment, and order the same to be put in execution, against any slave they shall find guilty of the crime or crimes whereof any such slave shall be accused, or acquit him or them thereof; which judgment shall be final.
