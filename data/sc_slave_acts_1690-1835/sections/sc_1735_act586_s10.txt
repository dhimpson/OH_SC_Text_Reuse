X. And he it Jurtlier enacted by the authority aforesaid. That in case any negro or other slave shall be accused and convicted before two justices and three freeholders as aforesaid, of feloniously stealing any goods, chattels, wares or merchandizes, exceeding the value of twenty shillings, current money, every such slave shall, for the first offence, be branded with an R on the right cheek, with a red hot iron, and be whipped not exceeding twenty lashes ; and for the second offence of the like or greater value, if it shall appear to the justices that he was before convicted of a former felony of the said value of twenty shillings, such slave shall, in such case, be branded with an R on the left cheek, with a red hot iron, and be whipped not exceeding forty lashes; and for the third ofience, every such slave shall suffer death.
