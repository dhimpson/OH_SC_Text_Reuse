But if any person or persons shall actually send or carry away out of this Province, the slave or slaves of another person, without the owner consent or privity, or shall be taken in the act of taking or carrying away any slave or slaves, in order to send or convey them out of this Province, every such offence is hereby declared felony, without benefit of clergy; and if the person or persons so offending, shall be legally convicted thereof at the court of general sessions, assize and general goal delivery, he shall suffer death as a felon, accordingly.
