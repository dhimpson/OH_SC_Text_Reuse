AN ACT TO REGULATE THE PILOTAGE AT THE PORT OF CHARLESTON.

Section 1. Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the State of South Carolina, now met and sitting in General Assembly, and by the authority of the same, That during the month of February succeeding the approval of this Act, and during the month of February of every second year thereafter, it shall be the duty of the Governor, and he is hereby authorized and required, to appoint a Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston, who shall serve and continue in office for the term of two years and until their successors are appointed: Provided, That the Board of Pilot Commissioners for the port of Charleston now in office shall continue to serve until the appointment of the new Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston shall be made as required by this Act. Sec. 2. That the said Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston shall consist of five members, of whom at least one shall be, or shall have been, a seafaring man, and of whom, also, at least one shall be a full branch pilot of the port of Charleston. Sec. 3. That the said Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston shall have power and authority to permit, at their discretion, any person to act as a limited licensed pilot or as a full licensed pilot of the bar and harbor of Charleston, and shall make such inquiries respecting him and his qualifications as to the said Commission, or a majority of them, shall appear necessary and expedient; full licenses to entitle the holder to pilot vessels of any draught of water; limited licenses to entitle the holder to pilot vessels of not more than twelve feet or ten feet, to be expressed in the licenses respectively: Provided, however, That before granting such license to any person it shall be the duty of the Board to appoint three nautical men, of whom one shall be a full licensed pilot of the port of Charleston, to examine the applicant as to his knowledge of the bar and harbor of Charleston, and as to his competency to manage and work vessels; and no license shall be granted to any person unless he received the certificate of such examining Committee of his knowledge and capability: And provided, That no license shall be granted unless the applicant shall have served a regular apprenticeship as provided in Section 4 of this Act: And also provided, That each and every applicant for a full or limited license, before he shall receive a certificate from a majority of the Examining Committee, shall pay to the said Committee the sum of five dollars to defray the expenses of such examination and of issuing said certificate. Sec. 4. That apprentices shall be taken by full licensed pilots only, with the approval of the Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston, and shall serve four years previous to receiving a ten foot branch or license. After holding a ten foot branch or license for two years, if competent to the satisfaction of their masters, they may receive a twelve foot branch or license, which they must hold in service for two years, at the end of which time they may receive a full branch or license. In all cases, how ever, the applicant shall be twenty-one years of age previous to his receiving a ten foot branch or license It shall be the duty of the masters, during the last year of each of the above stated periods of service, whenever in the discretion of the master the same can be properly done, to take the said apprentices on board of vessels of the respective draughts of water to a license for which at the end of the said year the apprentice may be entitled as an applicant, so that they may become completely competent to do their duty Sec. 5. That the Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston shall give to every person appointed by them, either a limited licensed pilot or a full licensed pilot, a certificate of his appointment, signed by a majority of them, or by their Chairman by their direction, and shall administer to such person the following oath, which he shall subscribe, and it shall be filed among the papers of the said Board, to wit: “I, do solemnly swear (or affirm) that I will well and faithfully, and according to the best of my skill and knowledge, execute and discharge the business and duty of a licensed pilot for the bar and harbor of Charleston, and that I will at all times, wind weather and health permitting, use my best endeavors to repair on board all ships and vessels that I shall see and conceive to be bound for or coming into or going out of the port or harbor of Charleston, unless I am well assured that some other licensed pilot is then on board the same And that I will from time to time and at all times make the best dispatch in my power to convey safely over the bar of Charleston every vessel committed to my care in coming And that I will from time to time and at all In or going out times truly observe, follow and fulfill, to the best of my skill ability and knowledge, all such orders and directions as I shall or may receive from the Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston relative to all matters and things that may appertain to the duty of a pilot: so help me God.” Sec. 6. That after any such person or applicant shall have taken the oath or affirmation aforesaid and subscribed the same, the said Board of Commissioners shall require such persons to execute to them and their successors in office a bond, with two sureties, to be approved of by them, in the sum of five hundred dollars if his license be of ten or twelve feet, or in the sum of one thousand dol lars if it be a full license; which bond shall be conditioned for the faithful discharge of his duties as such pilot, whereupon the said Board of Commissioners shall deliver to such person a license, to be according to his respective draughts of water; said license to he signed either by the Chairman of the said Board, under the direction of a majority thereof, or by such majority themselves. That the County Commissioners of Charleston County shall pay all accounts of the said Commissioners incurred for printing licenses or certificates, furnishing stationery and the like: Provided, That such account shall not exceed in the whole the sum of fifty dollars. Sec. 7. That the Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston shall have power and authority for any cause or charge to them satisfactorily proven to order and direct any or all pilots for the bar and harbor of Charleston to deliver up his or their license or licenses and to take out a new license or licenses, but no pilot who shall satisfy them of the groundlessness of such cause or the falsity of such charge brought against him shall be And if the required to pay an additional fe> for his new license. said Board of Commissioners be not satisfied with the sureties on the bond executed by any licensed pilot for the bar and harbor of Charleston, every pilot whose sureties shall be deemed insufficient shall give a new bond, with sureties, to be approved by the said Board of Commissioners, and within such time as shall be required by the said Board of Commissioners, in which case the pilot shall receive a new license on surrendering the former one. And every pilot not complying with these conditions, or any of them, shall forthwith forfeit his license, and shall be disqualified to act as a pilot for the bar and harbor of Charleston for the period of twelve months. The number of pilots for the bar and harbor of Charleston shall be limited to the number of thirty, with power to the said Commissioners of Pilotnge to increase the number to thirty-five, if in their judgment the commerce of the port of Charleston require such increase: Provided, That such limit in number shall not operate as an exclusion of any pilot who at the time of the approval of this Act shall hold a regularly issued license: And provided, That the said Board of Commissioners shall make no distinction in the selection of pilots on account of race, color or previous condition: And also provided, That no pilot shall engage in any other business or calling while holding his license or branch without the consent of the Commissioners of Pilotage first had and obtained in writing. And if he shall refuse to give up the same to the Harbor Master on demand, the latter shall give notice for one week in the public daily papers that such person has no longer a right to act as a pilot for the bar and harbor of Charleston until he be reinstated by the said Board of Commissioners. Sec. 9. That no licensed pilot for the bar and harbor of Charleston shall hire out his pilot boat to any persons or person whomsoever, unless he substitute another, to be approved of by the said Board of Commissioners of Pilotage for the port of Charleston. Sec. 10.
